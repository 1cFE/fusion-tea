## 3 **Executive Summary**
**Lines:** 27-70

Re-examination of costing for four ARPA-E ALPHA fusion concepts using updated reactor costing paradigms, finding that modular design enables shorter construction times and lower costs, yielding ~2.4 $/W CapEx (~$1.2B) and average LCOE of 43 $/MWh for ~500 MWe plants — roughly halving prior Total Capital Cost estimates. Key cost drivers include centralized manufacturing, compact design, and shipping complete subsystems.

## 5 **Principal findings**
**Lines:** 75-282

Costing framework results for four ARPA-E ALPHA compact modular fusion concepts (~500 MWe average), including power balance parameters, detailed capital costs by Cost Accounting Structure (CAS 20–99), and LCOE calculations (average 43 $/MWh, CapEx ~$2.4/W). Key changes from the 2017 study include shortened construction time (6→3 years via modular manufacturing), updated balance-of-plant costs, multi-module power cores, and omitted contingency for nth-of-a-kind plants.
