## 21 Operations and Maintenance Costs, C<sub>O&M</sub> or Accounts 40-47
**Lines:** 3377-3382 | **Subsections:** 21.1

Fusion power plant Operations and Maintenance (O&M) cost accounts (40-47), originally established by Schulte in 1978, cover salaries, supplies, outside services, G&A, coolant makeup, fuel handling, and miscellaneous costs. Two estimation approaches exist—bottom-up (projected staffing/maintenance) and top-down—but both carry large uncertainty due to the speculative nature of operating a 10th-of-a-kind plant decades in the future.

## 23 Scheduled Component Replacement Costs, C$_{SCR}$
**Lines:** 3495-3566 | **Subsections:** 23.1, 23.2, 23.3

Scheduled Component Replacement (SCR) costs cover the periodic replacement of limited-lifetime power core components — primarily first wall/blanket modules, divertors, and heating/current drive elements — whose lifetimes are shorter than the plant's economic life due to neutron damage limits. The section traces the evolving methodology across Starfire, Generomak, Sheffield, and ARIES studies, debating whether SCR items should be capitalized in direct costs or annualized with fuel costs, with the ARIES approach calculating present worth over service periods using a formula: C_SCR = (FWB + divertor direct costs) × CRF × Present Worth × (1+contingency) / Lifetime.

### 23.1 **Fuel Cost, CF**
**Lines:** 3567-3614

Fuel cost (CF) for fusion plants covers deuterium purchase and lithium burnup, with tritium treated as zero-cost since each plant self-breeds it in the blanket. Historical estimates converge on a recommended nominal CF of ~$1M/year (2009$), combining deuterium (~$0.3–0.4M) and lithium burnup (~$0.2M), with scheduled component replacements tracked separately in the SCR account.
