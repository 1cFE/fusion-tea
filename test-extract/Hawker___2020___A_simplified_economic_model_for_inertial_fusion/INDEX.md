## 3 Results and discussion

Monte Carlo correlation analysis of 14 inertial fusion parameters against LCOE (using Pearson coefficients), finding discount rate, plant cost, and target cost as strongest drivers, followed by exploration of individual parameter dependencies (target cost threshold ~$10, gain threshold ~400, frequency optimum dependent on yield), the effect of two gain curve models on achievable cost, and identification of a minimum-cost design point at $24.6/MWh with specific parameter values.

## 4 Conclusion
**Lines:** 642-744

Summarizes key findings from the inertial fusion LCOE model: no single parameter dominates cost (discount rate, plant cost, and target cost matter most), a gain threshold around 400 yields diminishing returns, and higher energy-per-shot with lower repetition frequency unlocks fundamentally lower LCOE — potentially enabling cost-competitive designs at ~150 MWe scale. Also includes references (1–19) covering IFE plant designs (HYLIFE, OSIRIS/SOMBRERO, Z-IFE, LIFE, HiPER), renewable energy cost benchmarks, and foundational ICF physics.
