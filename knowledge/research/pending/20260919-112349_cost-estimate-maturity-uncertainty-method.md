## Maturity assessment

[INHERITED: AACE17R-97,2011 version reproduced with permission in DOE G413.3-21A,2018 AppendixG printedG-5–G-9] The generic framework applies across industries. It identifies Class5 as the lowest project-definition maturity and associates it with screening/feasibility and factor/model/judgment estimates. Its rough0–2% definition band is not itself the class determinant: actual key planning and design deliverables determine class. The appendix names scope, requirements, specifications, plans/schedules, drawings and calculations as relevant evidence. Accuracy must be assessed separately for the estimate; technology and reference-data quality matter. The official2020 AACE sample retains maturity as the primary criterion. Sources: `knowledge/sources/doe_g4133_21a_cost_estimating_guide_2018/` and `knowledge/sources/aace_17r97_generic_cost_estimate_classification_2020_public/`. Original image checks are retained as `evidence/method-source-images/doe-appendixG5.png`, `doe-appendixG6.png`, `doe-appendixG7.png` and `doe-appendixG9.png`. The DOE appendix is a historical2011 version; the2020 public sample confirms the governing principle but omits the full matrix.

[AGENT] Assign **provisional AACE17R-97 Class5 conceptual estimate**, applying the generic maturity criterion to the represented project scope. This is an assessment against published guidance, not AACE certification or a measured engineering-completion percentage. The program has analytical requirements and models, but its inspected evidence does not establish issued procurement specifications, qualified civil/pressure designs, complete process/electrical drawings, site definition or a construction execution schedule. Whole systems still contain scaling allowances and unresolved scope. An automated quantity estimate from assumed geometry does not elevate those deliverables to issued engineering design. Do not attach a generic class accuracy range. Independent review must test this assessment and its account distinctions against the actual estimate basis.
