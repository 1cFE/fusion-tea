This can be seen in Fig. 2 where, for example, 100MW net electric is only _∼_ 17% of the gross electric whereas 1.2GW net electric is _∼_ 42% of the gross electric. At 2GW net electric, this output is _∼_ 44% of the gross electric, meaning that for only 2% more efficient net-gross electric ratio, substantially more power needs to be generated in a larger device, driving up the capital cost and complexity of the plant for very little reduction in LCOE. The initial flatness of the red line in Fig. 1 is due to the initial design being able to generate more electricity in a machine of the same size. Despite minimising major radius, PROCESS cannot make the machine smaller as it is limited by the imposed engineering constraints on the inboard build.
