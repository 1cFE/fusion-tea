# Extrapolating_Costs_to_Commercial_Fusion_Power_Plants__1_ Index

## I INTRODUCTION
**Lines:** 17-249

Covers the introduction, methodology (PROCESS systems code and its LCOE cost modeling with the capital recovery factor formula), scan setup, results, and conclusions of a STEP-based study extrapolating spherical tokamak fusion plant costs to commercial scale, including a net-electric-output scan (100 MW–2 GW) and sensitivity analysis on parameters like blanket/divertor fluence, NOAK factor, thermal efficiency, availability, HCD efficiency, H-factor, and gyrotron redundancy. Concludes the LCOE "sweet spot" is 500 MW–1.2 GW net electric, with the inboard build constraining small STs and divertor heat load (P_sep/R) constraining larger ones.
