## A TYPOLOGY EXPLAINING DIFFERENCES IN TECHNOLOGIES’ EXPERIENCE CURVES
**Lines:** 23-163

Section A introduces a typology that classifies low-carbon technologies along two dimensions—design complexity and need for customization—to explain variation in experience rates (cost reductions per doubling of cumulative capacity). It defines three technology types (Type 1 standardized, Type 2 mass-customized/platform-based, Type 3 highly complex/customized), reviews five methods for quantifying design complexity (DSMs, N/K model on patents, economic complexity index, network analysis, expert elicitation), and derives policy implications for national green industrial policy versus international coordination (e.g., Paris Agreement Articles 6.1 and 10.2).
