# proposed_rules_fusion_regulatory Index

## 10 CFR Parts 20, 30, 37, 50, 51, 72, 110, 150, 170, and 171
**Lines:** 21-336

Proposes the NRC's regulatory framework for fusion machines under 10 CFR Part 30's byproduct material approach, defining "fusion machine" per the ADVANCE Act of 2024, establishing technology-inclusive licensing requirements (definitions, content-of-application, intruder assessment, environmental reporting) across 10 CFR parts 20, 30, 37, 50, 51, 72, 110, 150, 170, 171, and outlining near-term fusion characteristics (no criticality, passive shutdown, <1 rem offsite dose) that distinguish fusion from fission regulation.

## 5 Environmental Review
**Lines:** 415-428

Covers proposed § 51.60(b)(1)(viii) requiring fusion machine license applicants to submit an environmental report under 10 CFR part 51, the NRC's tiered review process (categorical exclusion, EA with possible FONSI, or EIS under § 51.20), Agreement State NEPA-equivalent requirements, and guidance via NUREG-1748 with pre-application NRC consultation under § 51.40.

## 6 Export Controls
**Lines:** 429-452

Section 6 explains why the proposed rule makes no changes to fusion machine export controls: the Department of Commerce already has jurisdiction (since fusion machines don't require IAEA safeguards), consistent with NSG Guidelines and the Additional Protocol which both exclude fusion reactors. Section 7 (Physical Security and Accountability) covers 10 CFR parts 20 and 37 requirements for byproduct material, noting tritium is not risk-significant but case-by-case NRC determinations may apply to unlisted activation products, with guidance in draft NUREG-1556 Volume 22.

## 8 Waste Management
**Lines:** 453-474

Covers proposed NRC amendments to 10 CFR part 20 for fusion machine waste management, explaining how existing 10 CFR part 61 land disposal regulations apply to fusion-generated radioactive waste following the ADVANCE Act of 2024's redefinition of byproduct material. Defines a new § 20.2008(a) requirement that fusion LLW either match § 61.7 waste class descriptions or be disposed at sites with site-specific intrusion assessments (0.5 rem/year dose limit), while deferring revision of § 61.55 waste classification tables for activation products to the integrated LLW rulemaking.

## 9 Reporting and Recordkeeping
