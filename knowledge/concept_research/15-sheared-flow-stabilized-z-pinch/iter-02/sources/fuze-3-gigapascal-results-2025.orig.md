# FuZE-3 Gigapascal Plasma Pressure Results (November 2025)

Source: ScienceDaily summary of Physics of Plasmas paper
URL: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2025/11/251120002836.htm
Also: https://www.zapenergy.com/news/zap-energy-exceeds-gigapascal-fusion-plasma-pressures-on-new-fusion-device-fuze-3

## Key Results

- **Electron pressure**: 830 MPa (megapascals)
- **Total plasma pressure**: 1.6 GPa (gigapascals)
- **Duration**: ~1 microsecond
- **Measurement**: Optical Thomson scattering
- **Electron density**: 3-5 × 10²⁴ m⁻³ (multiple repeatable shots)
- **Electron temperature**: Above 1 keV (~21 million °F)

## FuZE-3 Design

- Third generation of FuZE platform, fifth SFS Z-pinch device overall
- Plasma chamber ~12 feet long
- Plasma filament width: a few millimeters
- **Three electrodes** (new to FuZE-3, previous designs had two)
- Two capacitor banks
- Key innovation: independent control of plasma acceleration and compression (previously linked in earlier designs)

## Context

- Highest-pressure performance to date in a sheared-flow-stabilized Z-pinch
- Important marker on path to Q>1
- FuZE-3 described as "quasi-steady-state magnetic confinement"
