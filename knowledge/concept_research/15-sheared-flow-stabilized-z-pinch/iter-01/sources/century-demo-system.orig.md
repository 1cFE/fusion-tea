## Overview

Century is Zap Energy's first fully-integrated demonstration of three major plant-relevant technologies:
1. Repetitive pulsed power
2. Flowing liquid metal walls
3. Thermal management

## Key Specs

- **Input power**: Up to 100 kW average
- **Liquid metal**: Flowing bismuth (initial 70 kg, final >1 ton / 2,500 lbs)
- **Cooling**: Air-cooled liquid metal-to-air heat exchangers (100 kW duty)
- **Plasma gas**: Hydrogen or helium (no D-T, no fusion reactions, no neutrons)
- **Construction**: Started June 2023, phase 1 commissioning June 2024

## Milestones

- Initial commissioning: 1,080 consecutive shots at 0.1 Hz (1 shot per 10 seconds)
- Feb 2025 DOE milestone: 3-hour campaign, 1000+ consecutive shots at ≥100 kA each
- Sept 2025: 100+ plasma shots at 0.2 Hz (1 shot per 5 seconds), 500 kA current
- Represents 20x increase in sustained average power over 2024 commissioning

## Notes

- Century uses bismuth (not LiPb) as the liquid metal — bismuth chosen for engineering testing without tritium/neutron complications
- Vertically oriented Z-pinch (first for this design in an integrated system)
- DOE Milestone-Based Fusion Development Program participant (selected May 2023)
