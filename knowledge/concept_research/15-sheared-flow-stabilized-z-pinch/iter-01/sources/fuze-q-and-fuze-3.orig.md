## Device Lineage

1. **ZaP** — Original UW experiment (sheared-flow Z-pinch proof of concept)
2. **ZaP-HD** — High Energy Density variant at UW
3. **FuZE** — Fusion Z-pinch Experiment at UW; demonstrated 1-3 keV electron temperatures
4. **FuZE-3** — Zap Energy's third-generation device; achieved gigapascal plasma pressures (830 MPa electron, 1.6 GPa total)
5. **FuZE-Q** — Designed to reach Q=1 (scientific energy breakeven)
6. **Century** — Integrated engineering demo (power handling, liquid metal, thermal systems)

## FuZE-Q Specs

- **Capacitor bank**: ~1 MJ class
- **Maximum current**: Up to 1.5 MA (designed for ~1 MA operation, target 650 kA for key experiments)
- **Pulse forming**: Passive pulse-forming networks (PFNs) with inductively isolated capacitors; fast rise + long current flattop
- **Plasma column**: ~50 cm long, ~1 mm wide
- **Goal**: Scientific energy breakeven (Q=1)

## FuZE-3 Achievements

- Electron pressures up to 830 MPa (1.6 GPa total pressure)
- Highest-pressure performance in a sheared-flow-stabilized Z-pinch

## FuZE Achievements

- 1-3 keV plasma electron temperatures (11-37 million °C)
- Smallest, simplest, lowest-cost device to achieve these temperatures
- Independent neutron measurement by LLNL (2021) confirming fusion with deuterium fuel
