# Ultrafast Laser Architectures for Quantum Control of Nuclear Fusion
**Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.07417
**Author**: Jake Levitt
**Submitted**: 2023-08-14
**Length**: 11 pages, 2 figures

## Summary
- Discusses engineering quantum coherences to accelerate nuclear fusion tunneling
- Presents "a three-body fusion reaction which is amenable to quantum control"
- Describes ultrafast laser architectures that solve the Schrödinger equation
- Claims conditions for net electrical power generation using these optical designs
- References U.S. Patent Application No. 17/855,476 (2022)
