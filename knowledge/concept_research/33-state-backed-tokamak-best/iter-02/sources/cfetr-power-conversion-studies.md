# CFETR Power Conversion System Studies

## Source 1: Study of power conversion system for Chinese Fusion Engineering Testing Reactor
- **URL**: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0360544220326025
- **Journal**: Energy (2021)
- **Key findings**:
  - Exploratory analysis comparing S-CO2 Brayton Cycle vs Steam Rankine vs Helium Brayton for CFETR PCS
  - S-CO2 Brayton Cycle proved suitable for CFETR with advantages of compactness and high cycle efficiency
  - S-CO2 thermal performance ~36.18% vs He-gas ~29.81% under same conditions
  - Adding divertor heat source improves cycle efficiency from 30.5% to 34.7%

## Source 2: Design and optimization of power conversion system for a steady state CFETR power plant
- **URL**: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0360544224027683
- **Journal**: Energy (2024)
- **Key findings**:
  - Five cycle topologies based on Brayton cycle designed for CFETR
  - Optimized for both helium gas and supercritical CO2 working fluids
  - SCO2 Brayton cycle with multistage expansion, compression and intercooling proposed
  - Net power increased from 69.20 MW to 79.71 MW with optimized layout
  - Thermal performance 34.62%–39.85% at 24-28 MPa

## Source 3: The Selection of Power Cycle Technology for the Power Generation Island of a Thermonuclear Fusion Power Plant
- **URL**: https://www.energychina.press/en/article/doi/10.16516/j.ceec.2025-190
- **Key findings**:
  - Comparative analysis of steam Rankine, SCO2 Brayton, and helium Brayton cycles for fusion
  - SCO2 Brayton cycle recommended as the power cycle technology for thermonuclear fusion power plants

## Source 4: CFETR COOL Blanket
- **URL**: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0920379624001662
- **Key findings**:
  - sCO2-cooled Lithium-Lead (COOL) blanket is a candidate blanket for CFETR
  - Uses S-CO2 as both blanket coolant (removing plasma heat) and working fluid for power conversion
  - Operating at 8 MPa, inlet temperature 350°C
  - Advantages: high heat removal capacity, compact system design

## Relevance to BEST

BEST is positioned in the ASIPP roadmap as: EAST → BEST → CFEDR → PFPP. The CFETR/CFEDR power conversion studies represent the planned power conversion approach for the reactor lineage that BEST feeds into. BEST itself has no power conversion system (experimental device), but the COOL blanket concept being tested in BEST's TBM ports is directly linked to the sCO2 Brayton cycle being designed for CFETR/CFEDR.
