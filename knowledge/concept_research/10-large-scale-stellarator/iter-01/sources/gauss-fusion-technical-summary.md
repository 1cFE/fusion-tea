## GIGA Power Plant — Key Specifications
Source: MT29 abstract (CERN Indico), FusionXInvest, IPP event pages, web search aggregation

- **Stellarator type**: QI (quasi-isodynamic), HELIAS lineage (W7-X heritage)
- **Configuration**: HSR4/18-derived — 4 field periods, 18 m major radius
- **Power output**: 3 GW thermal → 1 GW electric
- **Plasma volume**: 1500 m³
- **Minor plasma radius**: 1.7 m
- **Major radius**: 18 m
- **Field on axis**: 6 T
- **Maximum field on coils**: 12–13 T
- **Field periodicity**: 4
- **Coil current**: 12.2 MAT
- **First wall neutron load**: 1 MW/m²
- **FW/blanket design life**: 5 years
- **Magnet and vacuum vessel life**: 40 years

## Magnet System (from MT29 abstract, Neil Mitchell)
- **40 coils total**: 5 different shapes × 8 (replicated via symmetry)
- **Coil type**: Non-planar modular coils
- **Coil perimeter**: ~30–35 m each
- **Coil weight**: ~300 tonnes each (comparable to ITER TF magnets)
- **Conductor**: Common cross-section (circular, ~55 mm diameter), 100 kA current
- **Dual-track development**: Both LTS and HTS conductor options being developed; common cross-section allows interchangeable use
- **Coil construction**: "Conductor-in-plate" concept — plates stack to form coils, avoiding traditional casings
- **Demountable joints**: ~250 joints per coil, target resistance ~1 nΩ, units under 20 tonnes for handling
- **Quench protection**: Low-voltage system to avoid high-voltage incompatibility with cryogenic vacuum

## HELIAS Heritage Context
- GIGA parameters match HSR4/18 (HELIAS reactor study): 4 field periods, 18m major radius, 3 GW fusion power, 40 non-planar modular coils
- Original HSR4/18 used NbTi superconductor, max field ~10 T
- GIGA upgrades: higher field (6T axis / 12-13T coil), LTS+HTS dual-track, demountable joints
- DCLL (Dual Coolant Lithium Lead) blanket has been studied for HELIAS configuration by CIEMAT
- Gauss Fusion mentions 75 tonnes lithium inventory and "breeder blankets" in supply chain, confirming lithium-based breeding
