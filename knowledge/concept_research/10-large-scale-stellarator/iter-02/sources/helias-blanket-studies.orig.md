# HELIAS Stellarator Blanket Studies

## Blanket Options Studied for HELIAS

### HCPB (Helium-Cooled Pebble Bed)
- **Source**: Bongiovì 2022, Int J Energy Research (HELIAS 5-B HCPB design)
- Lithium silicate (Li4SiO4) breeder, beryllium multiplier
- Helium gas coolant in first wall and breeding zone
- TBR design limit of 1.15 at 26 cm thickness
- URL: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/er.7343

### DCLL (Dual Coolant Lithium Lead)
- **Source**: CIEMAT studies (2023), ScienceDirect
- PbLi (lead-lithium eutectic) as breeder and self-coolant
- Helium cools first wall and structural materials
- TBR design limit reached at 46 cm thickness
- CIEMAT leads DCLL development for HELIAS
- URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360544223033649

### WCLL (Water-Cooled Lithium Lead)
- Also considered but less detail found for HELIAS specifically
- More commonly associated with EU DEMO tokamak

## HSR4/18 Heritage (GIGA Predecessor)
- **Source**: ResearchGate — "The Helias reactor HSR4/18"
- 40 modular coils, NbTi superconductor at 10T max field, 1.8K cooling
- 4 field periods, 18m major radius, 3 GW fusion power
- At 12-13T (GIGA target), NbTi is insufficient → Nb3Sn or HTS needed
- URL: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/27263728_The_Helias_reactor_HSR418

## ECRH Heating
- PROCESS stellarator code: ECRH is default heating method (isthtr=1)
- IPP states ECRH is "prime candidate for stellarator heating" due to beneficial power plant properties
- W7-X uses 140 GHz, 10 MW ECRH (being upgraded)
- No specific HELIAS/GIGA ECRH power level found, but would scale to ~50-100 MW for startup
