# Gauss Fusion European Partnerships (2025-2026)

**Source**: Modern Power Systems — "Gauss Fusion broadens European partnerships"
**URL**: https://www.modernpowersystems.com/news/gauss-fusion-broadens-european-partnerships/

## Key Partnerships

### Superconducting Cables
- **ENEA (Italy)**: HTS cables and joints capable of operating under extreme magnetic field strengths
- **ICAS (Italian Consortium for Applied Superconductivity)**: Manufacturing LTS cables and maturing industrial processes for HTS conductors

### Tritium Breeding Blanket (TBB)
- **KIT (Karlsruhe Institute of Technology)** + **FZJ (Jülich Research Centre)** + **IDOM (Spain)**
- Finalizing the industrial design of the Tritium Breeding Blanket
- Integrating TBB with plant's fuel-cycle systems
- Backed by German Federal Ministry of Research funding

### TBB Manufacturing
- **Alsymex (France)**: Feasibility assessment of TBB design + fabricate prototype sub-assemblies
- Operations from Gauss Fusion's "tritium centre of excellence" in Mérignac

## Notes
- Specific blanket type (HCPB, DCLL, WCLL, etc.) NOT disclosed
- KIT is the European lead for both HCPB and DCLL blanket concepts
- FZJ has tritium processing expertise
