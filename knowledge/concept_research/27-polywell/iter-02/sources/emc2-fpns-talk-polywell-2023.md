# EMC2 FPNS Proposal Discussion (Talk-Polywell Forum, 2023)

**Source**: https://talk-polywell.org/bb/viewtopic.php?t=6553
**Date**: June 2023
**Type**: Forum discussion of EMC2/SHINE DOE FPNS proposal

## Key Technical Details

- EMC2 partnered with SHINE Technologies for DOE Fusion Prototypic Neutron Source (FPNS)
- FPNS specs:
  - Plasma target radius: 8.5 cm
  - Temperature: 500 eV
  - Magnetic field: 2-3 T at boundary, 4-5 T on coil surface
  - Ion beam energy: 150-200 keV
  - Ion beam power: 5-6 MW
  - Fusion power: 350 kW
  - 14 MeV DT fusion neutrons
  - Estimated R&D budget: $20M for 24 months

## Operation Mode

- "Steady-state heat flux to PFCs: below 5 MW/m²" — indicates continuous operation for FPNS
- This is a neutron source, not a power reactor

## Company Status

- EMC2 actively engaged as of June 2023
- DOE report anticipated March 2025
- Contradicts earlier claims of EMC2 ceasing operations in 2019
