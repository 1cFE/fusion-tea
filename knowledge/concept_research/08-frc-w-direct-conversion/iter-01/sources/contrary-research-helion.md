https://research.contrary.com/company/helion

## Key Technical Details

### Magnet Materials
- "Regular aluminum magnets" — direct quote from CEO Kirtley
- Pulsed electromagnets, not superconducting
- "Pulse it with a large electric current, that generates a magnetic field... you can extract electricity directly just with Faraday's law"

### Energy Recovery
- Direct electricity capture efficiency: 85-95% without steam turbines
- 95% of input energy after each pulse must be recovered

### Plasma Parameters
- FRC with beta ratio up to 100% (vs typical 10% or less)
- Targeting 750 million Kelvin for He-3 operation

### Neutron Management
- "Pulsating nature also produces a substantial amount of radioactivity"
- "One meter" solid barrier around reactor

### Repetition Rate
- Trenta: pulse every 10 minutes
- Commercial: pulses "every few seconds"

### Power Output
- Microsoft PPA: 50 MW by 2028 with "significant monetary penalties"
- Nucor: 500 MWe partnership

### In-House Manufacturing
- Quartz tubes and high-voltage capacitors manufactured in-house
- Supply chain identified as "main potential risk"
