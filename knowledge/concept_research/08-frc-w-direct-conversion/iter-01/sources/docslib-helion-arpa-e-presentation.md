## Key Technical Details

### Magnetic Fields
- 20 Tesla: ARPA-E experiment compression capability
- 40 Tesla: Target reactor compression field

### Power and Repetition
- 50 MW at 2 Hz repetition rate (Fusion Engine design point)
- 2000 shots/month operational capacity (vs NIF's 20 shots/month)

### Plasma Parameters
- Formation density: 1E21 m⁻³
- Compressed density target: 1E23 m⁻³ deuterium
- Ion temperature goals: 8+ keV compressed ion temperature
- FRC velocity: >300 km/s

### Energy Efficiency
- η (=Ed/Eplasma) · Gain = 0.2 · 1.2 with magnetic energy recovery
- Magnetic energy recovery efficiency: η=0.7
- Input efficiency target: <$0.03/MJ

### Neutron Diagnostics
- 4 fast scintillator neutron and gamma detectors
- MCNP modeling for neutron yield prediction
- Yttrium activation-based yield detection

### Fusion Approach
- Dynamic formation: two FRC plasmoids formed by sequential field reversal
- Acceleration: FRCs accelerated to >300 km/s
- Merging: supersonic plasmoids collide, kinetic → ion thermal energy
- Compression: FRC magnetically compressed to fusion temperatures

### Key Publication
- "Creation of a high-temperature plasma through merging and compression of supersonic field reversed configuration plasmoids" — Nuclear Fusion, vol. 51, issue 5, 2011
