- **Published Machine/Plant?**: confirmed `No` (medium) — Orion announced but no published engineering design
- **Lab Experiments**: confirmed `FRX-L (LANL), TCS (UW), C-2W (TAE/UCI)` (medium) — FRC physics demonstrations
- **New sources**: 9 Helion website pages, 3 third-party analyses, 2 news articles, 3 government/academic references
