# Helion Energy Milestones Announcement (February 2026)

**Source**: https://www.helionenergy.com/articles/helion-achieves-new-fusion-energy-milestones/
**Fetched**: 2026-03-07

## Key Technical Details

- **Polaris** is Helion's 7th-generation prototype, operational since end of 2024
- Plasma temperature: **150 million degrees Celsius (150M°C)** = **13 keV**
- Surpasses previous record of 100M°C set by Trenta prototype
- First privately-funded machine to demonstrate **D-T fusion** (January 2026)
- Helion received regulatory approval to possess and use tritium
- Target: continue testing to reach optimal temperatures for **D-He3 fusion** (commercial fuel)
- Commercial machine: **Orion**, Malaga, Washington, construction began July 2025
- Contract to deliver electricity to **Microsoft** starting 2028
- Quote from CEO David Kirtley: "We believe the surest path to commercializing fusion is building, learning and iterating as quickly as possible"

## Orion Specifications
- **50 MWe or greater** after one-year ramp-up period
- Location: Chelan County, Washington (land leased from Chelan County PUD)
