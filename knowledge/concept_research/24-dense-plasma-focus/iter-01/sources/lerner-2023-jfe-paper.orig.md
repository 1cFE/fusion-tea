# Lerner et al. (2023) — Focus Fusion: Overview of Progress Towards p-B11 Fusion with the Dense Plasma Focus

## Citation
Lerner, E. J. et al. "Focus Fusion: Overview of Progress Towards p-B11 Fusion with the Dense Plasma Focus." Journal of Fusion Energy 42, 7 (2023).
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10894-023-00345-z

## Key Results (from abstract and search summaries)

- Highest confined ion energies of any fusion experiment: >200 keV
- Lowest impurities of any fusion plasma
- Highest ratio of fusion energy generation to device energy input (wall-plug efficiency) among private fusion efforts
- Highest nτT product: 3.4 × 10²⁰ keV·s/m³
- Calculations and simulations: quantum magnetic field effect allows great reduction in bremsstrahlung radiation with p-B11 fuel
- DPF can lead to 5 MW generators, mass-produced, cheaper than existing energy sources
