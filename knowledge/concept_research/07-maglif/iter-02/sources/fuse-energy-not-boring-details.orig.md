# Fuse Energy Technologies — Not Boring Deep Dive

**Source**: https://www.notboring.co/p/fuse-energy
**Retrieved**: 2026-03-07

## Key Technical Details

### TITAN I Device
- 238 Bricks, 14 stages, 17 units/stage
- Peak current: 0.8 MA
- Peak voltage: 1.6 MV
- Peak power: 1 TW
- "3× more compact, 1,000× higher lifetime, 2× more energy efficient, 5× cheaper" than existing generators

### Z STAR (planned 2027)
- 16 TITAN units in ring configuration
- Peak power: ~15 TW
- Peak current: 12.8 MA
- Target: ~10^14 D-T reactions (100 trillion neutrons per shot)

### APEIRON I (pilot plant concept)
- **IMPORTANT: Hybrid fusion-fission concept** — NOT pure MagLIF fusion
- 90 modified TITAN modules
- Current: 50–70 MA
- **Blanket**: Heavy atoms (uranium, spent nuclear fuel)
- Neutron-induced fission in blanket transmutes radioactive isotopes while generating power
- ~20 MW fusion output → ~3,000 MW thermal via blanket fission → ~1 GWe at ~30% conversion
- This is a fundamentally different power plant concept from pure MagLIF fusion

### Repetition Rate
- IMGs: >0.1 Hz capability, ~100 ns rise time, ~90% efficiency
- Multi-million shot lifetime target
- TITAN demonstrated 100+ repetitive shots

### Roadmap
- FAETON II: Near-term commercial (survivability testing)
- TITAN II: DoD nuclear effects testing (California)
- Z STAR: 2027 intermediate research
- APEIRON I: Hybrid fusion-fission demonstration
