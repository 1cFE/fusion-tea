# Z-IFE Power Plant Concept — SAND2006-7148

**Source**: https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/901970/ (SAND2006-7148, Sandia, Oct 2006)
**Retrieved**: 2026-03-07 (PDF not fully parseable, details from web search extracts)

## Key Technical Details

### Power Plant Configuration
- **Repetition rate**: 0.1 Hz (one shot every 10 seconds) per chamber
- **Target yield**: 2–3 GJ/shot (baseline), or alternative 20 GJ/shot single chamber
- **Multi-chamber option**: 12 chambers per power plant (10 operating at any time) → ~1 GWe total
- **Single chamber option**: Very high yield (20 GJ/shot) at 0.1 Hz

### Recyclable Transmission Line (RTL)
- RTL connects pulser to target, destroyed each shot
- Made from solid coolant (e.g., frozen FLiBe) or material easily separable from coolant (e.g., steel)
- Conical shape, provides standoff between target and chamber

### Blanket/Chamber
- **Thick-liquid-wall chamber** absorbs target emissions (X-rays, debris, neutrons)
- FLiBe identified as candidate blanket material (breeder, coolant, partial shielding)
- Mitigates blast effects on chamber wall

### Power Conversion (4 cycles evaluated)
1. Supercritical CO2 Brayton cycle
2. Rankine (steam) cycle
3. Brayton (gas) cycle
4. **Combined cycle (Brayton-Rankine)** — concluded as BEST for Z-IFE
   - Requires high-temperature materials availability
   - Higher thermal-to-electric efficiency than any single cycle

### Nuclear Design
- FLiBe serves as tritium breeder + neutron moderator + coolant
- Thick liquid wall provides shielding
- Neutron energy absorption in FLiBe blanket

## Significance for MagLIF Dossier
- This is the most detailed published power plant study for pulsed-power-driven MIF
- Combined Brayton-Rankine was the recommended thermal cycle
- FLiBe was the baseline blanket/coolant material
- 0.1 Hz was the baseline repetition rate
- No private company has published a competing power plant design
