# Helical Fusion - Technology Overview (compiled from multiple sources)

## Company
- Founded: 2021
- Location: Tokyo, Japan
- Spun out from: National Institute for Fusion Science (NIFS)
- Heritage: 25+ years of LHD (Large Helical Device) research at NIFS
- Total funding: ~USD 38 million (as of Dec 2025)
- Japan SBIR Phase 3: JPY 2 billion (~USD 13 million)

## Reactor Design: HESTIA
- Type: Heliotron (a type of stellarator with continuous helical coils)
- Two continuous helical coils in double-helix (DNA-like) structure
- Operates without plasma current
- Major radius of helical coils: ~8 m
- Magnetic field at plasma center: ~9 T (8 T at coil center)
- Power output: 50 MWe (first plant), 100 MWe (follow-on)
- Fusion gain Q: ~13
- Steady-state operation: ~1 year continuous
- Availability target: >80%

## Key Technologies

### HTS Magnets
- Conductor: WISE (Wound and Impregnated Stacked Elastic tapes) — uses REBCO tape
- Flexible enough for complex helical coil winding
- After winding, impregnated with low-melting point alloy
- HTS coil test (Oct 2025): 40 kA stable current, 7 T external field, 15 K
- Double pancake coil configuration for testing
- "World's first demonstration of large-scale HTS coil designed for commercial reactor"

### Plasma Heating
- Electron cyclotron resonance heating (ECRH) via gyrotrons
- R&D on 250 GHz, 1 MW, CW gyrotrons
- Joint research with QST on ECRH stability and robust design
- Integrated simulations targeting 24-hour operating conditions

### Blanket System
- Modular-type liquid metal blanket
- Liquid metal free-surface flow covers first wall including divertor strike zone
- No separate divertor system required
- Tritium self-production via lithium in liquid metal
- Gas-driven liquid metal pump (no rotating parts) — tested via GALOP system
- GALOP: ~4m×2m×2m test system at NIFS, developed with Sukegawa Electric

### Energy Conversion
- CO2 gas turbines mentioned in collaborative research list
- Likely supercritical CO2 (sCO2) Brayton cycle
- Not explicitly confirmed in primary sources

### Fuel System
- Solid hydrogen pellet injection for fueling

## Development Roadmap
- Helix HARUKA: Integrated demonstration device (under construction, late 2020s)
- Helix KANATA: Fusion pilot plant, 24/7 operation (2030s)
- HESTIA: 50 MWe commercial plant design
