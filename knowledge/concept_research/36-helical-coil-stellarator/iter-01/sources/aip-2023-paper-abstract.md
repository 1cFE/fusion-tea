# Development of steady-state fusion reactor by Helical Fusion

**Source**: Physics of Plasmas 30, 050601 (2023)
**URL**: https://pubs.aip.org/aip/pop/article/30/5/050601/2891604/Development-of-steady-state-fusion-reactor-by
**ADS**: https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023PhPl...30e0601M/abstract

## Key Technical Details (from abstract)

- **Reactor name**: HESTIA
- **Class**: 50 MWe steady-state helical fusion reactor
- **Type**: Heliotron stellarator with two continuous helical coils
- **Magnetic field**: 8 T at helical coil center (HTS magnet coils)
- **Conductor**: HTS WISE (Wound and Impregnated Stacked Elastic tapes) — stacked REBCO tapes
- **Plasma heating**: Electron cyclotron heating (ECH/ECRH)
- **Fusion gain Q**: ~13
- **Steady-state duration**: On the order of a year
- **Availability target**: >80% with 3-month maintenance cycles after 1-year continuous operation
- **Fuel**: Deuterium-tritium with self-produced tritium
- **Blanket**: Modular-type liquid metal (LM) blanket
- **First wall**: Covered by liquid metal free-surface flow (no separate divertor needed)
- **Plasma current**: None required (no current drive power needed)
- **Successor**: 100 MWe-class plant to follow HESTIA
- **Construction cost estimate**: USD 5 billion
