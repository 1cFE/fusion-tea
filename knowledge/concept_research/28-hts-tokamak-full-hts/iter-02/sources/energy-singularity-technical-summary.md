## Key Technical Facts Gathered

### HH70 Specifications
- Major radius (R0): 0.75 m
- Minor radius (a): ~0.25-0.31 m
- Toroidal field (B0): 0.6 T (initial), upgraded to >1 T after cryogenic system upgrade (Dec 2024, lowered to 9 K)
- Magnet system: 26 HTS coils (12 TF, 6 PF, 8 CS), all REBCO
- Heating: ICRF confirmed; also uses electron gun for pre-ionization
- Total shots as of Feb 2026: 5,755
- Record: 1,337-second steady-state plasma (shot 5,755)
- AI-based plasma control system
- Localization rate: >96% domestic components

### HH170 Specifications
- Target: Q > 10 (D-T equivalent)
- Field: 110% of SPARC → implied ~14 T on-axis (SPARC is ~12.2 T)
- Diameter: 90% of SPARC
- Volume: ~70% of SPARC
- Target magnet: 25 T peak field (D-shaped HTS magnets)
- Timeline: completion by 2027
- Claims: "world's smallest and lowest-cost tokamak capable of Q > 10"

### HH380
- Demo power plant
- Timeline: construction starts after 2030
- No engineering details publicly available

### Jingtian Magnet
- Peak field: 21.7 T (later sources say 22.4 T)
- REBCO HTS
- Featured in IAEA World Fusion Outlook 2025
- Surpassed CFS/MIT 20 T record

## Tritium Breeding
**NOT DISCLOSED.** Extensive search across English and Chinese sources found zero information about:
- Blanket type (FLiBe, LiPb, liquid Li, solid ceramic)
- Blanket design philosophy
- Tritium breeding ratio targets
- Any engineering subsystem details for tritium handling

This is expected given the company's stage: HH70 is an experimental tokamak (not D-T, no neutrons), and HH170 targets "D-T equivalent" Q > 10 but may not actually burn D-T. HH380 is the first machine where blanket design would be engineering-critical, and it's still post-2030.
