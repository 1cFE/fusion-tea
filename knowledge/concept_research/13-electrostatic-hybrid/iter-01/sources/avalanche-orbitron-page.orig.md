# Avalanche Energy — Orbitron Product Page
**Source**: https://www.avalanchefusion.com/orbitron
**Fetched**: 2026-03-08

## Key Technical Details

- **Ion confinement**: High speed ions electrostatically confined in precessing elliptical orbits around a negatively charged cathode
- **Electron confinement**: Magnetic bottle, specifically tuned to trap high-energy electrons, surrounding the co-axial vacuum chamber
- **Power target**: 1-100 kWe compact fusion machine, modular scaling to 100s of kW or MW-scale
- **Fuel options**: Deuterium-Tritium (D-T) and proton-boron-11 (p-B11)
- **Energy conversion (D-T)**: "heat generated from neutron bombardment will be converted to electrical energy with a thermal cycle, utilizing turbines"
- **p-B11 benefit**: "practically eliminates internal neutron radiation, resulting in longer life and lower shielding requirements"
- **Design philosophy**: Avoids "high-powered magnets or lasers"
