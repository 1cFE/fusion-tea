# Avalanche Energy — CWFest 2023 Blog Post (Behind the Scenes)
**Source**: https://www.avalanchefusion.com/blog/cwfest2023
**Fetched**: 2026-03-08

## Key Technical Details

### Confinement Physics
- Combines electrostatic ion confinement (orbitrap-like) with ExB electron confinement (magnetron-like)
- Addresses space charge limits and Coulomb collision losses — two major IEC criticisms
- Ions injected with azimuthal velocity, orbit cathode at fusion-relevant energies
- Ions and electrons co-rotate (same direction) — critical for managing Coulomb collisions
- Axial reflecting electrostatic forces pinch the device ends

### Electron Confinement
- "Hole cut off condition" — magnetic field strong enough to bend electron trajectories back toward cathode
- Reduces current to nearly zero, enabling electron density buildup

### Magnetic Field
- **Type**: Axial magnetic field
- **NEO prototype**: Permanent magnets, ~0.05 Tesla
- **Target**: 0.3 Tesla for full-scale operation
- **Plans**: Superconducting magnets for future devices
- Relatively low fields compared to other magnetic fusion approaches

### Fuel & Operating Conditions
- **Primary fuel**: D-T
- **Secondary fuel**: D-D for neutron source operation
- **Voltages**: 100 kV (NEO), 200+ kV (Marty prototype), targeting 300 kV

### Device Geometry
- Plasma core: "tens of centimeters in diameter"
- Full system: "could fit in a pickup bed"
- NEO prototype includes: ion gun, Einzel lens, high-voltage feedthrough, diagnostics
- Marty prototype: designed for 300 kV, with X-ray and neutron shielding ("concrete castle")

### Performance Targets
- Target fusion output: mid-10^11 neutrons/second (D-T); ~1 kW fusion power
- Power consumption: ~600 W (cathode), ~400 W (ion guns) baseline
- Targeting Q > 1

### Diagnostics
- Scintillators, He-3 counters, argon spectroscopy, X-ray spectroscopy, neutron camera
