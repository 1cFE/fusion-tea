# Summary of Key Technical Papers from Fisch Group

## 1. Alpha Channeling in Mirror Machines
- **Reference**: Fisch, Phys. Rev. Lett. 97, 225001 (2006)
- RF waves can cool charged particles trapped in a magnetic mirror
- Waves resonate with ions at different axial locations
- Ions forced to diffuse along constrained orbits, exiting the trap at low energy
- Alpha particle energy channeled into waves → available for other purposes
- Application to p-B11: extract fusion ash (He) quickly while capturing its energy

## 2. Wave-supported hybrid fast-thermal p-B11 fusion (2022)
- **Authors**: Kolmes, Ochs, Mlodik, Rubin, Fisch
- **Reference**: Physics of Plasmas (Letter, 2022)
- Hybrid scheme: most protons thermal, but alpha channeling puts power into fast protons
- Fast protons near peak reactivity dramatically improve fusion rate
- Can lower required confinement time for ignition by factor of ~6.9 (fast proton channeling)

## 3. Confinement time and ambipolar potential in relativistic mirror-confined plasma (2023)
- **Authors**: Ochs, Munirov, Fisch
- **Reference**: Physics of Plasmas (2023), arXiv:2302.12346
- Relativistic effects reduce confinement time in mirror machines
- Important for p-B11 which requires very high temperatures (approaching relativistic regime)
- Quantifies reduction factor for realistic mirror parameters

## 4. Lowering the reactor breakeven requirements for p-B11 fusion (2024)
- **Authors**: Ochs, Fisch
- **Reference**: Physics of Plasmas 31, 012503 (2024)
- Alpha channeling reduces required energy confinement time for ignition by factor of 2.6 (thermal) to 6.9 (fast proton)

## 5. Preventing ash from poisoning proton-boron 11 fusion plasmas (2025)
- **Authors**: Ochs, Kolmes, Fisch
- **Reference**: Physics of Plasmas 32, 052506 (2025)
- Multi-region approach: fusion region (all species) + alpha storage region (alphas + electrons only)
- Spatial separation prevents helium ash from killing the burn

## 6. Ion Mix Can Invert Centrifugal Confinement (2025)
- **Authors**: Kolmes, Ochs, Fisch
- **Reference**: arXiv:2504.18634 (submitted to PRL)
- Lower charge-to-mass ratio ions (e.g., Li-7) can create potential barriers
- "Inverted" end-plug design provides superior confinement
- Key for the multi-chamber CHARM concept

## 7. Ponderomotive barriers in rotating mirror devices using static fields (2025)
- **Authors**: (Fisch group)
- **Reference**: Physics of Plasmas 32, 062104 (2025)
- Static azimuthal perturbations create species-selective ponderomotive barriers
- Enables "one-way walls" for ion traffic control between chambers
- Critical technology for CHARM's differential confinement

## Related: Direct Energy Conversion
- **Standing Wave Direct Energy Converter (SWDEC)**: RF device that converts alpha particle kinetic energy directly to electricity
- Alpha particles leaving the reactor front converted to electricity
- Power recirculated to support radial electric field driving plasma rotation
- Patent: US20230298771 "Direct Energy Converter for Axisymmetric Mirror Fusion Reactor" (2023)

## Related: CMFX (Centrifugal Mirror Fusion Experiment)
- **Location**: University of Maryland (separate group, but validates centrifugal mirror physics)
- **Magnets**: Two LTS superconducting magnets, 3 T throat / 0.3 T midplane (mirror ratio 10)
- **Chamber**: 6.7 m length, 0.8 m diameter, center electrode up to 100 kV
- **Status**: First plasma Oct 2022, DC power supply (100 kV, 100 kW) installed May 2024
- **Fusion yield**: Measurements reported 2025 (arXiv:2505.23047)
