## Laser System
- Coherent Beam Combined (CBC) fiber lasers injected into Optical Enhancement Cavities (OEC)
- Based on Fabry-Pérot resonator with two highly reflective mirrors (>99.9995%)
- 1.5 m prototype: finesse 419,000, enhancement factor 59,000
- 15 m systems under construction at Goleta and Osaka University (targeting 100 J, 10 kW)
- 150 m scale: 10-16 kJ per module, enhancement factor up to 100,000
- Full system: 500 modules × 10 kJ = 5 MJ class
- Wavelength: 1060 nm (fiber), frequency-tripled to 350 nm UV
- Pulse duration: 0.1-10 ns
- 500 beams total: 360 compression + 140 ignition

## Fusion Scheme
- **Direct-drive** shock ignition (SI)
- Cryogenic D-T targets
- Slow implosion (low inflight aspect ratio) → shock ignition pulse
- Compression: 5-10 ns, ~5×10^14 W/cm²
- Ignition: 0.5-1 ns, 10^15-10^16 W/cm²
- LPI mitigation via multicolor beams, broadband (16 THz), slowly rotating polarization (SRP)

## Reactor Design
- **Magnetized dry-wall chamber**, radius 8-10 m
- First wall: tungsten facing + RAFM steel structural support, helium gas cooled
- Embedded magnetic fields at laser ports to deflect charged particles toward DEC exhaust ports
- **Blanket**: Natural lithium (7.5% ⁶Li, 92.5% ⁷Li) as breeding material, lead (Pb) as neutron multiplier
- Blanket cooled by helium gas (SiC-based ceramics), investigating HTGR technology integration
- Thermal-to-electric: η_th = 0.40 + 10% from exothermic ⁶Li(n,α)T = 0.44 effective
- **DEC**: Direct energy conversion for 30% of fusion energy (alpha particles + plasma exhaust)
- DEC: electrodes positioned along magnetic field lines, η_DEC = 0.44

## Energy Split
- ~70% of fusion energy → 14.1 MeV neutrons → blanket → thermal conversion
- ~30% of fusion energy → charged particles (alphas) → DEC → direct electricity

## Tritium
- Tritium bred in lithium-lead blanket
- IFE advantage: tritium inventory in chamber limited to a few mg (vs. larger inventory in MFE)
- Tritium cycle: separation/recovery from blanket and plasma exhaust → new target → inject at 1-10 Hz

## Timeline
- 2024: 1.5 m OEC prototype demonstrated (finesse 419,000, enhancement 59,000)
- 2025: 15 m OEC systems under construction (Goleta + Osaka)
- 2027: First full-scale CBC-OEC prototype
- Early 2030s: Pilot fusion reactor demonstration
