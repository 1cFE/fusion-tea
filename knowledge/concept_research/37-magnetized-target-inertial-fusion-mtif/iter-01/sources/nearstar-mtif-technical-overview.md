## Approach

NearStar Fusion pursues **Magnetized Target Impact Fusion (MTIF)** — a hybrid
combining magnetic confinement (embedded magnetic field) with inertial
confinement (projectile-driven implosion). The magnetic field suppresses
thermal conduction during compression, reducing the driver energy required to
reach fusion conditions.

## Driver — hypervelocity plasma-armature railgun

- Projectile / fuel capsule mass: **~50 g** ("50-gram fuel capsules")
- Launch velocity: **~10 km/s** (≈ 22,369 mph; ~30× speed of sound)
- Repetition rate: **~1 Hz** ("once per second")
- Kinetic energy: **> 1 MJ** per shot
- Construction: commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) technologies

## Target / magnetization

- Builds on the **Sandia National Laboratory Z-Machine** method of imploding
  metallic fuel-target liners, applied to **magnetized fuel targets**.

## Fuel & first wall

- Fuel: **D-D ("tritium-free")** — explicitly chosen to avoid tritium
  supply-chain, breeding-blanket, and first-wall complexities.
- First wall: **molten lead** to minimize neutron-embrittlement damage.

## Plant concept & status

- Stated scalability: **50 MW to 1 GW+**.
- Stated development timeline: "as little as 10 years".
- Validation: implosion performance modeling with the University of Alabama in
  Huntsville (UAH); prototype fuel-target impact experiments at the **Texas A&M
  Hypervelocity Impact Laboratory (HVIL)**.
- Company: founded 2021 (Dr. Douglas Witherspoon, Chris Faranetta), HQ
  Chantilly, VA. Early-stage; equity crowdfunding (StartEngine) plus investment
  from Virginia Innovation Partnership Corp (VIPC), Virginia Venture Partners,
  Ecosphere Ventures.
- **No published energy gain, net power, capital cost, or LCOE figures** in
  public materials (gap — `truly-unknown`/`proprietary`).
