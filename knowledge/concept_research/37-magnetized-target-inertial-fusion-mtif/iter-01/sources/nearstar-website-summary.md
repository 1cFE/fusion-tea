# NearStar Fusion — Website Summary

Source: https://www.nearstarfusion.com/ and /learn-more (fetched 2026-05-17)

## Company
- Founded 2021, Chantilly, VA
- Funded by DOE, ARPA-E, NASA, NSF
- University partners: University of Alabama in Huntsville (UAH), Texas A&M
- Investors: Virginia Venture Partners, Ecosphere Ventures, VIPC

## Concept: Magnetized Target Impact Fusion (MTIF)
- Hybrid: simultaneously compresses, heats, and magnetizes fusion fuel
- Driver: hypervelocity plasma-armature railgun
  - Launches 50-gram fuel capsules at 10 km/s (~Mach 30)
  - Delivers >1 MJ kinetic energy
  - Repetition rate: 1 Hz
- Target: molten lead chamber; "manages the First Wall Problem using molten lead thus minimizing damage from neutron embrittlement"
- Heritage: Sandia Z-Machine method of imploding metallic fuel target liners

## Fuel
- D-D primary: "By avoiding tritium as a fuel source, the overhead and complexity required to operate the power plant is significantly reduced."
- D-T backup mentioned in CSV description

## Roadmap
- Break-even experiments: ~5 years
- Prototype power plant: ~10 years
