## Summary

NearStar Fusion (Chantilly, VA; founded 2021 by Dr. Douglas Witherspoon and
Chris Faranetta) pursues **Magnetized Target Impact Fusion (MTIF)** — a hybrid
of magnetic and inertial confinement. A hypervelocity **plasma-armature
railgun** launches ~50-gram fuel capsules at ~10 km/s at ~1 Hz (>1 MJ kinetic
energy, built from commercial-off-the-shelf technology); the projectile
implosion compresses a **magnetized fuel target** (Sandia Z-Machine-style
imploding metallic liner approach). The embedded magnetic field suppresses
thermal conduction during compression, reducing required driver energy.
**Tritium-free D-D** fuel avoids tritium breeding/supply and first-wall
complexity; a molten-lead first wall mitigates neutron embrittlement. Stated
plant scalability 50 MW–1 GW+. Validation via UAH implosion modeling and
Texas A&M Hypervelocity Impact Lab experiments. Early-stage (equity
crowdfunding; VIPC / Virginia Venture Partners / Ecosphere backing).
