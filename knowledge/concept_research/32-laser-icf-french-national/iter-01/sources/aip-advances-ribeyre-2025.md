## Key Details (from search result summaries — full text behind paywall)

- CEA and CNRS joined expertise to support GenF under Taranis project
- GenF described as spin-off of Thales, created 2024, supported by France 2030 plan
- Discusses tritium breeding through neutron irradiation of lithium:
  - Li6 + n → T + He (exothermic, increases blanket gain)
  - Li7 + n → T + He + n (endothermic)
- Notes that **liquid lithium blankets** inside the chamber could be used for tritium production and energy capture
- This is the most authoritative technical paper from GenF/CEA researchers on the reactor system concept
