# CFS 2025-2026 Updates

## Timeline and Milestones
- First of 18 TF magnets installed (announced CES 2026)
- Full magnet ring expected by end of summer 2026
- First plasma: 2027
- Net energy demonstration: 2027
- ARC construction: late 2020s
- ARC grid connection: early 2030s

## ARC Commercial Details
- Location: James River Industrial Park, Chesterfield County, Virginia (near Richmond)
- Power output: 400 MWe (upgraded from earlier 270 MWe design)
- Google PPA: 200 MW (signed June/July 2025)
- Eni PPA: >$1B deal (September 2025) — covers remaining capacity
- Combined PPAs fully subscribe the 400 MW capacity

## Operation Mode Clarification
- Fortune (Jan 2026): ARC described as "the world's first fusion plant providing steady power to the grid"
- CFS Blog: "ARC operations will pulse for longer, perhaps for tens of minutes at a time"
- SPARC: 10s ramp-up, 10s full power, 10s ramp-down, 20min-1hr cooling gap
- Key insight: FLiBe blanket thermal mass enables continuous steam generation despite pulsed plasma
- Grid output is continuous; plasma operation is quasi-steady (long pulses with brief interruptions)

## Funding
- $863M Series B2 (September 2025)
- Total funding: ~$2B+
