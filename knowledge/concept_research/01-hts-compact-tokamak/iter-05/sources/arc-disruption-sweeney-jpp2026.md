**Abstract**

Commonwealth Fusion Systems plans to operate a tokamak power plant called ARC. The paper assesses mitigation strategies for plasma disruptions using comprehensive modeling. A baseline massive gas injection (MGI) system is proposed alongside a runaway electron mitigation coil option. "All ARC disruption loads are within a factor of 2 of the disruption loads in SPARC," indicating SPARC will serve as a valuable testbed. The operational goal targets one mitigated disruption daily with rapid restart capability within tens of seconds.
