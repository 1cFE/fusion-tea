# ARC Fusion Reactor Specifications

**Primary source**: Sorbom et al., "ARC: A compact, high-field, fusion nuclear science facility and demonstration power plant with demountable magnets," Fusion Engineering and Design, 2015.
**ArXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.3540
**Additional**: Grokipedia synthesis, https://grokipedia.com/page/ARC_fusion_reactor

## Core Dimensions
- Major radius: 3.3 m
- Minor radius: 1.1 m
- Aspect ratio: ~3

## Magnetic Field
- On-axis toroidal field: 9.2 T
- Peak toroidal field (in coils): 23 T
- Technology: REBCO HTS magnets operating at ~20 K
- Demountable joints for maintenance access

## Power
- Fusion thermal power: ~525 MW (some sources say ~1 GW for latest design updates)
- Net electrical output: ~270-400 MWe (varies by source/design iteration)
- Thermal-to-electric efficiency: 30-40%
- Fusion gain Q: >10, target ~13.6

## Plasma Parameters
- Plasma current: 7.8 MA
- Normalized beta: 3.3
- Energy confinement time: ~0.64 s
- Operating in I-mode regime

## Heating and Current Drive
- 25 MW lower hybrid current drive (LHCD) — for steady-state current drive
- 13.6 MW ion cyclotron fast wave (ICRF) — for plasma heating
- Combined RF system enables steady-state operation far from disruptive limits

## Blanket
- FLiBe (LiF-BeF2) liquid immersion blanket
- Operating temperature: ~900 K (outlet scalable to 1200 K)
- Tritium breeding ratio (TBR): ≥1.1 (up to 1.22 with optimization)
- Captures ~80% of fusion neutron power
- Serves as breeder, coolant, and neutron shield

## Operation Mode
- Design target: Steady-state operation (via LHCD + bootstrap current)
- SPARC precursor: pulsed (10-second flat-top)
- ARC commercial: quasi-steady-state to steady-state

## Energy Conversion
- Baseline: Rankine cycle (~30% efficiency)
- Under study: Brayton cycle (sCO2 or He), combined cycle (higher efficiency)

## Neutron Management
- FLiBe blanket provides integrated breeding + shielding
- First wall: 1 cm tungsten
- Neutron shielding: Titanium hydride (TiH2)
- Vacuum vessel: Double-walled Inconel 718

## Notes on SPARC vs ARC
- SPARC: R=1.85m, Bt=12.2T, 140MW fusion, Q≈11, pulsed (10s flat-top), ICRF-only heating (25MW at 120MHz)
- ARC: R=3.3m, Bt=9.2T, 525MW+ fusion, Q≈13.6, steady-state target, LHCD+ICRF heating
- SPARC proves the physics; ARC is the power plant design
