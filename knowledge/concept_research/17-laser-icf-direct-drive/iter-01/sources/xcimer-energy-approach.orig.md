# Xcimer Energy — Approach Page (xcimer.energy/approach/)

Retrieved: 2026-03-07

## Laser Technology
- Type: KrF (Krypton Fluoride) gas excimer laser
- Wavelength: 248 nm (deep UV)
- Energy: 10+ MJ per pulse
- Cost: >30x reduction in cost per joule vs NIF
- Optical footprint: under 1 sq meter final optical area
- Heritage: SDI "Star Wars" program of 1980s; KrF used in DUV lithography

## Target/Drive Scheme
- "Hybrid Direct Drive" (HDD): two-sided UV laser drive
- Two opposed beams illuminate target
- Larger fuel capsules — easier to produce, more robust
- D-T fuel

## Chamber: HYLIFE III
- Flowing liquid lithium salt (FLiBe or FLiNaBe)
- Protects structural walls from fusion neutrons
- FLiBe moderates neutron spectrum to fission-like levels
- Breeds tritium (TBR ~1.17 per other sources)
- Heat exchanged with helium to drive gas turbine
- ~45% thermal-to-electric conversion efficiency
- First solid wall designed as lifetime component

## Repetition Rate
- Less than 1 Hz (sub-Hz)
- Lower rates "simplify engineering"

## Prototype
- Phoenix: prototype laser system at Denver facility (70,000 sq ft)
- Long Pulse Kinetics (LPK) platform completed early 2025
- First private-sector electron-beam excimer laser in 20+ years
- 3 microsecond pulse length achieved (global record for this laser type)
- Phoenix on track for completion in 2026
