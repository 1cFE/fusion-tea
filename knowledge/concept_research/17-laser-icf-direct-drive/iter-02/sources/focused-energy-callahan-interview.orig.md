# Focused Energy - Debbie Callahan Interview (Physics World)
**URL**: https://physicsworld.com/a/focusing-on-fusion-debbie-callahan-talks-commercial-laser-fusion/
**Fetched**: 2026-03-07

## Key Technical Details

### Energy Conversion
Direct quote: "We will use a conventional steam cycle to convert the heat into electricity."

### Laser Efficiency
NIF laser: "less than 1% efficient"
Industry requirement: approximately "10% efficient" lasers driven by diodes (diode pump solid-state lasers)

### Target Gain
Targets gain levels of "over 50" (NIF current: 4.1x)
For commercial viability: gains of "50 to 100"

### Repetition Rate
"900,000 shots a day – about 10 per second" (vs NIF's "400 shots per year")

### Target Design (Pearl)
Must be a "perfect sphere" for uniform compression
Filled with "deuterium-tritium fuel derived from sea water and lithium"
~4mm diameter

### Tritium Breeding
Collaborates with Savannah River National Lab on tritium extraction systems
Breeds tritium from "lithium blankets" using fusion neutrons
Specific blanket type (FLiBe, LiPb, etc.) NOT specified

### Timeline
Pilot plant (LightHouse): "by the end of the 2030s"
