# Ten Minutes With Mike Dunne — ENR Interview

## Key Technical Details
- Thunderwall delivers 10 MJ, 10 times per second
- 20x more efficient than NIF
- 1 million times more average power than NIF; 1/10th the footprint per beamline
- Diode-powered laser emitters
- Fires hundreds of times per minute for sustained electricity production

## Gain Targets
- Prototype plant: 18x more energy out than in (target gain ~18)
- Grid-scale: input-output power ratio must exceed 30
- (NIF achieved ~1.5x net target gain in Dec 2022)

## Plant Specifications
- Pilot plant: 50 MWe net to grid
- Full scale: 1.5 GW capacity
- 1,000 lasers power full-scale plant
- Location: Technology development in Livermore, CA; pilot plant siting TBD

## Target Manufacturing
- Prototype target manufacturing facility planned
- Millions of tiny pellets required
- Lead hohlraum instead of gold (cost reduction)
- Target cost goal: <$1 each
