# Inertia Enterprises — $450M Series A Press Release

**Source**: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/11/3236274/0/en/Inertia-raises-450-million-to-commercialize-the-only-proven-fusion-science.html

## Key Facts
- $450M Series A led by Bessemer Venture Partners
- Participation from GV (Google Ventures), Modern Capital, Threshold Ventures
- Founded 2024, based in Livermore, CA
- Co-founders: Jeff Lawson (CEO), Annie Kritcher (Chief Scientist), Mike Dunne (CTO)

## Technical Details
- Thunderwall: 10 kJ beam, 10 Hz, 10% wallplug efficiency
- Semiconductor diode-based laser technology
- 50x more average power than any prior laser of its type
- Hybrid-E ICF design (Kritcher's NIF ignition design)
- First fusion target assembly plant for mass manufacturing
- Grid-scale fusion electricity through pilot plant → gigawatt utility-scale within next decade

## Timeline
- Construction start: 2030 (pilot plant)
- Commercialization: within next decade
