# Inertia Enterprises — Website Technical Details

**Source**: https://inertia.com/ and FAQ pages
**Accessed**: 2026-03-07

## Laser System
- 10 MJ total laser energy (4.5x higher than NIF's 2 MJ)
- ~1,000 smaller beamlines, each 20x more efficient than NIF
- One million times more average power than NIF
- 1/10th the footprint per beamline
- Diode-pumped solid-state laser (DPSSL)

## Thunderwall (Single Beamline Prototype)
- 10 kJ beam
- 10 Hz repetition rate
- 10% wallplug efficiency
- 50x more average power than any prior laser of its type
- Semiconductor diode-pumped

## Fuel Targets
- 4.5 mm hohlraum targets
- Less than $1 each (target cost)
- 10 targets per second production rate
- Lead (Pb) hohlraum instead of gold (NIF uses gold)
- Based on Annie Kritcher's Hybrid-E ICF design
- Each target produces "15x more thrust than a SpaceX Falcon rocket at liftoff"

## Power Plant
- 1.5 GW baseload, dispatchable electricity (full scale)
- 50 MWe net to grid (pilot plant)
- 1,000-4,000 beamlines for commercial facility
- Zero emissions

## DPSSL vs Alternatives (from FAQ)
- DPSSL ~14% efficient (vs excimer ~7%)
- Chosen because closest match to NIF light character (pulse shaping, coherence)
- Challenge: need ~100x expansion of semiconductor laser diode supply

## Tritium Strategy (from FAQ)
- Initial tritium from US government-controlled supplies
- Breed tritium on-site using liquid lithium
- "Lining the fusion chamber with pipes full of liquid lithium"
- On-site tritium inventory: hundreds of grams (vs tokamaks needing 20x more)
- Lithium requirement: equivalent to 20 EV batteries per year for 1.5 GW plant
- Tritium extraction from flowing liquid lithium is "still an area of active development"

## D-T Fuel Choice (from FAQ)
- D-T at 150 million °C (vs D-D at 500 million °C)
- D-T releases ~3.5x as much energy as D-D
- Neutron carries most energy → steam turbines for electricity
- Explicitly mentions steam turbine cycle for power conversion

## Energy Conversion
- Neutron energy heats liquid lithium → steam turbine cycle
- (Consistent with LLNL LIFE heritage: ~45% thermal efficiency)
