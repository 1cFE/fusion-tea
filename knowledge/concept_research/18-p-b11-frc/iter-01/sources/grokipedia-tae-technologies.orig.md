## Machine History & Generations

**Early Prototypes (1999-2004):**
- "Sewer Pipe" (1999-2000): Proof-of-concept, demonstrated initial FRC plasma formation
- Device B: Tested fuel injection methods
- C-1: Achieved ~1 ms confinement by 2004

**Mid-Stage Development (2005-2014):**
- C-2 (2005): Introduced NBI systems delivering ~20 keV with several MW power
- C-2U (2014-2021): >10 MW NBI at 15 keV hydrogen; 10x improvement in plasma lifetime; confinement >10 ms

**Current Generation (2021-2025):**
- C-2W/Norman (2021-present): World's largest compact-toroid device; sustained plasmas up to ~40 ms; achieved 70 million °C
- Norm (2025): Streamlined — plasma formation exclusively through NBI without auxiliary plasma guns

## Magnet Types & Confinement

- Self-generated poloidal magnetic field from azimuthal plasma currents
- Minimal or no toroidal magnetic field
- High beta approaching unity
- Eliminates need for central solenoid or toroidal field coils
- External copper coils for equilibrium/mirror field (from C-2W papers)

## Key Parameters (C-2W/Norman)

- Separatrix radius: 0.4 m
- Axial length: 2 m
- Plasma current: 300-350 kA
- Trapped poloidal flux: ~16 mWb
- Total plasma energy: ~13 kJ
- Electron density: 1-3 × 10^19 m^-3
- Peak electron temperature: ~1 keV
- Volume-averaged beta: Near unity (~90-100%)

## Energy Conversion

### Inverse Cyclotron Converter (ICC)
- Decelerates alpha particles in tapering magnetic field
- Creates helical orbits past segmented electrodes
- Induces AC at ~5 MHz under 0.6 T fields
- Targeting >90% conversion efficiency for 3 MeV alphas
- Captures fusion energy without thermal cycles

### TAE FAQ (steam approach)
- TAE's FAQ describes thermal conversion: fusion heat → working fluid → steam → turbine → generator
- This appears to be a simplified public explanation or fallback approach

## Neutral Beam Injection

- Eight injectors on Norm: 13 MW total power
- High-energy neutral deuterium atoms injected tangentially
- Provides heating, current drive, and momentum for plasma stabilization
- 2025 breakthrough: magnetic field reversal by precisely angled high-energy atom injection

## p-B11 Fusion Details

- Reaction: p + 11B → 3 4He + 8.7 MeV
- Cross-section peaks ~600 keV
- Required plasma temperatures: 100-200 keV
- 2023: First detection of p-B11 fusion in magnetically confined plasma (with NIFS Japan)
