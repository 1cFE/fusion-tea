# TAE Technologies C-2W/Norman Machine Details
**Retrieved**: 2026-03-07
**Sources**: TAE website, IAEA FEC papers (Gota et al.), New Atlas interviews

## Machine Dimensions
- ~24 meters (80 ft) long
- ~7 meters (22 ft) high
- ~27 metric tons
- Center sits ~3m (10 ft) off floor
- Divertor vessels on each end: ~3.4m (11 ft) diameter

## Machine Structure
Central confinement section surrounded by:
- Two inner divertors
- Two formation sections
- Two outer divertors

## Coil Systems
- Theta pinch coils: 13 or 17 on each side (formation section)
  - Push plasma and accelerate to ~300 km/s
- Confinement equilibrium coils
- Mirror coils (magnetic-mirror plugs at ends)
- Saddle and trim coils (for active feedback control)
- In-vacuum fast-switching coils
- DC formation/divertor coils

## Coil Material
- External copper coils for equilibrium/mirror field (from Grokipedia, consistent with experimental machine tradition)
- No superconducting magnets mentioned for C-2W
- FRC approach uses "simple geometry magnets" (New Atlas)
- Magnetic efficiency: ~90% in FRC vs ~10% in tokamak

## NBI System
- Eight NB injectors total
- Four fixed-energy at 15 keV
- Four tunable-energy at 15-40 keV
- 13 MW total power on Norm

## Diagnostic Systems
- 1000+ B-field signals
- 28 internal + 45 external flux loops
- 64 three-axis Mirnov probes
- 22 axial-only Mirnov probes

## Key Distinction: C-2W vs Da Vinci
C-2W/Norman and Norm are experimental machines using copper/resistive coils.
Da Vinci reactor magnet technology has NOT been publicly specified.
Given FRC's near-unity beta (~90-100%), external magnet requirements are minimal — this is a secondary engineering concern, not a primary technology bet.
