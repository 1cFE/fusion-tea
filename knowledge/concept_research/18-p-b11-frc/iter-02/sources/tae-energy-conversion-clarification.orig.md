# TAE Technologies - Energy Conversion Clarification
**Retrieved**: 2026-03-07
**Sources**: TAE FAQ (tae.com/faq-fusion/), New Atlas interview (2024), DJT merger announcement

## Resolution of the Three-Narrative Tension

### Narrative 1: Thermal/Steam (TAE FAQ - confirmed)
TAE FAQ directly states: "Hydrogen and boron atoms will fuse inside a stable, superhot plasma to produce energetic light. When that light heats the walls of the plasma confinement vessel, a network of pipes will transport that heat via working fluid to a steam generator. The steam spins a turbine which drives an electric generator."
- This is the official public-facing explanation

### Narrative 2: Inverse Cyclotron Converter (ICC - patented, research-stage)
- Patented (US7459654, US6628740, US6888907, KR100907675B1, WO2018208620A1)
- 5 MHz operation, 0.6 T field
- Decelerates 3 MeV alpha particles via tapering magnetic field
- >90% conversion efficiency target
- TAE has 2,500+ patents globally, 1,600+ granted

### Narrative 3: X-ray Direct Capture (speculative/future)
New Atlas interview quote: "We're looking into a direct capture system that would work a little like solar cells on steroids."
- Soft X-rays from fusion reaction free electrons in absorber material → DC power
- "Can theoretically be much higher efficiency than solar cells"
- Early-stage research, not Da Vinci baseline

## Assessment
Da Vinci's baseline power conversion is thermal/steam — confirmed by TAE FAQ.
ICC and X-ray capture are future upgrade paths / long-term research.
The "aneutronic = no thermal conversion needed" claim from some sources is aspirational, not Da Vinci's actual design.

For differentiation table: the concept's long-term architectural advantage is Direct (charged particle), but Da Vinci itself uses Thermal (steam).
