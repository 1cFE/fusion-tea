## Summary

ENN (ENN Energy / Xinao, China) is developing **EHL-2** ("ENN He-Long-2"), a
**spherical torus** targeting thermal **proton-boron-11 (p-B11)** aneutronic
fusion. Target device parameters: R₀ ≈ 1.05 m, aspect ratio A ≈ 1.85,
B₀ ≈ 3 T, plasma current Iₚ ≈ 3 MA, core ion temperature Tᵢ₀ ≈ 30 keV with a
hot-ion mode Tᵢ/Tₑ ≥ 2, and a core boron concentration of 10–15%. p-B11
viability requires the hot-ion mode plus high wall reflection to suppress
electron radiation (bremsstrahlung/synchrotron) losses. The configuration is
**central-solenoid-free (CS-free)**: very limited CS volt-seconds force
plasma start-up and the MA-level current ramp via non-inductive current drive
(a central design challenge). Roadmap progresses from the EXL-50 / EXL-50U
spherical tori to EHL-2, with EHL-2 construction estimated complete ~2026.
