## Device — EHL-2 spherical torus (ENN He-Long-2)

Main target parameters (peer-reviewed):

- Major radius **R₀ ≈ 1.05 m**
- Aspect ratio **A ≈ 1.85** (spherical torus / low aspect ratio)
- Toroidal field **B₀ ≈ 3 T**
- Plasma current **Iₚ ≈ 3 MA**
- Core ion temperature **Tᵢ₀ ≈ 30 keV**
- Hot-ion mode: **Tᵢ / Tₑ ≥ 2**
- Core boron concentration **10 %–15 %**

## Physics objective — thermal p-B11 (aneutronic)

- Goal: realize the **thermal proton-boron-11 (p-¹¹B)** reaction and establish
  its physics basis.
- p-B11 viability requires the **hot-ion mode** plus **high wall reflection**
  to minimize electron radiation (bremsstrahlung/synchrotron) losses.

## Central-solenoid-free (CS-free) challenge

- The central solenoid provides very limited volt-seconds, so plasma
  **start-up and the MA-level toroidal-current ramp must be achieved by
  non-inductive current drive** — identified as a central design challenge.

## Roadmap & timeline

- Progression: EXL-50 → upgraded **EXL-50U** → next-generation **EHL-2**.
- **EHL-2 construction estimated complete ~2026.**
- Energy-extraction / direct-conversion scheme for p-B11 is exploratory
  ("Preliminary considerations and challenges of proton-boron fusion energy
  extraction on the EHL-2 spherical torus" — companion paper).
- **No commercial plant net-power / capital-cost / LCOE figures** (EHL-2 is a
  physics-basis device, not a power plant — gap).
