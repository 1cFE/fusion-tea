# ENN's Roadmap for Proton-Boron Fusion Based on Spherical Torus

Source: arXiv 2401.11338 (Phys. Plasmas 31, 062507, 2024)
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.11338 / https://pubs.aip.org/aip/pop/article/31/6/062507/3297400

## EHL-2 main parameters
- Major radius R0 ≈ 1.05 m, aspect ratio A ≈ 1.85
- Toroidal field B0 ≈ 3 T (maximum, central)
- Plasma current Ip ≈ 3 MA
- Central ion temp Ti0 ≈ 30 keV, Ti/Te ≥ 2

## Heating
- 17 MW Neutral Beam Injection (primary)
- 6 MW ECRH for local current drive and MHD instability control

## Operation scenarios
1. High Ti (Ti0 > 30 keV)
2. High-performance steady-state
3. High triple product

## Timeline
- EXL-50U first plasma Jan 2024; 1 MA achieved (reported 2025)
- EHL-2 construction estimated complete 2026 (some sources mid-2027)

## Goals
- Verify p-11B thermal reaction rates
- Establish ST scaling laws at 10s of keV ion temperature
- Provide design basis for subsequent p-B11 burning plasma device

## Notes
- EHL-2 is an experimental device (no blanket / power conversion specified)
- Challenges: non-inductive current startup, divertor heat flux > 20 MW/m at low density
