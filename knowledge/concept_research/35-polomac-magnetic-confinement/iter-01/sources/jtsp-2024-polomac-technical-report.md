## Key Technical Details

- **Concept**: Polomac — a poloidal magnetic configuration where the outboard magnetic lines are deviated aside together with the plasma, to open accesses ("magnetic tunnels") to the dipole coils located inside the plasma.
- **Magnetic tunnels**: Used to support, feed, and cool the dipole coil. These avoid plasma contact with supports, which led to the abandonment of earlier poloidal confinement experiments.
- **D-T capability**: Poloidal confinement achieves D-T reactor conditions with magnetic field 3x weaker than tokamak, in steady state.
- **D-D capability**: With same high field as tokamak, poloidal confinement can achieve D-D reaction, avoiding tritium breeding blanket.
- **Confinement efficiency**: 70-80% (beta).
- **Development strategy**: Small prototype to validate magnetic tunnels, then scale to commercial.
- **Prototype**: Small prototype with vessel described; water-cooled copper coils producing 0.2-0.3 T.
