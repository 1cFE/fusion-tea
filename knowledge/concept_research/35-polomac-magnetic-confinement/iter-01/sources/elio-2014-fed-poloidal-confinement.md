# Revisiting the poloidal magnetic confinement

**Authors**: Filippo Elio (likely sole or lead author)
**Journal**: Fusion Engineering and Design, Vol. 89 (2014)
**URL**: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0920379614003834
**DOI**: 10.1016/j.fusengdes.2014.04.013 (inferred from URL)

## Key Technical Details (from search result abstracts)

- **Beta**: 20-30% (pressure to magnetic energy ratio)
- **Configuration**: Closed poloidal magnetic configuration with in-vessel coil system held by shielded supports
- **Reactor dimensions**: Dipole radius 5.4 m, plasma of constant poloidal cross-section ~40 m², volume ~1300 m³, minimum outboard thickness 1 m
- **Magnetic field**: 1.4 to 1.8 T
- **Operation**: Steady-state

This is the foundational paper where the Polomac concept was first published. The 2024 JTSP paper builds on this with the magnetic tunnel refinement and Deutelio's commercial development strategy.
