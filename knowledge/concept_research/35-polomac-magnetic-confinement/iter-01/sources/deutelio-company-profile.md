# Deutelio AG — Company Profile

**Company**: Deutelio AG
**Registration**: Luxembourg (originally Swiss-associated, Boldbrain challenge is Swiss)
**Founded**: ~2022
**Name origin**: "DEUTerio" (deuterium) + "ELIO" (helium) in Italian
**Website**: https://www.deutelio.com/

## Team
- **Francesco Elio** — Co-Founder & CEO
- **Filippo Elio** — CTO (University of Padua professor, concept originator since 2014)
- **Marco Leone** — Co-author on technical papers
- **Tiziano Fulceri** — Co-author on technical papers
- **Carlo Sborchia** — Co-author on technical papers

## Key Facts
- 4th place at Boldbrain Startup Challenge 2024 (received 10,000 CHF prize)
- Launched first seed round, led by a venture builder specialized in nuclear technologies
- Innosuisse support received
- Collaboration agreements with research and industrial partners
- Plans: 3 years to fine-tune prototype in lab → go-to-market → heat generators with D-D → electrical generation plants with superconducting magnets
- Vision: Energy generation by 2030; multiple commercial applications (energy, life science, aerospace)

## Development Roadmap (from multiple sources)
1. Small prototype to validate magnetic tunnel concept (hydrogen plasma, copper coils 0.2-0.3 T)
2. Design and sell small heat generators operating with Deuterium
3. Develop and sell electrical generation plants with superconducting magnets
