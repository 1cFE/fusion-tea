## Key Facts (Confirmed)

- **Founded**: 2025, San Diego, CA
- **YC Batch**: Fall 2025
- **Founders**: Dr. Galen Burke (ORNL, DIII-D) and Dr. Edward Hinson (LLNL, W7-X, DIII-D)
- **Background**: Met at UW-Madison 2010, operated spherical tokamak, 35+ years combined experience
- **Publications**: Physical Review Letters, Nuclear Fusion, Physics of Plasmas
- **Team size**: 2 employees
- **Investor**: Pioneer Fund
- **Target**: Megawatt-class in-orbit fusion power source
- **Confinement**: Dipole magnetic confinement using space vacuum
- **Magnets**: HTS ("~10x more field per kg improvement in last decade")
- **Scale**: Meter-scale coil, fits on Falcon 9
- **Claimed performance**: Magnetized volume exceeding ITER for <$30M
