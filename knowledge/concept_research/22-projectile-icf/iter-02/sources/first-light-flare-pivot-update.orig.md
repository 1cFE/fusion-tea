# First Light Fusion — FLARE Pivot and 2025-2026 Updates

**Sources**: firstlightfusion.com, ipgroupplc.com, neimagazine.com, world-nuclear-news.org, powerengineeringint.com (via enlit.world)
**Retrieved**: 2026-03-07

## FLARE Pivot Timeline

- **February 2025**: Cancelled Machine 4 development; announced no longer planning to build own power plant; would instead partner with other IFE companies offering amplifier technology
- **March 2025**: Published updated strategy shifting toward commercializing amplifier technology
- **September 2025**: Published FLARE white paper — resumed own power plant development path via FLARE
- **February 2026**: Validated TBR of 1.8 for FLARE concept (independently verified by TÜV SÜD UK's Radiation Physics team)

## FLARE Technical Details

### What FLARE Is
- **Full name**: Fusion via Low-power Assembly and Rapid Excitation
- **Core innovation**: Decoupled compression and ignition (fast ignition variant)
- **Driver**: Low-voltage pulsed power devices driving cylindrical liner implosions
- **NOT a projectile approach**: Uses pulsed-power-driven metallic liners, not electromagnetic gun projectiles
- **Ignition**: Auxiliary source — "short-pulse laser or pulsed power system"

### How It Differs from Original Projectile Approach
| Aspect | Original Projectile (2011-2025) | FLARE (Sept 2025+) |
|--------|--------------------------------|---------------------|
| Driver | Electromagnetic gun | Low-voltage pulsed power |
| Mechanism | Projectile impact → shockwave | Liner implosion → fast ignition |
| Geometry | Cubic target | Cylindrical target |
| Gain target | 200-1000x | Up to 1000x |
| Rep rate | Sub-Hz (30s between shots) | "Lower pulse rates enabled by high gain" (unspecified) |

### Performance
- Gain target: up to 1,000x (minimum 200 for commercial viability)
- Driver cost: $2/joule (vs $6-13 for alternatives)
- Demonstrator cost: $100-200M (1/20th of NIF)
- Energy delivery system: 1/10th cost of previous fast ignition schemes

### Power Plant Design (Retained from Original Approach)
- Liquid lithium pool reactor with 1m-thick flowing lithium curtains
- TBR 1.8 (highest announced, validated Feb 2026 by TÜV SÜD UK)
- 333 MWe design point; net tritium surplus 25 kg/year
- Steam Rankine cycle energy conversion
- Vessel lifetime: lifetime-of-plant (neutrons don't reach wall)

## Implications for Projectile ICF Concept Row

The "Projectile ICF" concept as defined in the differentiation table refers specifically to the electromagnetic gun approach that First Light pursued from 2011-2025. With Machine 4 cancelled and FLARE (pulsed power liner implosion) replacing the projectile approach, **there is no active commercial pursuer of pure projectile ICF**.

FLARE is properly classified as a separate concept (closer to "Pulsed power implosion" or a fast ignition variant) rather than projectile ICF.

NearStar Fusion's MTIF approach uses a railgun driver but magnetizes the fuel, making it more MIF than pure IFE projectile.
