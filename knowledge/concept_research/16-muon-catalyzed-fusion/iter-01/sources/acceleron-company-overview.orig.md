## Company Info
- **Founded**: 2023 (spun out of NK Labs)
- **Location**: Cambridge, Massachusetts
- **Founders**: Ara Knaian and Seth Newburg (previously founded NK Labs)
- **Employees**: ~10
- **Resident at**: The Engine (MIT-affiliated accelerator)

## Funding
- $2M ARPA-E BETHE grant (2020, to NK Labs) — "Conditions for High-Yield Muon-Catalyzed Fusion"
- $0.5M ARPA-E grant (2023, to NK Labs) — "Active-target Muon Source for Muon-Catalyzed Fusion"
- $24M Series A (Dec 2024) — led by Lowercarbon Capital and Collaborative Fund

## Technology Summary
- Muon-catalyzed fusion: muons replace electrons in DT molecules, shrinking bond length ~200x
- Operates at 500-1,000°C (vs 100,000,000°C for plasma fusion)
- No plasma confinement needed — fuel contained by materials
- Developing: (1) high-efficiency muon source, (2) high-density fusion cell
- Target: small 100 MW power plants

## Key Milestones
- Oct 2024: Ran machine with highly compressed DT fuel at PSI
- 28 hours continuous DT fusion + 100+ hours deuterium testing
- Ran at higher pressures than previously measured for muon-catalyzed fusion rates
- Using PSI's High Intensity Proton Accelerator and Swiss Muon Source
