# Muon-Catalyzed Fusion Physics

**Sources**:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muon-catalyzed_fusion
- Various physics papers and reviews

## Mechanism
1. Negative muon replaces electron in hydrogen isotope atom, forming muonic atom
2. Muonic atom approaches another hydrogen isotope, forming muonic molecule
3. Muon orbital is ~207x smaller than electron orbital (muon is 207x heavier)
4. Nuclei brought close enough for quantum tunneling → fusion occurs
5. Muon is released (99% of the time) and catalyzes another fusion
6. Process repeats until muon decays (2.2 μs lifetime) or sticks to alpha particle

## Key Parameters
- **Alpha sticking probability**: ~0.5-1% (best measurements ~0.3-0.5%)
- **Fusions per muon (measured)**: 100-150 (experimental, at conventional pressures)
- **Fusions per muon (theoretical limit)**: ~300 (if sticking reduced or conditions optimized)
- **Breakeven requirement**: ~300-500 fusions per muon (depends on muon production cost)
- **Energy per D-T fusion**: 17.6 MeV (14.1 MeV neutron + 3.5 MeV alpha)
- **Additional breeding energy**: ~4.8 MeV from Li-6 neutron capture

## Muon Production (conventional)
- Proton accelerator → protons hit target → pions produced → pions decay to muons
- Conventional energy cost: ~5-6 GeV per muon
- Accelerator efficiency: ~40% (electrical to beam energy)
- Major facilities: PSI (Switzerland), TRIUMF (Canada), RAL (UK), LAMPF (US)

## The Energy Balance Problem
- At 100 fusions/muon: 100 × 17.6 MeV = 1.76 GeV fusion energy
- Muon production cost: ~5 GeV electrical
- Net: deeply negative — need ~5x more fusions per muon OR much cheaper muon production
- Acceleron's approach: reduce muon cost to 3 GeV AND increase fusions to 300

## Lab Experiments (Historical)
- **1957**: Luis Alvarez discovers muon-catalyzed fusion from cosmic ray muons
- **1977**: Soviet theorists predict high resonant formation rate of dtμ molecules
- **1980s-2000s**: Systematic experiments at PSI, TRIUMF, RAL, LAMPF, Dubna
- **PSI**: Measured fusion yields in liquid DT mixtures, pt fusion, sticking probability
- **RAL/RIKEN-RAL**: Precise determination of α-sticking at various tritium concentrations
- **TRIUMF**: Various muon-catalyzed fusion experiments

## Why This Is "Other" Confinement
- No plasma confinement (magnetic, inertial, or otherwise)
- Fusion occurs in cold/warm dense matter (solid, liquid, or gas)
- The "confinement" is provided by the muonic molecular bond itself
- Fundamentally different physics from all other fusion approaches
