# Confinement performance predictions for a high field axisymmetric tandem mirror

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06644
Authors: Realta Fusion / UW-Madison team (2024)

## Key Technical Details

- Integrated end plug simulation model: heating + equilibrium + transport
- POPCON technique reformulated for tandem mirror central cell
- End plug: HTS magnets + modern neutral beams → classical tandem mirror pilot plant with Q > 5
- Design uses lower temperatures, neutral beam energies, and end plug performance requirements than historical designs (e.g., MARS)
- Non-Maxwellian conditions in end plugs; near-Maxwellian in central cell
- Classical radial transport identified as significant factor
- Machine learning optimization applied to design parameters
- Requires stabilization against MHD and trapped particle modes

## Hammir Design
- 50-meter center cell → Q > 5
- Longer center cell → Q > 10+
- Pre-conceptual design publication expected 2026
