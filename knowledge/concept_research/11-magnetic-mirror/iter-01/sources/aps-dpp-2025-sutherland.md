# APS DPP 2025 - Realta Fusion Development Pathway

Source: https://meetings-archive.aps.org/dpp/2025/gm12/2/
Presenter: Derek A Sutherland (Realta Fusion)
Session: DOE Milestone Awardee Physics Basis I
Date: November 18, 2025

## Key Details

### WHAM Experiment
- Currently operational
- Two 17 T HTS magnets made by Commonwealth Fusion Systems

### Anvil Device (Next Step)
- Commercial-scale high-field simple mirror
- Primary objective: demonstrate stable sustainment of end-plug plasma conditions required for tandem mirror pilot plant
- Secondary application: deuterium-tritium volumetric neutron source for materials testing

### Hammir Facility (Pilot Plant)
- Two end-plugs connected to a central cell → axisymmetric tandem mirror fusion pilot plant
- Target: electric gain Qe > 1, net electricity Pe,out > 50 MWe, for at least 3 hours continuously
- Meets National Academies pilot plant standards
