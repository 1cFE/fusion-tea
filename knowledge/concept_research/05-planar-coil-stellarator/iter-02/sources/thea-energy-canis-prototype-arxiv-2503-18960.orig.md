# Canis HTS Planar Coil Array Prototype

**Source**: arXiv:2503.18960v1 — "Prototyping and Test of the 'Canis' HTS Planar Coil Array for Stellarator Field Shaping"
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18960v1
**Date**: March 2025
**Relevance**: Confirms REBCO conductor, 20 K operation, and validates planar coil approach

## Array Configuration

- 9 HTS planar shaping coils in a 3x3 grid
- Each coil operates independently (own power supply + cooling circuit)

## HTS Conductor

- **REBCO (rare-earth barium copper oxide) coated conductors**
- Sourced from three different commercial suppliers (demonstrates manufacturing flexibility)
- Current density exceeding 200 A/mm² at operating conditions

## Operating Parameters

- Temperature: 20 K (supercritical helium cooling)
- Nominal current: 150 A per coil
- Maximum current: 250 A per coil
- Self-field on conductor: 3.7 T at nominal operation
- Array stored energy: 34.5 kJ

## Magnetic Field Performance

- Generated stellarator-relevant magnetic field shapes
- Two Eos-relevant configurations tested (EOS1 and EOS2 patches)
- Peak fields: ~47 mT at scanning plane 25 cm distant

## Key Validation Results

- Closed loop field control to within 1% of predicted field
- EOS1 patch: 0.56% RMS field error
- EOS2 patch: 0.60% RMS field error

## Significance

Confirms that planar shaping coils can generate complex 3D magnetic field geometries required for stellarator plasma confinement. Validates Thea Energy's approach of using simple, mass-manufacturable coils with software-defined field control.
