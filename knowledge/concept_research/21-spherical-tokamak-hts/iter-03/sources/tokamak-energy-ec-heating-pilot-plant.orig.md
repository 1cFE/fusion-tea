# Tokamak Energy: EC Heating and Current Drive for Pilot Plant

**Source**: EPJ Web of Conferences, 2026
**URL**: https://epjwoc.epj.org/articles/epjconf/abs/2026/02/epjconf_rfppc2026_02014/epjconf_rfppc2026_02014.html
**Title**: "Progress in the pre-conceptual design of the auxiliary heating and current drive system for the Tokamak Energy Fusion Pilot Plant"
**Authors**: Include Alexandra Alieva (RF Physicist at Tokamak Energy)

## Key Findings

- Tokamak Energy FPP flat-top phase investigations rely **exclusively on electron cyclotron (EC) waves** as auxiliary heating and current drive source
- Parametric optimization of flat-top EC current drive scheme via ray-tracing simulations for **three plasma scenarios**, each having different magnetic field and aspect ratio
- Purpose: maximizing the normalized current drive efficiency ζ_ECCD
- EC waves in **O mode polarization** are capable of efficiently driving plasma current throughout the plasma volume
- Supports the notion that **EC waves can be the single auxiliary power source for flat-top operations**

## Implications

- This is a significant shift from the combined NBI + ECRH approach used on ST40
- For the pilot plant (ST-E1), ECRH appears to be the primary/sole heating method during burn phase
- NBI may still play a role in startup or non-flat-top phases, but the pilot plant design is converging on EC-only for the main operational phase
- "Initial investigations" qualifier suggests this isn't fully finalized
