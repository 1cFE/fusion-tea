# Tokamak Energy: DPP 2025 Overview of Fusion Pilot Plant Program

**Source**: APS Division of Plasma Physics 2025
**URL**: https://meetings-archive.aps.org/dpp/2025/gm12/8/
**Title**: "Overview of Tokamak Energy's Fusion Pilot Plant Program"

## Confirmed ST-E1 Revision D Parameters

- **Major radius**: 5.0 m
- **Aspect ratio**: 2.3
- **On-axis toroidal field**: 5.25 T
- **Net electric power**: 450-750 MWe (depending on technology and physics assumptions)
- **Blanket**: Outboard-only liquid lithium breeder blanket, TBR = 1.2
- **Design designation**: ST-E1 Revision D (final pre-conceptual design point)
- **Configuration**: High-field, low aspect ratio tokamak

## Design Process

- Iterative methodology: two-week cycles → two-month iterations → one six-month iteration
- Maintenance scheme and its implications on other systems was an early-stage priority
- Demonstrated compatibility with reactor-level performance and availability factor

## Not Mentioned in Abstract

- Q value / fusion gain
- Primary heating method details
- Power conversion cycle type
- Pulse length for ST-E1 specifically

## Power Output Discrepancy vs Earlier Designs

| Parameter | Pre-Revision D (2023-2024) | Revision D (DPP 2025) |
|-----------|---------------------------|----------------------|
| Major radius | 4.25 m | 5.0 m |
| Aspect ratio | 2.0 | 2.3 |
| Toroidal field | 4.25 T | 5.25 T |
| Net electric | 85 MWe | 450-750 MWe |
| Fusion power | 800 MW | Not stated explicitly |

Note: Some sources (press releases) cite "up to 200 MWe" which may represent an intermediate design point or a more conservative public target.

## Disruption Modelling Paper (Dec 2025)

A separate paper (arxiv:2512.16604) uses parameters R0=4.25m, A=2.15, Bt=4.0T, Ip=13.6 MA — these appear to be pre-Revision D values, suggesting the disruption modelling work may have used an earlier design point.
