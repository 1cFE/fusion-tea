# Tokamak Energy ST-E1 Design Evolution

## Source
Multiple sources compiled from DPP 2024, DPP 2025, and press releases.

## Initial Design (October 2024, DPP 2024)
- Aspect ratio: 2.0
- Plasma major radius: 4.25 m
- Magnetic field: 4.25 T
- Fusion power: 800 MW
- Net electricity: 85 MW
- Blanket: Liquid lithium tritium breeding blanket
- HTS magnets

Source: World Nuclear News, NEI Magazine, Tokamak Energy press release (Oct 2024)
- https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/tokamak-energy-gives-details-of-pilot-fusion-energy-plant-design
- https://www.neimagazine.com/news/tokamak-energy-unveils-fusion-pilot-design/

## Revision D (November 2025, DPP 2025)
- Major radius: 5.0 m
- Aspect ratio: 2.3
- On-axis toroidal field: 5.25 T
- Net electric power: 450-750 MW (depending on technology and physics assumptions)
- Outboard-only liquid lithium breeder blanket, TBR = 1.2
- HTS magnets

Source: APS DPP 2025 abstract
- https://meetings-archive.aps.org/dpp/2025/gm12/8/

## Key Changes (Initial → Rev D)
- Major radius: 4.25 m → 5.0 m (+18%)
- Aspect ratio: 2.0 → 2.3 (less "spherical", more conventional)
- Toroidal field: 4.25 T → 5.25 T (+24%)
- Power output: 85 MW net → 450-750 MW net (massive increase)
- Fusion power: 800 MW → presumably much higher given net output increase

## Notes
- Rev D is described as "final pre-conceptual design point"
- A=2.3 is at the upper boundary of "spherical tokamak" — the company still self-identifies as ST
- The large increase in power output from initial to Rev D suggests significant design philosophy change
- Part of US DOE Milestone-Based Fusion Development Program
