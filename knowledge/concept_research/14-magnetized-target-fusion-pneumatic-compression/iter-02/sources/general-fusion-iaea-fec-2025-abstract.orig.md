# IAEA FEC 2025: LM26 Abstract — Hildebrand et al.

**Source**: 30th IAEA Fusion Energy Conference (FEC 2025), October 13-18, 2025
**URL**: https://conferences.iaea.org/event/392/contributions/35891/attachments/19864/33918/IAEA%20FEC%202025%20LM26%20Abstract%20-%20Hildebrand.pdf

## Key Technical Details

- LM26 is a 50% scale plasma of a commercial fusion machine
- Uses Marshall gun to inject deuterium plasma into target chamber
- Compression via electromagnetic theta-pinch of solid lithium liner (demo surrogate)
- Commercial compression solution remains liquid metal with pneumatic pistons

## Milestones/Targets
- 2025 target: achieve fusion conditions >100 million degrees Celsius (10 keV) via compressional heating
- 2026 target: achieve Lawson criterion (nTτ > 1e21 m⁻³·keV·s)
- First stage operational, compression experiments began early 2025

## Key Results (as of publication)
- Initial results from solid wall MTF experiments shared
- Measurements from plasma and solid liner position diagnostics
- Confirmed plasma energy confinement time >10 ms (required pre-compression time for LM26)

## Significance
- First peer-reviewed confirmation of LM26 operational parameters
- Validates electromagnetic compression as a viable surrogate for studying MTF physics
- 50% scale claim gives insight into commercial machine dimensions
