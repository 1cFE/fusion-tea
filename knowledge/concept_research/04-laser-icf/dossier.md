# Laser ICF (p-B11)

**Company**: HB11 Energy
**Last updated**: 2026-03-07
**Iterations completed**: 2
**Overall confidence**: medium

## Summary

HB11 Energy is an Australian startup (founded 2017, Sydney) pursuing proton-boron-11 (p-B11) fusion via laser-driven inertial confinement using a two-laser "Proton Fast Ignition" scheme. A nanosecond laser generates a kilotesla-scale magnetic field (via capacitor-coil target) that radially confines a cylindrical HB11 fuel pellet, while a picosecond petawatt CPA laser accelerates protons into the compressed fuel to trigger fusion — described by the company as a "hybrid burn target design." The reaction produces three alpha particles (8.7 MeV per reaction) with negligible neutron output, making it nearly aneutronic. The company targets ~1 Hz repetition rate and 1 GW baseload power using arrays of thousands of commercial lasers, with energy conversion via conventional steam cycle. HB11 is currently at an early stage (~10^10 alpha/sr demonstrated at Osaka LFEX, ~4 orders of magnitude from net energy gain) with 12 experiments completed at international laser facilities and is pursuing a "components first" commercialization strategy (selling lasers and targets) while developing the full reactor concept.

### Magnet Type
- **Value**: `None (IFE)`
- **Confidence**: medium
- **Citation**: Patent US10410752B2 (iter-01/sources/hb11-patent-reactor-design.md)
- **Notes**: HB11 uses laser-driven kilotesla magnetic fields (generated by a capacitor-coil target) to radially confine the reaction. These are transient, laser-generated fields (~ns duration), not external superconducting or resistive magnets. Per schema definition, `None (IFE)` means "no magnetic confinement of plasma (driver subsystem may contain magnets, but these confine the beam, not the plasma)." In HB11's case, the kT field does confine the fusion plasma radially, making this a borderline case. However, there are no conventional magnet systems (superconducting, resistive, or pulsed EM), so `None (IFE)` is the best schema fit. Flag for schema review if more laser-ICF concepts use laser-driven confinement fields.

## Remaining Gaps

1. **Energy Capture (medium confidence)**: The pivot from direct electrostatic conversion (2018 patent, 2020 messaging) to "conventional steam cycle generator" (2025 website) is now documented but the underlying engineering rationale is unclear. A p-B11 concept with nearly all energy in charged alphas would seem better suited to direct conversion — the steam cycle choice may reflect practical constraints or simplified public messaging. A future Hora et al. review paper or detailed technical presentation could clarify the design trade.

2. **Magnet Type (medium confidence)**: The laser-driven kilotesla field remains a borderline case for the `None (IFE)` schema value. The kT field does confine the fusion plasma radially, which is functionally different from "no magnetic confinement." This is a schema-level issue more than a research gap.

3. **Published Machine/Plant?**: No specific reactor design published beyond the 2018 patent. The patent describes a conceptual reactor geometry but not a complete plant design.

4. **Lab Experiments**: 12 experiments at international facilities (Osaka LFEX, Belfast TARANIS, PALS Prague). Osaka LFEX (2022) demonstrated ~10^10 alpha/sr. DOE INFUSE collaboration with LLE/Rochester ongoing. Phys. Rev. Research paper (2025) on alpha particle production from novel targets exists but content not extracted (PDF binary).

5. **Funding/Scale**: Very early stage — total funding ~A$12.8M (A$4.6M pre-seed 2021 + A$8.2M Defence Trailblazer 2025). Small relative to other fusion startups.
