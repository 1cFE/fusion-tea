# SHINE Technologies — Beam-Target D-T Fusion Neutron Source

## Key Facts (from web research, 2026-05)

- **HQ**: Janesville, Wisconsin
- **Approach**: Compact linear particle accelerator drives beam-on-target fusion
- **Reaction**: Deuterium ions accelerated to up to **300 kV**, fired into **tritium gas target** → D-T fusion → 14.1 MeV neutrons
- **Output**: Up to **5 × 10^13 fusion reactions/second**; described as "world's brightest steady-state D-T neutron source"
- **Operation**: Continuous / steady-state (not pulsed)
- **Products**:
  - Mo-99 (medical isotope) via fission of LEU subcritical aqueous solution driven by D-T fusion neutrons
  - Lu-177 (medical)
  - **FLARE** = Fusion Linear Accelerator for Radiation Effects — neutron irradiation for aerospace/defense electronics testing
  - **LIBRTI** = 14 MeV D-T neutron source delivery to UKAEA in 2027 (fusion materials testing)
- **Not a power reactor** — no electricity generation, no breeding blanket
- **Precursor tech**: Phoenix Nuclear Labs neutron generator (spun out / sibling company)
