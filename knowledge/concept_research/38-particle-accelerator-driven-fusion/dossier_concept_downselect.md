## Summary

SHINE Technologies (Janesville/Fitchburg, WI) uses **accelerator-driven
beam-target fusion**: high-current particle beams strike a tritium gas target,
producing an intense flux of **14 MeV D-T fusion neutrons** (no confined
thermonuclear plasma). The company follows a four-phase roadmap: (1) industrial
component inspection, (2) medical-isotope production (the **Chrysalis**
facility — positioned as the world's largest Mo-99 source, NRC Part-50
licensed, ~8 fusion systems, DOE/NNSA-supported), (3) spent-nuclear-fuel
recycling, and (4) fusion power. SHINE reported "visible proof of fusion"
(2023). The energy phase (4) is a long-horizon objective; current systems are
neutron sources, so commercial fusion-power-plant parameters are not yet
engineered or disclosed.
