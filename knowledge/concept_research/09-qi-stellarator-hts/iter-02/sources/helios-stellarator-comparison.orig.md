# Helios Stellarator Design - Comparison Reference

**Source**: Overview of the Helios Design: A Practical Planar Coil Stellarator Fusion Power Plant
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/html/2512.08027v1
**Company**: Thea Energy (planar coil stellarator approach)

Used as comparison point for Stellaris where Proxima hasn't disclosed specifics.

## Key Parameters:
- **Heating**: ECRH at 170 GHz (ITER-spec gyrotrons), 10 MW startup, 1 MW in ignited phase
- **Power conversion**: Steam Rankine cycle, superheated steam at 635°C, ~40% efficiency
- **Blanket**: Lead-lithium eutectic, 65% Li-6 enrichment, TBR 1.1 (idealized 1.3)
- **Shielding**: Multi-layer (tungsten carbide + boron carbide + 316L steel + borated water + borated HDPE), minimum 1.2 m plasma-coil distance
- **Fusion power**: 958 MW
- **Thermal power**: 1.1 GW
- **Electrical output**: 390 MW net (438 MW gross - 48 MW facility loads)

## Relevance to Stellaris:
- Both are QI-optimized stellarator power plant designs
- Both use lead-lithium blankets (Helios: PbLi eutectic; Stellaris: WCLL)
- ECRH is confirmed as the heating method for Helios → supports inference that Stellaris also uses ECRH
- Steam Rankine cycle used by Helios with water-cooled blanket → supports inference for Stellaris WCLL
- Different coil approach: Helios uses planar coils, Stellaris uses 3D non-planar HTS coils
