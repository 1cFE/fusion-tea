# Stellaris Paper - Collected Technical Details

**Source**: Stellaris: A high-field quasi-isodynamic stellarator for a prototypical fusion power plant
**DOI**: 10.1016/j.fusengdes.2025.114868
**Journal**: Fusion Engineering and Design, Vol. 214, May 2025
**Authors**: Proxima Fusion and partners (KIT, IPP, PSI, BNET, others)

## Key Parameters (from secondary sources - paper is paywalled)

- **Fusion power**: 2.7 GW (peak)
- **Thermal power**: ~3.1 GW
- **Electrical output**: ~1 GW (net)
- **Volume-averaged plasma beta**: ~2.76%
- **Magnetic field**: up to 20 T (HTS REBCO)
- **Blanket**: WCLL (Water-Cooled Lithium-Lead), TBR 1.07
- **Structural material**: EUROFER97, <500°C operation
- **Divertor**: Island divertor geometry
- **Operation**: Steady-state, disruption-free

## Paper Scope (from KIT repository abstract)
"A comprehensive reactor study, ranging from optimization of the plasma confinement region to first wall cooling, divertor considerations, blanket design, magnet quench safety, support structures, and remote maintenance solutions."

"Trade-offs between physics and engineering constraints can lead to a compelling stellarator design, suited for power plant applications."

"This class of QI-HTS stellarators, labeled Stellaris, is shown to achieve an extensive set of desirable properties for reactor candidates simultaneously for the first time."
