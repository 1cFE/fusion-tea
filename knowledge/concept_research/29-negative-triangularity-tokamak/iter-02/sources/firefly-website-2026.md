## Key Information

- Tagline: "The Rapid Reliable Path to Commercial Fusion Energy"

- Emphasizes leveraging established technologies rather than novel designs

## Team

- **Rustem Ospanov** (Founder/CEO): PhD experimental physics, 18 years at CERN and Fermilab
- **Stephen Gibson** (Lead Mechanical Design Engineer): Former CERN fellow, senior engineer at ADAM SA

## Advisors
- **Jérôme Bucalossi**: Director of IRFM, WEST project leader — significant because WEST uses ICRH (55-65 MHz) + LHCD (3.7 GHz) + ECRH (105 GHz) for heating
- **Mitsuru Kikuchi**: JT-60 tokamak expert, negative triangularity plasma pioneer (authored "Negative Triangularity Tokamak as Fusion Energy System" 2014)
- **Michel Huguet**: ITER and JET experience
- **Michiel Peters**: Tokamak/stellarator physics PhD
