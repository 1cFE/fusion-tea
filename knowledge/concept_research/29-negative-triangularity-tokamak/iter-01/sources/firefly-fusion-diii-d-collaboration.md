# Firefly Fusion – DIII-D National Fusion Facility Collaboration

**Source**: https://d3dfusion.org/fireflyfusion/
**Accessed**: 2026-03-06

## Key Technical Details

- Firefly Fusion collaborates with DIII-D to investigate negative triangularity plasmas
- Focus: high-confinement, disruption-resilient operation
- Research areas: diagnostics, edge physics, control strategies
- Work supports validation of predictive simulation tools and design space refinement for prototype tokamak LUCIOLE

## Magnet Approach (Prototype)
- LUCIOLE uses "actively-cooled copper magnets" — described as enabling "high-performance experiments and rapid technology iterations"
- This is the prototype approach; commercial plants target HTS magnets

## Status
- No full-time staff on site at DIII-D yet
- Involved in collaborative experiments, analysis, and proposal planning
