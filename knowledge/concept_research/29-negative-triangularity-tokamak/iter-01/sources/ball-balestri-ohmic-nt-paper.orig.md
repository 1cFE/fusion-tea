# On the Feasibility of Ohmically Heated Negative Triangularity Tokamak Power Plants

**Source**: https://arxiv.org/html/2407.06439v2
**Authors**: A. Balestri, J. Ball, S. Coda (EPFL Swiss Plasma Center)
**Published**: 2024

## Key Findings

- Explores whether NT tokamaks could operate with ohmic heating alone (no auxiliary heating)
- Ohmic NT scenarios advantageous for "design points with high fusion gain (Q≳10), especially when achieved using a strong magnetic field"
- Ohmic heating underperforms for large, lower-field devices (ITER-like); external heating necessary there
- For compact, high-field designs, ohmic-only operation may be viable
