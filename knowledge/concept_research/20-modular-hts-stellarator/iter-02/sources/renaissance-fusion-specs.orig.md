# Renaissance Fusion — Stellarator Technical Specifications

**Source**: Nuclear Fusion 64 (2024) 026007; J. Nuclear Materials 599 (2024) 155239; company website; TechCrunch (2025)
**Extracted**: 2026-03-06 (iter-02)

## Key Parameters (from Nuclear Fusion paper — optimized design point)

- **Net electric power**: ~1 GWe
- **Thermal power**: 2 GW (heat production)
- **Fusion gain**: Q = ∞ (ignited design)
- **Magnetic field**: 9-15 T on-axis (optimized range; 10 T nominal)
- **Peak coil field**: 20-40 T
- **Major radius**: ≤4 m (compact design)
- **Aspect ratio**: ~4 (low aspect ratio — "more like cored apples than bicycle tires")
- **Plasma beta**: ~5%
- **Temperature**: 10 keV
- **Rotational transform**: ι₂/₃ = 0.9
- **Confinement re-normalization factor**: 1.4 (ISS04 scaling)
- **Plant availability**: 85%

## Heating

- **Primary**: Negative Neutral Beam Injection (NNBI)
- "60% neutralization efficiency compared to positive ion beams"
- Auxiliary power minimized through alpha heating (ignited design)
- NOTE: This is distinct from ECRH — Renaissance Fusion paper explicitly uses NNBI

## Blanket and Tritium (from J. Nuclear Materials paper)

- **Type**: Flowing liquid Li-LiH metal wall with Pb pebbles
- **Composition**: 5% lithium, 95% lithium hydride
- **Structure**: 15 cm Pb layer + 18 cm Li-LiH breeding layer
- **Neutron shield**: 50 cm vanadium hydride (VH₂)
- **Bioshield**: 1.3 m concrete
- **Neutron energy multiplication**: fm = 1.24
- **Neutron absorption**: 99.99% of neutron energy (company claim)
- **Wall loading**: Up to 25 MW/m² (enabled by liquid metal technology)
- **TBR**: ~1.60 (from iter-01 research; paper gives fm = 1.24 energy multiplication)
- Liquid metal serves as: first wall, shield, coolant, tritium breeder, heat extraction working fluid

## Energy Conversion

- **Cycle**: Combined supercritical CO₂ Brayton-Rankine cycle
- **Thermal efficiency**: 49-51% (cycle efficiency)
- **Net plant efficiency**: 34% (including reactor auxiliaries)
- **LM operating temperatures**: 700-900°C
- Optimized via genetic algorithm (Energy Conversion and Management paper, 2023)

## HTS Magnet Technology

- **Material**: REBCO HTS
- **Operating temperature**: 20 K
- **Manufacturing**: Direct deposition of HTS film on ~1 m diameter cylinders
- **Patterning**: Laser ablation to create current paths (1D/2D coils)
- Roll-to-roll deposition: 24 cm-wide Hastelloy tape (20m MOCVD, 10m PVD)
- **Demonstrated**: 6 T peak Helmholtz-type HTS magnet, 1.2 m diameter, 20 K
- Critical current safety factor: ~1.33

## Milestones

- €32M Series A raised (March 2025)
- Wide HTS "carpets" production in coming months
- Demonstrator integrating tubular HTS magnets + liquid Li walls: end of 2026
- Full stellarator reactor: early 2030s
- Strategy: retrofit coal/fission plants by replacing heat source with fusion
