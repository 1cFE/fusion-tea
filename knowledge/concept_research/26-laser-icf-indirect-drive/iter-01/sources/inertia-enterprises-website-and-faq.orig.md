## Company Overview
- Founded 2024 by Jeff Lawson (ex-Twilio CEO), Dr. Annie Kritcher (NIF lead designer), Prof. Mike Dunne (ex-SLAC director)
- $450M Series A led by Bessemer Venture Partners, with GV, Modern Capital, Threshold Ventures
- Goal: gigawatt-scale, utility-scale fusion power plant within a decade

## Laser System — Thunderwall
- Type: Diode-Pumped Solid-State Laser (DPSSL)
- Unit cell: 10 kJ beam, 10 Hz repetition rate, 10% wallplug efficiency
- Commercial plant: 1,000–4,000 beamlines → 10 MJ total on target
- "50 times more powerful (in average power) than any prior laser of its type"
- One-tenth the size of NIF footprint
- "1000 beamlines built in factories and delivered by truck"

## Target Design
- Indirect drive: laser → hohlraum → X-rays → capsule compression
- Based on Annie Kritcher's "Hybrid-E" ICF design (the design that achieved NIF ignition Dec 2022)
- 4.5 mm targets, mass-manufactured, < $1 each
- 10 shots per second target injection

## Energy Capture
- Not explicitly specified beyond thermal conversion
- 1.5 GW baseload, dispatchable electricity

## Tritium Breeding
- Liquid lithium pipes lining the fusion chamber
- Neutrons from fusion convert lithium to tritium
- Startup tritium from U.S. government stockpiles
- Operational inventory: hundreds of grams at any time
- Per-shot: milligrams of tritium
- Annual lithium consumption: equivalent to 20 EV batteries
- Tritium extraction from flowing lithium "is still an area of active development"

## Why Indirect Drive (from FAQ)
- All LLNL ignition achievements use indirect drive
- $20B+ government investment over decades
- Direct drive has unresolved physics: laser-plasma interactions, preheat, symmetry
- Modular design allows potential future switch to direct drive
