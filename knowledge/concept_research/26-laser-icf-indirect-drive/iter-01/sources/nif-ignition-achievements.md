# NIF Ignition Achievements — Lab Experiments for Laser ICF Indirect Drive
**Sources**: LLNL websites, Wikipedia
**Retrieved**: 2026-03-07

## Key Milestones
- Dec 5, 2022: First ignition — 3.15 MJ output from 2.05 MJ laser input (target gain ~1.54)
- Jul 30, 2023: 3.88 MJ output from same laser energy
- Oct 2023: Two shots — 2.4 MJ and 3.4 MJ output
- Feb 2024: 5.2 MJ output from 2.2 MJ input (136% energy surplus, target gain ~2.4)

## NIF Facility
- 192 laser beams, 1.8-2.2 MJ total
- Indirect drive: lasers → hohlraum → X-rays → capsule ablation
- Flashlamp-pumped Nd:glass laser (not suitable for power plant — low efficiency, low rep rate)
- Located at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

## Significance for IFE
- Demonstrated that indirect-drive ICF can achieve ignition and net energy gain
- Repeated ignition multiple times — reproducible achievement
- However, NIF laser is ~0.5-1% wallplug efficient vs. 10%+ needed for power plant
- NIF fires ~once per day vs. Hz-class needed for power plant
