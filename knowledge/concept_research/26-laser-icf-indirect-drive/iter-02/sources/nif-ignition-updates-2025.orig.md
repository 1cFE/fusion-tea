## Updated Ignition Record

- **Dec 2022**: First ignition — 3.15 MJ yield from 2.05 MJ input (gain ~1.54)
- **Jul 2023**: 3.88 MJ yield from 2.05 MJ input (gain ~1.89)
- **Oct 2023**: 3.4 MJ yield from 2.2 MJ input (record laser energy)
- **Feb 2024**: 5.2 MJ yield from 2.2 MJ input (gain ~2.3) — previous record
- **Apr 2025**: 8.6 MJ yield from 2.08 MJ input (gain ~4.1) — NEW RECORD
- **Oct 2025**: 3.5 MJ yield from 2.065 MJ input (gain ~1.74) — 10th ignition

## Key Takeaways

- 10 successful ignition experiments achieved as of Oct 2025
- Peak yield of 8.6 MJ (Apr 2025) — more than 4x laser energy input
- Enhanced Yield Capability (EYC) project: boost laser to 2.6 MJ → yields >30 MJ possible
- All experiments use indirect drive with hohlraum targets
- Variability in yields reflects sensitivity to target manufacturing and laser delivery precision
