## Phoenix Laser System

- Completed first private-sector electron-beam-pumped excimer laser (Jun 2025) — first such laser built by any organization in 20+ years
- May 2025: Achieved record 3 microsecond pulse length — longest pulse of any KrF laser
- Phoenix system fully complete H1 2026
- "Long Pulse Kinetics" (LPK) platform

## HYPER-LASER DOE Award

- DOE categorical exclusion for "HYbrid Pumped ExcimeR Laser (HYPER-LASER)"
- Technology to enhance power delivery via efficient microwave sources combined with electron beams to energize excimer gas lasers
- $9M DOE award (2023) for laser-driven IFE development

## Vulcan Facility (Next Generation)

- Up to 12 MJ laser energy — "highest laser energy in the world"
- Largest laser amplifiers ever built
- Target: complete construction by 2030
- Goal: achieve engineering breakeven (wall-plug breakeven) from fusion for first time
- Site search underway, led by Brian Boggs
- Expected to employ hundreds of people
- Power plants delivering electricity "as soon as mid-2030s"

## Tucson Manufacturing Facility

- Expanded U.S. manufacturing footprint with new Tucson facility for laser component production
