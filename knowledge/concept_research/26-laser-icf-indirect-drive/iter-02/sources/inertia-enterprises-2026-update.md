## Company

- Founded 2024 by Jeff Lawson (CEO, ex-Twilio), Dr. Annie Kritcher (Chief Scientist, NIF ignition team lead), Prof. Mike Dunne (CTO, former NIF director)
- $450M Series A (Feb 2026) led by Bessemer Venture Partners, with GV, Modern Capital, Threshold Ventures
- Working closely with LLNL on target design, fabrication, and laser technology

## Thunderwall Laser (DPSSL)

- 10 kJ per beam, 10 Hz repetition rate, 10% wallplug efficiency
- 1000-4000 beamlines → 10 MJ total on target
- "50 times as powerful (in average power) as any prior laser of its type"
- Modular: beamlines "delivered by truck"
- 3ω (frequency-tripled, UV) laser

## Power Plant Design

- Pilot: 50 MWe net to grid (initial)
- Commercial: >1 GWe net capacity
- Target gain: 18x (prototype), >30x (commercial)
- 1.5 GW baseload electricity (long-term goal)
- "turning heat into steam, which then drives a turbine" — confirms steam Rankine
- Targets: 4.5 mm diameter, <$1 each, 10/second

## Tritium Breeding

- Liquid lithium pipes lining the chamber
- Tritium extraction "still an area of active development"
- Startup tritium from U.S. government stockpiles
- Lithium quantity: "equivalent to what is used in 15 electric vehicles"
- On-site tritium inventory: ~few hundred grams

## Timeline

- Construction of pilot plant targeting 2030
- Grid-scale during 2030s

## Energy Conversion (Clarification)

Mike Dunne (ENR interview): "one million times more powerful than NIF, 20 times more efficient, 1/10th the physical footprint" — this refers to laser average power (10 MJ × 10 Hz = 100 MW average vs. NIF's ~100 W average). Hyperbolic but directionally correct for average power metric.
