Comprehensive overview of inertial fusion energy (IFE) covering candidate driver technologies (diode-pumped solid-state lasers, KrF lasers, light-ion accelerators, heavy-ion accelerators) with their efficiencies and required target gains; three reactor designs (Sombrero dry-wall, Osiris liquid-wall, Cascade granular-bed) with a comparative operating parameters table (driver energy, gain, thermal efficiency, cost of electricity); and target factory requirements, tritium handling, and environmental/safety advantages over fission. Key quantitative benchmarks include the fusion cycle gain relationship (η×G product of ~10), driver efficiencies ranging from 7.5% (KrF) to 35% (heavy-ion), and 1992 electricity costs of 3.6–6.7 cents/kWh.
