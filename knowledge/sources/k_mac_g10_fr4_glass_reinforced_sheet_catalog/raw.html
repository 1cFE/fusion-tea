G10/FR4 Phenolic Sheets

G10 Glass Reinforced Epoxy is the most versatile all-around composite laminate. G10 is a continuous glass woven fabric base impregnated with an epoxy resin binder. It has extremely high mechanical and electric strength with excellent dielectric loss properties in both wet and dry environments.

NEMA G-10 certs to MIL-I-24768/2 GEE, IPC 4101/20, IEC 60893-3 Type 2 EP GC 201
NEMA FR-4 certs to MIL-I-24768/27 GEE-F, IPC 4101/21, IEC 60893-3 Type 2 EP GC 202
