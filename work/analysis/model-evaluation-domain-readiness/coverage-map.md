## Probe outcomes and MR-7

The corrected capture contains 21 calls: twelve returned full native evidence and nine raised native failures. Every returned point has at least one failed native plant predicate. At baseline all 67 predicates were assessed: 61 satisfied, 6 violated. The failures are facility occupancy, divertor heat, water electrical rating, breeding adequacy, reference conductor current and pack fit. A report saying its predicate coverage is complete does not imply the underlying scientific domains are complete.

Cases4/5 perturb only electron density to 0.9/1.1 times baseline. The retained typed-input comparison proves every rating, geometry and price input stayed fixed. Primary total flow changes 3009.756→2542.920/3502.318kg/s, while selected cooling purchases and the compared turbine/rejection amounts remain bit-identical. Capital is unchanged at 17.751262billionUSD; LCOE changes 318.738→382.059/283.304USD/MWh through operation. The helium offered state becomes undefined in both cases, exactly as WI-080 documents. These are evaluated demand consequences, not a claim of feasible equipment or free capacity.

A geometry example illustrates why LCOE alone is unsafe: case3 a=2.2m returns 236.238USD/MWh but breeding and inventory definedness are false and 37 predicates fail. Case1 R=12.6m has undefined breeding too. Case2 R=15.7m aborts at the conductor domain. The desired historical geometry envelope therefore cannot be called supported by this package.

Native failure records contain responsible module, phase and `partial_artifacts=[]`; the evaluator exposes no upstream partial-output dictionary on these failures. [descendants.json](descendants.json) supplies graph-reachable unavailable dependent nodes, not invented results. ## Obstructions and proposed dispositions

- **Justified diagnostic repair:** conductor field/temperature refusal should include actual value, units, supported domain and calculator identity. Preserve the 20 K/20–32 T guards and explicit extrapolation semantics. Normative body is `handwritten/mfe_conductor_current/rebco_conductor_current_impl.py:25–34`; it is a preserved manual seed, registered by WI-062's regeneration inventory.
- **Justified mathematical-domain repair:** guard positive finite primary suction before `p/(p−dp)` and fractional power in `handwritten/mfe_primary_loop/primary_coolant_loop_impl.py:27–29`. Case14 supplies p=100000Pa with dp=300319.896Pa from unchanged demand; complex compressor outputs currently escape into a six-field Pydantic output-validation error. Reject with actual p/dp/suction values in Pa. This preserves the mathematical/physical limit and does not add a compressor map.
- **Genuine current limits:** fixed breeding geometry, fixed conductor temperature/product and restricted field range, conditional plasma transport, single-point thermal offers, fixed-pressure steam tables and coupled two-phase endpoint restrictions, conceptual cost transfers and omitted capacity/qualification scope. No empirical widening is proposed as a quick repair.
- **Independently supportable future extension, not yet released:** new breeding transport states could expand geometry coverage; independently checked conductor data could cover other fields/temperatures; new property tables could support a broader steam state region. Each requires its own source/math evidence and independent review. None is established by the bounded probes or by a finite extrapolated formula.
