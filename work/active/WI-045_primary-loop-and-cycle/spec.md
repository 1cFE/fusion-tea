## Why this item exists

Two of the plant's three held multipliers live here. Pumping power `p_pump` = 195 MW and thermal efficiency `eta_th` = 0.333 are instance constants; the power balance reads them as scalars, so nothing the design does — a bigger plasma, more heat, a different outlet temperature — changes what the coolant loop draws or what the cycle returns. The repository's own error history says these are the load-bearing ones: re-basing `p_pump` from 1 to 195 MW moved LCOE 21 % and grew the recirculation fence from 32 to 184 violating points (`.project/active/demo-depth-rubric/gap-report.md@fc80e5b2` Row 7); the cycle efficiency moved LCOE 13–23 % in the committed A/B while the equipment rates moved it under 1.1 % (Row 8). Rubric Row 7's P3 anchor asks that "the computed loop feeds the recirculation/net-power constraints so coolant choices push back on feasibility"; Row 8's P2 asks for "cycle efficiency computed from coolant outlet temperature and a stated cycle model, verified" (`.project/active/demo-depth-rubric/rubric.md@dc0f0b6d`). Both cells score P1 today.

The owner's two rulings on `p_pump` both hold here and do not conflict. The 2026-08-28 ruling rejected expressing `p_pump` as a fixed fraction of `p_th`, because that asserts a linearity across swept (R, a) no source establishes and retires a study lever (`work/orchestration/goals/p-pump-basis/trail.md@07b82c6d` § Goal close Ruling 2; `work/completed/20260828_WI-033_p-pump-rebase/spec.md@83ccd8f9` line 22). The 2026-09-01 correction says a goal that computes pumping power from a real loop — flow, pressure drop, parasitics — is not the fraction form and needs no reopening (`.project/concepts/stellarator-demo-maturation.md@81a4fee8` § Corrections — 2026-09-01). This item builds the loop; the 195 MW survives as the compatibility term and the named historical comparison arm.

The grounding probe (`work/orchestration/goals/plant-closure/evidence/grounding_probe/summary.md`) predicts what the two closures do at the design point: the loop lands at 174.6 MW (5.6 % of source heat — inside DI-008's 4–6 % helium-primary band, from a stated circuit with 14 loops, not a fraction), the cycle at 0.411 on the printed helium-primary Rankine fit at a 480 °C turbine inlet, and LCOE moves 322.32 → 305.58 (loop alone) and → 247.71 (cycle alone). Unlike WI-041..044 this item moves the baseline by design; what makes the move honest is the held-reproduction rule (packet § 3): with the direct terms at their held values the new package reproduces the entering pin bit-for-bit, so the round's study assigns every $/MWh of the move to one closure.

## What must be true afterward (requirements)

#### MR-WI045-1: A concept-agnostic primary-loop calc computes the loop from the source heat and a representative circuit, in sized-flow mode
The library SHALL add `calc def 'Primary Coolant Loop'` (`models/library/analyses/mfe_primary_loop.sysml`, flat arithmetic) taking `q_source_in` [MW], `T_in_in` [K] (blanket inlet), `dT_blanket_in` [K], `cp_in` [J/(kg K)], `gamma_in` [1], `p_loop_in` [Pa] (compressor discharge, the loop's nominal pressure), `n_loops_in` [1], `mdot_loop_ref_in` [kg/s] (the reference per-loop flow, also the rated capacity), `dp_loop_ref_in` [Pa] (the per-path loss at the reference per-loop flow), `f_loss_in` [1] (a loss multiplier, 1.0), `eta_is_in` [1], `eta_drive_in` [1], `loop_live_in` [0/1], and producing `mdot = q_source·1e6 / (cp·dT_blanket)`, `T_out = T_in + dT_blanket`, `mdot_loop = mdot / n_loops`, `dp_loop = f_loss·dp_loop_ref·(mdot_loop / mdot_loop_ref)²`, `r_comp = p_loop / (p_loop − dp_loop)`, `T_comp_in = T_in / (1 + (r_comp^((γ−1)/γ) − 1) / eta_is)`, `w_fluid = mdot·cp·(T_in − T_comp_in) / 1e6`, `p_elec = w_fluid / eta_drive`, `q_ihx = q_source + w_fluid`, `p_elec_live = loop_live·p_elec`, `q_recovered_live = loop_live·w_fluid` (the packet § 2 names; the research § 2 equations, lines 108–129). Sized-flow mode is the mode: the flow follows the source heat at a held blanket temperature rise, the outlet temperature is held by construction, and the pressure loss and work respond to the flow (packet § 8 item 1). The doc SHALL state the dependency order (heat → flow → loss at nominal density → compressor → work → IHX duty) and that there is no edge from `p_th` back to the flow. *Priority:* P0. *Rationale:* the goal's (a), first bullet; Row 7's P2 anchor ("pumping and parasitic loads forward-computed from loop flow and pressure drop"). *Validation:* the calc exists with every formal; the reference-circuit reconstruction (MR-WI045-13). *Source:* `goal.md` § Answered when (a); packet §§ 2, 8; research § 2.

#### MR-WI045-2: The loop count is sized at the design point and held as an instance integer

#### MR-WI045-3: The loss law is a constant loss coefficient at nominal density, with the constant-property error bounded
The pressure loss SHALL follow `dp = f_loss·dp_ref·(mdot_loop / mdot_loop_ref)²` at the reference's nominal density — the research's "constant-loss-coefficient approximation about a reference layout" (line 141) — with each of blanket, external piping and IHX carried in `dp_loop_ref` as the flow-weighted per-path sum (329.2 kPa; the IB and OB paths never added in series), and the design SHALL bound the error of ideal-gas constant properties over the 300–500 °C, 8 MPa window (the research's 0.1 % `cp` check against the source's implied 5187.6 J/(kg K); the density ratio `rho_ref / rho` set to 1 and said so). A broad R/a extrapolation of the loss law is disclosed as unsupported in the doc. *Priority:* P0. *Rationale:* the sourced circuit gives component losses, not channel geometry; the constant-coefficient law computes loss from flow, which is the difference from assuming pumping proportional to power. *Validation:* the published hydraulic bookkeeping (MR-WI045-13); the design's error bound recorded. *Source:* research § 2 lines 131–143; packet § 8 item 1.
