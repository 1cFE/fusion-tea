## Chosen architecture and physical meaning

One handwritten state solver owns the coupled water circuit. Named component occurrences expose actual inlet/outlet states, flow, heat and work from that solver. Components are main steam generator, HP turbine, reheater, LP turbine, open feedwater heater, condenser, condensate/feedwater pumps and generator under `turbine`. Turbine and pump definitions are reusable. They are not empty labels or separate iterative solvers. Component-owned assumptions feed the solver, while component results are aliases of its outputs. Water, heat, shaft and electric ports describe physical exchanges; scalar bindings execute the analysis. The cyclic physical circuit therefore does not introduce a cyclic executable graph.

The closed water path is feed pump → SG → HP turbine → reheater → LP turbine → condenser → condensate pump → open heater → feed pump, with a separate HP extraction into the open heater. Salt splits into parallel SG/reheater branches, each returning at the existing pre-pump return temperature. Duty fractions determine salt-flow fractions. There is no second full-flow salt heat credit. The existing heat-transport module owns source heat, primary compression, secondary salt work and the salt boundary. Cooling-water circulation and final cycle rejection live under existing CAS25 `heat_rejection`; the condenser remains CAS23. The additional physical subcomponents receive no invented individual capital charges.

The original draft's specialization alternative is resolved in favor of the demonstrated additive route. `structure-options.md` and `evidence/structure-probe/` retain successful generation/execution and the failed retyping/calculation-replacement probes. The successful toy demonstrates branch-before-property behavior, explicit invalid mode refusal and state outputs remaining outputs. It does not establish production integration or physics. Generic fitted/direct modes must still pass their actual regression contracts.

## States, domains and failure semantics

Use the full three original NIST grids in a canonical physical asset `models/library/data/matched_steam_properties.json`, with original source paths/hashes, units, reference state, phase labels and separate saturation endpoints. A fixed-pressure interpolation table is adequate for this bounded topology. It is not a general water property solver. Do not install a new runtime dependency or parse HTML at runtime. Embed the exact normalized physical table under the existing WI-066 staged-asset convention with `SOURCE_ASSET_SHA256`; preserve canonical/staged equality and generation receipts.

Supported main/extraction pressures are exactly 6.2/0.8 MPa. Main feed and steam endpoints must lie inside the corresponding liquid/vapor tables; main steam is above its 277.73289°C saturation endpoint and at most 455°C. Reheat is superheated at 0.8 MPa and at most 455°C. Condenser saturation lies within 20–60°C, below the extraction saturation temperature, and the actual/ideal LP endpoints must lie within the supported two-phase enthalpy/entropy range. Report raw quality within [0,1]. No 0.95 moisture fence is adopted; turbine qualification remains false/unresolved. Steam pressures, performance factors, temperatures, heat and salt inputs must be finite; efficiencies satisfy 0<eta<=1; available heat, salt capacity flow and pump pressure rises are positive. Mixing must yield 0<bleed<1. The actual boundary join must satisfy available heat = total salt flow times salt heat capacity times supply/return difference under the arithmetic residual policy; inconsistency is an explicit input-boundary refusal, not permission to predict two different heat sources. Refuse unsupported states before interpolation; never use a clamping interpolator as extrapolation policy.

Pumps use the declared inlet-volume approximation. The independent feed-pump check is about 0.1453% above the local table h(s) work; condensate comparisons are also recorded, with no 40°C compressed-state benchmark. The condensate pump outlet therefore exposes only pressure/enthalpy/flow and work. It declares no temperature or entropy output. Other supported states expose their table-derived temperature/entropy. Water ports carry only pressure/enthalpy/flow, so they make no false universal full-state promise.

Enabled numerical/domain invalidity raises a precise retained refusal. Finite but physically adverse heat profiles retain raw minimum gaps and failed admission checks; undefined UA has an explicit availability flag and cannot be consumed as valid zero capacity. Do not clamp a negative gap or replace a failed result with a passing scalar. The minimum required heat-flow direction is strictly positive gap. Nominal 20 K follows the selected temperatures and is not a new universal equipment constraint. The earlier Round 1 agent-proposed minimum-20 K screening and its failed 10 K example remain intact as historical scenario evidence. The current positive-gap heat-direction check does not supersede that screen or qualify installed equipment; report the distinction explicitly when comparing those records. Production calculates exact piecewise-linear profile minima and analytic segment UA integrals, including both boiling endpoints. Stable logarithmic integration uses the constant-gap limit for equal segment endpoints and a numerically stable log-ratio otherwise.

Inactive matched mode exits before any water property access and returns documented inactive placeholders with zero incremental pumps. Its outputs are unavailable predictions, not a valid zero-power cycle. Mode inputs accept exactly 0 or 1; arbitrary mixtures are rejected. Every independent component fact and newly selected mode receives an explicit entry-point classification. State aliases must never appear as independent inputs.

## Cooling-water reference scenario

The current design selects the proposed 25→35°C, 20 m head, 0.80 pump/0.95 motor reference scenario. Selection makes its conditional electrical consequence reproducible; it does not establish site truth. Both motor and hydraulic work are assumed ultimately transferred to the represented water. Solve flow with the positive denominator `delta_h_water - specific_pump_electric`; expose pump shaft/electric input, motor loss, total rejection and residual. Require finite positive denominator and flow. Expose the raw condenser-to-hot-water gap and strict positive-approach check. The nominal gap is 7 K. Reject invalid temperature order or temperatures outside the 20–60°C liquid table; require finite nonnegative head and efficiencies in (0,1]. Saturation liquid enthalpy is explicitly a near-atmospheric approximation. Condenser/cooler UA, hydraulic distribution, NPSH, water availability and environmental discharge remain unqualified.

Generic cooling-water enable is zero. It may be enabled only with matched mode enabled. Disabled execution branches before property access and contributes zero incremental demand. Report `reference_scenario=true`, `site_qualified=false` when selected. Its total rejection covers only this cycle and its declared losses, not all plant heat. No output is mislabeled whole-plant rejection.

Use a fresh independent oracle that reconstructs the original NIST rows and physical balances without importing the production solver or author prototype. For independent calculations using the same displayed tables, retain the existing full-scalar comparison policy: relative 1e-9 and absolute 1e-6 in declared units, including new state/work/flow/efficiency outputs and analytic UA. Preserve any stricter historical quantity-specific policy unchanged. Use exact Boolean/applicability/source identities. None of these tolerances changes a strict physical inequality or turns an unqualified component into a passing one.

## Reviewed heat-account correction during implementation

[AGENT; independent critic released] Native mixed-mode probes showed that admitted salt heat and the plant power-balance heat could differ when historical recovery modes were combined with the active matched cycle. The matched calculation therefore additionally consumes the existing source heat and mode-selected recovered heat. It checks their sum against admitted heat using the established arithmetic closure policy before property work. This preserves acyclic ordering and refuses contradictory input combinations; it does not bind downstream power balance back into the cycle or change a scientific acceptance band. Disabled mode retains its early branch. Exact evidence and review ruling are recorded in the goal T-009 heat-account correction.
