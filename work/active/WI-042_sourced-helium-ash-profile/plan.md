## Design summary

The helium-ash profile follows the source's own rule (the fusion-rate shape, Eq. A.5 pointwise), the electrons close by quasi-neutrality, and W and β read one volume-averaged pressure computed inside the sustainment calc; the bound electron exponent retires and every reader of the electron profile takes the derived one (design D1–D6). The prototype (design § Prototype and validation report) already carries the model edits in the working tree; this plan takes them to a regenerated, re-pinned, oracle-guarded package with every committed study restated first.

## Prototype baseline

Four canonical model files are edited in the working tree and validated (Levels 1–5 pass; Level 2's 12 pre-existing placeholder literals; Level 6 unchanged from the pre-existing set — design § Prototype and validation report): `models/library/analyses/mfe_plasma_sustainment.sysml` (the rewritten doc contract; `alpha_n_e_in` removed; four outputs added), `models/library/analyses/mfe_plasma_scaling.sysml` ('Volume-Averaged Beta' on `p_avg_in`), `models/designs/generic_mfe/mfe_plant.sysml` (the attribute and binding retired; `beta_calc` on `sustain.p_avg`), `models/designs/stellarator_09/stellarator_plant.sysml` (the beta-referent block and the `sustainment_ok` comment rewritten). Their twins under `exploration/stellarator_e2e/models/` are **not** yet synced. The reference calculation and the window table sit at `prototype/` (design D8). Nothing under `exploration/stellarator_e2e/generated/`, the oracle, the manifest, the census or the fixtures has been touched.

## Phasing approach

The restatement **before** the regeneration commit; the expensive, irreversible regeneration last. Six phases in two commits, the WI-041 shape:

- **Commit A (phases 1–4):** the model edits in both trees, this plan, and the restatement — the tree is model-complete and the restatement is on the record before any regenerated byte (MR-WI042-14; trail § T-001 scope (round 2) narrower constraint (i)).
- **Commit B (phases 5–6):** regeneration (twice — the manual-stage re-stencil), the handwritten impl, the oracle, the re-pin, the fixtures, the batteries, the SV rows.

Why the restatement sits in phase 4: it is a statement about *meaning* — "no multiplier re-reads a sustainment-dependent column; the honest re-read is a re-execution" — derivable from the design's expected-behaviour table before any number is generated, and writing it after regeneration invites fitting it to what came out.

## MR-WI042-14 restatement — the profile family's meaning change and the committed studies (2026-09-05, written before regeneration)

**Ordering.** This section is committed with the model edits (commit A) and before any regenerated byte (commit B), the WI-041 shape. Every number below is the design's prediction from the oracle-side prototype (`prototype/baseline_old_new.json`); phase 5 checks the package against it and derives any difference rather than fitting it.

### (a) What the profile family now means, and why no multiplier re-reads a committed column

Before WI-042 the sustainment calc gave the helium ash the fuel's profile exponent (0.33) and the electrons a point-A exponent (0.596) bound from the printed vol-av/peak pair, and the beta calc carried a second copy of that pressure average. After WI-042 the ash follows the source's own rule — n_He(ρ) = n_He0 · S(ρ), S the fusion-rate shape (Eq. A.5 pointwise, τ* uniform in ρ) — the electrons are the ions' charge pointwise, W and β read one ⟨p⟩, and the ISS04 line average and the radiation integrals take the derived electron profile. Every quantity downstream of the sustainment calc re-closes through the fixed point: a lower W raises τ_E, more τ_E means more ash, more ash means less fuel at the peak, less fuel means less fusion power, and the wall, the lifetime, the net power and LCOE follow. None of those moves is a constant factor over the levers (W_new / W_old runs 0.77–0.99 across the geometry window, `prototype/window_corners.json`), so **no committed sustainment-dependent column re-reads by a multiplier** — not `W_th`, `tau_E`, `n_bar19`, `n_He0`, `n_D0`, `n_T0`, `p_rad`, `p_alpha_heat`, `p_aux_required`, `p_fus`, `wall_load`, `wall_load_peak`, `beta`, `cas72`, `lcoe`, `lcoe_1cfe`, `p_net`, `rec_frac`, nor the verdicts `sustainment_ok`, `wall_load_ok`, `beta_ok`, `recirc_ok`, `net_positive`, nor an `ignited` flag or a driven set. **The honest re-read is a re-execution**, which is what goal `stored-energy-basis` round 2's study is: the `20260904-wall-and-heating` window's four arms at the new pin. The two constant-scale counterfactuals on record (0.915 and 0.940 of W, `work/orchestration/goals/stored-energy-basis/evidence/w_counterfactual/`) remain what they were — counterfactuals under a stated constant-scale assumption, not readings at the rule.
