## Why this item exists

Round 1 of the goal attributed the model's stored-energy gap (`learnings.md` L-001..L-003, accepted by the fresh review 2026-09-05). The model integrates power-law profiles for W and gives the helium ash the **fuel's** exponent (`models/designs/stellarator_09/stellarator_plant.sysml:578`, `alpha_n = 0.33`, read for the ash at `mfe_plasma_sustainment.sysml:25-26`), while the paper it cites for the ash *amount* (Appendix A, Eq. A.5) also states the ash *shape*: "helium ash profiles are obtained using a fixed ratio of particle-to-energy confinement time" (raw PDF p. 9), i.e. n_He(ρ)/τ*_α = n_D(ρ) n_T(ρ) ⟨σv⟩_T(ρ) with τ*_α = ρ* τ_E, ρ* ~ 8 (p. 32, A.5), then n'_He = f_suppr n_He (A.6). Applied pointwise with τ* uniform in ρ that rule peaks the ash at an effective exponent of about 4 at point A, and quasi-neutrality then re-closes the electrons. The model's family over-counts ion charge off-axis (7.5 % at ρ 0.5, 31 % at 0.8, 55 % at 0.9; `evidence/w_counterfactual/attribution_arithmetic.json` at `3687d2f6`) and carries a sourced ~6 % over-count of W against the paper's own rules, about five points of it the ash shape (L-003: 551.3 → 524.5 MJ at the model's exponents on the printed peaks; `attribution_sourced_definition.json` at `5cc30ac0`). The rule applied at point A reproduces the printed n_e0 (5.04 vs 5.06) and ⟨n_e⟩ (3.16 vs 3.17), which is the corroboration for the shape.

This is not a tuning toward a number: the model already cites A.5 for the ash peak and gives the ash profile a shape the same source contradicts. The item makes the model do what its own source says, with the electrons closed consistently, and computes the shape from the rule at every lever point so it holds for a larger machine — the owner's second clause.

#### MR-WI042-1: The ash profile shape follows the source's own rule, computed at every lever point
The helium-ash density profile in the pressure integral SHALL be n_He(ρ) = n_He0 · [n_D(ρ) n_T(ρ) ⟨σv⟩(T_i(ρ))] / [n_D0 n_T0 ⟨σv⟩(T_i0)] — Eq. A.5 applied pointwise with the particle-to-energy confinement ratio uniform in ρ — evaluated from the fuel and temperature profiles and the Bosch–Hale reactivity at the lever point, and SHALL NOT be a bound exponent, a digitized shape, or a value fixed at point A. *Priority:* P0. *Rationale:* the owner's ruling and its second clause ("make sure this scales up for larger stellarators"); `goal.md` § Invariants (amendment (b)). *Validation:* the shape's effective exponent is a computed output that moves with T_i0 (4.7 at 10 keV, 3.5 at 20 keV) and with the fuel exponent; no literal near 4 appears in the model. *Source:* `[OWNER-VERBATIM 2026-09-05]`; Stellaris raw PDF p. 9 (Fig. 14 caption region text: "Helium ash profiles are obtained using a fixed ratio of particle-to-energy confinement time"), p. 32 A.5–A.6 (page renders read this session); L-003.

## Assumptions & risks

1. **τ* uniform in ρ is the paper's reading.** *Confidence: medium-high.* The paper says "a fixed ratio of particle-to-energy confinement time" and applies A.5 in its 0.5-D balance; the pointwise reading reproduces the printed densities and Fig. 16(a). **Risk if wrong:** the shape's peakedness is off by the τ*(ρ) the paper did not state. *Mitigation:* stated in the model text as the reading; the author query (owner-held) is the only act that could sharpen it.
2. **The codegen accepts the form.** *Confidence: high.* The chain is already a manual stage; the interface change is outputs added and one input retired or rebound. **Risk:** the manual-stage re-stencil (recorded gotcha) — restore by hand, delete `handwritten/backup/`, regenerate twice.
