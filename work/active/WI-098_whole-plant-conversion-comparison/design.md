# WI-098 whole-plant conversion design

Status: submitted for independent design review, 2026-09-27. No implementation is authorized by this document alone. Decisions below are [AGENT] design choices unless explicitly marked inherited. Requirements are in [spec.md](spec.md); quantities, ownership and proposed binding names are in [configuration.md](configuration.md).

## 1. Result and premise

Build an additive native plant assembly that keeps the repaired steam and Brayton conversion models and adds a shared supplied reactor, primary equipment, fuel system, station power and lifecycle accounts. Both branches consume the same source and fixed reactor inventory. The model calculates both complete plant LCOEs and net exports; study code selects and reports supported rows.

The result is conditional economics for a supplied thermal source. It is not a validated plasma operating point. A new 48 kA magnet scenario changes axis field to 8.64 T and peak field to 23.904 T. The source is independently supplied at 2500 or 2800 MW before recovery of primary circulation work. No confinement or sustainment claim follows from this pairing. `source_qualified=0` must survive every report, together with the assumed source and blanket transport authority.

This distinction cannot waive component failures. The original magnet has inadequate fit and current capacity. The exact 50 kA larger-pack probe repaired those two checks but remained outside the conductor field domain and failed cryoplant capacities. That offer stays failed in [evidence/magnet-probe/report.md](evidence/magnet-probe/report.md). The present design selects a different, explicit in-domain 48 kA offer and a larger priced cryoplant. Independent review precedes its native evaluation. If its component checks fail, it cannot rank. The inherited 3000 MW primary pressure and divertor failures likewise remain exclusions.

The wider casing still lacks a global manufactured-fit/structural qualification. A named common source installation allowance prices that unresolved scope; it does not change a physical failure into a pass. Reports must distinguish conditional source authority, supported component calculations and unqualified global reactor construction. A conclusion may compare conversion choices under these assumptions; it may not label the complete reactor empirically feasible.

## 3. Source, hardware and interfaces

The source calculation uses `a=3.52/17.58`, neutron multiplication `m_n=1.2`, deposited heating `H=50 MW`, and `P_fusion=(Q_source-H)/(m_n*(1-a)+a)`. Forward reconstruction must recover `Q_source` within numerical tolerance. The retained primary loop then calculates recovered work and `Q_IHX=Q_source+W_primary`. The heating wall-plug demand is `H/(eta_source*eta_coupling)`, initially 100 MW. Its deposited 50 MW is already in the supplied thermal source and must not be added again.

Use the inherited divertor proxy convention, which differs slightly from the reaction-energy fraction: `P_heat=0.95*0.2002*P_fusion+H`; `q_div=9.5*0.1*P_heat/50` MW/m², limited to 10. At 2500/2800/3000 MW source this gives about 8.582493/9.517084/10.140145 MW/m². Wall loading scales the fixed-geometry native reference 3.9788448937763854 MW/m² by `P_fusion/2652.5632625175904`. This is a conditional irradiation surrogate; the capture must bound all ranked fusion points and neutron heating. Retain separate transport qualification flags.

Select the magnet and cryoplant inputs in configuration.md, then execute exactly that offer and its control in the existing native full-system package after design approval. Deposit every input, output, predicate, package fingerprint and source/body identity. Requote its material prices in USD2025 explicitly, preserving physical inventory. Capture the revised magnet purchase breakdown, coil drive, cold/intercept duties, refrigeration electricity, fit/current/stress/strain/domain margins, and all global failures. The new plant includes these as a fixed supplied equipment record with independently supplied capacities and direct margin assertions. Main-study axes cannot modify geometry/current or mix another capture with these numbers.

The 48 kA native run may produce a different calculated fusion point. Do not use that calculated plasma output as validation of the supplied source. Establish the capture's nuclear load envelope explicitly: retain the original reference fusion of 2652.5632625175904 MW as a supplied maximum for cold nuclear heating, and recompute the selected magnet's cryogenic demand at that envelope using its inherited equations. Assert ranked `P_fusion` is below it. Capture the conservative fixed refrigeration/coil demand at that envelope; no credit for decreasing it at lower loads. This additional algebra belongs in the capture, with an independent check and native inputs/outputs retained.

The primary offer retains 14 paths and the actual installed pressure/flow/temperature conditions from the baseline. Cost is independent of the steam connector's chosen 10/11/12/14 loops. Keep circulation shaft heat recovery separate from electric input when drive efficiency changes. Retain the supplied pressure-rise screen; 3000 MW fails the existing offer. Also retain flow, heating, fuel-processing and startup-stock screens.

Use actual exchanger branch return for terminal gaps: `hot_gap=T_primary_hot-T_secondary_out`; `cold_gap=T_primary_exchanger_return-T_secondary_in`. Mixed return must still meet the source-required return through the existing bounded bypass controller. The actual-return assertions supplement the inherited steam transport diagnostic, which used mixed return. Minimum required terminal/profile gap remains strictly positive, matching the inherited definition; no global 30 K requirement is introduced. Preserve steam SG/reheater profile checks, Brayton finite cooler/property/pressure constraints, cooling-water limits and invalid-root outcomes.

## 4. Fuel and complete electrical boundary

Reuse native fuel flow and full startup inventory calculations with the parameters in configuration.md. For fusion reaction rate `F=P_fusion*1e6/(17.58*MeV_J)`, tritium consumption is `F*m_T`, circulating injection is `F/burn_fraction`, and unrecovered exhaust follows the inherited recycle calculation. Selected stock is 5 kg; assert it exceeds full calculated startup need. Processing capacity is independently selected at 0.00015 kg D+T/s. Stock purchase uses selected 5 kg, not calculated minimum.

At availability `A`, annual gross tritium need is `A*seconds_year*(burn_mass+loss_mass)+selected_stock*lambda*seconds_year`. Annual usable breeding is `A*seconds_year*TBR*F*m_T*extraction_efficiency`. External supply is `max(gross-breeding,0)`; surplus receives no revenue. The lower TBR remains a sensitivity and a separate self-sufficiency failure where applicable. External supply is an explicit assumed purchased feed, not demonstrated breeding. Deuterium and Li6 purchases follow reaction counts and stated recovery/feedstock assumptions. Do not add the legacy aggregate DT fuel cost.

For branch `b`, `P_net_b=P_conversion_net_b-P_primary_electric-P_heating_wall-P_coil_drive-P_refrigeration-P_TF_cooling-P_fuel_vacuum-P_house-P_reactor_controls-P_aux_rejection-P_source_allowance`. Existing conversion net already subtracts local pumps, cooling water, controller and shaft import. Gas compressor work is already inside its shaft balance. No term is subtracted twice.

Auxiliary heat rejection is a separately selected common package. Its load is the heating wall-plug loss, coil/refrigerator/TF/fuel/house/control/source-allowance electricity, cold/intercept extracted heat, primary motor losses, and its own electric draw. These loads go to that sink; they are not silently added to the conversion condenser or source heat. Its 200 MW thermal rating and 2 MW electric draw at a conditional 25°C site water supply are independent offers, priced at USD2025 50 million including connection and installation. An inherited auxiliary cooling residual is replaced by this offer. Assert duty <= rating and offered conditions match. Quote validity and water availability remain assumptions, with explicit price/duty sensitivities.

During outages, use fixed captured refrigeration, house load and 2 MW auxiliary-rejection electricity as the selected standby demand; set other equipment off as an operating assumption. Annual export is `8760*A*P_net`; annual imported energy is `8760*(1-A)*P_standby`; also expose their difference. Value imports at USD2025 50/MWh, with 0/50/150 scenarios. LCOE uses discounted exported energy and includes imports as an expense. Nonpositive operating net or nonpositive annual net grid delivery cannot rank. Report both energy quantities so this convention cannot hide imports.

## 5. Disjoint costs and finance

All amounts are USD2025. configuration.md lists every account and its authority. Legacy amounts without a defensible price year become explicitly assumed new USD2025 quotes equal to those numbers; they are not presented as inflation-corrected evidence. Primary equipment and ARIES conversion factors retain their documented CPI treatment. Magnet rates are explicitly requoted and its detailed capture records the change. Price stress ranges are assumptions, not statistical confidence limits.

The native assembly contains readable `Costed Component` leaves for each major reactor scope, each branch conversion purchase group, selected fuel stocks, extra source installation and auxiliary rejection. `source_accounts` sums those leaves, exposes category subtotals and asserts reconciliation. Branch ledgers reference their exclusive conversion leaves. No old whole-plant CAS20/CAS29/CAS30/CAS50, total cooling, old steam/rejection or reserve totals are added.

One part owns finance: `finance.rate=0.05`, `finance.years=30`, `finance.availability=0.80`, `finance.construction_years=8`. Bind both `steam_ledger.{rate,years,availability}` and `gas_ledger.{rate,years,availability}` to those attributes. Retire the six old input keys from the new generated public inputs. The migration maps old complete points only when their paired rate/years/availability agree; disagreement fails ingestion. Conversion-control replay supplies common availability 0.85; main whole-plant cases use 0.80. All conversion replacement PV therefore uses the same rate/horizon as upstream events.

Let `C0_b` be all initial plant purchases, initial stocks, owner and installation scopes for branch b. Construction finance is `C0_b*(1+r)^(construction_years/2)` as an explicit midpoint assumption. Future replacement PV is added once and never construction-financed. Annual expense PV is `sum_{y=1..N} annual_expense/(1+r)^y`; energy PV is `sum_{y=1..N} annual_export/(1+r)^y`. Terminal dismantling less salvage is discounted at N. `LCOE_b=PV_cost_b/PV_export_b` in USD/MWh. Use the currency-neutral DCF helper only if its construction argument is zero after initial finance has been applied; otherwise implement these finite sums directly.

Retain the existing conversion service/makeup/replacement streams once, extracting their components rather than adding their full LCOE or accounted PV. Existing 20% year-15 generic conversion replacement excludes explicitly replaced machines/bundles and stocks as before. Explicit upstream replacements are blanket/divertor at irradiation life, magnets at 10 full-power years, primary active machines every 10 calendar years, and a 5% year-20 overhaul of the remaining eligible reactor equipment. The latter excludes buildings/land/stocks, magnet, blanket/divertor, primary machines and all conversion equipment. Event scopes and removal/refill allowances are in configuration.md.

For component full-power life L, event times are `t_k=k*L/A`, retaining only `0<t_k<N`. Calendar replacements similarly exclude an event exactly at N. Blanket life is `18/q_wall_peak` full-power years. Sum the selected blanket and magnet outages plus a selected 0.5-year lifetime allowance for other nonoverlapping outages. Assert the total is <= `N*(1-A)`. This necessary outage budget does not claim a detailed maintenance schedule; it must not silently adjust A. Replacement dates and discounted contributions are exposed for verification.

The old USD50.617 million/year whole-plant O&M estimate is not added wholesale to conversion 2% service. The design adopts 80% of that estimate as a new assumed common reactor staffing/routine-service allocation, with 50/80/100% sensitivity; conversion service is separately retained. This allocation is a new assumption, not a sourced disjoint decomposition. Fuel, electricity imports and explicit replacements are excluded from the assumed routine-service allocation. No fixed decommissioning reserve is added to the terminal event.

## 6. Public surface and study policy

Prefix is `whole_plant_conversion__plant__`. Preserve predecessor public conversion outputs and constraints with prefix changes, except the retired shared finance input keys. New public inputs are the chosen/scenario attributes listed in configuration.md. Do not expose individual fixed captured physics values as freely varied study axes; retain their full provenance record and reject inconsistent capture IDs/maps.

Each of `steam_whole` and `gas_whole` exposes: `conversion_net_MW`, each named upstream electric term, `net_export_MW`, `standby_MW`, `annual_export_MWh`, `annual_import_MWh`, `annual_net_grid_MWh`, `initial_capital_USD2025`, `construction_financed_capital`, `annual_service`, `annual_fuel`, `annual_import_cost`, `conversion_replacement_pv`, `source_replacement_pv`, `terminal_pv`, `total_cost_pv`, `energy_pv`, `lcoe_USD2025_MWh`, `power_residual`, and `cost_residual`. Shared outputs expose source/fusion/heating/primary heat and work; fuel burn/loss/bred/external/startup/processing quantities; all account subtotals; replacement counts/outage budget; source qualification and captured domain/capacity margins; auxiliary rejected heat/margin; and the two actual endpoint gaps per branch.

Rankability requires the applicable conversion predicates, actual endpoint checks, primary/source-demand/captured component domain and capacity checks, stock/processing checks, outage budget and positive net checks. A missing or unavailable predicate is not a pass. `source_qualified=0` is a permanent claim qualifier for this supplied-source study, visibly separate from component support. The old field-extrapolated and cryoplant-failed offer is never a rankable alternative.

Replay all 498 retained predecessor records as controls, including failures and aliases. Rerank all finite relevant nominal offers, rather than only the old winners, at common supplied source conditions. Preserve 3000 MW as an exclusion/control. Suggested finite shared scenario families are finance/availability, common reactor quotes, primary pipe quote, tritium price and breeding, heating and primary drive efficiency, source residual electric load, auxiliary rejection quote, magnet service and routine O&M allocation. Exact Cartesian products and point budget belong to the coordinator's reviewed study plan. Every selected scenario applies equally to both branches; hardware does not change when only load changes. Efficiency changes require re-executing all affected source/capacity/heat checks.

## 7. Verification and release gate

1. Validate the reviewed native 48 kA offer and conservative nuclear envelope capture. Independent arithmetic checks field, fit, current, cryogenic stages, pricing and all margins. Preserve the failed 50 kA offer and baseline exactly. Block empirical ranking if the new offer remains outside any required component domain.
2. Parse/validate model and generate isolated package. Execute two generation passes and compare semantic/executable fingerprints. Deposit actual source and handwritten-body census plus hashes of protected predecessor files before/after.
3. Replay all predecessor control rows with shared legacy finance. Compare inherited outputs and predicate verdicts with declared tolerances; the only intended added exclusions are new whole-plant checks. No tolerance change may turn a material shortfall into a pass.
4. Independently verify every new output and predicate using source inputs, separate equations and separately enumerated replacement dates. Reconcile capital by leaf and every electrical subtraction. Check units, currency, construction timing, strict event horizon, startup versus annual decay, breeding shortfall, terminal signs and discount rate zero.
5. Demand-only tests change 2500 to 2800 MW with chosen hardware and initial purchase cost invariant. Sufficient/insufficient tests independently select smaller/larger primary pressure rating, cold/intercept capacity, stock and auxiliary rejection capacity, holding other choices fixed; each alternative includes an explicit selected quote. A rating-only diagnostic is labelled diagnostic, not a purchasable study offer.
6. Adverse tests include 3000 MW source, out-of-domain field capture, inadequate cryoplant, inadequate processing/stock, actual exchanger crossover, unsupported coolant property, lower TBR, negative whole-plant net by declared load, import-dominated annual delivery, excessive availability/outage budget, incompatible finance migration and mismatched capture identity. Retain finite failure diagnostics and prevent ranking.
7. Deposit integration report with conditional-source interpretation, unresolved manufactured-fit/facility qualification, exact fixed input/capture map, financial assumptions and preservation receipts. Independent integration review must find at least one supported common operating point for both branches before native main-study execution.

## 8. Review decisions requiring attention

- The 48 kA offer is a substantial assumed magnet/casing scenario. Its source sustainment and global construction qualification remain unknown. Review whether conditional component comparison at this boundary meets R1/R4; if not, the source premise must be revisited rather than hidden by an allowance.
- Auxiliary rejection is a conditional rated supplier package with a deliberately explicit USD50 million quote and 2 MW draw. A qualified heat exchanger/water network is outside this bounded model; price/load sensitivities must show whether that assumption affects the comparison.
- Extra source installation, O&M allocation, outage durations, overhauls and terminal fractions are agent assumptions. Their ranges test economic dependence; they do not create engineering confidence bounds.
- No complete 48 kA capture exists yet. The design gate authorizes its exact execution; implementation must stop and report any new material failure before changing the offer.
