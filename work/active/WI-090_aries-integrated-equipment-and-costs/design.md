# Integrated equipment and cost design

## Architecture and ownership

Continue the existing thermal/electrical assembly. Add generic calculations in `models/library/analyses/integrated_equipment_costs.sysml` and cost-bearing part definitions in `models/library/structure/integrated_equipment_parts.sysml`. Every new cost-bearing occurrence specializes `costed_component::'Costed Component'`, exposes `capital_cost` and has a CAS code. The assembly owns selections and consumes calc-output EXPOSE interfaces; library definitions own computations. Keep the stock generation route, typed completions, adapters, source census and fixed-point checks.

New physical owners are `magnet_inventory`, `blanket_inventory`, `shield_inventory`, `divertor_inventory`, `lipb_inventory`, `he_hx`, `pbli_hx`, `divertor_hx`, `he_pump`, `pbli_pump`, `divertor_pump`, `conversion_equipment`, `electrical_equipment`, `fuel_equipment`, `fuel_inventory`, `facilities` and named residual service accounts. They represent selected hardware/materials and declared budgets. Existing `*_capacity` occurrences remain sole owners of their selected scalar ratings. Cost calculations reference those choices directly. Do not create duplicate independent rating entries in the new occurrences.

The physical chain remains plasma/source → deposited heat → coolant/primary exchanger → cycle → net electricity. The added ownership chain is selected material/geometry/rating → installed inventory and capital, with demand → adequacy and operating expenditure. Source-budget placeholders have a selected package count of one and a scope declaration. Such counts are inventories of packages, not fabricated estimates of physical mass.

## New generic equations and domains

**Selected inventory purchase.** For selected quantity `q`, reference quantity `q0`, reference installed-direct cost `C0` and uncertainty factor `u`, `C=C0*(q/q0)*u`. Both q and q0 must be finite and nonnegative/strictly positive respectively; u is positive. This is a transparent local linear estimate around a declared source package. It is not a vendor quote or demand-sizing law. Report selected quantity, ratio, original budget, estimate factor and installed amount separately. Restrict equipment comparisons to 0.5–1.5 times reference quantity pending better cost laws; wider diagnostic points retain an extrapolation flag. Fixed package alternative uses `C=C0*u`, with quantity irrelevant to price and that response limitation exposed.

**Exchanger inventory.** Selected area A in m² and assumed effective overall coefficient U in W/(m² K) produce `UA=A*U/1e6` MW/K. Exchanger price consumes A, not UA, so a U uncertainty sweep leaves purchased area/cost unchanged. Baseline A=50000 m² and U=1000 W/(m² K) reproduce each inherited UA=50 MW/K exactly. Both values are explicit engineering approximations; the geometry and pressure-drop qualification flags stay zero. Each branch's selected heat-duty rating remains an independent package rating and a separate screen against full available branch duty.

**Pumps.** Preserve the primary-flow operating inputs. Add independent purchased flow capacity and efficiency. Use the explicit fixed-path proxy `P=P0*(m/m0)^3*(eta0/eta)` MW, with `eta0=.8`, reference flows 3261/26860/500 kg/s and reference powers 156/.01/10 MW for He/PbLi/divertor respectively. This adopts a constant-density quadratic pressure-drop approximation; it is not qualified gas/MHD hydraulics. P0 is the inherited assumed operating power at m0, not a fitted source validation. Domain m>=0, m0>0, 0<eta<=1; restrict sensitivity to .5–1.5 m0. Installed rated flow affects purchase cost and flow margin, while operating flow drives electrical demand and recovered heat. At fixed operating flow, buying a larger capacity has no invented efficiency gain. Pump loss/recovery fractions remain inherited assumptions. Retain a named fixed-power source mode for literal source reconstructions; all modes expose their support/status and exact canonical inputs.

**Selected machine ratings.** Use actual existing ratings as purchase quantities (MW thermal, MW shaft, MW electric or particles/s as identified below). Capacity changes capital and margin. It does not change compressor work or turbine efficiency without a separate supported machine model. This is a capacity-class inventory, explicitly not a rotor/material takeoff. Conversion cost allocates its package budget among compressor/turbine/generator and fixed recuperator/services before scaling; generator is not charged again in electrical distribution.

**Annual fuel and inventory.** `annual_burn_kg=burn_rate*atom_mass*seconds_per_year*availability`; `annual_loss_kg=loss_rate*atom_mass*seconds_per_year*availability`. Initial selected tritium inventory in kg is priced only in initial capital. Its adequacy requirement is `exhaust_rate*atom_mass*selected_processing_residence_seconds`; it never sets chosen inventory. Annual decay is selected inventory times the inherited decay constant times all calendar seconds, distinct from productive burn time. Annual deuterium demand uses the reaction burn plus unrecovered fuel-flow fraction and a declared deuterium mass/price. External tritium purchase is `max(annual_burn+annual_loss+annual_decay-supplied_annual_recovery,0)` times declared tritium price. Supplied annual recovery is a scenario boundary, never evidence of breeding sufficiency. Baseline recovery may equal an independently supplied source scenario amount, but must not be rebound to calculated burn to guarantee adequacy. Expose both gross requirements and supplied recovery, allowing deficit changes under demand perturbation.

**Replacement schedule.** For component group j with selected life Lj in full-power years and constant assumed availability a, event times are `k*Lj/a` calendar years for positive integers k strictly before the selected plant lifetime. Initial purchase is t=0 and excluded from event total. Replacement event amount is selected replaceable scope cost times a separate refurbishment/installation multiplier; it cannot include all permanent inventory by default. Output life, event interval, number of events, per-event amount and undiscounted lifetime total. Annualized reserve `event_cost*a/Lj` is a separate planning channel, not a second expense added to the event schedule. The financial successor chooses event cashflows or reserve, never both. No automatic fluence-lifetime relation is claimed.

**Cost rollup.** Graph-owned leaf sums produce CAS22 and parent totals. Overnight capital is direct + selected indirect + contingency + explicit owner/commissioning allowances. The Lyon-inclusive factor applies only to the separate source comparison. Annual operating total includes fixed O&M, throughput consumables and annual external-fuel estimate; replacement reserve is separately exposed. Electricity auxiliaries already reduce net export and are not repriced as purchased electricity during positive net operation. For negative net output, expose annual import MWh and a separately declared purchase-price channel; do not claim normal power-sale economics.

## Thermal sensitivity before ranking

Principal axes are deposition multiplier1–1.25, helium partition.30–.46 and inter-coolant exchange.02–.06; bulk temperature bounds±50K; PbLi cp170–220 J/(kg K); U.5–1.5x; compressor efficiency.84–.92, turbine.88–.95; recuperator.6–.95; pumping efficiency.6–.9 and heating efficiency.4–.7; unmodeled cryo/control/other loads.5–1.5x. Use one-factor diagnostic sweeps first and retain every failure. Deposition axes change heat quantity/allocation, bulk bounds and U limit accepted heat, fluid/cycle assumptions change conversion and pumping, and auxiliary assumptions change net export directly. This set covers the known premises behind423.1 MW; no source/thermal property is treated as a costless optimized design lever.

Keep all selected hardware fixed in these assumption sweeps. Source-conditioned cases retain literal inputs and their known thermal failures. After interpreting sensitivity, selected-area and selected-rating alternatives can demonstrate cost/capability coupling; they do not establish an economic optimum. Record unresisted assumptions and absent physical responses before execution. Native study metadata must enumerate full qualified attributes and generated entry keys after generation; guessed keys cannot be used as evidence.
