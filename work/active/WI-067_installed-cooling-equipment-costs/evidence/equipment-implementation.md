# Equipment calculation implementation

[AGENT] Implemented the released combined design in `models/library/analyses/mfe_cooling_equipment.sysml` with guarded manual completion in `seeds/cooling_equipment_impl.py`. `equipment-interface.json` owns the ordered input/output contract. The coordinator owns plant wiring, regeneration, promotion and integrated validation. The typed wrapper now follows the first native v3 generation. Its109-output tuple order was extracted from the generated stub and saved in `equipment-generated-abi.json`; it differs from SysML declaration order. No generated package was edited by this author.

The calculation separates primary packages, primary pipes, fixed-capacity exchangers, salt packages, salt pipes, inventories and initial spares. Purchased, installation, delivered shipping exclusion, removal and annual replacement/consumable costs are separate outputs. All monetary totals are USD2025 annual-CPI purchasing-power proxies. Source raw price outputs retain source currency years and do not follow costscale.

HX masses and surface areas are per exchanger. Pipe and inventory masses/volumes are plant totals. Salt flow is per circuit; salt pump flow and shaft power are per machine; aggregate salt shaft/electric power is plant total. Inventory volume is modeled wetted geometry before reserve; mass and helium standard volume include reserve. The BNL duty is shaft power, following the coordinator's correction of the earlier candidate's electric-duty convention; this distinction is tested with nonunity drive efficiency.
