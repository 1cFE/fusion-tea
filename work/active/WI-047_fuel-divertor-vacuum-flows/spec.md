## Why this item exists

Three rubric rows have no physical quantity at all. The fuel and tritium cycle (Row 10) is one cost line: annual fuel follows fusion energy with a held burn fraction and a held recovery factor, and nothing in the package knows a tritium flow, a required breeding ratio or a decay (`.project/active/demo-depth-rubric/grading.md@fc80e5b2` R10.P = 1 / target 2). The divertor (Row 5) is a power-scaled cost sharing the first wall's replacement event, with no surface heat load anywhere and so nothing that could push back on the operating point (R5.P = 1 / target 3). The vacuum system (Row 6) is a shell-volume cost whose own doc says the gas-load pumping term is omitted (R6.P = 1 / target 2). Tritium breeding (Row 2c) is a held 1.074 compared to a held 1.05 — the rubric's own type case of a fence that cannot resist (R2c.P = 1 / target 3).

The breadth report established that each has a tractable reduced form with an honest gap (`goal.md` § Question fact 4): burn, throughput and required breeding follow from conservation and a NIST half-life; the held 99 % recovery read as physical tritium recovery would need a breeding ratio of 1.19 at 5 % burn — a semantics conflict to surface, not a deficit to erase; the design source prints one divertor case (90 % radiated, 500 → 50 MW, two transport cases at 5 and 9.5 MW/m² against an adopted 10 MW/m²); the gas load follows from the fuel flows, but no admissible source prints an exhaust pressure. This item builds the three reduced calcs, asserts the one fence the source supports, and surfaces every missing input by name with its retrieval target, so that the round's study can report these quantities beside every point and the fresh re-grade (`goal.md` § Answered when (c)) has evidence to score.

#### MR-WI047-2: The fuel calc carries the conservation chain from the breadth report, with the decay constant a cited input
`'Fuel Cycle Flows'` SHALL compute, from `p_fus_in` [MW], `E_fus_J_in` [J per reaction — the plant passes `fuel_q_eff × mev_to_joules`], `burn_fraction_in`, `t_recycle_in`, `tbr_available_in`, `eta_extract_in`, `lambda_T_in` [s⁻¹], `I_total_in` [atoms], `G_stock_in` [atoms/s], `m_T_kg_in` [kg per atom] and `s_per_fpy_in`: `burn_rate = p_fus × 1e6 / E_fus_J`; `inject_rate = burn_rate / burn_fraction`; `exhaust_rate = inject_rate − burn_rate`; `loss_rate = (1 − t_recycle) × exhaust_rate`; `tbr_required = (burn_rate + loss_rate + lambda_T × I_total + G_stock) / (eta_extract × burn_rate)`; `tbr_margin = tbr_available − tbr_required`; `burn_kg_per_fpy = burn_rate × m_T_kg × s_per_fpy` (the breadth report's equations at `20260907-163520_demo-breadth-disposition-prework.md:43-54`).
