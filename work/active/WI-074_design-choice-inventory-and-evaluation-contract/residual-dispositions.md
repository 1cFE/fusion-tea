## Meaning of a disposition

`compliant-limited` means the inspected source implements the stated identity, operating closure, explicit policy or price assumption without silently selecting the supplied hardware within that contract. It does not mean every empirical equation is validated or every downstream plant constraint passes. `violated-entering` records an actual entering selection defect and its repair item; it is not a claim the current source still contains that defect. `unverified` names the missing disclosure, native/generated propagation or independent review. Approved designs and local tests are not completed native acceptance. No unreviewed scope is marked technically remediated.

The initial parsed traversal and 511-parameter generated census are entering snapshots. The CSV keeps those rows even when keys are retired, and adds new generated keys only after the coordinator supplies the updated package. A source-level retirement cannot be called a removed public input while the live generated interface still exposes it. Exact package hashes and current consumers will be captured separately from initial ones. The structural census cannot by itself establish semantic compliance.

## Maintained stock and startup decision

[AGENT] Retain the existing forward maintained-stock scenario as the supported analysis. Its independent quantities are operating streams and explicit residence/coverage policies, rather than offered active fuel mass. This is an existing contract, not a new inferred scientific rule: the Fuel Inventory model doc already states its equations, maintained-through-shutdown assumption, abstract startup supply boundary, dormant held-stock behavior and undefined-breeding propagation. The source and actual stock/TBR consumers were read together. The first independent architecture review already classifies this bounded policy contract as compliant.

The represented reserve is not an automatically adequate physical store: it is the mass implied by the chosen coverage fraction and duration under the scenario. No reserve-capacity predicate exists, and the model must not claim independently selected reserve/buffer/startup mass evaluation. A supplied-stock variant could use supplied masses and compare the current coverage requirement without requiring tank physics, but adding it would be a new supported analysis contract; this disposition does not silently pretend it exists. No owner decision is required merely to describe the implemented scenario honestly. Broader physical storage or continuous supply qualification requires missing relationships and stays unavailable.

The annual decay term uses the actual **scenario-maintained** stock. The passive shutdown calculation does not replace it. Changing tau_buffer or reserve duration changes the selected policy and therefore changes nominal mass and its decay/TBR consequence; it is not an analysis holding fuel mass fixed while varying demand. Zero or small coverage can be evaluated within the source domain without an adequacy claim. Undefined breeding forces defined_flag=0 for production-dependent stock/startup interpretation; numerical zero carriers cannot be called a zero-production success.

Required evidence is thus equation/consumer agreement and disclosure of the supported policy, not manufactured insufficient/sufficient tank tests. Existing tests in `tests/models/test_fuel_inventory*.py` should be checked through the active runtime for working-stock sums, calendar-vs-running seconds, passive shutdown separation and undefined flags. Native offered-stock acceptance remains inapplicable because the public active contract does not offer that direction. The independent reviewer approved this explicit policy-scope disposition; no offered-stock capability is inferred.
