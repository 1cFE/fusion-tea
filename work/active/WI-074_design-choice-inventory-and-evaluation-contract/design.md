The exact four-wing supplied geometry/segmentation contract is approved in architecture-review-r2.md. 3. Fuel processing: supplied per-module throughput [kg D+T/s] and module count determine installed processing capacity and represented price. Running exhaust remains demand. A capacity constraint compares consistent plant totals and supports insufficient designs; no capacity-margin lower bound forces adequacy. The source-condition flag remains independent of capacity adequacy. 4. Cooling: keep heat-balance required flow separate from installed machine choice. The current correlations price operating shaft duty/flow/head; substituting a nameplate rating without source support would be a new approximation. A supplied rating alone cannot establish a pump curve. The bounded WI-078 design-point price and procurement contract has been reviewed; the scalar offered-flow and represented-fill checks do not qualify off-design pump performance. Native acceptance and residual proxy disclosure remain separate obligations.
5. Steam/cooling-water closures, cryogenic/turbine and other aggregate proxies: retain declared operating-point contracts. Make missing installed-equipment evaluation explicit; do not rebrand required flow or electric demand as supplied equipment capacity. These require meaning checks, not manufactured sufficient/insufficient tests.
6. Inventory/calendar policies: retain derived required stocks and lifecycle schedules where declared as scenario policies. Distinguish supplied startup/reserve stock if that freedom is included in the intended analysis; the current working-stock scenario does not establish storage hardware performance.
