# WI-058: Coil Winding Length from the Coil Bore — the winding circumference follows the coil the radial build sets, anchored at the printed 25 m

## Why this item exists

`[INHERITED]` The coil winding length is `c_coil = k_coil × R0` ('Coil Winding Length', `models/library/analyses/mfe_magnet_field.sysml`; `k_coil = 25.0 / 12.7` bound in `models/designs/stellarator_09/stellarator_plant.sysml`), from WI-036 D3, which traded a held 25 m for a held shape factor so the length would respond to "machine scale". It feeds the cold volume ('Winding Pack Cold Volume'), the ampere-metres and the winding procurement ('Winding Pack Procurement Cost', WI-040) and the legacy comparison account ('Winding Pack Cost'). Ninety-seven percent of the magnet account ($1,570.4M of $1,624.8M at the entering pin) is proportional to it.

`[INHERITED]` The goal's grounding probe (`work/orchestration/goals/magnet-coil-realism/evidence/grounding_probe/summary.md`) shows the consequence: along `a` at the design radius the winding procurement is bit-constant ($1,570,369,801.03 at every `a` from 1.3 to 2.2) while the coil bore grows from 3.15 m to 4.05 m and fusion power rises 24 %; along `R` at fixed `a` the procurement scales exactly with `R` although the bore does not move. The chain prices the wrong geometry.

`[INHERITED]` What the corpus supports. Stellaris § 2.9: "The coils have an approximate size of 7 × 5 × 10 m, with a typical circumference of 25 m" (`knowledge/concept_research/09-qi-stellarator-hts/iter-01/sources/stellaris-design-details.md` L1862; raw.pdf § 2.9). The coil's size is stated on the bore: at the design point the model's coil-centre radius is 3.15 m ('MFE Radial Build' `r_coil_centre`, WI-044 D1), a 6.3 m loop, and 25 m is the perimeter of a loop of that size with its non-planar excursion. Lion 2021 (PROCESS-stellarator, `knowledge/sources/a_general_stellarator_version_of_the_systems_code_process/output.md` L118–120) fixes "the coil number and the coil shapes" and scales "only the overall size of the coils"; its two scan parameters are "the major overall size of the machine (coil and plasma size)" and "the minor plasma radius `a` at constant coil radius". So PROCESS scales the coil with its own size at fixed shape, and never lets `a` change the coil — the plasma approaches a fixed coil, bounded by the coil–plasma distance (its § 3.10, the `f_geo` bound this model does not carry, WI-044 disclosure (i)). This model's radial build does the opposite coupling: the coil bore is `a` plus a fixed layer stack (WI-021, WI-044), so the coil moves with the plasma. Under that build the coil's size **is** its bore, and a coil of fixed shape scaled to its bore has a circumference proportional to the bore radius. The `R`-form and the bore-form coincide only when the bore scales with `R`. This build's bore is `a` plus a fixed layer stack, so scaling `R` and `a` together at fixed aspect ratio does not scale the bore proportionally and the two forms differ: at R 15.7 with `a` at the design aspect ratio (1.607 m) the bore form gives 27.44 m and the `R`-form 30.91 m. The identity that does hold, and that the restated consumers use, is at fixed bore: at `a` 1.3 the bore ratio is exactly 1.0, so binding `c_coil_ref = k_coil × R` reproduces the `R`-form length to the double (audit F1, 2026-09-14). The two forms disagree in every committed study window.
