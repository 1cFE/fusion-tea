# WI-057 Design — the Stellaris plant as nested physical parts with declared interfaces, at unchanged numbers

The generic MFE plant becomes a tree of physical parts: the plasma; the plasma-facing radial build with the first wall inside the blanket; the magnet system as coils, winding pack and casing; the primary heat transport loop; the power cycle on the turbine plant; the cryoplant; the fuel cycle; the vacuum pumping; and the existing cost-account subsystems. Every attribute is declared on the part whose quantity it describes. Every calc usage is a template calc owned by the definition of the part whose subject it computes, so the plant only wires the values that cross part boundaries. Every calc output read across a boundary is surfaced by an EXPOSE attribute on the definition, and the EXPOSEs a trade study may replace are declared `default` — the swap seams a variant definition rebinds. Port definitions and `connect` statements declare the energy topology the calcs already compute. No calc def, constraint def, formula or value changes. The prototype (`prototype/README.md`) proved each mechanism on a copy of the real model — regroup, nest, declare, template calcs on the definition, and a swap that changes the priced number only when the variant's own formula does — with every existing parameter mapped, all 155 channels and 14 verdicts identical, and the LCOE at `224.60952472804465`. Written under goal `structural-decomposition` round 1, task T-001; revised after the fresh review (`design-review.md`, § Amendments); the form is `[AGENT] — proposed under the owner's direction of 2026-09-13` (`goal.md` § Reserved gates 2, 3, 7).

**Departures from the spec's list and the mental model's depth map, each a decision here:**

- (a) No ports on `shield`, `structure`, `vessel` although the depth map says "ports only": no computed exchange crosses them (they contribute volumes to the radial build and cost to the rollup), and MR-WI048-3 forbids a connection with no chain behind it. Their depth is met by owning their layer thicknesses.
- (b) The power cycle is not a separate part: the CAS23 `'Turbine Plant'` is the power cycle, so the cycle calc and its nine fit facts live there. A `power_cycle` part beside `turbine` would be two names for one machine.
- (c) The first wall and the blanket are "fully decomposed" only as far as the model has quantities: the first wall owns its ten facts and three calcs; the blanket owns its breeding and multiplication facts and the first wall. No breeding-zone or manifold sub-part exists because no attribute would live on it.
- (d) **The parameterized-only rule (this design's, stricter than the spec's "flat where the source gives only a cost rate"):** a part exists only if at least one attribute or calc lives on it. So no support-structure sub-part — the source's inter-coil plates and rings are the plant's `structure` part already (CAS22.1.5, WI-035 D5: "casings only; plates/rings/legs stay CAS22.1.5") — and no current-leads or cryostat parts. The goal's answer (c) names "support structure": it is `structure`, and the trail says so at T-001's return.
- (e) Radial order is not expressed by the tree: the layers are siblings on the plant (with `first_wall` under `blanket`), and their order lives in `rb`'s formals and in the layer docs. A radial-build container part would own nothing (rule (d)).

## 2. Decisions

**D1 — Two views of one tree.** The CAS account hierarchy stays the cost view: every costed component keeps its typed specialization (AD-005) and its `capital_cost`, and the rollups keep reading them. The part tree is the physical view: sub-parts and the new systems are plain `part def`s, not costed components. MR-2 is satisfied at the level that carries cost — the magnet is the costed component; its sub-parts contribute through its calcs. A costed component gaining physics attributes (the blanket's `mn`, the turbine's cycle fit) is not a mixing of views: the attributes describe the component, the CAS type says which account it bills to; each extended definition's doc says so. (Spec open decision 4, risk 4.)

**D2 — File layout: a `structure/` library folder.** New concept-agnostic physical definitions go in `models/library/structure/` — `mfe_interfaces.sysml` (item and port defs), `mfe_plasma.sysml`, `mfe_magnet_parts.sysml`, `mfe_radial_build_parts.sysml` (`'First Wall'`), `mfe_plant_systems.sysml` (`'Primary Heat Transport'`, `'Cryoplant'`, `'Fuel Cycle'`, `'Vacuum Pumping'`) — and the existing costed definitions are extended in place (`mfe_subsystems.sysml`, `mfe_power_core.sysml`). Twin path `exploration/stellarator_e2e/models/structure/`; the five files join the MFE family's owned list in `tests/model_families.py`. AD-004's three folders separate base types, cost structure and calculations; physical structure is a fourth concern, so the implementation registers **AD-008** (`pm register-decision`): "physical, non-costed part definitions and interface definitions live in `library/structure/`; definitions that own template calcs import the analysis packages they need". The prototype generated from a tree with a `structure/` folder without configuration. (Spec open decision 6.)

**D3 — Attribute homes.** `prototype/attribute_homes.json` is the complete map of the 251 existing attributes — the plant's 199, the instance's 6, the magnet definition's 31 and the costed subsystems' 15 — each exactly once: 164 get a component home, 87 stay on the plant with a reason class. Inherited costed-component attributes (`capital_cost`, `cas_code`, `account_name`, `scope`) are unchanged and not listed; new EXPOSE and input attributes are D5's, listed there. Names never change — only the owner path — so the rename ledger is mechanical. Rules applied: a fact lives on the part whose physical quantity it describes, and a component's own limit lives on the component (`coil_life_fpy` → `magnet.coil`, `fluence_limit` → `blanket.first_wall`, `q_nuc_cryo` → `magnet.winding_pack`, `vol_cold_cryo` → `magnet`; the calendar and the cryoplant read them one hop); a layer thickness lives on the layer, the two clearances on the component they stand off from (`gap1_t` → vessel, `gap2_t` → magnet); a cost *base* of a sub-account lives with the system it prices (`coolant_*_base` → heat transport, `aux_cryo_base` → cryoplant, `fuel_handling_base` → fuel cycle) while the exposed *capital* siblings stay on the plant because the aggregation producers sum plant siblings; the recirculating powers go to their consumers (`p_tf`, `p_pf` → power supplies; `p_tfcool`, `p_pfcool` → cryoplant; `p_trit` → fuel cycle) and `p_house`, `f_sub`, `n_mod`, `aux_per_mw` stay plant-wide (the aux-cooling rate prices the plant total, not the cryoplant); economics and the lifecycle calendar's own inputs stay on the plant. `T_cold_cryo` stays on the cryoplant: it is the cryoplant's cold-end temperature, equal to the coil operating temperature by design. The instance's own six stay at instance level. (Spec open decisions 2, 3; review F3, N1.)

**D4 — Template calcs on the definition; the plant wires inputs.** Each calc usage is owned by the *definition* of the part in § 1's "Template calcs" column, with its bindings inside the definition: sub-part attributes by path (`coil.I_coil`), the definition's own attributes bare when the formal's name differs, owner-qualified when it does not (`'Magnet System'::r_coil`), and a producing sibling calc read directly where an EXPOSE shares the formal's name (`in B = field_calc.B_axis`). A value from outside the part enters as an **input attribute** on the definition (`r_coil_centre`, `p_fus`, `q_source`, `T_hot`, …), named for the quantity so the calc formal's `_in` suffix keeps D-5, and the plant binds it in the usage — `part magnet : 'Magnet System' { :>> r_coil_centre = rb.r_coil_centre; }`, the existing `r_coil` pattern. No calc inside a definition reads outer scope. The plant keeps the radial build (eleven layers), the power balance, the CAS rollups, the calendar and the LCOE chain. Why the definition and not the usage: the prototype's Stage B (usage-hosted) and Stage E (definition-hosted) both executed with identical numbers and 76 stencils preserved, but only definition-hosted calcs can be replaced by a variant definition (D9), the definition then declares its interface completely (its inputs are the port items), and the reviewer's unproven outer-scope reads disappear. (Spec open decision 1; review F1, F5.)

**D5 — EXPOSE at every part boundary; swap seams as `default`.** A calc output consumed outside its part is declared as an attribute on the definition bound to the producer (`attribute B_peak : Real = peak_field_calc.B_peak;`), and every outside consumer reads `<part>.<name>`, one hop. Where the plant wires one part's EXPOSE into another part's input, the usage binds it (`part plasma : 'Plasma' { :>> B = magnet.B; }`). The EXPOSEs this design adds: on the magnet `B_peak`, `sigma_wp`, `eps_cond`, `vol_cold_total`, `capital_cost_1cfe`, and the two seams `winding_cost`, `structure_cost`; on the plasma `V`, `p_fus`, `p_avg`, `p_alpha_heat`, `p_aux_required`, `p_rad`, `n_D0`, `n_T0`, `beta`; on the first wall `wall_load`, `wall_load_peak`, `calibration`, re-exposed on the blanket as `wall_load`, `wall_load_peak` (a two-level EXPOSE; N2: the same-name form is tried first and the diagnostic recorded if it refuses); on the blanket `q_source`; on the heat transport `T_out`, `q_recovered_total`, `p_pump_total`, `p_loop_margin`, `mdot_loop`; on the turbine `eta_th`, `domain_product`; on the heating `p_coupled`, `p_wallplug_total`, `p_delivered`; on the cryoplant `p_elec`, `cryo_cost`; on the divertor `q_target_peak`; on the fuel cycle `annual_fuel`, `exhaust_rate`, `burn_rate`. The exact list is fixed by the implementation from the plant's cross-part reads and recorded in the plan. **Swap seams**: an EXPOSE a trade study may replace is declared `default <producer>.<output>` instead of `=`, because a `=` binding on a definition is final (`prototype/redefinition_probe/`). This item declares two seams, the magnet's `winding_cost` and `structure_cost` (the two legs the rollup sums); any other EXPOSE can be promoted to a seam later by changing `=` to `default`, with no numeric effect. Rule from the prototype: the EXPOSE binds the calc output; only outside consumers read the EXPOSE (`SI_ALIAS_CYCLE` otherwise).

**D6 — Reference shapes: executed, and expected by the same route.** *Executed in the prototype with identical numbers* (`prototype/README.md` § Reference shapes): inside a definition — a sibling sub-part's attribute, the definition's own attribute owner-qualified, a producing sibling calc, the definition's own input attribute, a nested usage binding an outer definition-level calc output, an EXPOSE bound `=` and a seam bound `default`; at the plant — a `:>>` of an outer calc output into a nested usage's definition attribute, a one-hop EXPOSE read, a two-segment attribute chain (`magnet.casing.m_casing_ref`). *Expected by the same resolution route, executed in commit B before it is relied on*: the plant's `:>>` of another part's EXPOSE into a definition input (`part plasma { :>> B = magnet.B; }` — the r_coil pattern with a part attribute as source); a template calc two levels deep (the first wall's under the blanket); the two-level EXPOSE. Forbidden: a multi-hop chain to a calc output (`plant-idiom.md` D3 — surface it through EXPOSE), a bare self-named binding, an indexed reference, any read of outer scope from inside a definition. (Review F5.)

**D7 — Ports and connections, declarative.** `mfe_interfaces.sysml` defines items `'Coolant Stream'`, `'Electric Power'`, `'Neutron Power'`, `'Material Stream'`, `'Cryogenic Heat Load'` and ports `'Thermal Port'` (out `hot`, in `cold`), `'Electric Port'` (out `power`), `'Neutron Port'` (out `neutrons`), `'Material Port'` (out `stream`), `'Cryogenic Load Port'` (out `heat_load`). The producer side declares the port; the consumer declares the conjugate (`~`). The plant connects exactly the exchanges § 3 ties to a calc chain: `plasma.neutron_out → blanket.neutron_in`; `plasma.alpha_out → divertor.heat_in`; `heating.power_to_plasma → plasma.heat_in`; `blanket.coolant → heat_transport.hot_in`; `heat_transport.hot_out → turbine.coolant`; `turbine.gross_electric → electric_plant.gross_in`; `electric_plant.recirculating →` each of `heating`, `heat_transport`, `cryoplant`, `power_supplies`, `fuel_cycle` `.electric_in`; `magnet.cold_mass → cryoplant.heat_load_in`; `blanket.tritium_out → fuel_cycle.tritium_in`; `fuel_cycle.fuel_out → plasma.fuel_in`; `fuel_cycle.exhaust_out → vacuum_pumping.exhaust_in`. Not connected, on purpose: `electric_plant.grid` (no grid part); no `magnet.electric_in` (the coil drive and cooling powers are `power_supplies` and `cryoplant` terms in the balance, so the magnet's electrical draw is not a computed exchange); no first-wall coolant port (the model computes one loop; the source's separate helium first-wall loop would be a second exchange with no chain — spec open decision 5); no `plasma → vacuum_pumping` (the exhaust the model computes is the fuel cycle's stream). No `flow of` declarations: a flow doubles every connection with an item-level endpoint no consumer reads; the port item types already say what moves (spec open decision 8). Stage C showed ports, connections and a flow leave the semantic fingerprint unchanged. (Review F2, N6, N7.)

**D8 — The Stellaris instance mirrors the tree.** Bindings nest in `part :>> <part> { … }` blocks following the plant's structure, each with its doc comment intact; the instance imports the packages whose definitions it names (`mfe_power_core::*`, the `structure/` packages, in addition to today's three); its own constraints and exposures stay at instance level and read EXPOSEs (`plasma.beta`, `blanket.wall_load_peak`, `plasma.p_aux_required`, `heating.p_coupled`, `blanket.tbr`). The blanket doc's existing "HCLL" sentence is not edited (goal gate 6); the new heat-transport doc names the loop "the representative helium primary circuit of WI-045 (Moscato 2017), not the source's water-cooled breeding zone — `goal.md` fact 6", and the same sentence sits on the `blanket.coolant → heat_transport.hot_in` connection and on the blanket's `coolant` port doc, because that connection is where the premise now lives structurally (N5). The `'Coolant Stream'` item names no fluid.

**D9 — The swap mechanism: a variant definition rebinds a seam.** A trade-study variant is a `part def` specializing the interface definition (`'NI HTS Magnet System' :> 'Magnet System'`); it adds its own facts as attributes, adds its own template calc with its own formula, and rebinds the seam — `:>> structure_cost = magnet_structure_cost_ni.cost;`. The instance swaps by retyping — `part :>> magnet : 'NI HTS Magnet System' { … }` — and binds the variant's facts there; no calc, plant or binding is edited. Stage E executed this on the real magnet: with the variant's markup at 0 every channel is identical; at 0.1 the variant's casing formula moves the magnet rollup and the LCOE (224.6095 → 224.7276). Rules from the probes: bound (`=`) values on a definition are final, so a variant can neither re-bind a template calc's formals nor redefine a calc by the same name (`redefinition_probe/README.md` a–d); a variant must not retype a sub-part the instance also redefines (codegen refuses the shadowing member); an instance binding a retyped sub-part's attribute restates the type. A fact-variant (a different `cost_per_kAm`, `B_max`, `m_casing_ref`) needs no retype at all — rebinding in the instance is the swap — and the structure makes that legible because the facts sit on the sub-part they describe. The base template calc still executes in a retyped instance, dormant; its channel stays. Where the variant lives (N4): the committed library holds no variant — an uninstantiated calc-bearing definition would be a Level 6 finding in the canonical tree — so the demonstration's variant definition, its prototype-only calc def and its instance are deposited under `prototype/swap/` with the script that materializes a scratch copy of the tree, and the executed results beside them. (Spec MR-WI048-7, open decision 7; review F1.)

**D10 — Number neutrality, proven by construction and by diff.** Three commits, each regenerated and diffed against the entering baseline through the contract-derived ledger: **A** parts, attribute homes, bindings re-pointed (parameter names move); **B** template calcs on the definitions with EXPOSEs, inputs and seams (channel names move); **C** ports, connections, the swap demonstration under `prototype/swap/`, the viewer screenshots (nothing in the contract moves). The ledger is generated from the two contracts by owner path (`prototype/scripts/proto_compare.py` generalized to the full move map), never typed. After each commit: `tests/models` (63 passed / 13 skipped at entry), Levels 1–6 at or below the entry residue (Level 2: 10 placeholder bindings; Level 6: 227 issues, 101 incomplete design attributes, 57 unextractable), the twins byte-identical, the baseline execution equal channel by channel, `tests/study` at or better than entry (86 failed, all in `test_study_publication_fail_closed.py`, 424 passed, 1 skipped — 2026-09-13 reading; one further failure, `test_integrate_preconditions.py::test_simkit_probe_refuses_without_the_teax_root`, appears only when `PYTHONPATH` carries the teax simkit path and passes clean). The consumers re-keyed with commits A and B (spec MR-WI048-9): `tests/models/data/mfe_census.json`; `tests/models/test_model_family_spines.py` (`BLANKET_T_SOURCE` → `{P}blanket__blanket_t`; the `CAS28_*` and `rb` keys stay); `tests/study/data/axes.known_answers.json` and `axes.extras.json`; the six `*.expected.json`; `studies/manifest.json` (baseline point keys `plasma__R`, `plasma__a`, `magnet__coil__R0`, `availability_direct`; headline channel unchanged at `lcoe_calc__lcoe`); `study_route.py` (`AXES`, `R_TIE`, `CHANNELS`: `magnet_cost__capital_cost` → `magnet__magnet_cost__capital_cost`, `fusion__p_fus` → `plasma__fusion__p_fus`, `geom__V` → `plasma__geom__V`, `wall_load_calc__wall_load` → `blanket__first_wall__wall_load_calc__wall_load`); `oracle_entry.py`; `run_stellaris.py`; `run_stellaris_single.py` (its channel stems checked, not assumed); `handshake_1costingfe.py`; every `tests/study/test_*.py` that names a key (eight at review time, enumerated by `grep` in the plan). `modeling_project/VALIDATION_MATRIX.md` history stands; the new MR-WI048-11 rows use the new names. ## 4. Risks and how each is checked

1. **A template calc on a definition other than the magnet's fails to resolve.** Stage E executed the magnet's thirteen; the plasma's, first wall's, blanket's, heat transport's, turbine's, cryoplant's, heating's, divertor's and fuel cycle's are the same shape with their inputs wired by the plant. *Check:* commit B is regenerated and executed definition by definition; a refusal is a `MECHANICAL_FAILURE` to fix by an input attribute or an EXPOSE, never by moving the calc back silently — if a calc cannot be hosted on its definition, the plan records it and it stays on the plant.
2. **The depth-2 template calcs (the first wall's) or the two-level EXPOSE do not surface.** *Check:* the baseline diff after commit B; fallback for the EXPOSE is the plant reading `blanket.first_wall.wall_load_peak` two hops (the two-segment attribute chain executed in Stage A) — recorded either way.
3. **The plant's `:>>` of a part EXPOSE into a definition input (`:>> B = magnet.B`) resolves differently from the calc-output source executed in Stage E.** *Check:* the first binding of that shape (the plasma's `B`) is regenerated and diffed alone before the rest of commit B.
4. **Rename churn breaks a consumer the list misses.** *Check:* `grep -rn "stellaris__"` and a stem grep on every moved calc's channel name over the repository after re-keying; `tests/study` at or better than entry.
5. **The census loses or gains entry points.** Attributes that were entry points remain entry points under new names; computed attributes (`c_coil`, `wp_side`, `m_casing`) and the new inputs/EXPOSEs/seams are not parameters. *Check:* the ledger's `unmatched old` and `new-only` counts are zero after each commit, as in every prototype stage at markup 0.
6. **The viewer's fixture hash test fails.** `tests/model_viz` pins the current snapshot's SHA-256 (plan PD5); the new snapshot is a new fixture. *Check:* the model-viz tests are re-pinned in the commit that recaptures the snapshot, with the counts re-derived — a coding-PM touch inside this item's scope because the fixture is the snapshot this item regenerates.
7. **The IFE family sees a changed shared file.** None of the three shared foundation files changes. *Check:* the IFE spine tests unchanged.
8. **A definition importing analysis packages creates an import cycle.** `mfe_power_core` will import `mfe_magnet_field`, `mfe_magnet_cost`, `mfe_plasma_scaling`; none of those imports `mfe_power_core`. *Check:* Level 1 and the twin generation; the prototype's Stage E tree had these imports.
