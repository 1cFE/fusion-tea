# WI-057: Stellaris structural decomposition — nested parts, ports, and connections, at unchanged numbers

Backlog name: "Stellaris structural decomposition — nested parts, ports, and connections" (`work/BACKLOG.md`, standalone, P1). Written under goal `structural-decomposition` round 1, task T-001 (`work/orchestration/goals/structural-decomposition/trail.md`). The direction is the owner's `[OWNER-VERBATIM 2026-09-13]`: "a structural change to make the sysmlv2 more navigable, as well as help for display purposes (not being just flat). But unless you find real issues, it seems like the end math itself for LCOE shouldn't change" and "the point of using SysMLv2 is to ground the modeling to the system it is meant to represent, and support rapid trade studies (e.g. swapping out different magnet definitions)". The form of every requirement below is `[AGENT] — proposed under that direction` unless marked otherwise; `goal.md` § Reserved gates records the calls it rests on.

## Why this item exists

The Stellaris model prices a machine it cannot describe. Measured from the committed snapshot (`exploration/stellarator_e2e/stellarator.snapshot.json@d235dde4`): 14 occurrences at depth 2 (the plant and 13 flat cost-account subsystems); 292 attributes of which 194 sit on the plant root; 76 calcs all on the root; zero ports, connections, flows or interfaces. A reader can learn what the magnet costs but not that it is coils in casings on support rings, nor that the blanket's heat goes to a primary loop and then to a cycle. The calc viewer built for this model (`.project/active/model-viz/`) has an occurrence grouping mode that is degenerate on it because everything scopes to the root (`design.md@1a550d42` § Occurrence mode).

The research report the owner named (`knowledge/research/pending/20260912-115614_stellaris-structural-behavioral-modeling.md@16d83308`) maps what the source describes physically and recommends an incremental path: nested parts first, then ports and connections, then flows, with actions and states deferred. The mental model (`.project/mental-alignment/runs/20260912-120800_stellaris-structural-model_resumed.html@16d83308`) tabulates where each magnet attribute belongs and which subsystems have source depth for decomposition. The goal's grounding probe (`work/orchestration/goals/structural-decomposition/evidence/grounding_probe/summary.md`) settled the report's blocking question: syside parses every construct, codegen generates with ports and connections present (ignored), nested part usages become nested occurrences, and a calc reads a nested attribute through a multi-segment chain.

The price of nesting is that a nested attribute's generated name carries its path (`stellarator_09__stellaris__magnet__casing__m_casing_ref` for what is today `…__magnet__m_casing_ref`). Every consumer that keys on qualified names is re-keyed in this item, and the new package is a new study lineage.

## What must be true afterward (requirements)

#### MR-WI048-1: Physical decomposition where the source describes it, flat where it does not
The generic MFE plant SHALL decompose its subsystems into nested parts wherever the Stellaris source describes distinct physical components, and SHALL stay flat where the source gives only a cost rate. At minimum: the magnet system into modular coils, winding pack, coil casing and support structure (the sources' sec. 2.10 casings, inter-coil plates and support rings); the plasma-facing cluster as the radial build — first wall, breeding blanket, neutron shield, vacuum vessel — as distinct parts in radial order; a plasma part owning the plasma geometry and profile quantities; the power conversion chain as distinct parts — the primary heat transport loop and the power cycle — alongside the existing turbine, electric, heat-rejection and miscellaneous plant, which stay flat; a cryoplant part and a fuel-cycle part where the model already computes their chains. The depth for each subsystem follows the mental model's depth map (§ 4): full decomposition for magnet, first wall and breeding blanket; a part with ports only for shield, vessel, structure, heating and divertor; flat for the balance of plant; buildings absent from decomposition. *Priority:* P0. *Rationale:* the owner's "ground the modeling to the system it is meant to represent"; the report's recommendation 4 ("don't model what the sources don't describe"). *Validation:* the snapshot's occurrence table shows the nesting; every new part is cited to the source section that describes it. *Source:* `stellaris-design-details/output.md` secs. 2.10 (magnets), 2.11–2.12 (first wall, blanket, shield, vessel), Table 2 (power flow); the mental model § 2, § 4.

#### MR-WI048-2: Every attribute lives on the part whose quantity it describes
Every attribute SHALL be declared on the part that physically owns the quantity it describes — coil current on the coil, winding-pack current density on the winding pack, casing mass anchors on the casing, radial-build thicknesses on the layers they size or on the radial-build owner the design names, loop conditions on the primary loop, cycle-fit constants on the power cycle, cryoplant constants on the cryoplant, fuel-cycle constants on the fuel cycle, plasma geometry and profiles on the plasma — and plant-level quantities (cost rollups, financial rates, the lifecycle calendar, the power balance outputs) SHALL stay on the plant. The design SHALL name every attribute that moves, its old and new qualified name, and its new owner, in one rename ledger deposited as evidence; an attribute the design leaves on the plant SHALL be listed with the reason. *Priority:* P0. *Rationale:* 194 of 292 attributes have no subsystem home; navigability is the owner's first stated purpose. *Validation:* the ledger is complete against the snapshot's attribute table (every attribute appears exactly once); the snapshot after the change shows the plant root holding only the listed plant-level attributes. *Source:* MODELING_PROCESS § Architecture & Design "Own quantities and responsibilities".

#### MR-WI048-3: The energy topology is declared as ports and connections
The library SHALL define port definitions for the exchanges the model computes — at least thermal (heat carried by a coolant), electrical power, and neutron/fusion power — and the generic plant SHALL declare ports on the parts that exchange them and SHALL connect them in the plant: plasma → first wall and breeding blanket (neutron and radiated power), blanket cluster → primary heat transport loop, loop → power cycle, cycle → electric plant → grid, and the recirculating electrical loads (heating, cryoplant, primary-loop pumping, auxiliaries) from the electric plant back to their consumers. The declared topology SHALL match the calc wiring it describes: every connection corresponds to a binding chain the calcs already compute, and the design SHALL show that correspondence. Ports and connections are declarative in this pipeline (the grounding probe: no diagnostic, no parameter, no channel) and SHALL parse and generate clean. The primary loop SHALL be named by what the model computes today — a representative helium primary circuit — not by the source's water-cooled breeding zone (`goal.md` § Reserved gates 6). *Priority:* P0. *Rationale:* the owner's "not being just flat"; the report's step 2. *Validation:* syside diagnostics zero; codegen exit 0 with the contract's parameters and outputs unchanged by the port and connection declarations alone; the correspondence table in the design. *Source:* the report § Energy and Material Topology; SysML v2 Part 1 §§ 7.12–7.13.

#### MR-WI048-4: Number-neutral, channel by channel, with no tolerance
At the regenerated package's baseline point every channel value and every verdict SHALL equal the entering pin's (`goal.md` § Invariants), each old channel mapped to its new name through the rename ledger, the headline LCOE `224.60952472804465` included. A difference SHALL stop the work: derive why before continuing, name any floating-point reordering that explains it exactly, and never tune to restore it. *Priority:* P0. *Rationale:* `[OWNER-VERBATIM 2026-09-13]` "the end math itself for LCOE shouldn't change". *Validation:* a channel-by-channel diff of the new baseline execution against `T-007_pin/baseline_result.json` through the ledger, deposited; the six known-answer fixtures reproduce under their re-keyed axes. *Source:* the goal's answer contract (b).

#### MR-WI048-5: The calc math is not edited
No calc def's formals, formulas or return SHALL change; no constraint def SHALL change; no numeric binding value SHALL change. What changes is where attributes are declared, how calc formals address them (`in x = magnet.coil.I_coil` for what was `magnet.I_coil`), and what structure the plant declares. Whether calc usages move under the parts they belong to or stay on the plant is the design's decision, taken on prototype evidence through the intended execution route (MODELING_PROCESS § Architecture & Design: "parsing establishes only syntax support"). *Priority:* P0. *Validation:* a diff of every `calc def` and `constraint def` body between the entering and the new tree is empty; the semantic fingerprint changes only through names, which the ledger accounts for. *Source:* `goal.md` § Invariants "The calc math is not the subject".

#### MR-WI048-6: Library / design boundary and concept-agnosticism
New part definitions and port definitions SHALL be concept-agnostic library elements (`models/library/`), parameterized by attributes with no Stellaris value; the generic MFE plant SHALL compose and wire them; the Stellaris design SHALL bind values through nested `:>>` redefinitions and SHALL carry no definition. The three foundation files shared with the IFE family SHALL keep their meaning for it, and the IFE designs SHALL generate unchanged. *Priority:* P0. *Rationale:* MR-3; AD-007. *Validation:* Levels 1–3 over `models/`; the IFE family's spine tests unchanged. *Source:* MR-3, AD-007, `goal.md` § Reserved gates 3.

#### MR-WI048-7: A magnet definition can be swapped without editing a calc
The structure SHALL allow a second magnet-system definition — a different coil technology or a different casing basis, expressed as a part def specializing the same interface — to be bound in place of the current one in an instance by changing only the part's typing or redefinition, with no calc, binding or plant edit; the design SHALL demonstrate this in a prototype instance (not a new committed concept) and record what the swap needed, or SHALL record as a bounded negative exactly which wiring prevents it. *Priority:* P1. *Rationale:* `[OWNER-VERBATIM 2026-09-13]` "support rapid trade studies (e.g. swapping out different magnet definitions)". *Validation:* the prototype generates and executes; its record is deposited under the item. *Source:* `goal.md` § Answered when (d), § Reserved gates 7.

#### MR-WI048-8: Citations travel with their attributes; nothing is re-sourced by moving
Every moved attribute SHALL keep its Source/Ref/Basis doc comment verbatim at its new declaration or binding; every new part and port definition SHALL carry a doc comment citing the source section it represents. The blanket's existing "HCLL" doc reading and the loop's helium basis SHALL NOT be edited here (`goal.md` fact 6, § Reserved gates 6); the new structural doc text SHALL name the loop as the helium circuit the model computes. *Priority:* P0. *Rationale:* MR-4. *Validation:* the traceability audit clean over the changed files; a text diff shows every doc comment relocated, none dropped. *Source:* MR-4.

## Assumptions & risks

1. **Calc usages nested under parts.** *Confidence: medium.* The grounding probe kept calcs on the root; the viewer's occurrence mode was tested on nested calcs only synthetically. If codegen or the handwritten-stage routing cannot carry a calc scoped under a sub-part, calcs stay on the plant and the structure is still delivered (parts, attributes, ports). The design's prototype answers it before the decision is taken.
2. **Chained bindings deeper than two segments across the whole model.** *Confidence: high.* The probe resolved a three-segment chain; the codegen's exact-path resolution refuses indexed segments only (`binding_source.py`). A refusal on the real model is a `MECHANICAL_FAILURE` with a retry, or a `PREREQUISITE` if it is a seam limitation.
3. **Rename churn.** *Likelihood: certain. Impact: medium.* Around 200 qualified names change. Known cost and known recipe (`gotcha_repin_after_regeneration`); the ledger is generated from the two snapshots, not typed by hand.
4. **Costed-component interface on sub-parts.** MR-2 requires every part contributing to cost to specialize the costed interface. A coil or a casing contributes to the magnet's cost through the magnet's calcs but carries no `capital_cost` of its own. The design decides whether sub-parts are plain part defs (the magnet remains the costed component and rolls up) or costed components with a rollup — the former keeps the CAS tree a cost view and the part tree a physical view, as the report recommends.
5. **Reserved words and conjugation.** `loop` is reserved; conjugated ports flip item directions; a def-level `= value` is a binding a nested `:>>` cannot override (grounding probe § What it establishes 1). Authoring rules, carried into the design.
6. **A fresh reviewer reads the declarations as decoration.** *Mitigation:* MR-WI048-3's correspondence table ties every connection to the calc chain it describes; MR-WI048-10's screenshots show the display consumer using the nesting; MR-WI048-7 shows a swap.

## Open decisions (for design)

1. **Calc placement** — keep all calc usages on the plant (safe, proven) or move each under the part it prices (more navigable; prototype must prove the execution route and the handwritten-stage routing).
2. **Where the radial-build thicknesses live** — on each layer part (`first_wall.t`, `blanket.t`, …) with `'MFE Radial Build'` reading them through chains, or on one radial-build owner part.
3. **The plasma part's type** — a new library `'Plasma'` part def (not a costed component) owning geometry, density, temperature and profile quantities.
4. **Sub-part typing** — plain part defs versus costed components (risk 4).
5. **Port granularity** — one thermal port def carrying a coolant item versus separate helium and water port defs; whether the first wall and the blanket declare separate coolant ports now (the source's two loops) while both connect to the one modelled loop.
6. **File layout** — new library files (`mfe_plasma.sysml`, `mfe_radial_build_parts.sysml`, `mfe_ports.sysml`, …) versus extending `mfe_power_core.sysml` and `mfe_subsystems.sysml`; each new file joins the MFE family's owned list.
7. **Which second magnet definition the swap prototype uses** — a casing-basis variant is the smallest; a conductor-technology variant is closer to the owner's example.
8. **Step 3 flows** — whether any `flow of` declarations are added at all in this item, given they carry no execution meaning here.
