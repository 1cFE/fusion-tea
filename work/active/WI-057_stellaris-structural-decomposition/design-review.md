# WI-057 Design Review — fresh reviewer, 2026-09-13

**Verdict: REVISE.** No decision reverses. The tree, the two-views split (D1), the EXPOSE rule (D5), the number-neutrality proof (D10) and the commit plan are sound and the prototype backs them. What must change before a plan is written from this design: three sentences claim more than the prototype executed or the model shows (D6, D9, § 3 row 2), four attributes sit against their own doc comments (D3), three declared connections have no calc chain behind them (§ 3), and the design departs from the spec's depth map in four places without saying so (§ 1). All of it is text and a handful of homes and connections; only one item (D9) may need a prototype run to settle.

Mapping to the review metadata: `concerns` — the design stands, its text does not yet.

## Findings

### Should-fix

**F1 — D9 demonstrates a relabel, not a swap.** `design.md` § 2 D9; `prototype/README.md` Stage D; `prototype/model_diff_stage_D.patch` lines 174–179 and 785, 1007. The variant `'NI HTS Magnet System'` adds one attribute (`t_charge_h`), `'NI Winding Pack'` adds one (`f_insulation`); no calc reads either, so the retype changes nothing and cannot. D9's own sentence admits it: "a variant's added fact becomes a parameter only when a calc reads it." Every magnet variant the spec names (a coil technology, a casing basis) is, in this model, a set of *facts* — `cost_per_kAm`, `B_max`, `T_cold_cryo`, `m_casing_ref`, `W_mag_ref` — and facts are already swapped by rebinding in the instance with no retype at all. Retyping only earns its place if a variant can bring its own *calc* (a different cost formula). D4 hosts the thirteen magnet calcs on the plant's `part magnet` usage, not on the `'Magnet System'` def; plant-idiom's swap route (§ "Retyping to pull in a subtype's calcs (D2)") pulls in *def*-owned calcs with same-name redefinition, and the prototype hit `SYSML_NAMESPACE_NOT_DISTINGUISHABLE` the one time a variant def and the instance both named the same member. So the design has not shown that a variant with different pricing math can enter by retyping, and D4 may foreclose it. *Resolve:* either run the one missing case in the prototype (a variant def carrying a same-named calc against the usage-hosted calcs) and record what happens, or write D9 as the bounded negative MR-WI048-7 allows — "a fact-variant needs no retype; a formula-variant needs a calc-usage edit in the plant while calcs are usage-hosted" — and carry that wording into the goal's answer (d).

**F2 — Three connections in § 3 have no calc chain, and one row cites a calc that does not read the value.** `design.md` § 3. (a) `electric_plant.recirculating → magnet.electric_in`: `pb` (`mfe_plant.sysml` lines 559–575) carries no magnet term; the coil drive and coil cooling powers land on `power_supplies` (`p_tf`, `p_pf`) and `cryoplant` (`p_tfcool`, `p_pfcool`, `cryo_elec.p_elec`) in the design's own home map. The magnet port declares an exchange the model does not compute. (b) `plasma.exhaust_out → vacuum_pumping.exhaust_in`: the chain is `vacuum.exhaust_rate_*_in = fuel.exhaust_rate`, `helium_rate_in = fuel.burn_rate` (lines 1082–1088) — fuel cycle to vacuum, not plasma to vacuum; the row says so and then declares the other thing. (c) `heating.power_to_plasma → plasma.heat_in`: the row says "`sustain` / `pb` read `heating.p_coupled`". `sustain` (lines 245–263) does not read `p_coupled`; the readers are `pb`, `source_heat`, `divheat` and the instance's `sustainment_ok`. No calc hosted on `plasma` consumes heating power, so the plasma-side port has nothing behind it. Conversely a computed cross-part chain has no connection: `cryo_elec.vol_cold = wp_volume.vol_cold_total` (line 486) is the magnet's cold volume handed to the cryoplant, a real thermal interface the calcs compute and the topology omits. MR-WI048-3 says every connection corresponds to a binding chain and the table shows it. *Resolve:* drop `magnet.electric_in` or re-source it from `power_supplies` with the chain named; connect `fuel_cycle → vacuum_pumping` (or declare both and mark the plasma one as source-described, not computed); correct row 2 to the actual readers; add or explicitly decline a `magnet → cryoplant` thermal connection.

**F3 — Four attribute homes contradict the attribute's own doc comment.** `prototype/attribute_homes.json`; `design.md` D3. `q_nuc_cryo` is "winding-pack mean nuclear heating [W/m³]" (`stellarator_plant.sysml` line 1014) and `vol_cold_cryo` is "ADDITIONAL cold volume beyond the winding pack" (line 1017): both describe the magnet's cold mass, not the plant that cools it, and both are placed on `cryoplant`. Placing `vol_cold_cryo` on the magnet also removes the `cryoplant.vol_cold_cryo`-from-inside-`magnet` read that D6 leans on. `coil_life_fpy` is "coil lifetime (99th quantile) [FPY]" (line 1301) and `fluence_limit` is the "DT first-wall/blanket neutron-fluence replacement limit" (line 1396): both are a component's limit, both stay on the plant under the reason class "lifecycle". MR-WI048-2 asks for a reason per attribute left on the plant; "the calendar reads it" is where the consumer is, not who owns the quantity, which is the rule D3 states. `T_cold_cryo` ("coil operating temperature", line 1069) is a judgement call — the cryoplant's cold-end temperature equals it — and can stay if the design says why. *Resolve:* move `q_nuc_cryo` and `vol_cold_cryo` to `magnet.winding_pack` or `magnet`; move `coil_life_fpy` to `magnet.coil` and `fluence_limit` to `blanket.first_wall` (the calendar reads them one hop, the same shape as `rb` reading a layer thickness), or give each a stated physical reason to stay.

**F4 — Four departures from MR-WI048-1 and the depth map are not stated as departures.** `design.md` § 1 and the paragraph after the table. (a) The spec's depth map (mental model § 4) gives shield, vessel and primary structure "ports only"; § 1 gives them no ports. (b) The spec asks for the primary heat transport loop and the power cycle as distinct parts; § 1 puts `cycle` and the nine fit facts on `turbine`. Physically honest (the CAS23 turbine plant is the cycle), but it is a change to the spec's list. (c) The depth map calls first wall and breeding blanket "full decomposition"; § 1 gives the blanket one child and the first wall none. (d) The paragraph justifying no support-structure sub-part and no current-leads or cryostat parts cites "spec MR-WI048-1: no structure the model does not parameterize". The spec says "flat where the source gives only a cost rate"; the parameterized-only rule is the design's own, and a stricter one. The rule is reasonable — a part with no attributes is decoration — but it must be owned as a decision, and the goal's answer (c) names "coils, winding pack, casing and support structure", so the omission needs a sentence there too. *Resolve:* add one short "Departures from the spec" list under § 1 naming (a)–(d) with the reason each, and restate the parameterized-only rule as D-something rather than as a spec quote.

**F5 — D6 lists two shapes as executed that were not, and misses a third the design relies on.** `design.md` D6: "Permitted, each executed in the prototype with identical numbers: … (`rb.r_coil_centre`, and by the same shape `magnet.B` from inside `plasma`, `cryoplant.vol_cold_cryo` from inside `magnet`)". The README's executed list has `rb.r_coil_centre` (an outer *calc* output from inside `magnet`) and `vol_cold_cryo` read by *bare name* as a plant attribute — not `cryoplant.vol_cold_cryo`, and nothing from inside `plasma`. An outer sibling *part's* attribute read from inside a nested usage is a different resolution route from an outer calc's output (plant-idiom § "Supported reference shapes": feature chain root is a part occurrence, not a calc). § 6 says this honestly; D6 does not. Also unexecuted and unnamed: the first wall's three calcs sit two levels deep (`stellaris.blanket.first_wall.wall_load_calc`) and read `plasma.p_fus` and `rb.wall_area` from there; every nested calc the prototype ran was one level deep. *Resolve:* split D6 into "executed" and "expected by the same route, to be executed in commit B", and add the depth-2 calc as risk 1's named case.

**F6 — Two consumers of qualified names the re-key list misses; the `tests/study` set is understated.** `design.md` D10; spec MR-WI048-9. `grep -rl stellaris__` excluding `.git` and `__pycache__` finds, beyond the generated package, the committed study records and the design's list: `tests/models/test_model_family_spines.py` (line 103 `P = "stellarator_09__stellaris__"`; `BLANKET_T_SOURCE = f"{P}blanket_t"` becomes `{P}blanket__blanket_t`; `CAS28_*` and `{P}rb` stay) and `exploration/stellarator_e2e/CODEGEN_FINDINGS.md` (historical text, leave it). A grep on the moving calcs' channel stems (`geom__`, `fusion__`, `magnet_cost__`, `cycle__`, …) without the `stellaris__` prefix also finds `exploration/stellarator_e2e/run_stellaris_single.py` (35 hits; its keys `calendar__…`, `total_capital__…`, `cas20_capital__…` stay on the plant, but it must be checked, not assumed) and `modeling_project/VALIDATION_MATRIX.md` (16 hits in existing SV rows — history, not to edit; the new MR-WI048-11 rows must use the new names). Eight `tests/study/test_*.py` files name keys, not seven. *Resolve:* name `test_model_family_spines.py` and `run_stellaris_single.py` in D10's list; enumerate the eight `tests/study` modules; note that VALIDATION_MATRIX history stands.
