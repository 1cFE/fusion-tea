## Evaluation contract

Five components own `purchase_cost_per_module` alongside their supplied specifications. Their existing purchase output paths stay unchanged. Turbine and heat rejection multiply the amount by module count once. Power supplies/divertor retain their existing power-core aggregation scope. Cryo retains its existing per-module amount and separate auxiliary allowance. The guarded native calculators restrict this release to one module; this makes existing mixed aggregation conventions explicit rather than asserting unsupported multi-module correctness.

Electric plant prices its independently offered gross-MWe rating with the existing dollar/MWe coefficient. WI-080 owns that field and its capability screen. Blanket, shield, structure and vessel price selected geometric volume with an independently chosen procurement duty class. Other broad budget accounts and reachable legacy facilities/cooling/fuel paths use independent selected classes. Every new class passes through the model-owned `Supplied Cost Class` identity guard before its cost consumer. Its guarded EXPOSE preserves the supplied value; it is never a physical adequacy rating.

All new amounts/classes must be finite nonnegative numbers and not Booleans. Zero selected amount is a declared zero-price assumption; it does not set or prove equipment capability. The three normative completion files are listed with exact formals in [interface-migration.json](evidence/interface-migration.json). There are no operating-power fallbacks and no price-selection modes.

O&M staffing uses a selected staffing class. Operating fuel consumption, power balance, calendar replacement frequency and exported energy remain responsive to actual operation. Replacement unit price uses the selected blanket/divertor design cost. Installation, contingency, indirect, shipping, taxes, insurance and spares continue to use the existing purchased-account subtotals and exclusions. This work does not change account scopes or add child-equipment capital to aggregate thermal package prices.
