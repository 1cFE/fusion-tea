## Registered evidence and checks

1. **Solid-metal density:** [Reliable Source Metals Material Densities](../../../../../knowledge/sources/reliable_source_metals_material_densities/raw.pdf), page 1. Image checked: C101 and C110 copper each 0.323 lb/in³; SS316 0.289 lb/in³. Multiplying by `0.45359237 / 0.0254^3` gives 8,940.6092 and 7,999.4925 kg/m³. Supplier sheet is undated and marks the figures as reference information. [AGENT] Round to 8,940 and 8,000; the paper identifies SS316 but does not identify a copper grade.

2. **Solder density and alternatives:** [Indium Corporation captured raw HTML](../../../../../knowledge/sources/indium_corporation_bar_solder_alloy_properties/raw.html), Bar Solder Products table. Native `output.md` dropped the table; raw HTML was checked directly. Sn63Pb37 specific gravity is 8.39; Sn60Pb40 is 8.50; SAC305 is 7.35. Thus plausible catalog alloy choices change density by roughly 12–14% around the assumed Sn63Pb37 value. This is an alloy-choice scenario range, not statistical uncertainty. The source establishes commercial alloy properties, not Stellaris solder identity or cryogenic performance.

3. **Solder procurement reference:** [RotoMetals captured raw HTML](../../../../../knowledge/sources/rotometals_aim_sn63pb37_one_pound_solder_bar_price/raw.html), product structured data for SKU `63Sn37PbBar1#Barextruded`, and matching main product price. Captured 2026-09-14 UTC: one-pound AIM Sn63Pb37 bar at 29.23 USD; `29.23 / 0.45359237 = 64.4411192 USD/kg`. The generic extraction omitted the price. [AGENT] Round to 65 USD/kg. A proposed 40–100 USD/kg sensitivity range is analyst judgment about purchase scale and commercial variation, not a vendor quoted range. It excludes freight, tax and magnet processing. The same price must not silently be reused for an indium-rich or silver-bearing substitute.

4. **Helium at the published reference state:** [NIST 20 K isotherm](../../../../../knowledge/sources/nist_helium_isotherm_20_k_15_to_20_bar/output.md), Fluid Data table, checked against native raw HTML. Density is 36.138 kg/m³ at 15 bar and 47.458 kg/m³ at 20 bar; both states are supercritical. With `M = 0.004002602 kg/mol`, `R = 8.31446261815324 J/(mol K)`, ideal-gas values are 36.1052 and 48.1402 kg/m³. Errors relative to the real-fluid table are approximately −0.09% and +1.44%. A fixed 47.458 kg/m³ is suitable only for the stated 20 K/20 bar inventory assumption; a varying temperature requires a state-dependent estimate.

5. **Helium temperature sensitivity:** [NIST 20 bar isobar](../../../../../knowledge/sources/nist_helium_isobar_20_bar_10_to_50_k/output.md), native table checked against raw HTML. Real-fluid densities at 10/15/20/25/30/40/50 K are 108.89/66.369/47.458/37.214/30.758/22.996/18.441 kg/m³. Ideal-gas errors are −11.58/−3.29/+1.44/+3.49/+4.34/+4.67/+4.42%. [AGENT] An ideal-gas inventory model is reasonable for this estimate near the published operating state. Error is below 5% at the sampled 15–50 K points at 20 bar, not a proven continuous-domain bound. At 10 K the larger error must be disclosed or corrected. No broad pressure-domain accuracy claim follows from these two tables.

6. **Helium price:** [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025, Helium](../../../../../knowledge/sources/usgs_mineral_commodity_summaries_2025_helium/raw.pdf), printed pages 88–89; both page images checked. The 2024 estimated Grade-A base price is about 14 USD/m³. Footnote 1 defines metric gas volume at 101.325 kPa absolute and 15°C. Footnote 7 excludes transport-related costs; producers also charge surcharges. Ideal-gas standard density is `101325 * 0.004002602 / (8.31446261815324 * 288.15) = 0.169280219 kg/m³`, giving `14 / 0.169280219 = 82.7031068 USD/kg`. [AGENT] Use 83 USD/kg as a historical base material estimate. A 83–200 USD/kg scenario range is analyst judgment for surcharges and delivery uncertainty, not a documented quote. This is not a September 2026 spot price, nor a refrigeration cost. Multiply cold-channel mass by USD/kg, never cold-channel volume directly by the standard-volume price.
