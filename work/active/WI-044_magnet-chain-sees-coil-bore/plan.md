## MR-WI044-11 restatement — the comparison meaning at the new pin (2026-09-07, written before regeneration)

**Ordering.** This section is committed with the model edits (commit A) and before any regenerated byte (commit B), the WI-041/042/043 shape.

### (a) The committed magnet columns keep their meaning, and are never edited

Every committed record since `20260901-sustainment-fence` carries `B_peak`, `sigma_wp`, `eps_cond`, `magnet_capital` (and `magnet_structure` where exported) computed by the held-ratio / held-casing chain: `B_peak = B_axis × 2.7666666666666666` and `m_casing = 63,000 kg` at every point. Those columns mean exactly that and stand at their own pins; no file under `exploration/stellarator_e2e/studies/2026*/` changes.

### (b) Where the new pin's channels differ from the committed ones

At the new pin the same channel names carry the geometry-aware chain. They equal the committed values at every point whose `R` and `a` are the design point's (`R` 12.7, `a` 1.3 — the baseline column) and differ everywhere else: `B_peak` by the factor `bore_norm = [R/(R − a_coil)] / [12.7/(12.7 − 3.15)]` (rising with `a`, falling with `R`); `sigma_wp` and `eps_cond` by the same factor; `magnet_structure` by `(W_mag / 111 GJ)^0.78` with `W_mag = 111 GJ × (I/15.4 MA)² × (a_coil/3.15)² × (12.7/R)`; `magnet_capital` (the rollup) by the structure delta only. A committed column cannot be re-read at the new chain by a multiplier in one variable: the correction depends on `R`, `a` and `I_coil` jointly, so the round's study re-executes, it does not re-read.

### (c) No verdict is added; the count sites do not move

The verdict set stays ten (`manifest.json` `baseline.verdicts` unchanged); `EXPECTED_VERDICT_COUNT`, `EXPECTED_CONSTRAINT_COUNT` and the test literals stay at ten. What changes is which points the existing `peak_field_ok`, `wp_stress_ok`, `cond_strain_ok` catch: at the new pin they read a peak field that rises with the bore, so a committed point read `satisfied` on them may read `violated` at the new pin (P1 and P2 below); the committed verdict columns are not wrong, they are the held chain's.

### (d) The entry-point delta

`magnet__m_casing` retires (the mass is computed); `magnet__m_casing_ref`, `magnet__W_mag_ref`, `magnet__I_ref`, `magnet__R_ref`, `magnet__a_coil_ref` appear; `magnet__peak_ratio` stays. Census 205 → 209 predicted (phase 4 records the actual). Four channels appear: `geom__A`, `rb__r_coil_centre`, `stored_energy__W_mag`, `casing_mass__m_casing`. No held key of any committed study is touched (no `axes.json` names `m_casing` or `peak_ratio`).

### (e) What the round's study does

Re-executes the `20260905-stored-energy-basis` arms at the new pin, joins by case id to the committed `20260907-burn-control` record, and reports every verdict flip and every cost move with the three new channels beside `a` (`goal.md` § Answered when (b)).
