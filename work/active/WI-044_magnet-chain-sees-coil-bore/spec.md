## Why this item exists

Every committed design-search optimum sits at the top of the minor-radius window (`a` 2.2 m at both heating levels), and the ten-verdict cheapest machine `c2823` (202.19 $/MWh; R 15.7, a 2.2) has aspect ratio 7.1 against the source's 9.8. Nothing in the executed package pushes back on a fatter plasma: at the WI-043 pin the grounding probe read ten magnet channels bit-constant in `a` on every column — `heat_coupled`, `B_axis`, `B_peak`, `sigma_wp`, `eps_cond`, `vol_cold`, `p_cryo`, cryo cost, magnet capital and heating capital (`work/orchestration/goals/minor-radius/evidence/grounding_probe/summary.md` Probe A, 48 points) — while the plasma volume rose 2.86×, fusion power 1.65× and LCOE fell to 0.72× from a 1.3 to 2.2 on the cheapest machine's column. The one magnet channel that moves, the 1cfe-form shadow `magnet_capital_1cfe_form`, is exactly linear in `a` and does not enter `total_capital`.

The grounding established what the sources do about this (`goal.md` § Question facts 1–4; `evidence/grounding_sources/report.md` § Q1, Q2, Q5):

- The sister systems codes (Stellarator-PROCESS, Lion 2021 and the 2023 thesis) hold the aspect ratio as a fixed configuration input and iterate R; the minor radius is derived. No published run sweeps `a` at fixed R. The owner prefers the model freely settable, so the aspect ratio is not held here (option 1 not chosen).
- The sourced bound on `a` is the coil–plasma distance inside a fixed coil set (eq. 57–59), which needs the unprinted `f_geo`. This model builds the coil bore outward from `a` (`r_coil = vessel_or = a + 1.70 m`), so the stack always fits. What the codes then carry, and this model does not, is the consequence: a bigger coil.
- The sources give three shapes through which a bigger coil bore costs something, and the Stellaris design point anchors each: (i) the peak field on the conductor rises as the bore approaches the major radius — Lion 2021 eq. 39 (image `knowledge/sources/a_general_stellarator_version_of_the_systems_code_process/images/lion_2021_nf_stellarator_process.pdf-0009-19.png`, read this session): `B_max(A_wp) = μ0 I N / (R − a_coil) × (a0(C) + R a1(C) / √A_wp)`; anchor 24.9 T at 9.0 T axis (Table 2 render `evidence/grounding_sources/stellaris_p03_table2.png`, read this session). (ii) The coil-set inductance scales with the bore squared over R — thesis eq. 2.82 (image `…/lion_2023_phd_thesis_stellarator_systems_code_models.pdf-0074-12.png`, read this session): `L = L(C) (a_coil² / â_coil²)(R̂ / R)`, with `W_mag = ½ L I²` (eq. 2.81 on the same page gives the per-coil stored energy `E_stoTF = L I² / (2 N_TF)`); anchor: stored magnetic energy 111 GJ at 15.4 MA, R 12.7 (Table 2). (iii) The coil structure mass follows the stored energy — Lion 2021 eq. 56 (image `…pdf-0012-18.png`, read this session): `M_struct = 1.348 W_mag^0.78`, "an empirical scaling law from existing machines" (`output.md` L607); the source prints no units for the constant. Anchor: the casing floor the model already carries, 63 t per coil (WI-035 D5, a knowing lower bound with the seam named).
- The configuration coefficients `a0(C)`, `a1(C)`, `L(C)` are not printed for Stellaris. Each form is therefore anchored at the design point and scaled by the sourced shape — the WI-041 wall-peak calibration's pattern — with the model text saying so.

How hard the shapes push is bracketed, not known (`goal.md` fact 4): on the design column the eq.-39 factor `R / (R − a_coil)` rises about 10 % from a 1.3 to 2.2, enough to break the conductor ceiling at every `a` above 1.3 with the baseline at equality; at `c2823`'s R 15.7 it rises about 1–2 %. Stored energy rises about 1.65–1.69× and the casing mass about 1.48–1.51× over the same bore change at fixed current and R (the range is the bore-radius choice, open decision 1). Whether `a` 2.2 still wins is what the round's study measures; this item makes the package able to answer.

## What must be true afterward (requirements)

#### MR-WI044-1: The conductor peak field is computed from coil geometry in eq. 39's shape, anchored at the design point
The library SHALL compute the peak field on the conductor from the axis field, the major radius and the coil-bore radius in the shape of Lion 2021 eq. 39 — `B_peak = B_axis × k_peak × R / (R − a_coil)`, with `k_peak` a held residual bound per instance so that the design geometry reproduces the printed 24.9 T at 9.0 T axis — and the held `peak_ratio` SHALL retire as a direct multiplier (it becomes the residual's basis, or is retired outright — the design decides and says why). #### MR-WI044-2: The stored magnetic energy is computed from the coil-set inductance shape, anchored at the printed 111 GJ
The library SHALL compute the coil-set stored magnetic energy as `W_mag = W_mag_ref × (I_coil / I_ref)² × (a_coil / a_coil_ref)² × (R_ref / R)` — thesis eq. 2.82 for the inductance with `W_mag = ½ L I²` — with the reference quantities bound per instance (Stellaris: 111 GJ at 15.4 MA, R 12.7, the design geometry's bore), reproducing 111 GJ at the design point.
