For clear rectangle L,W,H and exterior wall thickness t, external footprint is `(L+2t)*(W+2t)`. Exterior wall concrete volume is `((L+2t)*(W+2t)-L*W)*H`, less physically represented door/link apertures times t. For a three-strip wing let W_clear be the sum of the three usable strip widths, W_inner=W_clear+2*t for two occupied partitions, and W_external=W_clear+4*t. Use W_inner in the exterior wall-ring subtraction, then add the two partition solids `2*t*L*H` exactly once; their ends stop at the exterior walls' inner faces. Transfer links have two side walls, floor and roof, and no end walls. Include wall and partition openings only where routes actually require them; active doors require shielding/containment equipment whose price remains unestablished.

Formwork is net contact area. For an exterior wall ring use `(P_external+P_internal)*H - 2*sum(opening_area)`, then add each ground-level doorway reveal `t*(2*door_height+door_width)` for its two jambs and lintel. Partition faces use `2*partition_length*H - 2*sum(partition_opening_area)` plus the corresponding reveals; partition ends joined to exterior walls have no exposed contact credit. Roof-underside formwork equals clear ceiling area between walls/partitions, not external roof footprint. Add formed floor/basemat and roof perimeter edges `P_external*(floor_thickness+roof_thickness)`. No bottom formwork is charged beneath ground-supported basemats. For the two full-length wing partitions remove `4*t*H` from the exterior-ring inner-face formwork at their four wall attachments; partition end faces have already been excluded. All slab perimeter edges are assumed formed during separate construction, including later connection interfaces, rather than cast against earth; this construction-sequence assumption is explicit. Temporary shoring, slab-top finish and supporting frames remain unitemized. Rebar mass equals the chosen intensity times concrete volume. Keep substructure floor/basemat quantities separate from superstructure wall/roof/partition quantities. This is a constant-intensity takeoff, not a structural reinforcement design.
