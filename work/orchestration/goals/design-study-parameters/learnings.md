# Learnings: Operating choices in the extended stellarator assembly — cycle flow and pressure ratio

Append-only, newest last. An entry is appended only after a round review accepts or corrects the delta the round result proposed (`work/orchestration/GOAL_RUNBOOK.md` § The fresh review). Each entry is one claim with its evidence, scope, implication, supersession and acceptance.

## Round 1 — accepted 2026-09-26 (fresh round-1 review FINDINGS, corrections applied)

- L-001 [AGENT] On the costed C-1 assembly the passing region of the flow × ratio grid is bounded below by heat removal at the exchanger (the recuperated heater inlet against the 773 K source through 50 MW/K) and above by the compressor rating, then by nonpositive net; where the boundary lies inside the grid (2,250–3,000 kg/s) the ridge of net lies one grid step on the failing side, so the best passing point (2,250 / 1.5183 or 2,750 / 1.375, about 595 MW) is 22 MW below the electricity-only best (2,500 / 1.425, 7.8 MW unmet). Evidence: `exploration/costed_loop_brayton/studies/20260926-design-study-parameters/record.md` § 6, `results/readout.md` § 2–3; finding #1.
- L-002 [AGENT] Within one priced inventory the LCOE ranking of operating points is the net-electricity ranking; the tritium purchase carries most of the paired delta (−408 of −446 USD/MWh at the best point) and the plant side the rest (−38). Between inventories only the booked purchases differ; the ARIES-selected inventory prices the same points 8–11 USD/MWh lower and supports none of them (helium-duty package 1,500 MW against a constant 3,301 MW duty). Finding #5; contract § 6 confirmed on the 200 grid points (the sensitivities S4–S6 vary numerator terms by design).
- L-003 [AGENT] The exchanger area is the priced hardware lever: 75,000 m² (+29 MUSD) moves the boundary at 2,500 kg/s from 1.45 to 1.40 and buys +74 MW of passing net for +0.7 USD/MWh at unchanged net. Finding #2; a modeling item would be needed to make the area a chosen design variable with its own screen.
- L-004 [AGENT] The fixed-efficiency treatment's first-order effect is one grid step of boundary (compressor 0.85 or turbine 0.90 fails 2,250 / 1.5183; 2,500 / 1.45 passes under every level), and the loop pump law's ×2 fails every boundary anchor through the friction heat it delivers while leaving the slack-rich starting point passing. Findings #3, #4; the operating claim is scoped near the design flow.
- L-005 [AGENT] The verifier's relative rule cannot pass a near-zero difference channel (a 0.363 K hot-bound margin at 1.5e-9 relative, 5e-10 K absolute), and the manifest transcription from the contract carried neither the precedent package's `unmet` classes nor any class for the margins. Whether such channels get declared absolute classes before execution is the owner's ruling under G-001; if ruled so, the rule is that a manifest carries every class of the precedent package on the same bodies plus one for every near-zero difference channel. Finding #6.
- L-006 [AGENT] A sensitivity anchor rule of the form "best point plus its grid neighbours" can collapse onto the starting point on a refined grid; declaring an extra anchor before execution and retaining the first preparation keeps the record honest. Finding #7.
- L-007 [AGENT] The starting configuration's auxiliary register sums to 65 MW electric (40 + 10 + 5 + 5 + 5), not the 60 MW written in `evidence/starting-configuration.md` § 4; the stored `auxiliary_electric` 240.281 = 175.281 + 65.0 confirms it. Recorded in the T-005 return; the evidence file is left as written.

## Round 2 — accepted 2026-09-26 (fresh round-2 review FINDINGS, corrections applied)

- L-008 [AGENT] The 'Primary Coolant Loop' definition states a required return condition (the IHX must deliver `T_comp_in` so that the held blanket inlet holds, `models/library/analyses/mfe_primary_loop.sysml` doc) that no assembly checks. In the C-1 family the residual `T_comp_in − he_return` equals the stored helium hot-bound margin wherever all heat is removed, so the exactly consistent circulating loops are the heat-removal boundary points; a design point with a large margin (48.8 K at the C-1 design point, 0.70–13.6 K at the study's leading passing points, 0.70–125.4 K with median 67.0 K over the 52 passing I-R grid points) is a loop whose blanket-inlet control (a bypass fraction) is unmodeled. A return-condition screen with an owner-declared tolerance, or an explicit bypass fraction, is the missing piece. Evidence: `evidence/return-condition-check.{py,json,md}`; answer § 4.
- L-009 [OWNER] Report electricity and the nonfuel cost contribution first; the tritium term is an assumed price spread over more electricity and must not carry the story; "X MW for Y USD/MWh" names the added capital contribution, not the total cost of electricity (`evidence/owner-ruling-g001.md` item 3, applied in the answer § 2, § 3, § 6 and the passage).
- L-010 [OWNER] A tolerance class ruled after execution is a verification tolerance on calculated margins only, never permission to accept a physical constraint violation; the manifest basis carries that wording and the verdicts stay exact (`evidence/owner-ruling-g001.md` item 1; `exploration/costed_loop_brayton/studies/manifest.json`).

## Round 3 — accepted 2026-09-26 (fresh round-3 review FINDINGS, corrections applied)

- L-011 [AGENT] The C-1 design point is over-provisioned on its exchanger (3,836.5 MW of capability against 3,301.2 MW of duty) and satisfies the loop's return requirement only with 31.1 % of the primary flow bypassed around the exchanger; under consistent operating settings without a bypass it is not an operating point. Evidence: `exploration/costed_loop_brayton/studies/20260926-design-study-parameters-b/results/readout.json` and `record.md` § 15; finding `-b#1`.
- L-012 [AGENT] With the return requirement enforced, the consistent operating points on the existing inventory are the matched-exchanger points (all heat removed, no bypass), one ratio per flow, falling from 1.651 at 2,000 kg/s to 1.244 at 3,500; along that locus the net peaks at the design flow (620.8 MW at 2,500 / 1.4273, 617.9 at 2,750 / 1.3629) and spans 85.6 MW across the window; round 1's best band was a grid-resolution artifact 23–26 MW below the matched point at the design flow. Findings `-b#2`, `-b#3`.
- L-013 [AGENT] A mixing estimate of a bypass (residual / (T_out − return)) under-predicts the control setting (18.8 % against 31.1 % at the design point) because reduced primary flow lowers the exchanger's effectiveness; only a control solved on the exchanger's own performance is a valid statement of the setting. Finding `-b#4`.
- L-014 [AGENT, from the OWNER direction of 2026-09-26] Documenting a missing physical requirement does not establish that reported points satisfy it: a requirement stated in a definition must be enforced in the calculation and checks (without a tolerance chosen to admit an observed residual), the starting configuration and the leading alternatives re-evaluated near the relevant boundary, and the comparison recomputed on cases that satisfy the completed model; numerical verification certifies agreement with the implemented equations, not the completeness of the relationships (`evidence/owner-direction-round3.md`).
- L-015 [AGENT] A control that must exist for a stated requirement can be modeled additively without changing any existing channel when the controlled side sees only the transferred duty; the pre-change bit-exact replay is then the control that the model increment changed nothing it should not have (WI-095: 245 channels bit-exact on every receipt). Evidence: `work/active/WI-095_loop-return-control/evidence/return-control-verification.json`.
