## Question

For the C-1 assembly (the Stellaris helium primary loop delivering its supplied blanket heat to the ARIES three-stage intercooled recuperated Brayton chain) with a fixed, explicitly priced equipment inventory, how should the cycle mass flow and the compressor stage pressure ratio be chosen together to raise net electricity and lower conditional LCOE, and which check (heat removal, a selected equipment rating, net power, or unsupported applicability) stops the improvement?
