## Question

> Does the magnet's winding length, cold load and structure follow the coil the model builds — the bore the radial build sets, the leads and shield a 20 K coil set needs, the whole support structure the source's mass shape describes — and once they do, does the machine the model favours change: what does a fatter plasma now cost at the magnet, where does the cheapest feasible machine sit, and how much of the plant's recirculating power is the cryoplant?

## What the model does today, and why it matters

`[INHERITED]` The magnet account is the largest single cost channel: $1,624.8M of the $4.53B CAS22 at the entering pin, of which winding procurement is $1,570.4M (tape $804.0M, winding fabrication $750.4M, material inventory $16.0M) and casing structure $54.4M (`evidence/grounding_probe/summary.md` § Baseline). Three of its chains do not follow the coil the model builds:

1. **The winding length is the major radius times a held factor.** `c_coil = k_coil × R0` (`models/library/analyses/mfe_magnet_field.sysml@173ac157` 'Coil Winding Length'; `k_coil = 25.0 / 12.7` in `models/designs/stellarator_09/stellarator_plant.sysml@48417c9e`; oracle `exploration/stellarator_e2e/verify_stellaris.py@0e3bf944` L695), from WI-036 D3 (`work/completed/20260903_WI-036_winding-pack-sizing/design.md@f937be2c`). The winding length feeds the cold volume, the ampere-metres and the procurement (`verify_stellaris.py` L703–711). So along `a` at the design radius the winding procurement is bit-constant ($1,570,369,801.03 at every `a` from 1.3 to 2.2) while fusion power rises 24 % and the price falls 16 %; along `R` the procurement scales exactly with `R` (ratio 1.236 for 15.7/12.7) (`evidence/grounding_probe/summary.md` Transects A, B). The corpus puts the coil's size on the bore: "The coils have an approximate size of 7 × 5 × 10 m, with a typical circumference of 25 m" (Stellaris § 2.9, `knowledge/concept_research/09-qi-stellarator-hts/iter-01/sources/stellaris-design-details.md@e5a2cb23` L1862); the model's coil-centre radius at the design point is 3.15 m, a 6.3 m loop. The `R`-form and a bore-form agree exactly under uniform scaling (the case Lion 2021's scaled reference configurations describe) and disagree exactly when the aspect ratio changes, which every committed study does. The minor-radius goal made the peak field, the stored energy and the casing mass see the bore (WI-044, `work/completed/20260908_WI-044_magnet-chain-sees-coil-bore/design.md@0b5de534`) and left the length as it was; its L-002 read that on the cheap columns "the minor radius is closed by the plasma chain, not the magnets" (`work/orchestration/goals/minor-radius/learnings.md@cc44c041`) — at a magnet account that could not see `a` in 97 % of its cost.

2. **The cryoplant sees two of its heat loads.** 'Cryoplant Electrical Power' (`models/library/analyses/mfe_cryo_plant.sysml@3d9711e2`) sums nuclear heating at the winding pack (35.5 W/m³ × 136.56 m³ = 4.85 kW) and the printed joint dissipation (7.5 kW), at 20 K through a 0.20-of-Carnot plant: 12.3 kW cold, 0.864 MW electric, $16.7M of cryoplant capital. Current leads (96 at 50 kA per the § 2.9 turn current and 48 coils), thermal-shield radiation into the cryostat and support conduction are absent; `f_uplift_cryo = 1.0` is a declared lower bound — "casing, coil-case, and 20 K support-structure nuclear heating … 'will be examined in future studies'; current-lead / radiation loads are unprinted. No admissible uplift value exists; the parameter names the seam" (`stellarator_plant.sysml@48417c9e` the `f_uplift_cryo` binding, WI-024 D6, `work/completed/20260718_WI-024_recirc-power-derivation/design.md@72f7d054`). A separate held `p_tfcool = 15 MW` "coil cooling power" carries the 1costingFE default with its relation to the cryoplant chain unresolved. The cryo-volume-basis goal ruled the cold volume held (`[OWNER 2026-08-27]`) with the reversal condition "reopen … if a future study's verdicts turn on cryoplant load"; that goal's arm B — computing the cold volume — has since landed through WI-036; this goal does not touch the cold volume.

3. **The structure shape and its anchor describe different things.** 'Magnet Casing Mass' scales Lion 2021 eq. 56 — that paper's **total** support-structure mass ("we choose to model only the total structure mass, while not being sensitive to the details of support structure", `knowledge/sources/a_general_stellarator_version_of_the_systems_code_process/output.md@d4059ef1` L607) — from an anchor that is the **casing cast-part floor**, 63 t per coil (`m_casing_ref`, the WI-035 D5 seam, `work/completed/20260901_WI-035_magnet-closure/design.md@384e380e`). 'Magnet Structure Cost' (`models/library/analyses/mfe_magnet_cost.sysml@4ca1f299`) prices that mass at the 1costingFE $6/kg × 3.0 fabrication markup and states that "inter-coil plates, support rings, and legs remain CAS22.1.5" — where no such mass is sized. The magnet-design-transfer goal closed with "the inherited steel price has an ambiguous fabrication basis" and "pack/casing fit" among the places transfer stops (`work/orchestration/goals/magnet-design-transfer/transfer-claim.md@1792edf6` § Where transfer stops; `evidence/transfer-evidence-assessment.md@d249daaf`).
