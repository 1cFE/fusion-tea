## Same-point and control-volume contract

Stellaris Table 5 Point A reports fusion 2700 MW, operating auxiliary 0 MW, W 504.65 MJ and tau 1.46 s. Its caption identifies Point A as the default for subsequent studies, but subsequent wall/divertor calculations introduce distinct conservative scenarios. Appendix A explicitly writes `P_rad + W/tau = f_alpha P_alpha + P_aux`. Thus the additive loss structure is compatible with the model; the exact Table 5 implementation, energy species boundary, radiation components and averaging definitions are not fully specified.

## Decision and missing capability

No production numeric correction is justified by the inspected evidence. The implemented action is an explicit source-to-model classification, original source registration, finite verified attribution and corrected interpretation of the source's synchrotron units. Historical forward equations, signed predicates and alternative assumptions remain intact. Independent review supports this restraint; a plausible alternative radiation result is insufficient to establish the paper's hidden implementation.

A verified source ignition reproduction needs the unrounded Point-A implementation vector, thermal/fast-energy split and integration volume, the exact ISS04 implementation and averages, and independently integrated Aurora/ADAS and synchrotron powers with their atomic-data, profile and reflection settings. Reimplementing a source-specific profile/atomic-radiation solver without these inputs would not resolve that missing evidence. Such capability is essential for a stronger independent reproduction claim, but not essential to the owner's permitted quantified-unresolved outcome.
