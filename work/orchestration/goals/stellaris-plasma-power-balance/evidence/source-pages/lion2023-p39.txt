It should be noted that the imposed profile shapes are not per se consistent with
the implied heating schemes or transport properties. However, in practice, the profile
shapes can be determined by transport simulations independent of the systems code.
Results from such simulations can then be used as input for Process, e.g. in profile
shapes or heating source.
