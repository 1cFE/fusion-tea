In this section, we apply the model described in the pre-
vious section to study the possible parameter space of
future reactor-grade tokamaks allowing high toroidal ﬁeld
and neglecting, for the moment, the present technological
limitations to the increase of the ﬁeld. The study will
analyse different routes from present day experiments to

Fusion power Pfus: even though high ﬁeld may allow

Hence, we explore Pfus up to 3.5 GW, aiming at around
1 GW of electrical power at conventional efﬁciency.
