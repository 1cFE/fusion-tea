## Finding

A material blanket-definition and applicability decision remains. The acquired evidence supports genuine calculated breeding, including a recoverable HCLL neural-network surrogate. It does not establish that the current helium-primary, generic-build stellarator lies within that surrogate's physical or numerical domain. The simplest defensible next step is to reproduce the published HCLL surrogate in its own domain while preparing an explicit current-assembly mapping. A new transport solver is not a prerequisite to that reproduction.

## Candidate methods

- **Source Stellaris:** Original PDF pp. 17–19 confirms water-cooled PbLi, a separate helium first-wall loop, 73.5/12.5/14 volume-percent PbLi/water/EUROFER97, and 70% Li-6 enrichment. Figure 34 visually confirms 3 cm first wall and 43.1 cm mean breeder. The reported 1.1070 ± 0.0002 becomes 1.074 after the 3% port allowance. These are results of a particular 3D transport assembly. Replacing water with helium changes neutron moderation; the shielding exponential is not a breeding equation. Source: original at `knowledge/concept_research/09-qi-stellarator-hts/iter-02/sources/publikationen-1000179851-172386752/tmpissrtbos/raw.pdf`.
- **PROCESS:** The assessed correlation concerns solid ceramic breeders. It cannot establish PbLi breeding without new applicability evidence. A technology change would be a scenario decision, not an implementation convenience (`internal-source-assessment.md`, PROCESS §3.6 finding).
- **Lyytinen2024:** Original PDF pp. 5, 7–8 distinguishes the simple 80 cm LiPb cross-code benchmark from the later DCLL thickness scan. Figure 6 visually confirms five DCLL points, approximately 0.81–1.32 across 25–75 cm, and explicitly reproduces reference [8]. These are useful computational benchmarks, not two independent datasets or an experimental validation. Current 80 cm breeding thickness exceeds the plotted domain; wall and surrounding layers also differ. Register: `knowledge/sources/proof_of_principle_of_parametric_stellarator_neutronics/`; inspected original `/tmp/breeding-serpent2.pdf`.
- **Shimwell2019:** Original PDF pp. 8–9 and Figure 6 describe a coupled DEMO HCLL geometry/enrichment scan, not a universal enrichment law. Reference TBR is 1.235 at 90% enrichment and 34.5 mm PbLi poloidal height; optimum is 1.278 ± 0.010, a reported 5σ surrogate confidence interval, not total predictive uncertainty. Printed Eq. 2 is internally inconsistent: `Ps/(1.1 SmD)` with 10 MPa and 274 MPa gives 0.03318, whereas its numerical right side prints 3.332. Resolve against author code or cited engineering derivation before using either form. Eq. 1 gives 10.96–21.46 mm walls over the sampled 10–120 mm heights, excluding the current 50 mm wall. Register: `knowledge/sources/multiphysics_analysis_with_cad_based_parametric_breeding/`; original `/tmp/breeding-shimwell2019.pdf`.
- **Martinez2012:** Original PDF pp. 135–137 contains an executable 26-input, nine-hidden-neuron TBR network with normalization and weights. This removes the presumed missing-executable obstacle. However, its input domain includes first wall 2–4 cm, inboard breeder 30–60 cm, outboard breeder 50–90 cm and elongation 1.8–2.2. Current 5 cm wall and uniform 80 cm breeder cannot be silently mapped into these intervals. Original p. 61, Table 4-4, visually verified, reports RZ-versus-3D DEMO TBR deviations −1.29% to +1.42% over ten cases. This supports that reduction on those cases; neither those extrema nor surrogate test error bounds stellarator transfer. Register: `knowledge/sources/neutronic_fusion_thesis_martinez_arroyo_javier/`; original `/tmp/breeding-hcll-thesis.pdf`.

## Fuel threshold

The current equation correctly expresses a conditional atom balance: `T_req = [B + (1-r)(B/f-B) + λI + G]/(ηB)`. Independently checked against `models/library/analyses/mfe_fuel_cycle.sysml:80` and instance assumptions at `models/designs/stellarator_09/stellarator_plant.sysml:1349`: with f=0.05, r=0.99, η=1 and I=G=0, required TBR is 1.19. A value of 1.074 then has margin −0.116, while passing the 1.05 floor by 0.024. Required recovery is 0.996105 for 1.074, or 0.997368 for 1.05, under those same assumptions.

These are conditional sensitivities. The 0.99 input is inherited cost semantics, not isotope-recovery evidence. The 1.05 floor is not a substitute for the balance. Neither changing recovery to erase the deficit nor converting this conditional deficit into demonstrated physical failure is justified.

`threshold-check.py` and its JSON correctly implement this normalized balance. All five reported requirements and margins agree with the equation, including floating-point residue at zero margin. The explicit unity extraction, zero decay/growth and assumed recovery labels support PASS as an arithmetic diagnostic only; this is neither a plant study nor isotope-recovery validation.
