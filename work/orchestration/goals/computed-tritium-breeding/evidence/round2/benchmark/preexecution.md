# Benchmark reconstruction freeze

[AGENT] 2026-09-18, recorded before transport results. Reconstruct the unreflected OKTAVIAN natural-Li and Pb-plus-Li spheres as concentric spherical shells. Source proceedings printed pp. 87–90 specify natural lithium, 60 cm outer radius, 10 cm central void, and either Li from 10–60 cm or Pb from 10–20 cm and Li from 20–60 cm. Figure 1 shows beam/access penetrations and text names stainless-steel containment, without sufficient numerical details to reconstruct them exactly. These are approximate integral benchmarks, not exact experimental replicas.

[INHERITED: IAEA 1994 compilation, Table 1.1] Experimental comparison targets are Li 0.685 with 5.6% relative standard deviation and PbLi 0.530 with 6.0%. The earlier proceedings printed p.88 gives preliminary 0.73 and 0.62; these are retained as superseded context, never interchangeable targets. Report C/E and error divided by the root sum square of experimental standard deviation and Monte Carlo error. Values within two such standard deviations are consistent with this approximate test; reconstruction uncertainty remains separate and is not silently added to manufacture agreement.

[AGENT] Baseline assumed densities: elemental Li 0.534 g/cm³ and elemental Pb 11.34 g/cm³; natural Li-6 atom fraction 0.0759 and Pb atom fractions 0.014/0.241/0.221/0.524 for masses 204/206/207/208. These are declared conventional room-temperature reconstruction values, not established assembly cards. Material temperature 294 K. Isotropic point source at origin, monoenergetic 14.1 MeV, open vacuum exterior. Tritium production tally `(n,Xt)` for Li6 and Li7 plus total in the lithium zone, per emitted neutron. Use runtime ENDF/B-VIII.0 processed data unchanged.

[AGENT] Predeclared sensitivities, each on both assemblies: source 14.0 and 14.8 MeV; Li density 0.50 and 0.56 g/cm³; Li6 fraction 0.074 and 0.080; and a 0.2 cm stainless-equivalent inner/outer casing occupying lithium material at its two boundaries (density 8 g/cm³, elemental mass fractions Fe/Cr/Ni = 0.70/0.19/0.11). Casing is an illustrative omission test, not a sourced vessel reconstruction. Baseline repeated with independent seed. Each run uses 40 batches × 10,000 source histories. Preserve all cases and compare their spread to experimental errors; do not choose the best match. No graphite case is attempted without scattering-treatment validation. Angular anisotropy is irrelevant for a complete concentric sphere with a central source, but it matters together with unresolved beam openings. The omission is unquantified here.

[AGENT] Acceptance scope: test the transport/tally chain against an external integral Li and Pb-multiplier experiment; flag failed comparisons and inadequate reconstruction. No result certifies stellarator geometry, the plant's enrichment/material mixing, or total physical uncertainty.
