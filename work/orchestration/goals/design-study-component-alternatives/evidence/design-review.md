### Blocking finding: coupled physical solves remain outside the model

[STUDY_POLICY.md](../../../../../modeling_project/STUDY_POLICY.md) is owner-ratified. Section 5.3 bars “Outer solve loops in harness or study scripts” and requires “Solves live in the model (R2/R3) or nowhere.” Sections 3 and 5.1 require internalizing physically coupled quantities instead of retaining equality constraints over swept inputs. Section 11 preserves those scientific rules despite process simplification.

Design §3 locates public source heat by repeatedly calling the source/loop/match oracle until exchanger duty equals the loop's delivered duty. Section 5 similarly locates pressure ratio until the Brayton source capability exactly matches delivered duty and required return. These roots supply required steady physical consistency at the selected hardware and flow. They are not merely choosing the best objective among independently complete, physically closed points. Calling them operating-selection policies, retaining public inputs and replaying their results satisfies the MR-7 separation concern but does not satisfy the study-policy rule about where the physical solve lives. A source-only oracle subset avoids downstream exceptions; it does not change that classification.

The predecessor [WI-095 review](../../design-study-parameters/evidence/design-review-r3.md), Q4, explicitly accepted a ratio search as an MR-7-compliant study policy. It is an agent review, not an owner exception to STUDY_POLICY. Its model also contained the internal primary-bypass solve that maintained return before the study selected the zero-bypass family. That precedent does not establish permission to leave WI-096's required source/return closures outside the model. No inspected owner instruction waives the policy here.

The smallest correction in principle is to put the required matched operating closure inside reviewed model calculation boundaries, reusing the existing pure component bodies without copying their equations unnecessarily. Expose solved source heat/ratio, residuals, numerical failure status and unchanged installed choices. Record the resulting role changes explicitly; MR-7 permits a declared matched-analysis direction and does not require these two quantities to remain independently swept inputs. The study then varies the actual independent equipment/flow choices. This requires a revised design and review, not an implementation-time adjustment.

### Section 4 tripwire is relevant

The proposed remedy names a source-match solve and a Brayton-ratio solve in addition to the finite-water-cooler solve. Implemented as three separate new handwritten coupled-solve calculations in this round, it reaches §4's “third-or-more handwritten rung in one round” tripwire. Record that observed demand before proceeding; do not silently add those calculations. This counts numerical closures, not every copied Python body or arithmetic ledger. It is a workflow/toolchain tripwire, **not evidence that major new physical theory is required**. Whether a scientifically coherent merged calculation boundary is appropriate needs design consideration and independent review; moving boundaries solely to bypass the tripwire would not resolve it.
