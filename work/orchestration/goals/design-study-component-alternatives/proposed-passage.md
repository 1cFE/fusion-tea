# Proposed passage — partial result

The component comparison started with the same helium source and connected it to the selected steam offer and a catalog of Brayton offers. Source heat, pressure ratios and purchased equipment remained design choices; the model calculated bypass operation, recuperator behavior, cooling-water flow and pumping loads. The connecting equipment mattered: in the native results, selecting steam exchanger circuits for the lower source duties substantially reduced its cost per net MWh compared with holding the largest connecting assembly. But the study also exposed a numerical limit. Six cases failed the independent accuracy requirement, including two efficiency sensitivities, because the cooler's local convergence criterion did not ensure accurate downstream flow and power. Those cases were retained and the economic comparison stopped at verification. The exercise demonstrates a conditional component-comparison capability and a specific remaining numerical requirement; it does not establish a preferred technology or whole-plant LCOE.
