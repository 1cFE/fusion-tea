# Proposed passage — partial comparison result

[AGENT] A direct comparison of the existing steam and helium Brayton plants would mix differences in heat sources, auxiliaries and costs. The component audit instead identified an isolated helium boundary and three candidate heat duties, about 2.82–3.21 GW, that agree with the existing primary-loop and steam-exchanger equations under an imposed loop-resistance assumption. It also exposed the work needed for a fair comparison: sufficient salt pumps, finite-capacity Brayton cooling, explicit equipment prices and consistent loss accounting. The matched assemblies have not yet been implemented. Final review stopped the proposed study-script closure solves because the project requires those physical relationships to live in the model. The evidence therefore establishes comparison readiness and specific remaining dependencies, without supporting a performance or economic preference between steam and Brayton.
