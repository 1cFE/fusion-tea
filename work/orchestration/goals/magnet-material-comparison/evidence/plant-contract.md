## 1. What is compared, and what the answer looks like

One plant model, the Stellaris plant (`exploration/stellarator_e2e`, isolated derived package per § 9), evaluates supplied plant designs for each conductor material. Two axes of **explicit assumptions** span the map: confinement (§ 3.1) and coil geometry (§ 3.2). In each assumption cell a bounded search (§ 5) proposes supplied designs per material; the plant evaluates each as supplied (MR-7); the **best supported design** per material is the lowest-LCOE design with status `supported` (§ 7). The answer per cell: which material's best supported design has the lower LCOE, by how much, decomposed by account (§ 8); the REBCO tape price at which the cell's preference changes; and the evidence labels of the two best designs and of the policy assumptions their LCOE rests on (§ 3, § 8). Failed, unsupported, ignited and capacity-limited designs are retained and reported under those names, never as "expensive".

**Two comparisons connect to Round 1 (F3).** (i) *Equal duty*: in every cell the REBCO grid includes designs at the same supplied ampere-turns as each Nb₃Sn design (peak 10, 11, 12 T under the cell's geometry), each material with its own supplied pack; the LCOE difference of an equal-duty pair is the magnet, cryo and structure accounts only, and is the plant-level counterpart of Round 1's anchor-S pairs. (ii) *Own highest field on the reference coil set*: REBCO at the Stellaris reference duty (24.9 T peak) against Nb₃Sn at 12 T on the same coil set, both in the anchored × 1.0 cell. Both are reported before the map, under those names.

## 2. Plant model, what it computes, what it does not

Chain (audit § 2): ampere-turns and R set the axis field (`B_axis = μ0 k_link N I/(2π R)`); the peak field is `B_axis × peak_ratio × bore factor`, where the bore factor `[R/(R − a_coil)]` normalized to the anchor responds to the radial allocation `coil_t`; the field reaches the plasma through the ISS04 confinement time (required heating, ash) and beta; fusion power reads density, temperature and volume; heating draw, cryogenics and coil drive enter net electricity; magnet capital follows the supplied winding; the radial-build accounts follow the shell volumes and supplied power classes; conversion, heat rejection, power supplies, divertor and cryo are supplied packages with ratings and purchase costs; buildings follow the layout; LCOE is the plant's DCF over overnight capital, O&M, replacements and net energy.

Held facts the map does not move (all Stellaris-anchored; audit § 2): `k_link` 0.7731 (variant in the HELIAS-class cells, § 3.2), `n_coils` 48, the profile exponents, `Ti/Te`, the ash and impurity factors, the wall-load calibration, the divertor shadow, the achieved TBR, the DCF finance parameters, the winding-operations rate, the stored-energy shape and `W_mag_ref` (`mfe_magnet_field.sysml:361-362`; N8), the plant's unit costs and class exponents. **Stated limit:** every held fact is anchored at one point; away from it the model's outputs are the anchored shape's prediction (D8). The evidence labels (§ 3) carry this into the map.

Not computed, and not claimed (audit D2, D7): a casing mass or transverse-cavity cost response; equipment response to field or stored energy beyond what the supplied-design policy (§ 5) supplies with a stated basis; structural adequacy; a fully qualified magnet or plasma.

## 3. The assumption axes and their evidence labels

Labels: **[S]** directly supported at the Stellaris design point; **[A]** sourced analogue, a printed value for another configuration (HELIAS line, ITER/DEMO), not this coil set; **[D]** derived from sourced values with stated arithmetic and caveats; **[U]** assumption with no source at this field, pack or geometry.

**Labels are per design, not per cell (F4).** Each assumption value has a label that depends on where a design sits (field, pack area, size); the map shows, per cell, the label of each material's best supported design, the cell's comparison label as the weaker of the two, and a third column with the policy assumptions the LCOE rests on (§ 5: uniform `f_ren`, structure-mass scaling, heating and package re-supply rules, field-blind equipment D7), so no cell can read as fully supported.

The exact Ampère floor `B_peak ≥ μ0 I_coil/(4√A_wp)` (square pack, coil current; independently checked, check-field-relations.md § Relation 1: exact for any coil shape, the other coils cannot lower the mean tangential field around the pack, applies to the surface and interior peak alike; all six Stellaris Table 8 coils reproduce, 13.44 T against 24.6 T at coil 0) is evaluated on every case as the check **`ampere_floor_ok`** (C): a design whose modeled `B_peak` lies below the floor has an impossible peak field that would favour small REBCO packs, and is filed `failed` (§ 7). It can bind on the anchored and the HELIAS-class cells at large R and small pack (more readily on the HELIAS-class cells), never under the arm (F6, C).

`B_max`, the plant's ceiling check, is a **supplied per-material design envelope** chosen at the support-band edge (13.0 T Nb₃Sn, 25.0 T REBCO; § 4) and reported as an envelope flag; it is not a material field limit, and only the conductor `supported` status decides `unsupported` (F9; goal invariant; Round 1 answer "No Nb₃Sn field limit"). The Stellaris 24.9 T envelope is a second flag on REBCO cases.

Cells: {anchored, HELIAS-class, pack-size arm} × {1.0, 1.4, 1.8} = nine.

## 6. Accounting: one basis inside the plant

Nothing from Round 1's annualization is added to the plant (audit § 4). In the material instances:

- **Conductor purchase** = supplied element count × turns × coils × turn length × price per metre (Round 1 basis, USD2021), replacing the plant's composition-implied tape metres at 20 $/m of 6 mm tape. The composition fractions become consequences of the construction rule (copper, steel, insulation areas per turn) priced at the plant's unit prices. Helium inventory keeps the plant's ideal-gas basis, roughly 15 % high at 4.5 K on a sub-M$ account (N3, disclosed).
- **Winding operations** stay the plant's always-on rate on turn-metres, both materials (N4).
- **Refrigeration**: Round 1's cold-load and staged-refrigeration calcs replace the plant's 0.20-Carnot chain and supplied 31.48 M$ package price for both materials; the 77 K intercept stage is common; cold-stage and intercept electricity enter the plant's recirculating power where the plant's cryo electricity enters today. Round 1's "combined unfavourable 20 K" variant is not carried (cryo is second-order, L-001; N7).
- **Structure**: the plant's account on the supplied `m_support` (§ 5).
- **Packages and classes**: re-supplied per design per § 5, priced by the plant's own laws and the declared [U] purchase scaling.
- **Everything else** (blanket, vessel, buildings, heat transport, conversion, fuel cycle, O&M, replacements, availability, DCF): the plant's, for both materials.
- **Money year (F12)**: conductor prices are USD2021 by declaration inside a plant whose other accounts are mixed-year (winding operations escalated to ≈ 2026 dollars). The mismatch is not neutral: the mixed basis **overweights every non-conductor LCOE difference relative to the conductor account** by roughly the 2021→plant-year escalation, so the break-even price moves down where REBCO carries a non-conductor plant advantage and up where it carries a penalty. A CPI 2021→2026 scalar on both conductor prices and on the Green refrigerator capital (also USD2021) is run as a post-processing variant and reported beside the base (r4a, design flag).
- **Basis bridge (F12)**: before the map, the reference design is evaluated on the reference instance and on the REBCO-material instance (same supplied design, supplied count equal to the composition-implied tape count) and the LCOE difference is reported by account (conductor basis, cryo chain, packages), so the pin and the map's REBCO reference point are reconciled numbers.

The untouched reference instance keeps every plant basis, so its outputs must reproduce today's pin bit-for-bit (§ 9).

## 7. Checks and statuses

Per design, every plant predicate is evaluated (the current 67 verdicts) plus the magnet checks from Round 1 (`acceptance_ok`, `copper_ok`, `steel_ok`, `capacity_ok`, conductor `supported` status) and the new `pack_area_ok`. Statuses, mutually exclusive, tested in this order:

1. **unsupported**: the conductor status is `unsupported` at the design's field and temperature, or a REBCO design is above 25 T, or the plasma point is outside the model's stated domain (`R − a_coil ≤ 0`, non-convergence). No ranking.
2. **ignited** (F1b): matched fusion power is attainable only with `p_aux_required < 0` at every ladder temperature, so the plant's burn-control convention (`burn_hold_ok`, `stellarator_plant.sysml:2407-2425`) cannot hold it. No ranking; reported with its `p_aux_required` and the companion `power_short` design, because it says the coherent design at that confinement is smaller or lower-field.
3. **capacity-limited**: a re-supplied rating or class is at its list or rule bound and the screen still fails (e.g. the cryo rating list exhausted). No ranking; the screen is named.
4. **failed**: any other magnet, plasma, power-balance, wall, building or package check, or the new `ampere_floor_ok` (C), is violated after re-supply, **except `peak_field_ok`** and **except `divertor_heat_ok`** (P, Q5: the asserted screen reads the source-anchored unscaled `q_target_peak`; the R-scaled channel is an unasserted shadow because no source supports the scaling, `stellarator_plant.sysml:1143-1144`, so at matched fusion power the screen passes only when `p_aux_required` ≤ 21.8 MW at every size and the published reference itself fails it at 49 MW; it is carried like `tbr_ok`, see item 5), which compares `B_peak` with the supplied per-material envelope `B_max` and is carried as `envelope_flag` (so a 13 T edge design rounded to 13.08 T is not misfiled). No ranking; the failing checks are named. `divertor_heat_ok`, `facility_occupancy_ok`, `facility_geometry_ok` and `water_electric_capacity_ok` are ordinary checks here: they respond to R, `coil_t` and thermal power (F8), and the package/occupancy re-supply of § 5 gives every design the same chance the reference had.
5. **supported**: all checks pass except `tbr_ok` (design-independent: held achieved TBR against a held requirement; fails at the published reference and identically everywhere) and `divertor_heat_ok` (source-anchored: cannot respond to the design's geometry by the model's own statement; fails at the reference); both margins are reported on every design, both are named in every result as open plant gaps, and the divertor margin discriminates on required heating, so § 8 reports the divertor-passing subset beside the supported set (P, Q5).

Flags carried on any design: `extrapolated` (REBCO 20–24 T; Nb₃Sn edge 13 T), `beyond_law_extents` (REBCO 24–25 T), `above_stellaris_envelope` (REBCO `B_peak` > 24.9 T), `envelope_flag` (`B_max`), `green_extrapolated`, `power_short`, `arm_extrapolated`, `free_capacity`, the two beta verdicts (0.05, 0.04), the Ampère-floor margin (whose sign is the `ampere_floor_ok` verdict), `R/√A_wp`.

"Best supported design" per material and cell is the supported design with the lowest LCOE. If a material has no supported design in a cell, the cell reports "no supported design" with the nearest non-supported design's status and reasons, and no preference.

## 8. Reporting

- **Before the map**: the basis bridge (§ 6); the equal-duty pairs and the own-highest-field comparison (§ 1).
- **Per cell**: the two best supported designs (all supplied inputs, all flags, ladder temperature, `R/√A_wp`, the divertor margin and a `divertor_pass` column; and, where one exists, the best supported design that also passes the divertor screen), LCOE of each, the difference, and the LCOE decomposition by account group (conductor purchase; other winding materials and operations; structure; refrigeration capital and electricity; heating capital and draw; radial-build accounts; conversion, heat rejection and other packages; buildings; fuel cycle and O&M; net electricity) as differences REBCO − Nb₃Sn.
- **Break-even REBCO price per cell (F15)**: every design is evaluated at 80 and 30 USD/m; LCOE is affine in the tape price at a fixed design (spares and O&M are not capital-proportional, `mfe_plant.sysml:596-604, 710-719`), so the slope is measured from the two evaluations, the crossing price against the best Nb₃Sn design is solved, and one evaluation at the solved price verifies it; the best REBCO design is re-selected at each price (80, 30, 10) and the re-selection is reported.
- **The map**: a 3 × 3 table (geometry × confinement) with, per cell, the winner at 80/30/10 USD/m, the LCOE gap, the per-design evidence labels ([S]/[A]/[D]/[U] of the two assumptions at each best design), the comparison label (the weaker), the policy-assumption column, and the REBCO flags.
- **Interaction**: for the anchored and arm geometries, the break-even price against field and against size, and against `f_ren`, to show whether higher field or reduced size changes the value of a lower REBCO price, and whether construction limits (fit, stress, pack area) or re-supplied equipment erase it.
