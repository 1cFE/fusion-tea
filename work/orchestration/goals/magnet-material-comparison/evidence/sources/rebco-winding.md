# REBCO winding evidence note (T-003 wave 2, request REQ-MMC-REBCO-WP-02)

Scope: an insulated or cable-in-conduit fusion REBCO conductor with composition, tape count, operating point and current density, ideally at 12–20 T near 20 K. The note records evidence. It builds no model and mints no DI.

Headline: two forced-flow REBCO cables now have sourced tape counts or geometry with measured current at 20 K. The SPC twisted-stack TF prototype has 320 tapes (4 mm × 0.1 mm) and carries about 36–38 kA at 12 T background and 20 K before cycling. The MIT VIPER cable (insulated, soldered, 27.7 mm diameter) carries 31.5 kA at 10.9 T and 20 K, but its tape count is not given. Neither source gives a winding-pack composition (jacket, insulation, helium fractions), so Stellaris Table 7 stays the only sourced insulated winding-pack breakdown. No source here reaches 20 T.

## Equations

No scaling equation is printed in either source. SPC Fig. 3 shows "prediction from the individual tape performance" as solid lines, but the scaling law is not given in this preprint (it points to Uglietti et al., SuST 28 (2015) 124005, not captured). All current densities above are simple I / A ratios, with the denominator named in each row: whole-tape area, strand area (tapes + solder + copper), an inferred bare-cable envelope, or the full VIPER outer diameter. None is a winding-pack current density.

## Gaps

- **No winding-pack breakdown for a forced-flow or insulated REBCO conductor.** Neither source gives jacket thickness, turn insulation, helium fraction or case share. The comparison cannot claim a REBCO winding-pack current density at 12 T and 20 K from these sources; it can bound conductor-level values (about 33–63 A/mm² SPC, about 52 A/mm² VIPER) with the denominators named.
- **No point at 15–20 T near 20 K.** SPC stops at 12 T and VIPER at 10.9 T background. The only 20 T-class REBCO winding points in hand remain the SPARC TFMC (no insulation) and Stellaris Table 7 (24.9 T design).
- **Background versus peak field.** Both facilities report background field. Self-field on a 36–60 kA cable adds to peak conductor field; neither source gives the peak value. The 12 T / 20 K SPC point should be read as a lower bound on the field actually seen.
- **VIPER tape count missing.** The comparison cannot convert VIPER's 31.5 kA into a per-tape or tape-area current density.
- **Cycling degradation at 20 K for SPC.** The 10 % and 20 % losses are not tied to a stated (T, B) point in this source.
- **Registration typo.** The SPC `--use-for` line in `knowledge/SOURCE_INDEX.md` says "1000 m cable pitch"; Table II says 1000 mm. The table here is correct. I did not edit the index by hand; the owner may correct it through the registry.
