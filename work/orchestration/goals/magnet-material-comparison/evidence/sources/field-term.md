# Evidence note — winding-pack term of the stellarator peak-field relation (REQ-MMC-FIELD-01)

Question: what permitted primary evidence fixes the winding-pack term of Lion 2021 eq. 39 for the Stellaris/HELIAS coil sets. Short answer: nothing prints a0 or a1 for any coil set; the appendix A cuboid-beam formula is fully transcribable and closed-form, so a1 is computable from a coil filament set, but no registered source holds Stellaris filaments; the Stellaris paper prints six same-coil-set (I, A_wp, B_max) triples and the Helias 5 line has three PROCESS design points that give a poorly conditioned derived fit; a rigorous lower bound on the pack term follows from Ampère's law; and tokamak PROCESS documents a pack-size peaking fit for 16–20 planar coils only.

Grading follows the capture-fidelity rule: every number below is `[INHERITED: source]` unless marked `derived` (my arithmetic) or `[AGENT]`.

## Equations

Eq. 39 (Lion 2021 p. 8; thesis eq. 2.69): B_max(A_wp) = μ0 I N / (R − a_coil) · ( a0(C) + R a1(C) / √A_wp ). "The first summand approximates the ideal part (due to an ideal toroid), the second summand includes the fitted scaling with changing winding pack size." Areas: A_wp is the whole winding-pack cross-section (the same area under j_WP in Tables 1–3 and in Stellaris Table 8). No units for a0, a1 are printed; by construction a0 is dimensionless and a1 has units of length⁻¹·length = dimensionless if R/√A_wp is dimensionless, which it is.

Eq. 37–38: B_coil(x) ≃ Σ_i B_i^Beam(x) over O(100) straight cuboid beams per coil; B_max = max_x Σ_coils B_coil(x). Assumptions stated: rectangular pack, homogeneously filled with current-carrying material, "for the calculation of B_max only".

Appendix A (Lion 2021 p. 17–18; thesis appendix B), transcribed from the equation images: Fig. 13 defines a beam by 8 corner points, current along t; text defines b along the beam's longitudinal (y) direction and n along the normal (x) direction, with "2b the dimension of the beam in x and 2d in y". A.1: F1(p) = ∫_{−h}^{h} dy ∫_{−b}^{b} dx (y_p − y)/N(x − x_p, y − y_p, z)³; A.2: F2(p) = same integrand with (x_p − x); A.3: N(x, y, z) = √(z² + y² + x²); A.4: B^Beam(p) = (μ0 |j0| / 4π) ∫ dz [F1(p) e_x − F2(p) e_y] for uniform j along z; A.5: F(x, A, B) ≡ ∫ dx / √(x² + A² + B²) = arctanh( x / √(A² + B² + x²) ); A.6: I(A, B, x) ≡ ∫ dx F(A, B, x) = x F(A, B, x) − x + A F(x, B, A) − |B| arctan( [B² + A(A + √(x² + A² + B²))] / (|B| x) ); A.7: F1(p) = F(b − x_p, d − y_p, z) − F(−b − x_p, d − y_p, z) − F(b − x_p, −d − y_p, z) + F(−b − x_p, −d − y_p, z); A.8: F2(p) = F(d − y_p, b − x_p, z) − F(−d − y_p, b − x_p, z) − F(d − y_p, −b − x_p, z) + F(−d − y_p, −b − x_p, z); A.9: B^Beam(p) = (μ0 |j0| / 4π) { e_x [ Σ of eight I(±b − x_p, ±d − y_p, ±h) terms ] − e_y [ Σ of eight I(±d − y_p, ±b − x_p, ±h) terms ] }; A.10: B_coil(p) = ∫_coil dB ≃ Σ_i B_i^Beam(p). Stated accuracy ΔB/B ~ 10⁻⁴ against μ0 I/(2πR) for an ideal toroid and against a filament Biot–Savart at the W7-X axis; near the coils (x ~ 5.2 m) the filament method "diverges" and A.10 is the accurate one (Fig. 14).

Printed inconsistencies a later implementer must resolve, all visible in the images: (1) A.1–A.2 integrate y over ±h while the text and A.7 use ±d for y and ±h is the beam half-length along z in A.9; (2) A.9 as printed repeats arguments so that terms cancel (e.g. the e_x bracket has "− I(−b − x_p, −d − y_p, h) + I(−b − x_p, −d − y_p, h)" and the e_y bracket the same with the roles swapped) and its −h half does not carry the sign flip that ∫_{−h}^{h} dz implies; the sign pattern in A.7–A.8 is the correct template. So A.9 must be re-derived from A.4–A.8 (or taken from Urankar 1982, ref [50]) rather than copied. The z-integration of A.4 over the beam's length is what A.6 supplies; A.6 itself I did not verify symbolically.

Judgement on "bounded calculation or new solver": with a coil filament set, a pack cross-section (side s, or b, d) and the pack orientation rule (Lion 2021 §3.6, `output.md:488`: pack normals along the cylindrical toroidal and radial directions), computing B_max is a closed-form sum, O(100 beams × N coils × sample points) arctanh/arctan evaluations, with a printed validation target (Fig. 14) and, for Stellaris, six printed (I, A_wp, B_max) checks in Table 8. That is a bounded calculation, and a1(C) follows by repeating it at two or more A_wp. What is missing is the input: no registered source contains Stellaris coil filaments.

## Gaps

- a0(C), a1(C) are printed nowhere: not in Lion 2021 (Tables 1–3 print outputs only), not in the thesis, not in Stellaris. The pack term cannot be fixed from evidence.
- No second same-coil-set (B_max, A_wp) pair exists for Stellaris at a different pack area. Table 8's six coils are six different coil shapes at six pack sizes, so they constrain the whole coil set's FE result but do not separate a0 from a1 for one coil; they are a validation set for a computation, not an anchor pair.
- Computing a1 for Stellaris from registered geometry is not possible: the Proxima CAD is a scaled W7-X (a different C) and was not captured; the two augmented-Lagrangian sources are landing pages. A computation on the scaled-W7-X CAD would anchor a different configuration.
- The Helias-5 three-point fit (ratio ≈ 0.15 + 0.064 R/√A_wp) is derived, poorly conditioned, and may mix coil sets; it can motivate a sensitivity arm, not a held constant. Its slope says the printed PROCESS ratios move by about 0.06 per unit of R/√A_wp; at Stellaris (R/√A_wp = 35.3) a pack growth 0.130 → 0.279 m² (audit D1) lowers R/√A_wp to 24.0, which on that slope is a ratio change of about −0.7 of 2.77 `[AGENT]`, i.e. not a small effect if the slope transferred, which is unestablished.
- The Ampère bound B_max ≥ μ0 I/(4√A_wp) is exact but is only a floor (13.4 of 24.6 T at coil 0); it bounds how fast the peak can fall with pack growth from below, not the peak itself.
- Tokamak PROCESS's pack-size peaking fit covers 16–20 planar coils and its coefficients are in the code, not the page; it cannot be transferred to 48 non-planar coils. Kovari 2016 eq. 33 has no pack-size term beyond the radial position of the pack's plasma-facing edge.
- Schauer 2013 (HELIAS 5-B magnet system) was not obtained (Elsevier; the MPG PuRe record returned 403); whether it prints B_max against pack dimensions is unknown.
- What this prevents: the comparison cannot claim a field response to REBCO's smaller-winding lever or to a larger Nb3Sn pack on this coil set with evidence; the plant model's fixed peak ratio under pack changes (audit D1, D3) stays an undeclared-sign error until either Stellaris filaments are obtained and the appendix A sum is run, or a sensitivity arm is declared on the derived slope with its caveats.
