## Equations

- **Cost per kA·m at a reference condition (PROCESS, `supercond_cost_model = 1`).** Per-metre superconductor cost per turn = `sc_mat_cost_0[i] · j_crit_str_0[i] / j_crit_str_tf` (original model `output.md:4349-4360`). Here `sc_mat_cost_0` is in USD/kA·m at 6.4 T, 4.2 K, and the Jc ratio is per strand. As printed, the expression has no current factor. It multiplies USD/kA·m by a dimensionless ratio and labels the result USD/m. Either the per-turn current is carried elsewhere or the code is dimensionally inconsistent. The capture does not settle which.
- **Additive coil cost (HTS Appendix I).** C_Coils = C_SC + C_Cable + C_CoilManufacturing (Eq. AI.2); C_SC = M_Strand · c_Strand; M_Strand = d_Strand · A_Strand / cos θ with θ = 15° (Eq. AI.4); C_Cable = f_Cable · C_SC (Eq. AI.5). All quantities are per metre of magnet, and c_Strand is per kg of strand or tape (`output.md:345-369`). This is an accounting form calibrated on accelerator dipoles.
- **ITER-referenced power-law scaling (PROCESS 2014).** C = C_ref · (k / k_ref)^0.8, with C_ref and k_ref as in the table (Kovari `output.md:1780-1856, 2142-2152`).
- **1990 cryoplant.** C_2263 [M$] = 1e-6 · 93000 · (4.5 / T_cryo) · P_He[W]^0.67, then multiplied by the safety-assurance and Nth-of-a-kind factors (original model `output.md:5036-5050`).
- **Currency and area notes.** HTS values are "present" euros, with inflation escalated to 2024 at an index of 1.26 for 2017–2024 (footnote 4, PDF p21). Treat them as about 2024 EUR (bounded assumption). The paper itself contains an inconsistency: C_ColdMassMaterials is given as 25 kEUR/m and as a reference value of 26.4 kEUR/m in the same paragraph (PDF p21). Table AI-I uses 25.

## Gaps

- **No common-condition price pair.** REBCO is priced at 20 T, 4.2 K (tape); Nb₃Sn at 16 T, 4.2 K (accelerator strand); PROCESS defaults at 6.4 T, 4.2 K with no material mapping. None is at 20 K or in the 12–13 T overlap. What this blocks: the comparison cannot state a sourced cost per kA·m for either material at matched duty. Any conversion requires the Ic(B,T) laws from the other workers, and the converted value is `derived`, not sourced.
- **The 150–200 USD/kA·m band has no stated condition.** Its magnitude matches Cooley & Pong's 200 USD/kA·m at 20 T, 4.2 K, but a match is not a definition. What this blocks: using the band as a price at any particular field or temperature. It can only be cited as an unconditioned "present range".
- **No fusion-grade REBCO purchase with an amount purchased.** The REBCO numbers are a DOE baseline, CERN orders shown in arbitrary units, and a per-kg conversion. What this blocks: claims about fusion-scale REBCO procurement prices.
- **Scope of the ITER per-kg Nb₃Sn figure.** Whether the 210 t scaling mass is non-Cu or strand mass changes the result by about 2.4 times (2505 vs 1052 USD/kg).
- **20 K refrigerator capital cost.** No registered data-backed source. What this blocks: any sourced claim that a 20 K cryoplant costs less per watt of cooling than a 4.5 K plant. The PROCESS 4.5/T factor is a bounded assumption at most.
- **Green 2015 is unusable as registered.** The capture holds a bot-check page, so its cost fits cannot be cited until it is re-captured.
