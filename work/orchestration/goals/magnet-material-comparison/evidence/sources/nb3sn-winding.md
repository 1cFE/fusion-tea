# Evidence note — nb3sn-winding (REQ-MMC-NB3SN-WP-01)

Bottom line: two fusion Nb₃Sn conductor constructions are now sourced well enough to turn strand performance into conductor current density, and one of them reaches winding-pack current density. The EU DEMO react-and-wind (R&W) TF design (Demattè & Bruzzone, pre-existing) gives the full layer-by-layer conductor and winding-pack geometry at 104.95 kA and 12.04 T, so conductor and winding-pack current densities follow by arithmetic (40.7 A/mm² conductor for layer 1, 28.0 A/mm² winding pack). The ITER TF conductor (68 kA, 11.8 T) is sourced from strand to jacket (Takahashi et al. + Breschi et al.), but no captured source gives the ITER TF turn count, insulation or winding-pack dimensions, so its winding-pack current density is unavailable. Neither source separates turn insulation from ground insulation. The two EU DEMO sources count the temperature margin differently (2 K vs 1.5 K + 0.7 K nuclear heating); that difference is surfaced below, not resolved.

## Equations

- **DB eq. (1), non-Cu Jc scaling law** (raw.pdf p.3; exists only as an image, output.md:110): Jc = (C/B) · s(ε) · (1 − t^1.52) · (1 − t²) · b^p · (1 − b)^q. DB states it is the non-Cu Jc form from Bottura & Bordini [12]. Printed inputs: B_eff = 12.04 T, ε_op = −0.3 %, T = 6.5 K. C comes from DB ref. [11], which was "accepted for publication" and is not captured, so C is not printed. DB says "the parameters t, b, p and q from [13]" (BR). t and b are reduced variables, not parameters, so this is loose wording; the likely meaning is the fitted p, q, Tc0m and Bc20m of BR Table III. DB does not say which Table III row it used. It names Kiswire strands, and BR Table III has a Kiswire row (TFKO4–TFKO8). That the two are the same strand is an assumption. BR's C is printed in A·T and fits strand Ic, not non-Cu Jc, so BR's C cannot replace DB's C. The electric-field criterion for eq. (1) is not stated in DB.
- **DB conductor sizing**: A_Nb₃Sn = I_op / Jc = 104 950 A / Jc → 155.9 mm² (non-Cu). Copper: total Cu = I_op / J_Cu = 104 950 / 93.4 = 1123.7 mm². DB's sentence (output.md:137) calls 1123.7 mm² "the resulting cross-section for the stabilizer" after subtracting strand copper. The arithmetic shows 1123.7 mm² is the total copper, and the stabilizer is 1123.7 − 156.7 (strand Cu, since Cu:non-Cu = 1) = 967.0 mm² = 2 × 483.5 mm², which matches the printed Rutherford-cable area. Read 1123.7 as total Cu.
- **DB Table I area check**: cable space + steel (1618.2 + 982.7 = 2600.9 mm²) exceeds the 68 × 37.9 mm envelope (2577.2 mm²) by 23.7 mm² (0.9 %). The corner radii (R3 outer, R6 inner) would reduce areas, not add to them, so the table and the envelope are not fully consistent. Conductor current density above uses the printed envelope.
- **Current-density denominators used here**: non-Cu = strand area × 1/(1 + Cu:non-Cu); SC strand = whole superconducting strands only; cable space = area inside the jacket, including the central channel (ITER) or cooling pipe and stabilizer (DEMO); conductor = bare jacket outer envelope, no insulation; winding pack = gross WP rectangle including turn insulation, ground insulation and fillers.

## Gaps

- **Temperature-margin accounting differs between the two EU DEMO sources.** DB sizes the conductor at 6.5 K "for 2 K temperature margin" (implying 4.5 K). SE states the DEMO TF requirement as Tcs ≥ 6.7 K = 4.5 K inlet + 0.7 K nuclear heating + 1.5 K margin. At 6.7 K the same conductor would carry less Jc than DB assumes. Neither source reconciles the two. Until the owner or a later source picks one, the comparison cannot state a single EU DEMO temperature basis; both should be carried.
- **Insulation thickness unavailable (both constructions).** DB leaves about 72 mm toroidal and 73.7 mm radial for turn insulation, ground insulation and fillers together. Without per-turn insulation the comparison can compute winding-pack current density only from DB's gross WP rectangle. It cannot rebuild the pack for a different conductor.
- **ITER TF winding pack unavailable.** No captured source gives turns per coil, WP dimensions, radial-plate area or insulation. The comparison can claim ITER TF conductor current density (45.3 A/mm² bare) but not ITER TF winding-pack current density.
- **ITER central spiral and inlet temperature unavailable.** Without the spiral size the petal void fraction cannot be checked against geometry. The ITER Tcs margin (about 1.2 K) rests on SE's statement that DEMO copied ITER's 4.5 K inlet.
- **DB layers 2–8 strand counts not printed.** Table I gives subcable layout (1+6+12 or 1+6) and flat-cable size per layer but not the number of subcables, so non-Cu area per layer is unavailable. Only layer 1 supports a strand-to-conductor conversion.
- **Electric-field criterion unstated in DB and TK.** DB's non-Cu Jc and TK's 190 A specification carry no printed criterion here. The nb3sn-law note's Tsui source states 10 µV/m for the same ITER specification; DB's criterion remains unknown.
- **Field coverage.** Both constructions sit at 11.8–12.23 T. No captured source gives a fusion Nb₃Sn conductor design above about 12.2 T, so the comparison cannot claim sourced Nb₃Sn winding-pack densities at 13 T.
- **DB is a preliminary, untested design.** Its 104.95 kA prototype test in SULTAN was "foreseen in 2022" (output.md:157); no result is captured. SE's prototype results are for 63.3 kA.
- **No new domain insight minted.** DI-010's 28 A/mm² for the R&W pack is reproduced here (27.98 A/mm²), with the denominator now stated. Any DI amendment goes through the owner's `/research` flow.
