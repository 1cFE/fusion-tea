# Evidence note — Nb₃Sn / NbTi stellarator reactor design points (REQ-MMC-NB3SN-PLANT-01)

Worker C of T-011. Question: does a permitted primary source give a complete low-temperature-superconductor stellarator design point usable as a second coil-set anchor for the peak/axis field ratio and as an existence proof of an LTS stellarator plant. Short answer: yes for the anchor (HELIAS 5-B, Schauer 2013 with the 2011 winding-pack paper), with the caveat that it anchors a different configuration from Stellaris, and no source prints the peak field's dependence on pack size for a fixed coil set. Clean-room screen applied before every fetch: all material is HELIAS-line (IPP, EUROfusion, UKAEA); nothing ARIES-derived was fetched, read or cited.

## Equations

- No registered HELIAS source prints an equation for the peak field. Schauer 2011/2013 print the values only. Lion 2021 eq. (39) `B_max = a0(C)·B_axis·(…) + R·a1(C)/√A_wp` is the only form, its a0, a1 are unprinted for every coil set (`a_general_stellarator_…/output.md:448-455`), and Worker A's note `field-term.md` owns it.
- Ampere-turn identity used above: `B_ideal = μ₀ N I_coil /(2π R)`, with `I_coil = turns × cable current`. All turn-current products are per coil and are derived, not printed, except the printed totals in Lion 2021 Tables 2–3 and thesis Table 4.3 ("Total coil current", MA, summed over coils).
- Current-density bases: Schauer 2011 prints the cable section without channel (1489 mm²) and strand counts; 86 kA / 1489 mm² = 57.8 A/mm² per cable area is derived. Lion 2021 "WP current density" is per winding-pack area (`output.md:552-556` defines the WP composition); its printed unit "MA m⁻²" with values ≈3e7 is a typo for A m⁻² (≈30 MA m⁻²).

## Gaps

- Peak field vs pack size for one fixed coil set: unavailable. Schauer 2011/2013 give one pack per configuration. Lion 2021 Table 3 varies the pack (0.25–0.95 m²) at one plasma but changes the coil count with it, so pack and configuration effects cannot be separated; Fig. 5 gives one section at 14 MA/60 cm with the peak only on a colour bar. The 2001 remark that trapezoidal shaping lowered HSR4/18's maximum to 10 T is qualitative. Consequence: the omitted Lion term (audit D1) still cannot be fixed from HELIAS evidence; the two HELIAS anchors (2.12 at 0.53 m², 2.20 at ≈0.5 m²) and the Stellaris anchor (2.77 at 0.13 m²) differ in configuration, coil count and aspect ratio as well as pack, so they do not determine a1.
- A second anchor for this plant's `peak_ratio` (audit D3): HELIAS 5-B supplies a sourced ratio 2.12 (2.20 for HSR50a; 2.0–2.2 across every HELIAS-line and ITER row above; 2.3–2.7 only in Lion 2021's code-sized Helias 3/4 and W7-X-upscale sets). It is an anchor for a HELIAS-5 coil set, not for Stellaris's 48-coil set. Using it means re-anchoring the plant to HELIAS 5-B, not correcting Stellaris's ratio.
- What the plant model would have to re-anchor to use HELIAS 5-B, against the held constants of `plant-chain-audit.md` § 2: `n_coils` 48 → 50; `R` 12.7 → 22 m, `a` 1.3 → 1.8 m (2π²Ra² = 1407 m³ exactly, so `f_shape` 1.0); `k_link` 0.7731 → 0.92–0.97 (derived, cross-configuration); `reference_turns` 308 → 156 and `turn_current` 50 → 86 kA (I_coil 15.4 → 13.4 MA); `wp_side` 0.36 → 0.729 m equivalent (0.750 × 0.709); `coil_t` 0.30 → 0.709 m pack or 0.92–1.0 m casing; casing interior 0.40 → outer 1.0 × 0.92 m; `peak_ratio` 2.7667 → 2.12; `R_ref` 12.7 → 22; `a_coil_ref` 3.15 → unprinted (Lion 2021 `output.md:760` gives a 1.9 m coil–plasma distance for Helias 5 at reference size, so ≈1.8 + 1.9 + 0.36 ≈ 4.1 m, derived); `B_max` 24.9 → 12.5 T; `T_inventory` 20 → 4.7 K (2011) or 4.5 K (thesis); `c_coil_ref` 25 → 33.5–34.5 m; `W_mag_ref` → 160 GJ (152 GJ HSR50a); stress limit 800 → 750 MPa allowable with 630 MPa computed, strain 0.4 % → < 0.3 % computed; `m_support` 11.6 kt → no HELIAS 5-B structure mass printed (Lion 2021 Table 2 Helias 5: 15.1 kt structure, 7.61 kt coils; Beidler 2001 HSR4/18: coils with casing 4100 t, total below 10 000 t, 700 t NbTi cable); `f_ren` → 1.25 (Muldrew, uncaptured), 1.38 (thesis conservative), ≤ 1.5 (Warmer Option C); beta limit 5 % → 4.2 % MHD (2001) or 5 % (Lion); fusion power 3000 MW at 5 T (2013 footnote), 2850 MW at 5.5 T (Muldrew), 2370 MW at 17.1 m (thesis); net power 1000 MW (Muldrew), 789 MW (thesis); wall-load calibration → avg 1.2 MW/m² (Warmer Table 1), peaks in the two registered HELIAS neutronics sources; divertor limit 5 MW/m² (Warmer), 3.99 MW/m² computed (Muldrew).
- Self-consistency at field: HELIAS 5-B's 3000 MW is stated for 5 T on axis, not 5.9 T (2013 footnote b); the 12 T conductor field is a designer's structural choice with the confinement margin argued from B^0.8 (2011 p. 1). The self-consistent Nb₃Sn points are PROCESS outputs: Lion 2021 Helias 5 at 7.07 T (β 2.92 %), thesis conservative at 5.81 T (β at the 5 % limit, f_ren 1.38 near its 1.4 cap, ignited), Muldrew at 5.50 T (β 4.36 %, f_ren 1.25, ignited). None is at the 4.3–4.7 T this plant's coil set would give Nb₃Sn; the HELIAS route to Nb₃Sn is a 2.0–2.2 ratio coil set at R 17–22 m, not a 2.77 ratio set at 12.7 m.
- What limited the field: NbTi points, the conductor (10 T cap, 1.8 K). Nb₃Sn points, a chosen 12 T with structure named as the eventual limit (2011); in PROCESS, the quench constraint at high B and small R (Lion 2021 Fig. 11) and j_op/j_crit ≤ 0.8. Nothing ties any HELIAS field to a configuration (ripple, coil–plasma distance) limit at the coil.
- Unreachable: the Schauer 2009/2010 "Extrapolation of the W7-X magnet system to reactor size" paper (ISHW 2009 PPPL copy returns HTTP 403 here; Contrib. Plasma Phys. 50 (2010) 750 has no public IPP file), which is the cited home of the HSR50a 12 T pack design and would likely print the field at the conductor against the pack.
