## 1. Mental model in five lines

1. The designer supplies the winding (308 turns, 50 kA, 0.36 m pack, 0.30 m radial allocation, casing, Stellaris composition, 6 mm tape at 20 $/m, 20 K, cryo ratings and price) and the plasma operating point (R 12.7 m, a 1.3 m, n_e0, T_i0). The model calculates everything else and checks it against supplied limits; nothing is resized (`stellarator_plant.sysml:202-305, 358-447, 898-990, 1343-1428`).
2. Ampere-turns set the axis field (`B_axis ∝ k_link·N·I/R`), and the axis field times a held peak ratio 2.7667 and a bore factor sets the peak field. The pack size does not enter the peak field; only the radial allocation does, through the coil-centre radius (`mfe_magnet_field.sysml:57`; `mfe_plasma_scaling.sysml:426-442`).
3. The axis field reaches the plasma in exactly two places: the ISS04 confinement time inside the sustainment chain (so the required heating and the helium ash), and beta. Fusion power reads density, temperature and volume, not field (`mfe_plasma_sustainment.sysml:58-65, 95`; `mfe_plasma_scaling.sysml:155-157, 417`; `mfe_plasma.sysml:110-147`).
4. Field reaches cost and net electricity only indirectly: through the heating wall-plug draw (`p_aux_required / 0.5`), through verdicts (current, peak ceiling, stress, strain, beta, sustainment), and not through any equipment account. Magnet capital follows pack volume, turns and supplied masses; every other CAS22 account follows the radial build and a supplied power class (`stellarator_plant.sysml:1024`; `mfe_power_balance.sysml:159-172`; `mfe_magnet_cost.sysml:178, 203`; `mfe_account_costs.sysml:98-193`).
5. Cryogenics follows pack volume, temperature and lead current at a fixed 0.20 Carnot fraction; cryo capital is a supplied 31.48 M$ price; ratings are point-matched at 20 K and captured at zero margin. LCOE is a DCF over overnight capital (magnet capital × installation 1.14 × contingency 1.10 × indirect 1.20), levelized O&M, blanket/divertor replacements from a wall-load calendar that the magnet never touches, and net electricity (`mfe_cryo_inventory.sysml:79`; `mfe_plant_systems.sysml:470-651`; `mfe_plant.sysml:461-564, 669-807`; `mfe_lcoe_dcf.sysml:12-17`).

### (b) → axis field and peak field

- `B_axis = mu0 · k_link · n_coils · I_coil / (2π R0)` (`mfe_magnet_field.sysml:57`), with `n_coils` 48 (`stellarator_plant.sysml:223`), `k_link` 0.7731 (`:286`, a held set-distribution × axis-linkage fact from Stellaris Tables 2 and 8), `R0 = plasma.R` = 12.7 m (`mfe_plant.sysml:138`; `stellarator_plant.sysml:898`). Calculated; exposed as `magnet.B` with `=` (`mfe_power_core.sysml:104`). No domain beyond finite inputs. Published value 9.0 T minus one ulp (`mfe_magnet_field.sysml:29-32`).
- `B_peak = B_axis · peak_ratio · [R/(R − a_coil)] / [R_ref/(R_ref − a_coil_ref)]` (`mfe_plasma_scaling.sysml:426-428, 493`; bound `mfe_power_core.sysml:167-174`). Held constants: `peak_ratio` 2.7667 (`stellarator_plant.sysml:246`), `R_ref` 12.7 (`:250`), `a_coil_ref` 3.15 (`:278`). `a_coil = r_coil_centre` = a 1.3 + stack 1.70 (`:590-717`) + 0.15 = 3.15 m. Domain: `R − a_coil > 0` (`mfe_plasma_scaling.sysml:465`). The winding-pack term of Lion 2021 eq. 39, `R·a1(C)/√A_wp`, is not carried because `a0(C)`, `a1(C)` are unprinted (`:440-442`; the source says they come from pre-processing, `knowledge/sources/a_general_stellarator_version_of_the_systems_code_process/output.md:448-455`; the thesis repeats this, `…systems_code_models_for_stellarator_fusion_power_plants_and/output.md:1337`).
- Consumers of `B_axis`: sustainment `B_in` (`pipeline.yaml:319`), beta (`:355`), peak field (`:1964`), the 1cfe comparison channel (`:2048`, export only). Consumers of `B_peak`: the conductor law, stress, and `peak_field_ok` against `B_max` 24.9 T (`mfe_plant.sysml:856-859`; `stellarator_plant.sysml:436`). Stored energy `W_mag` (`mfe_magnet_field.sysml:361-362`) is export only (`pipeline.yaml:5467`); the support-mass calc that would read it is dormant (`mfe_magnet_cost.sysml:154`, `c_support` default 0; the route refuses the key, `study_route.py:184`).
- Stress and strain: `sigma_wp = k_sigma · I_coil · B_peak / wp_side` (`mfe_magnet_field.sysml:64`; bound `mfe_power_core.sysml:264-269`), 650 MPa at the reference against 800 MPa (`stellarator_plant.sysml:549`); `eps_cond = f_cond · sigma/E` (`mfe_magnet_field.sysml:307`), 0.217 % against 0.4 % (`:428`).

### (e) → net electricity

`p_th = mn·p_neutron + p_alpha + p_input + q_recovered`; `p_et = eta_th·p_th`; `recirculating = p_coils + p_pump_total + f_sub·p_et + (p_trit + p_house) + (p_tfcool + p_pfcool) + p_cryo + p_wallplug + cycle pumps + cooling water`; `p_net = p_et − recirculating` (`mfe_power_balance.sysml:141-172`; bound `mfe_plant.sysml:378-396`). Magnet- and field-sensitive terms:

- `p_cryo = cryoplant.p_elec` = cold stage `p_cold/(0.20 × 20/280)` + intercept `q_shield (300−77)/(0.20 × 77)` (`mfe_cryo_plant.sysml:8-10`; `mfe_cryo_inventory.sysml:79, 90`; `mfe_plant_systems.sysml:586, 613-645`), where `p_cold = 35.5 W/m³ × vol_cold + 7.5 kW + inventory` and the inventory area is `48 · c_coil · 4 (wp_side + 0.20)` with lead heat `∝ n_leads · |I_turn|` (`mfe_cryo_inventory.sysml:11-12`; `stellarator_plant.sysml:492, 1354, 1369, 1416`). Published total 2.14 MW (replay s0).
- `p_tfcool` 15 MW, a provisional non-cryogenic cooling allowance (`stellarator_plant.sysml:1442-1450`).
- `p_tf_extra = cryoplant.p_drive` (lead and joint drive) into `p_tf_total` (`mfe_plant.sysml:192`; `mfe_subsystems.sysml:281-286`); `p_tf` itself is 0 (`stellarator_plant.sysml:732`).
- `p_wallplug = p_aux_required / 0.5` (§ c).

- Availability: the calendar's `F/N` on the peak wall load, outage 7 months, unplanned 0 (`mfe_lifecycle.sysml:17-31`; `stellarator_plant.sysml:2053-2083`). ## 8. Premise conflicts

Surfaced per the capture-fidelity rule; none is resolved here.

1. **"A lower-field design point is inside the plasma scaling's supported domain" (trail § Round 2 Strategy, Assumptions) is not established, and the Nb₃Sn plant is not a design point on this plant.** With `peak_ratio` 2.7667 held (`stellarator_plant.sysml:246`), Round 1's Nb₃Sn support limit (12 T; edge 13 T) puts the axis field at 4.34–4.70 T; the 2026-08-23 A/B found the same 4.69 T ceiling and 0 of 4,144 feasible Nb₃Sn points (`20260823-magnet-technology-ab/record.md:37-42, 52`). `[AGENT]` at the Stellaris pressure, beta scales to about 2.53 % × (9.0/4.34)² ≈ 11 % against the 5 % limit, and the required heating rises by about (9.0/4.34)^2.15 ≈ 4.8× at fixed stored energy; holding beta at fixed temperature cuts density to ≈ 0.46× and fusion power to ≈ 0.2× (≈ 0.5 GW), and recovering it at fixed beta and aspect ratio needs volume ∝ B⁻⁴, so R ≈ 12.7 × (9.0/4.34)^(4/3) ≈ 34 m. Every held fact (`peak_ratio`, `k_link`, `c_coil_ref`, `W_mag_ref`, `f_wp_vol`, `k_sigma`, the wall-peak calibration, the divertor shadow, `f_ren`) is anchored at the Stellaris point and declares no validity band (D3, D8). So the strategy's abandonment condition "the plasma scaling or the plant's cost accounts have no supported domain at the field a Nb₃Sn design requires" is, on the model's own statements, the live case, not a contingency. The owner's evaluation already names this: a fair comparison "needs a lower-field, larger design (a coupled redesign)" (`evaluation.md:166`). Options for the owner: (a) accept "Nb₃Sn unsupported on this plant" as the whole-plant answer and quantify only the REBCO side; (b) compare both materials at a matched lower field inside Round 1's overlap (8–13 T peak, so 2.9–4.7 T axis) on a re-chosen operating point, which connects to Round 1 but does not test REBCO's high-field benefit unless the failing 24.9 T reference is also carried; (c) commission the configuration research (a second coil-set anchor or a Biot–Savart computation on a registered geometry) before any Nb₃Sn plant point is priced.
2. **No passing REBCO plant exists at the reference under the current model** (§ 5). Four of the six failures (`divertor_heat_ok`, `facility_occupancy_ok`, `tbr_ok`, `water_electric_capacity_ok`) are not magnet-related and would fail identically for either material; two (`reference_conductor_current_ok`, `wp_fit_ok`) are the magnet's own. "The best supported plant design points found for each material" therefore has no reference to start from. Either the search first finds a passing REBCO plant on the current model (the only prior all-pass neighbourhood, `lhs-0224`, is a sizing-mode point at 24.39 T with `coil_t` 0.65, and replay s2 shows a larger allocation raises the field and fails the building), or the non-magnet failures are held common and stated as such in every LCOE quoted.
3. **REBCO's two levers are blind or barred in the model.** The peak field does not see pack area (D1); the ceiling `B_max` 24.9 T is the owner-ruled design envelope with zero margin (`stellarator_plant.sysml:431-436`; `evaluation.md:135`); stress grows as B² at fixed geometry and reaches 800 MPa near 27.6 T `[AGENT]`; and the tape count is implied by composition (D4), so a smaller pack at the same field fails the current check by construction. Until D1 and D4 are repaired, "higher field or a smaller winding" changes only tape volume and verdicts, and the owner's rule "do not infer plant benefits from a conductor field limit alone" cannot be honoured because limits are the only field responses the model has.
4. **Equipment is field-blind (D7).** Structure, power supplies, cryo capital and installed heating are supplied prices and ratings; blanket, shield, vessel and structure scale with the radial build and supplied power classes. An LCOE decomposition will show the magnet account, cryo electricity and heating draw moving and everything else fixed. That is a statement about what the model prices, and must be labelled so, not read as "other plant costs do not erase the benefit".
5. **Accounting bases differ before any number crosses** (§ 4). Round 1's annualization, refrigerator capital and electricity charge are already inside the plant in other forms; the conductor-quantity basis (implied count vs supplied count), tape width (6 vs 4 mm), price and money year differ. A plant break-even REBCO price is in dollars per metre of 6 mm tape in an unstated year unless the basis is rewritten first; the trail's "conductor price bases per material" item has to be that rewrite, not an import.
6. **The only magnet-local pass on the current model rests on a known gap.** The WI-098 48 kA offer passes fit through a 0.19-aspect pack in a 1.30 m transverse cavity that carries no mass, thermal or cost response (D2; `evaluation.md:148`; `magnet-capture/report.md:7-9`). It should not stand as the REBCO "best supported design point" without D2 repaired or the limit stated in the result.
