Compute steam flow, economizer/evaporator/superheater duties, and the salt temperature at both section junctions from the existing available heat and salt flow. Check the full temperature profile, including interior minima with variable properties. Report minimum approaches and required UA; report area only with an explicit effective-U/flow-arrangement assumption. Required capacity is a sizing result, not evidence that hardware is installed.
