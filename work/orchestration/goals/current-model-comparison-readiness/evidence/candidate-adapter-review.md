Required repair: keep the raw native evidence and store, represent every nonfinite value explicitly in a separate serializable refusal record, and retain failure stage/native-started/request identity through final serialization and identity writing. Reproduction is `probe.json#/nonfinite_native_serialization_failure`; execution was stubbed and no physical model was run.
