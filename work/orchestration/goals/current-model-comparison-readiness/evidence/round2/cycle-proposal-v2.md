Their flow split follows their duties, and they recombine before the existing salt pump. There is no added salt heat source or duplicated heat credit.

## Equations and heat admission

[AGENT, physical identities] For each expansion, `h_out = h_in - eta_t (h_in - h_out_isentropic)`. The isentropic state is evaluated at outlet pressure and inlet entropy. At condenser pressure, the saturation mixture gives `x_s = (s_in-s_f)/(s_g-s_f)` and `h_s = h_f+x_s(h_g-h_f)`. Actual quality follows actual outlet enthalpy. The prototype refuses extrapolation or an LP endpoint outside its two-phase property domain.

[AGENT, approximation] Liquid pump work is `v_in (p_out-p_in)/eta_p`, with pressure converted to kPa. Pump work increases fluid enthalpy. Compressibility across the boiler feed pump is neglected; future reference-property checks should bound this approximation. Heater bleed is `y=(h_sat_liquid-h_pumped_condensate)/(h_bleed-h_pumped_condensate)`. Heat input per kilogram of main flow is `q_main+(1-y)q_reheat`; shaft output sums HP expansion of full flow and LP expansion of unextracted flow. Generator and mechanical factors apply only to turbine shaft output. Electric pump input is shaft input divided by motor efficiency.

[AGENT] Each salt branch pairs its linear heat-release profile with the corresponding water enthalpy profile. Water temperature includes the full constant-temperature boiling interval. Main and reheat branch heat split and mass split are equal because their salt end temperatures match. The script evaluates all source enthalpy knots and a 10,001-point enthalpy grid; piecewise linear temperature differences attain minima at knots, which are included. Effective conductance follows `UA = integral(dQ/(T_salt-T_water))`. Baseline main/reheater conductances are 41.074384/11.186763 MW/K; both minimum approaches are 20 K. The colder steam diagnostic has 30 K approaches. All temperatures use the pre-pump salt return consistently. These are required thermal conductances, not purchased or installed capacity.

[AGENT] Fixed-pressure heat exchange, zero steam-line pressure/temperature losses, no external heat leak, no extraction-line throttle loss and a saturated open-heater outlet are explicit reduced-model assumptions. They make this a coherent loss-parameterized Rankine calculation, while leaving detailed hydraulic and equipment design unresolved. The existing salt head is retained as a boundary; no branch-specific head or valve sizing has been established. This limits any claim about physically installed pumping or steam-generator adequacy.
