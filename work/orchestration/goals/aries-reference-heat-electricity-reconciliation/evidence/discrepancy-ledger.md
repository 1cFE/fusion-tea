## Status of every material discrepancy (owner brief, required accounting)

- **Unremoved heat (158.7 MW original):** mechanism 1 corrected, mechanism 2 bounded. Mechanism 1 (series order) is removed by the reviewed network increment (WI-092); mechanism 2 (at 1600 kg/s with 0.95 recuperation: helium-stage bound 53.5 MW and PbLi cold-end pinch 56.4 MW at the design split, the latter ≈ 34 MW mapping artefact that migrates rather than disappears) is bounded by a missing-input range, because it is removed only by a declared operating or hardware choice that the source does not state: 1700 kg/s with a 1700 MW compressor rating (all checks satisfied, net 891.003) or 0.8 recuperation at 1600 kg/s (all checks satisfied, net 759.886). No case with unremoved heat is presented as a steady reconstruction.
- **Gross and net shortfall:** unresolved, with the model mechanism identified. At the best tested steady case the entire remaining gap to 1253 / 1000 (109.987 / 108.997 MW) is the thermal-efficiency difference 0.3907 versus 0.43, itself the turbine-inlet difference 628 versus 708 °C. Our implementation of the published network with the published duties does not reach the published temperature, and the round-3 review shows the printed quantities cannot all hold under the stated heat-exchanger assumptions; the cited cycle method (Schleicher et al. 2001, round 4) takes the turbine inlet as an input and has no per-branch exchangers; what the ARIES-CS calculation itself did with the three branches is not stated in the obtained sources. Not an upper bound; not corrected.
- **Recirculating power and per-circuit heat:** corrected in round 1 (accounting alignment; source-informed partition), within budget except the helium circuit (+25 MW, bounded by the pump-heat return fraction).
- **Thermal composition (≈ 60 MW):** bounded, not corrected; no model input exists for the source's 90% return of balance-of-plant power.
- **Cross-paper flow scaling (our mapping):** bounded and quantified. Scaling the primary flows with the duties changes the 1600 kg/s network case by -14.195 MW unmet and 12.757 MW net; the PbLi flow alone accounts for it; no steady result changes. The contract's § 8 rule now scales flows with duties (§ 6a).
