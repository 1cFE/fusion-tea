## 6. Source-consistency question to be independently checked

Q1 `[DERIVED]`: In any counterflow network the cycle stream cannot be heated above the blanket-helium inlet temperature (456 °C) by blanket-helium heat. Within Raffray's own case (2822 MW over the 352 K span, C ≈ 8.0 MW/K), with cycle helium entering the train at 355 °C, the blanket-helium stage can deliver at most ≈ C × (456 − 355) × ε ≈ 800 MW at ε ≈ 0.98, or ≈ 570 MW at the printed 30 °C approach, of its 1192 MW `[r1]`. Stated without a flow estimate: in one series stream the heat fractions must equal the temperature-span fractions, and the blanket stage can span at most 426 − 355 = 71 K of the 352 K rise (20%) while carrying 42% of the heat, so no cycle flow rate satisfies both (the blanket stage needs ≥ 16.8 MW/K, the remaining stages ≤ 5.8 MW/K) `[r1, reviewer's strengthening]`. Fig. 12's temperatures, Table II's duties and the whole-flow series arrangement therefore cannot all hold at once; the systems paper's 43% applies the cycle efficiency to all 2916 MW as if it were available at the PbLi temperature level. The model's unmet heat and lower efficiency are the physical consequence of representing per-branch exchangers explicitly. This reading, and its arithmetic, are to be checked against the page images before it is used in attribution.

## 7. Comparison discipline

Source-conditioned cases supply only fusion power (and, in literal cases, the Raffray heat accounting); everything downstream is calculated from chosen hardware. A change that brings the model closer to 1000 MW is admissible only when its input is source-supported or independently justified and recorded here; no input is fitted to the published output. Thermal MW and electrical MW are never added.

## 8. Addendum 2026-09-25 — charged-power destination and the divertor circuit

`[SOURCE]` Lyon Table IV (p708): alpha heating power to plasma 462 MW, radiated power 365 MW (75%), power to SOL 97.0 MW, power to divertor 98.1 MW; alpha-particle loss rate 5%. `[SOURCE]` Lyon p704 text: P_div ≈ 0.1 P_α; Fig. 18 (p704): radiation goes 89% to the first wall and 11% to the divertor `[r2]`. Cross-check `[DERIVED, r2]`: Raffray's plasma-side divertor items 23.6 + 23.6 + 48 = 95.2 MW × 1.030 = 98.1 MW, matching Lyon's printed divertor power exactly, so the 98.1 MW includes the alpha loss. `[SOURCE]` Raffray Table V (p741): divertor helium removes 186 MW including 23.6 MW alpha loss, 23.6 MW conducted, 48 MW radiated and 24 MW friction at 2365 MW fusion; `[DERIVED]` the remaining 66.8 MW is nuclear heating of the divertor structure, 68.8 MW scaled linearly to 2436 MW.

`[ASSUMED, A1]` The model's partition sends (1 − f_rad) × charged power + deposited auxiliary heat to the divertor circuit with f_rad = 0.25, i.e. 385.4 MW at 2436 MW, against a source-informed ≈ 167 MW (98.1 charged + 68.8 nuclear) before friction. The divertor circuit therefore receives ≈ 218 MW too much; the blanket circuits receive ≈ 198 MW too little (2580.7 − 2382.4 MW), the difference being the 20 MW of deposited auxiliary heat that the corrected setting removes `[r2]`. `[DERIVED]` A source-informed setting of the existing partition input is f_rad = 1 − 167/487.2 = 0.657 with deposited auxiliary heat 0; this carries the divertor's nuclear share as charged power because the model has no divertor nuclear-heating term, and gives blanket deposition 2580.7 MW against Raffray's 2496 MW × 2436/2365 = 2571 MW. Inter-coolant exchange: Raffray 111 MW at 2365 MW is 0.0469 of fusion power against the inherited 0.04. These are inputs to the diagnostic study, marked source-informed, not fitted to any electrical output. The divertor circuit's heat sits at the highest temperature (573–700 °C), so moving ≈ 218 MW from it to the blanket circuits (≤ 456 °C helium, ≤ 738 °C PbLi) is expected to make the helium-stage limit in Q1 bind harder, not to ease it.

## 6a. Addendum 2026-09-25 — Q1 after the fresh thermal-cycle review (round 3)

`[AGENT]` A fresh thermal-cycle reviewer (`q1-thermal-cycle-review.md`, brief `q1-thermal-cycle-review-brief.md`) checked Q1 against the primary diagrams (Raffray Figs. 12–13, `WI-086 …/raffray-p736.png`), the temperature and duty definitions (Tables II, III, V), the cross-paper mapping and our cycle representation. Verdict: the apparent contradiction survives; deciding check: first-principles reconstruction. `[DERIVED, r3]` With the printed per-branch duties and temperatures and a cycle capacity rate that closes 2822 MW over the 352 K span (8.02 MW/K), the blanket-helium stage must put 1192 MW into cycle fluid entering at ≥ 355 °C and leaving ≤ 456 °C, which needs ≥ 11.8 MW/K (zero approach) or 16.8 MW/K (30 °C approach), more than the whole cycle flow; so no counterflow arrangement of the three exchangers (helium first then parallel, all parallel, helium in parallel with the others, helium last) delivers 707 °C, and the statement is no longer tied to the implemented network. `[SOURCE-FIG, r3]` Fig. 13's inset draws one lumped hot leg 385 → 737 °C against the cycle 355 → 707 °C with 30 K at both ends; the published 707 °C / 0.43 is consistent with a lumped single-hot-leg treatment in which heat fractions equal span fractions, while Table II gives the helium loop 42% of the heat over 21% of the span. `[r3]` Definitions confirmed: 456 °C is the helium exchanger hot inlet (3261 kg/s × 5193 × 70 K = 1185 MW); the 30 °C is the exchanger terminal difference; 0.95 is the recuperator effectiveness; the duties are the heat carried by each loop to its exchanger, friction included (p737). Cross-paper: 2822 × 2436/2365 = 2907 MW against Lyon's 2916, both including returned pump heat; the mapping moves the cycle flow by 3% and cannot supply the missing capacity rate; the contradiction is internal to Raffray's description. Our representation is generous to the source (approaches 3.6–4.8 K against the printed 30 °C; the published 355 °C inlet would leave the helium stage able to carry 569–810 MW of 1192).

**What this establishes and what it does not** `[OWNER framing, owner-supplement-r3.md]`: the published per-branch thermal description cannot be reconciled with the published cycle outlet by any exchanger arrangement, and our per-branch model is not the cause of the residual. It does not establish what the ARIES-CS cycle calculation actually did with the exchangers (its reference, p737 Ref. 7, is not retained) nor what the actual design's exchangers would achieve. Undetermined: that treatment, and whether Lyon's 2916 MW includes ≈ 50 MW of balance-of-plant heat.

`[DERIVED, r3]` Scaling artefact (reviewer finding 2): the § 8 rule scales duties by 1.030 while holding the primary flows at Raffray's 2365 MW values, so the PbLi return needed for full transfer falls from 455 to 447 °C, below the cycle-side inlet at 1600 kg/s (452 °C); part of the 56 MW PbLi shortfall at that flow is this mapping artefact. Flows scaled with duties (helium 3359, PbLi 27,666, divertor 291.5 kg/s, with the pump capacities carried along) are quantified in the round-3 study. Model approaches are much tighter than the source's 30 °C; imposing 30 K would enlarge the shortfall (reviewer note 5).
