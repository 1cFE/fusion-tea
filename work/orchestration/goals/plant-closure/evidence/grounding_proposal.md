## 3. The proposal — one goal, three closures and the breadth flows at one pin, then the acceptance packet

**The question.** The plant's three held multipliers — pumping power, cycle efficiency, availability — set the headline and the feasibility fences yet nothing in the design reaches them; the fuel, exhaust and vacuum systems have no physical quantity at all. Make each a computed producer of the design at one pin, carry the breadth as reduced flows with every missing input surfaced, measure what that does to the machine as designed and the cheapest feasible machine, and hand the owner a fresh consolidated re-grade with a disposition for every cell still below target — the evidence the reveal-readiness decision needs.

**Why one goal and not three.** The three reports share one interface contract (the heat ledger, operating-versus-installed heating, calendar-versus-operating time, one owner per quantity) and every track touches the same four files (`mfe_power_balance.sysml`, `mfe_account_costs.sysml`, `mfe_plant.sysml`, `stellarator_plant.sysml`) plus the oracle. Three sequential goals would pin three times and re-read the window three times; one goal with one coordinated integration pins once. The runbook bounds a round by one pin and one study, not by the number of tasks, so the ambition the owner asks for lives in the model increment before the pin.

**The shape of round 1 (the strategy, written when the round opens):** three model items through the modelling PM — the primary loop with a temperature-compatible cycle; the lifecycle calendar; the fuel, divertor-heat and vacuum reduced flows — each dormant-safe with a direct term that reproduces the held value exactly (the WI-024 / WI-039 pattern), so the pinned baseline is reproduced bit-for-bit in the new package and the study attributes every move; one pin; one study with a compatibility arm, single-closure attribution arms, the inherited window re-read at both heating levels, and the engineering-lever envelope (flow, loss, blanket outlet temperature, outage duration, unplanned fraction, burn fraction, recovery semantics, radiated fraction); then a fresh non-author re-grade of the touched rows with a disposition table for the rest.

**What is deliberately not modelled in round 1 and carried as a written disposition:** the primary-system equipment costs (pumps, piping, IHX — no admissible cost source; R7.S bounded), the fuel-processing plant cost (R10.S bounded), the building layout (R9.S bounded), computed neutronics for the breeding ratio (R2c bounded at the source geometry), the estimate-class statement (R12.S — the shared-driver envelope comes from the study; the maturity inventory and any AACE class are round 2's). The research is explicit that more research cannot manufacture these; the goal says so rather than pretending.
