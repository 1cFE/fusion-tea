## Question

Facts the grounding established (`evidence/grounding_proposal.md` § 2; `evidence/grounding_probe/summary.md`):

1. **Three held multipliers set the headline and nothing in the design reaches them.** `p_pump` 195 MW, `eta_th` 0.333 and `availability` 0.85 are instance constants (`models/designs/stellarator_09/stellarator_plant.sysml@d981670f` lines 779–785, 1117); the power balance reads them as scalars (`models/library/analyses/mfe_power_balance.sysml@b45b4b01` — `p_th = mn·p_n + p_α + p_input + eta_p·p_pump`, `p_the = eta_th·p_th`, `recirculating = … + p_pump + …`); availability multiplies every $/MWh in both LCOE forms and schedules the replacements (`mfe_lcoe_dcf.sysml@8f3b510c` `annual_energy = 8760·P_net·A`; the handwritten CAS72 impl `L_cal = L_FPY / A`).
