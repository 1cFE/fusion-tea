# Findings for the whole-plant write-up

[AGENT] Working observations, not accepted goal learnings or a released study result. Each claim keeps its evidence and qualification.

## What transfers

The repaired conversion assembly already owns source-return controls, installed equipment offers, finite cooling, separate power/cost ledgers and independent numerical evidence. Its primary loop also exposes the electrical demand excluded from the original subsystem metric. These interfaces let a whole-plant assembly add upstream scope without changing the verified cycle calculations. Evidence: conversion-interfaces.md and its original model/record references.

## What needs a new relationship

The supplied source heat must be converted to fusion/fuel demand with a stated neutron-multiplication and deposited-heating balance. It cannot be used directly as fusion power. The new plant denominator must subtract primary and all other upstream electricity while retaining primary friction heat already delivered to conversion. Evidence: upstream-accounting.md, source-role and power tables.

The complete numerator needs an explicit fixed reactor account inventory and service schedule. The predecessor's common-cost correction does not supply that inventory. Existing active account channels differ from dormant legacy totals. Known coil lifetime and replacement omissions also require explicit lifecycle treatment. Evidence: upstream-accounting.md, native WI-080 receipt and active accounting bindings.

## What remains to learn

The new native model and study have not run. No whole-plant winner, LCOE value or robustness claim is established. The design must distinguish conditional supplied-source economics from the inherited core's failed qualification checks, preserve capacity failures at unsupported loads, and show how unresolved reactor costs/loads affect the paired conclusion.
