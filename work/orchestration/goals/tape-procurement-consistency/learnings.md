# Learnings: Tape procurement consistency

Accepted learning deltas are appended after round review.

## L-001 — One physical tape quantity for procurement — 2026-09-15

[AGENT] Under the held same-tape construction and composition, tape volume divided by full composite cross-section supplies the procurement quantity. Reference density prices inverse-density inventory; the selected envelope enters once through effective density. Current and bore length retain their physical quantity response. Non-tape pack materials and composite-conductor winding operations retain separate accounts.

Accepted by independent Round 1 review [final-review.md@1c75bdde](evidence/final-review.md), supported by WI-060 and the [64-case native study@f7015074](../../../../exploration/stellarator_e2e/studies/20260915-tape-procurement-consistency/record.md). This is a bounded conditional accounting result, not a general conductor design qualification.

## L-002 — Quantity consistency leaves technology and price assumptions explicit — 2026-09-15

[AGENT] Higher density on unchanged tape reduces procurement by consuming unknown current margin. It does not establish improved critical-current performance. Cross-source construction, fixed ungraded fractions, continuous tape inventory, direct unit price, extrapolated envelopes, fit and manufacturing effort retain their stated assumptions and limits. Numerical agreement does not validate those assumptions.

Accepted by independent Round 1 review [final-review.md@1c75bdde](evidence/final-review.md). The [answer](answer.md) keeps those limits beside the economic and feasibility results; all open findings retain their [dispositions](evidence/finding-dispositions.md).
