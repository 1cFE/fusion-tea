## Geometry available for insulation

[INHERITED: WI061 implementation] `models/library/analyses/mfe_winding_pack_fit.sysml:4–7` computes nominal, internal-build, pack, insulated and required cross sections separately. Nominal area is `wp_side²`; internal dimensions add excluded-sheet pitch; ground insulation adds twice the per-face thickness; assembly clearance adds twice its own allowance. Stellaris currently assumes internal build fractions0/.025 and ground thickness.003m (P:338–345), plus assembly clearance.002m (P:496). These are conditional local centered/aligned envelopes, not a qualified bill of materials.

[AGENT] A prospective insulation inventory can expose internal area difference `pack_x*pack_y - nominal_x*nominal_y` and ground-shell difference `insulated_x*insulated_y - pack_x*pack_y`. Turning these into purchased volume requires a justified coil-set cross-section distribution and integrated path length, material occupancy and process/waste assumptions. The available typical circumference, coil count and pack-volume distribution can support an explicitly assumed approximation; the worst-coil section cannot silently represent every coil. Nominal Table7 fractions already fill the complete original pack volume, so internal-sheet inclusion must remain explicit. `required` includes assembly clearance: its excess volume is not purchased insulation or resin by identity. Thermal-area proxies and total support mass do not determine that inventory either.
