## Release limits and implementation instructions

1. **Use the current sized side.** The design's phrase “reference wp_side” is ambiguous. Its formula must bind the current `wp_sizing.wp_side`, as the later design text and existing fit binding imply. Only the relative set factors stay frozen. Bind V from geometric pack volume, and verify the area-factor identity at off-reference points. Do not scale by the field envelope again. Arbitrary independent changes to the two set factors need a disclosed distribution interpretation.
2. **Keep two zero alternatives distinct.** The design's additive sheet scenario is honest if identified as assuming PROCESS winding excludes that purchase. For the uncertain cost-coverage alternative, suppress only the incremental charge while holding sheet geometry and adverse fit/current results fixed. Setting internal build to zero instead changes the physical inclusion scenario and the fit result; it cannot serve as a cost-only overlap comparison. The stock charge is not a demonstrated missing cost or correction. A priced subtotal plus an explicitly unpriced remainder is permissible; a claim of complete manufacture is not.
3. **Preserve generic callers.** Give the new Magnet Capital insulation input an explicit zero default or bind zero at every nonparticipating caller. Zero means the incremental scenario is disabled, not that insulation manufacture is free. Ground remains an unpriced envelope; internal laminate resin is not charged again. The new cost-only pathway must preserve old material, current, support and fit outputs under held inputs, including failures.
4. **Support ABI recommendation.** Retaining the two legacy calculation inputs, documenting them as historical factors and exposing their product as the effective all-in rate is the smaller implementation. It preserves override/replay meaning without implying a scientific material/fabrication split. I recommend that option unless one public rate is an explicit delivery objective. The proposed retirement is also defensible if its two-input conversion, generic callers, manual body, mappings and current replay consumers are handled exactly as `consumer-map.md` requires. Neither choice improves the underlying rate evidence. Frozen historical records must retain their original meanings.
5. **Separate evidence tests.** Perturb sheet price at fixed geometry, geometry at fixed rate, winding rate independently and support rate independently. Test zero layers, unequal sections/aspect, clearance invariance, finite domains and overflow. Generate/reconcile the new capital once. Keep unknown grade, yield, waste, joints, cable manufacture, impregnation, assembly and mixed price years visible beside the subtotal. Those are acceptance limits, not inferred zero costs.

The bounded arithmetic and source review establishes the design's stated conditional validity. Native compilation, generic caller compatibility, integration, numerical perturbation checks and final economic interpretation remain implementation-stage evidence; none is claimed here. A parameter study is not required for this scoped accounting result: reference/off-reference checks and independent perturbations can establish the proposed identities without making sampled design-space claims.
