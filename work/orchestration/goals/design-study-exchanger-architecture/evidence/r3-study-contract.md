Undefined states and control failures cannot pass. Engineering acceptance and price/source/geometry support are distinct fields. A thermal pass does not establish physical procurement availability or qualify a plant.

Where only one architecture passes, report the tested feasibility difference and that case's conditional cost; there is no paired LCOE or extra-cost allowance against a failed case.
