## Missing capabilities and scope

[AGENT] Published-reference engineering feasibility requires a dimensioned local cavity/manufactured envelope and assembly contract, exact conductor construction with local field-angle capacity, and transferable divertor transport/target geometry. A source-faithful plant-power/cost reconstruction additionally requires separate water-blanket and helium-first-wall heat partition, circuits, pumping and cycle performance. The paper's subsystem assumptions and the present reduced model do not supply these capabilities.

These capabilities are essential to certify a published-reference plant. They are not necessary to answer the owner's allowed alternative of explaining unresolved deviations. No geometry enlargement, angle multiplier, radiation/transport/heat-limit change or coolant redesign is justified merely to recover a pass. Unpriced qualification and hardware, assumed tape price and mixed cost years remain separate from computed cost sensitivity.
