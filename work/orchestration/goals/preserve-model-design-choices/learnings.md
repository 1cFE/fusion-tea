# Learnings: Preserve model design choices

## Round 1 — accepted 2026-09-20

[AGENT] Accepted at the Round 1 review in trail.md, with fresh non-author approval in evidence/final-review.md.

- Inspect actual generated public roles and consumers. In the tested emitter, negative-literal parcel coordinates became constants; signed offset inputs restored translation freedom. This observation is specific to the tested emitter, not a general SysML rule.
- Explicit supplied-design contracts preserve the reviewed freedoms without a universal inverse solver. This result does not establish arbitrary future calculation directions.
- Physical inadequacy, unsupported empirical performance and conditional price applicability are different results. Preserve their separate margins, domain checks and definedness.
- Missing pump maps, support strength, offered fuel stock and arbitrary topology remain named scientific limits. Supplied-design evaluation does not justify inventing those relationships.

## Round 2 — accepted 2026-09-20

[AGENT] Accepted at the Round 2 review in trail.md, with fresh non-author approval and correction in evidence/round2/final-review.md.

- A supplied specification and price pair supports conditional equipment evaluation. It does not predict the purchase price of an arbitrary rating upgrade.
- Check operating-point support separately from the raw capacity margin. Unsupported or inactive equipment must not receive affirmative capacity credit from a zero-valued carrier.
- When exact floating-point categorical agreement matters, document and reproduce the declared arithmetic operation order while independently checking the equation. Never repair disagreement by snapping the capacity margin. This does not require copying implementation details for every independent calculation.
- Public-interface coverage requires exact consumer edges and retired parameter identities, including their original groups. Counts alone do not establish semantic completeness.
