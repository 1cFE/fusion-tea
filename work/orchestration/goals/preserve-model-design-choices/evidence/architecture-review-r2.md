The proposal retains distinct set-average and reference corrections rather than collapsing them into one factor.

Reuse that guard if generated execution still reaches it; unnecessary duplicate validation is not required. The plan correctly leaves structural qualification unavailable.

The new and retired controls are explicitly listed, including retirement of the old independent ampere-turn input and selection output channels. Removing mandatory selection nodes while retaining their definitions as optional construction helpers is consistent with MR-7. The direct evaluator has an explicit direction; the proposal replay case demonstrates that a construction direction can produce a design without remaining a mandatory dependency. This does not require a universal inverse dispatcher.

Migration literals are acceptable as agent-selected preservation of the existing modeled design, subject to the plan's native-output capture before regeneration. Their provenance must remain migration evidence, never new empirical support. If the old integrated plant cannot execute because of an unrelated domain refusal, record that refusal and capture the original native component outputs with their exact inputs; do not widen a domain to obtain defaults. Acceptance still requires the separately specified valid native propagation case.

The acceptance cases cover the consequential distinctions: supported insufficient/sufficient pack, unchanged hardware under excitation, independent fit failure, support thermal/cost propagation, ceiling versus current-performance separation, retired-key rejection and helper-independent replay. All retained stress/current predicates remain limited relationships; passing them cannot become a claim that supplied support mass or arbitrary winding shapes are structurally qualified. Whole-magnet MR-7 compliance remains **unverified pending implementation and native evidence**; the proposed binding contract itself satisfies the reviewed design-choice requirement.
