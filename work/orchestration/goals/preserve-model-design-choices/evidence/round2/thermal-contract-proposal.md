# Thermal equipment capability contract proposal

[AGENT] Proposal for independent preimplementation review, 2026-09-20. No production model changes or runtime experiments were performed. This investigation used existing model and executable code only. Authority: MR-7 in `modeling_project/REQUIREMENTS.md:87` and `../round2-owner-extension.md`. All parameter names and capability assumptions below are agent proposals, not owner-originated requirements or vendor qualifications.

## Result and boundary

Introduce independent supplied ratings beside the existing operating demands, with signed rating-minus-demand margins and native constraints. Keep the operating balances and their independent operating inputs. A capacity failure must leave the chosen equipment and represented purchase cost intact. An unavailable demand, invalid thermodynamic state or unsupported rating condition must not become a passing capacity result.

These are conditional scalar rating screens. A declared pump flow/head/power rectangle is a necessary screen against an offered specification; it is not a pump curve. A declared refrigeration load at 20 K is not interchangeable with the same watts at 77 K. A gross-MWe turbine class does not establish steam admission, exchanger conductance or condenser capacity. Preserve existing `machine_off_design_performance_qualified`, `turbine_equipment_qualified`, `installed_sg_capacity_qualified` and `cooling_site_qualified` as false until separate evidence establishes them.

The smallest coherent repair has four owned packages: primary/intermediate cooling, steam conversion, heat rejection and refrigeration. Electric distribution and magnet supplies admit narrower real-power checks. Each package owns its selected purchase class and its offered ratings; no runtime formula sets the selected class or rating from operating demand.

- `models/designs/generic_mfe/mfe_subsystems.sysml:268` owns a turbine with explicit steam-generator, reheater, HP/LP turbine, pumps, condenser and generator children. `models/library/analyses/mfe_matched_steam_cycle.sysml` exposes their operating flow, work, heat duty and required UA. These are currently demands without installed-capacity checks.
- `models/designs/generic_mfe/mfe_subsystems.sysml:655` owns heat rejection. Its computed water flow includes the circulating-pump heat balance; its total rejection includes conversion and pump losses. It does not include every plant auxiliary heat sink.
- `models/library/structure/mfe_plant_systems.sysml:304` owns separate cold and intercept loads. - Native verdict omission is confirmed: `ihx_capacity_ok` is a computed Boolean in the cooling executable, but there is no corresponding assertion in either plant SysML file and no IHX entry in `exploration/stellarator_e2e/generated/modules/constraints/constraintreportaggregatormodule.py:18`. ## Rating conditions and validity

For every active screen expose `demand`, `supplied_rating`, signed `margin`, `applicable`, `rating_conditions_supported` and `capacity_ok`. Multiple dimensions retain their individual margins; the package Boolean is their conjunction. Export these through the owned part and into regenerated native verdicts. Disabled equipment is explicitly not applicable. Unknown support is not a physical shortfall and must remain distinguishable from a negative supported margin; the generated verdict/report must not count either missing support or disabled equipment as affirmative physical adequacy.

The supplied ratings are conditional design declarations, not empirical predictions. Keep operating conditions visible beside them. Require explicit rating conditions or an explicitly declared assumed operating envelope supplied as part of the selected package; never create an envelope from the demands being tested. A point-rated default applies only at its declared state. Out-of-state demand can still be reported numerically, but capacity verdicts are unsupported until an envelope is supplied. Independent insufficient/sufficient rating changes at fixed conditions remain evaluable. Where changing load also changes state, fixed-hardware demand tests must either stay in a previously declared envelope or demonstrate the unsupported result.

- Helium conditions include gas properties, suction temperature/pressure, discharge pressure and represented parallel-machine topology. The current primary law uses a constant reference loss coefficient and constant-density approximation. Neither that law nor the purchase suction coordinate provides a compressor map.
- Salt conditions remain the modeled HITEC 270–465°C scenario and represented density/efficiency assumptions. Pump price domains remain separate diagnostics: size factor 400–100000, 50–3500 gpm, 50–200 ft, shaft at most 200 hp; motor source domains remain those in the current executable. A purchased design outside a cost-source range does not become a qualified design because its offered flow exceeds demand.
- Matched steam retains fixed table pressures 6.2/0.8 MPa, interpolated property-table bounds, superheated admission, two-phase LP endpoint validity, admitted-heat equality and positive exchanger approaches. Offered UA applies only to the stated temperatures/fluids; use existing available flags, never the zero placeholder on unavailable UA. Legacy/direct-cycle modes can screen gross MWe but have no steam demands with which to pass steam equipment screens.
- Cooling water defaults retain the declared 25→35°C water scenario and 20 m operating head with the modeled pump heat balance. Capacity uses total represented rejection, not condenser duty alone. Site temperature availability, tower performance and auxiliary heat streams outside this boundary remain unresolved.
- Cryogenic inventory currently admits cold 10–30 K, shield 77 K and ambient 300 K, with Carnot fractions in (0,1]. A scalar cold rating must carry its actual temperature; it must not silently retain qualification as temperature changes. Lead/support/radiation inventory transfer assumptions remain unchanged.

## Cost and default capture

Use the coordinator's `entering-oracle.json` capture at checkpoint `53a0366a` as the proposed migration basis, subject to author verification of exact output paths, units and numerical precision against native execution. Record selected package ratings and their declared conditions as literal defaults in the design. Label them `[AGENT] entering-model design capture`, not measured or qualified capacities. Store both old operating purchase driver and new supplied purchase driver for reconciliation. Never call the capture inside evaluation, provide a fallback `rating=demand`, or multiply live demand by a hidden margin. Existing independent helium/salt purchase points remain unchanged; a new check is allowed to find them insufficient.

For cryoplant procurement the minimally coherent offered-class conversion is `selected_electric_equivalent_MW = rated_cold_W*1e-6*(T_amb_rated-T_cold_rated)/(f_carnot_cold_procurement*T_cold_rated) + rated_intercept_W*1e-6*(T_amb_rated-T_shield_rated)/(f_carnot_shield_procurement*T_shield_rated) + rated_direct_electric_MW`. Feed this selected class to the inherited cryogenic cost law. The rated temperatures and procurement Carnot fractions are supplied equipment assumptions, distinct from operating values; the equation repeats the existing declared fraction-of-Carnot interpretation and needs independent review. Changing operating COP must change electric demand without repricing fixed hardware. The auxiliary-cooling part of the same cost remains a separate selected thermal procurement class because the current model lacks an allocated auxiliary cooling heat-demand balance.

An aggregate turbine or heat-rejection price cannot make every independent auxiliary rating cost-sensitive without an additional price relation. Preserve that limitation explicitly: rating dimensions are specifications of the supplied aggregate package, and the inherited price responds only to its selected class. A supplied package quote can replace the aggregate proxy if separate class/capability association is unacceptable. Do not invent scaling exponents or correlate flow and MWe to conceal the missing pricing science.

## Required acceptance evidence

1. For each rated dimension, keep operating conditions fixed and supply one below-demand and one above-demand rating. Check unchanged input echo, signed margins, native violated/satisfied outcome, and the documented purchase behavior. Test exact equality and adjacent representable values using the strict signed criterion `margin >= 0`, with no tolerance snapping. Numerical tolerances apply only to oracle agreement. For zero-demand stages use zero rating as the sufficient case; do not force a physically negative test rating.
2. Hold every hardware/rating/procurement input fixed while varying valid source heat. Verify propagation to primary flow/work, IHX required area, salt demand, steam flow/power/UA and rejection water flow/work. Purchase, spares and replacements remain fixed. Record conditional unsupported states when the chosen rating envelope is exceeded. Do not require the same whole-plant pass result.
3. For refrigeration change an existing independent cold-load driver at fixed cold/shield/ambient temperatures and fixed ratings. Cold/intercept loads, wall-plug demand and margins respond; selected refrigeration class/capital remain fixed. Separately vary operating Carnot fraction while retaining procurement fractions and verify demand changes without cost changes.
4. Verify the inherited installed IHX check in the native verdict inventory. The numeric predicate is `equipment_enabled AND rating_conditions_supported AND (ihx_installed_area - ihx_required_area >= 0)`, with disabled represented separately as not applicable; report the signed square-metre margin without combining it numerically with any other units. Use valid differing circuit counts with the existing fixed-per-unit geometry to get insufficient/sufficient supplied assemblies; compare area requirement, installed total inventory and price. Also test fixed assembly with changed heat. A new independent geometry interface is unnecessary for this acceptance pair.
5. Reject nonfinite/negative ratings and unsupported thermodynamic inputs. Preserve price-validity flags and equipment/site qualification false values. Exercise disabled and legacy/direct modes; inactive or missing physics must not manufacture affirmative capacity credit.
6. Check exact native producer/consumer paths, module count factors, child/package ownership, public schema exposure, frozen package operation and oracle/actual-output agreement. Regenerate the complete assertion inventory; a passing aggregate report must include all applicable new screens rather than only exporting their Boolean outputs.
