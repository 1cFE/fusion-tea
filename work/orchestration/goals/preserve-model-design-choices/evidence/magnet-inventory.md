## Finding

**Violated:** Installed winding cross-section is always selected from operating current and a density rule. Mode 0 uses a field-grade enlargement; mode 1 uses available tape current and an inventory multiplier constrained to at least one. Neither mode accepts a supplied pack side. A second coupling derives installed conductor length and winding labor from the ratio of operating ampere-turns to turn current. Freeing pack side alone would leave this hardware change active.

**Compliant locally, conditional globally:** Casing fit compares a required local envelope with independently chosen radial allocation, transverse cavity and allowances without enlarging the casing. The conductor-current routine evaluates supplied inventory and preserves negative current margins within its declared empirical domain. Their integrated hardware inputs still come from the violating selection chain.

**Unverified installed-design meaning:** Casing/support mass estimates are stored-energy cost proxies, disconnected from the chosen cavity/wall geometry. They cannot establish structural adequacy of an independently supplied casing or support mass. Do not relabel their empirical mass scaling as a validated required structural capacity.

## Proposed directional contracts and unresolved choices

[AGENT] Recommend a finite set of explicit construction/evaluation directions, selected in an analysis contract rather than a mode hidden inside a physical part:

1. **Evaluate supplied winding:** Choose pack area/side, installed reference turns (or an equivalent installed conductor-length specification), coil count/shape/distribution, composition, casing allocation and operating turn current. Calculate ampere-turns, axis/peak field, actual density, tape length/capacity, fit/stress margins and inventory-based costs. Changing operating current preserves pack, turns, tape, conductor length and their procurement/fabrication costs. Operating loads and demand-based comparison proxies may change, clearly identified.
2. **Construct a design from a chosen current density:** Explicitly choose ampere-turns and density, calculate area through the existing identity; choose either turns or turn current to complete winding construction. Persist resulting geometry and turns, then evaluate through contract 1. This preserves the existing legitimate calculation direction without making it universal.
3. **Propose inventory from conditional current performance:** Explicit policy uses current law and allowance to suggest area/tapes plus a declared reserve. Persist a separately identified design and evaluate it through contract 1. This is optional study policy, not mandatory component behavior. Field-grade relative enlargement is another explicit proposal policy with its own conditional authority, not a purchased material-grade identity.
4. **Choose operating field:** An explicit field-to-current analysis may invert the existing axis relation while installed turns remain fixed and turn current follows. This can coexist with current-to-field without a universal acausal solver. Validation must reject inconsistent overdetermined inputs, not silently pick one.

These recommendations add no new scientific law; they expose existing identities and conditional performance relationships. The owner/reviewer must establish which directional contracts are actually intended before implementation. Do not silently choose the supplied-geometry contract as the only replacement direction.

Scientific and scope choices that remain open:

- **Installed turns/distribution:** A single reference winding plus `f_set`/`f_wp_vol` is continuous and homogenized. Choose whether retaining this approximation with explicit installed reference turns is sufficient; discrete turns, per-coil windings, conductor grading and weakest-tape qualification need additional design/performance information. The present code does not support claiming those capabilities.
- **Structural mass and strength:** Existing stored-energy support/casing laws are proxies. An independently chosen mass can be priced, but no inspected relation maps chosen wall geometry/mass to allowable electromagnetic load. Decide whether this repair honestly exposes supplied mass with structural adequacy unavailable, or commissions a supported structural model. Do not invent a mass ratio as a strength margin.
- **Field capability:** The current law cannot evaluate below 20 T, above 32 T, other temperatures or other tape thicknesses. Broader coverage requires independent source work. Changing the domain solely to let an otherwise desirable design execute is prohibited. A proposed policy need not execute during direct evaluation, but the actual conductor-performance calculation retains its domain.
- **Shape freedom:** Bore-scaled circumference, reference inductance, peak-field transfer, fixed section distribution and stress concentration describe a fixed configuration family. Supplying arbitrary length/aspect/count does not provide scientific support for its field/stress response. Support the existing family explicitly or identify the new source/model need.
