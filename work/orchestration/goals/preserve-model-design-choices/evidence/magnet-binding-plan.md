# Proposed magnet binding and acceptance plan

[AGENT] Proposed implementation contract for independent review, 2026-09-20. No production files changed. Read with `magnet-inventory.md`, MR-7, and `owner-prompt.md`. The owner-originated principle is preserving design choices; the concrete parameterization below is an agent proposal under the coordinator's bounded assignment. The assignment explicitly excludes a universal solver, per-coil geometry and discrete-turn extensions. This plan uses existing square-effective-pack and continuous set-transfer identities.

## Contract and correction to the inventory disposition

A supplied winding consists of effective pack side, installed reference turns, coil shape/count/distribution, material composition, and installed support/casing masses. Operating turn current determines ampere-turns and field; it does not replace those supplied quantities. All positive-current evaluations retain the existing conductor-performance domain. Insufficient current capacity and local fit produce failed constraints; unsupported field/temperature/construction remains an error distinct from inadequacy.

The earlier inventory described support/casing laws as cost proxies with an unresolved installed-mass choice. Reviewer evidence establishes that support mass also feeds cryogenic nuclear heating (`mfe_plant.sysml:100` and `mfe_cryo_inventory.sysml:70`). The proposed repair therefore supplies both masses independently. Their energy-scaled correlations remain optional design suggestions outside evaluation. Supplied mass drives price and represented thermal inventory; structural adequacy of that chosen mass remains unavailable. No new stress-versus-mass law is proposed. This supersedes the earlier suggestion that disclosure alone could dispose of the support-mass binding.

## Exact public choices and equations

Prefix `P` below is `stellarator_09__stellaris__`. Paths refer to native physical parts; generated entry keys concatenate that prefix with double-underscore names.

| Calculated reference ampere-turns [A-turn] | Existing `magnet.coil.I_coil` becomes an output bound from new `magnet.winding_state.I_coil` | `I_coil = reference_turns * turn_current`. Retire old `Pmagnet__coil__I_coil` entry key; preserve its native meaning and downstream consumers. |
| Calculated effective density [A/mm²] | Existing `magnet.winding_pack.j_wp_effective` bound from `magnet.winding_state.j_wp_effective`; optionally expose at magnet level under the same descriptive name | `j_wp_effective = I_coil / (wp_side * wp_side * 1e6)`. | Chosen per-coil casing mass [kg] | Existing `magnet.casing.m_casing` becomes input; new key `Pmagnet__casing__m_casing` | Finite and nonnegative; retains existing `n_coils` and `legacy_casing_fraction` cost accounting. At zero legacy share it remains uncharged to avoid double counting included total supports. |
| Field ceiling [T] | Existing `magnet.winding_pack.B_max` remains input | Only the existing conditional peak-field ceiling assertion consumes it in evaluation; changing it does not enlarge inventory. It is not a certified material grade. |

Define reusable calc `'Winding Operating State'` in `models/library/analyses/mfe_magnet_field.sysml`, usage `winding_state` in `'Magnet System'`. Inputs are `reference_turns`, `turn_current`, `wp_side`; outputs are `I_coil`, `j_wp_effective`. Validate inputs and positive finite products/divisions before returning. Reusable calculations belong in the library under the sysml-conventions skill; use part reference bindings for composition. No formula referencing a sibling part is to be hidden in the design instance.

Change `'Winding Pack Procurement Cost'` to accept `reference_turns` instead of its `I_coil` and `turn_current` inputs. Its conductor length becomes `n_coils * reference_turns * f_set * c_coil`; tape volume/area, material cost, labor rate and sum equations remain unchanged. This directly represents installed length and avoids an unnecessary divide-by-operating-current identity. Parallel-tape and current-capacity calculations retain existing tape-length/conductor-length ratios and distinct set/reference factors.

Volume remains `f_wp_vol * n_coils * wp_side² * c_coil`. Composition still determines tape residual, so no independently redundant tape-volume input is introduced. Coil circumference remains the existing fixed-family bore scaling. A demand test that changes only excitation keeps all geometry fixed. Varying radial build is a design change and can legitimately alter length and inventory.

## Public controls retired from the mandatory evaluator

Remove native part-owned selection controls and their generated entry keys: winding-pack `j_wp`, `B_grade_ref`, `field_exponent`, `sizing_mode`, `inventory_multiplier`; magnet `c_support`, `e_support`; casing `m_casing_ref`. Remove calculated part `conductor_quantity_factor`. Remove mandatory usage nodes `conductor_grade`, `current_sizing`, `wp_sizing`, `support_mass`, `casing_mass` and their now-orphaned installed-selection exposes (`required_tapes`, `required_conductor_area`, `required_pack_area`, `required_effective_density`, `selected_effective_density`, `tape_available_current`). Their old channels must not silently acquire a different meaning. Native `I_coil` remains calculated, and `j_wp_effective` now explicitly reports supplied-design density.

Keep casing `W_mag_ref` and coil `I_ref`, `R_ref`, `a_coil_ref`: the stored-energy performance relation still uses those anchors. Keep all current-performance factors and domain controls, `B_max`, fit geometry, composition/material prices, cold extra volume, legacy cost-comparison coefficients and `legacy_casing_fraction`. Independent casing/support masses must pass a dedicated finite/nonnegative guard or an equivalent guard in their existing native cost/thermal consumers; do not rely on multiplying zero legacy share to conceal an invalid supplied casing mass.

Existing library definitions `'Winding Pack Sizing'`, `'Conductor Field Capability'`, `'Current Driven Pack Sizing'`, `'Magnet Support Mass'`, and `'Magnet Casing Mass'` may remain available to explicit proposal analyses. They are not instantiated in the mandatory plant evaluation graph. Their existing unit tests remain valid as tests of optional directional formulas. No automatic-selection switch is added to evaluation. An optional proposal record must identify inputs, relationship/policy, resulting side/turns/masses and domain, then submit those selected values to the same supplied-design evaluator. Building a new search UI or general inversion dispatcher is unnecessary for this repair.

## Compatibility and audit trail

This changes the active public design contract. Do not accept old `I_coil`, `j_wp`, sizing-mode/multiplier or grade/mass-correlation controls under their old names and reinterpret them as chosen hardware. New native route must reject retired keys with a migration message. A conversion of a legacy input set is an explicit one-time preparation using its old package/equations to record the selected side/turns/masses; the new evaluator then reads that supplied design. Historical packages, manifests, results and frozen replay stay byte-identical. Active package identity and bindings are regenerated; preserve a before/after entry-key and output-channel diff. Default migration alone does not imply all historical sweeps are equivalent: changing current previously changed hardware, and now does not.

## Acceptance cases for independent review

1. **Installed-turn identity and default round trip:** Capture the old native design, migrate the four selected quantities, and verify new native ampere-turns, volume, tape/conductor length and each magnet cost component agree within documented floating tolerance. Verify no mandatory selection module remains in the pipeline and all changed controls have actual consumers. This is compatibility evidence, not the sole compliance test.
2. **Insufficient/sufficient supplied pack at supported field:** Keep geometry, installed turns, composition, temperature and performance scenario fixed. Select an operating turn current by the existing linear field identity to give a peak field of 22 T with extrapolation disabled. Use side 0.20 m and 0.60 m as deliberate evaluation cases; confirm independently that the existing current law predicts opposite signs before running. If a case does not straddle the boundary, calculate the boundary from the existing law and record two selected areas strictly below/above it; never let evaluation resize either. Assert returned side and input side identical, current predicate fails/passes, tape/material/insulation inventory and costs follow selected area. Other plant predicates may fail and remain reported.
3. **Fixed hardware, varied excitation:** At the same supplied pack/turns/count/shape/composition/casing/support masses, use turn currents yielding 21 T and 23 T under the unchanged geometry. Assert side, turns, volume, tape/conductor length, parallel-tape counts, procurement/material/labor/insulation/structure costs, cryogenic support nuclear heat and static thermal surfaces stay fixed. Assert ampere-turns, field, density, current margins, stress and current-lead heat change according to existing equations. Stored energy changes but cannot change supplied masses. Cryogenic operating electric demand may change through leads; no false full-plant-cost invariance claim.
4. **Local fit pass/fail without resizing:** Hold excitation and radial allocation fixed. Vary supplied pack side across the independently computed positive cavity limit under existing allowance/aspect equations; verify both fit margins and exact retained side. Test transverse cavity separately by changing `interior_y`, which does not change field. Fit insufficiency is not a calculation error; no casing/support mass is inferred from cavity changes.
5. **Chosen mass propagation:** Independently vary supplied total support mass at fixed current and geometry; verify structure cost and structure-nuclear heat change linearly with mass under existing rates/density/load. Vary supplied casing mass with nonzero legacy share and verify its cost term; at zero share verify no double charge. No structural adequacy result is fabricated. Reject negative/nonfinite supplied masses even when their charged fraction is zero.
6. **Ceiling/performance separation:** Change only `B_max` around a supported evaluated peak field and verify the ceiling constraint changes without tape, side, turns or costs changing. Do not change current-law domain. Below 20 T, above 32 T, non-20 K or wrong tape thickness must remain unsupported; above 24 T requires the existing permission and extrapolation output flag.
7. **Domain and unit boundaries:** Reject zero/negative/nonfinite side or turns and overflow/underflow in operating-state products/divisions. Check 1e6 area-unit conversion. Do not claim zero-current integrated evaluation: its current-performance model remains outside domain. Confirm finite negative current/fit margins are returned, and exact equality behavior remains the existing predicate contract without snapping outputs.
8. **Explicit proposal replay:** For one retained optional density-to-area helper, select side from `(I,j)`, choose installed turns, record the resulting design, and evaluate with helper execution unavailable. The result must depend only on selected design and operating inputs. Optional current-based and energy-mass helpers, if retained as callable proposal routes, receive the same replay separation test.

Acceptance runs through the regenerated native package and active study mapping, with independent oracle arithmetic. Component cases may isolate valid magnet conditions when unrelated whole-plant domain restrictions prevent a full run; report those separately and obtain at least one supported full native propagation case. Review the exact implemented binding diff before acceptance; this proposal itself does not certify MR-7 completion.
