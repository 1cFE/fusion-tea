## Reconstructed definitions

The current equations are S=H-C, E=F H-C, N=H-F H, and q_peak=q_ref N/N_ref. Thus H=C+E+N and S=E+N. N is power available to divertor interception before the capture fraction; its existing name `p_target_nonrad` can be mistaken for deposited target power. The current source normalization already embeds the capture fraction, sharing and spatial footprint. Multiplying q_peak by 0.99 would count capture a second time.

## Plant boundary

[INHERITED: models/library/analyses/mfe_power_balance.sysml:118 and :138] Total plant thermal power is mn P_neutron+P_alpha_total+P_aux_operating+Q_pump_recovered. Radiation is a destination of existing plasma heating, not added thermal generation. The loop receives the source thermal sum. The model has no separately priced or sized divertor coolant loop. Retained alpha heating in the sustainment chain uses 0.95×0.2002 P_fus; total alpha in the plant uses (3.52/17.58) P_fus. Consequently H is not the whole non-neutron plant thermal term: their difference includes unretained alpha energy and the inherited small alpha-fraction rounding difference. Neither should be silently removed from the plant sum or diverted into the target normalization.

[AGENT] With capture c, the compatible accounting extension is target deposition D=cN and non-target non-radiated deposition U=N-D. Then H=C+E+D+U. Neither D nor U adds heat to the plant sum. This is an aggregate target group: individual target sharing is embedded in the simulated profile, not independently known. Total radiation F H is not a measured radiation load on any one surface; missing radiation view factors remain an explicit limit on total surface heat qualification.

## Accommodation and cost limits

[INHERITED: models/library/analyses/mfe_account_costs.sysml, Divertor Cost] Divertor cost scales as base×sqrt(P_th/1000 MW). It has no target-area, target-number, strike-line or transport/control cost term. That omission prevents interpreting such a sensitivity as a costed physical improvement.
