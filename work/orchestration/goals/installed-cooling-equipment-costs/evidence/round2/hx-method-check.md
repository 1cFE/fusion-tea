[INHERITED: `knowledge/sources/progress_in_the_design_development_of_eu_demo_helium_cooled/output.md`, §2.2.2/Table2, previously image checked] The retained exchanger is TEMA NFN, two tube passes/two shell passes, helium tube-side and HITEC shell-side. [AGENT] A defensible proposed construction is an explicitly assumed stainless exchanger with tube metal, shell, heads, tubesheets, headers/nozzles, baffles and internal supports individually counted. Use `Cfactory2017 = 310 USD/kg * Mdry` as the ANL-based material/fabrication analogy, and optionally `Cinstalled2017 = 1.026*Cfactory2017` as the separately declared ABR component-installation analogy. The numerical factor is sourced; its applicability to helium/HITEC and this construction is an assumption. Do not label the result vendor-qualified or assign unsupported confidence bounds. Main-loop pipes, valves, external supports, building services and controls require separately reconciled account ownership.

[AGENT] For straight tubes, `Mtube = rho*Aoutside*t*(1-t/Do)`. This exact cylindrical expression lets the current thermal area determine tube count/total length while wall thickness determines material inventory. Shell/tubesheet/head inventory must then be added. Pressure-driven thickness must reflect actual differential/design pressures and temperature/material assumptions;8MPa helium is tube-side and cannot silently be imposed on the salt-side shell. A conservative chosen shell rating may be stated as a costing assumption.

[EXAMPLE; AGENT arithmetic only] At the original OB area10310.6913m² and assumed steel density8000kg/m³, tube-wall choices1.0/1.5/2.0mm produce78.156/113.986/147.651t of tube metal and corresponding ANL-rate tube contributions24.228/35.336/45.772MUSD2017. They exclude all shell, head, sheet and support mass. This illustrates why explicit wall and component geometry matter; tube-area cost alone hides the material consequence.

[AGENT] Do not infer tube bore from the reported61.5m³ total helium volume. ## Decision-facing conclusion

[AGENT] Prefer a reviewed component-mass conceptual IHX design over forcing the generic Seider area method into range. The available original supports both exchanger finished-fabrication rates and a separately stated component-installation factor. The remaining work is to choose and expose a coherent construction and its uncertainty, then verify that its area/flow arrangement is consistent with the retained thermal calculation. This is a meaningful implementation route, not a finding that missing vendor qualification prevents conceptual costing. No external searches, source registrations or model edits were performed in this follow-up.
