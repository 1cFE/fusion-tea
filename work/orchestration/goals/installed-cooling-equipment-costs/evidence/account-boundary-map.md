## Existing physical chain

[INHERITED: models/library/analyses/mfe_primary_loop.sysml:14] Let Q be source heat in MW, N loop count, cp in J/(kg K), and ΔT blanket rise. The forward equations are `mdot=Q*1e6/(cp*ΔT)`, `mdot_loop=mdot/N`, `dp=f_loss*dp_ref*(mdot_loop/mdot_ref)^2`, `r=p/(p-dp)`, `T_comp_in=T_in/(1+(r^((gamma-1)/gamma)-1)/eta_is)`, `W=mdot*cp*(T_in-T_comp_in)/1e6`, `P_elec=W/eta_drive`, and `Q_IHX=Q+W`. Pump electricity and recovered heat feed the power balance independently. The generated implementation executes the same equations at `exploration/stellarator_e2e/generated/handwritten/mfe_primary_loop/primary_coolant_loop_impl.py:18`; finite-positive cp and ΔT guards are at line 14.

## Rollup and lifecycle

[INHERITED: models/designs/generic_mfe/mfe_plant.sysml:465] `CAS22=powercore+remote_handling+installation+coolant+aux_cooling+waste+fuel_handling+other_rpe+I&C`. `cas2x=buildings+CAS22+BOP+special_materials+CAS28`; CAS29 applies contingency to cas2x; CAS30 applies its construction-time-scaled indirect fraction to post-contingency CAS20 (`:474-503`). Reported CAS60 is excluded from this capital total because the headline DCF independently applies midpoint construction interest.

[INHERITED: models/library/analyses/mfe_account_costs.sysml:599] CAS50 separately adds shipping, tax and insurance fractions of CAS20/CAS30, plus spares on CAS23–28 and power-scaled startup/decommissioning. Replacing the direct account alone will propagate general contingency/indirects, but will not create dedicated equipment spares or maintenance.
