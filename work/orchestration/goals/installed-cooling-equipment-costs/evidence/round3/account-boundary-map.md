# Cooling account map after implementation

The cost account structure (CAS) is the hierarchy that adds equipment, installation, shared services and annual expenses into plant cost. In equipment mode the new cooling estimate replaces the entire old C220200 allowance. The old calculation remains executable only for matched comparisons.

| Owner | Previous treatment | New treatment and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| C220201 primary cooling |166MUSD times net power relative to1000MW | Two parallel helium circulators per circuit; explicitly dimensioned main pipes, nine hot/cold branches and fitting mass; separate installed accounts. No remaining primary power-law allowance. |
| C220202 intermediate transport |40.6MUSD times thermal-power ratio to the0.55 power | One fixed geometry helium/HITEC exchanger per circuit; two salt pumps per circuit; separate salt pipe/fitting account. No retained aggregate IHX allowance. |
| Cooling inventory and spares within C220200 | No separately sized account | Priced ex-vessel helium and intermediate salt inventory subsets, plus reserves; one uninstalled spare of each machine type. Unmeasured in-vessel and conversion inventory remains excluded. |
| CAS22 blanket | In-vessel cooling structures within blanket | Retained. Ex-vessel pipe bill does not repurchase blanket steel. |
| CAS23 conversion | Aggregate turbine-generator, condenser and feedwater coefficient | Retained. Steam generator assigned once to Row8; its inclusion in that coefficient is unverified. No extra steam-generator price or invented deduction. |
| CAS25 ultimate heat rejection | Separate power-related allowance | Retained; new transport piping ends at conversion supply/return. |
| Magnet cryogenics and auxiliary cooling | Separate cryogenic/auxiliary accounts | Retained; new helium inventory is hot transport coolant, not magnet coolant. |
| CAS21 buildings | Buildings and structural building supports | Retained. No second building or building-foundation factor added. Local pipe support/insulation detail remains residual scope. |
| CAS29/30 | Plant contingency and construction-scaled indirect services | Retained. CAS30 explicitly owns procurement services/project engineering. ORNL15.5% is used only to reproduce its installation denominator; supplier first-design work is distinct. |
| CAS50 | Shipping on direct capital; spare fraction applies only to CAS23–28 | Subtract delivered initial cooling purchases from shipping base. Cooling spare purchases are explicit and outside that spare-fraction base. Retain shipping on other capital and contingency; this is partial scope normalization, not a proven empirical decomposition. |
| CAS71 routine operation and maintenance | Staffing/direct O&M allowance | Retained as owner of routine cooling service labor by assumption. Add explicitly assumed helium/salt make-up purchases before existing annual-cost levelization. |
| CAS72 major replacements | Blanket/divertor calendar only | Add cooling machine and tube-bundle replacement present values, annualized over the plant horizon. Initial spares and first-design fees are not repeated. |

Fabrication and delivery use the ANL finished-component mass price and BNL supplier-package price where applicable. Separate sourced installation analogies cover site work. They are not vendor quotations for this plant. No raw-steel price is multiplied again into the finished fabrication price. The generated calculation exports purchased, installation, inventory, spare and delivered-price subtotals; tests check their sum and their downstream consumers.

The new cost estimate is incomplete in clearly identified places: detailed isolation valves, local supports/insulation, salt pump hot-shaft features, expansion/drain equipment, trace heating, nitrogen cover, waste handling and additional outage requirements. Their cost significance has not been established negligible. The account decomposition can meet R7.S3 without certifying a complete installed-plant price. See the independent final assessment after the focused study for the actual grade.
