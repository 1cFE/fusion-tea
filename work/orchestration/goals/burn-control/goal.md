## Question

> Can the sustainment check become the operating-point condition the sister systems codes use — the power balance closes with an auxiliary heating that is zero or positive and no larger than what is installed — so that a point whose alpha heating exceeds its losses is declared no operating point of this model, and once it is, what do the feasibility counts, the cheapest machine, and the reading of the machine as designed become?

`[AGENT] — proposed.` The form is chosen at grounding, on evidence, because the evidence decides it (below). The review's instruction was to ground before choosing; grounding did the choosing.

**What the model does today** `[INHERITED]`: `sustainment_ok` asserts `p_aux_required ≤ installed plasma-coupled heating` (`models/library/analyses/mfe_viability.sysml` 'Sustainment Limit'; the operand from `models/library/analyses/mfe_plasma_sustainment.sysml`, `p_aux_required = p_rad + W_th/tau_E − p_alpha_heat`). A point whose computed requirement is negative passes. At the current pin 3,654 of 7,712 executed points do; 510 of the committed record's 681 driven points ignite at the rule; every feasibility claim on the package is made on the driven set by hand (`stored-energy-basis` L-005).

**The six facts that decide the form** `[AGENT]`, each verified this session:

1. **The sister codes write the balance as an equality and define an ignited design point as `P_aux = 0`.** PROCESS "imposes a power balance as an equality constraint" (Lion 2021 `output.md` L164, L211; Eq. 7 `P_loss =! … + P_aux`); reactor runs add "Require ignited design points, Q ~ ∞" (Lion 2023 Tables 4.2, 4.5, 4.7) and the optimiser moves density, temperature and `f_ren` to sit on the contour. The over-ignited region is outside the described space in both POPCONs: Stellaris paints it white ("the plasma would ignite (high T, high n)", raw PDF p. 9 Fig. 15 caption); Lion 2023 says those points "would be ignited, given the imposed models" and are held back only by wall-load and beta limits (L2154). No source models what an over-ignited plasma does; no source quantifies a burn-control mechanism (research report § Q2, § Gaps).
2. **The paper's own design point is ignited, and its 50 MW is a start-up number.** Table 5 (raw PDF p. 10, page render): point A fusion gain ∞, auxiliary power at operation point 0 MW; point B Q = 182 at 14.77 MW. The prose (p. 9): the red dashed line "indicates a path to reach the operation point 'A' with minimal auxiliary heating power. According to the employed model, about 50 MW of auxiliary heating power would need to be installed to reach the desired operation point." The model's fence reads Table 2's "Required plasma-coupled ECRH power 50" as the installed steady-state ceiling. That is the paper's *point-B* sense of the number (a driven point under the installed heating), not its point-A sense. Sighted at the `stored-energy-basis` grounding (`evidence/handoff_20260904-170919.md` line 15; `evidence/w_counterfactual/NOTES.md` § Verified bases), never routed. Lion 2023's one study with an installed power treats it as a scanned input for a non-ignited pilot plant (L2140, L2411); installed heating is never derived from the operating-point balance in any source.
3. **Density control relocates an ignited point to a far more expensive one, and it is still unstable there.** Oracle-side probe at the pin (`evidence/grounding_probe/summary.md` § Reading (a)): at every probed ignited point a lower density exists where the balance closes at zero heating at the same temperature (0.56–0.71 of the swept density for the fence-feasible ones); every fence then holds, but fusion power falls to 0.40–0.57× and LCOE rises 1.52–2.34× (200.9 → 305.0; 217.4 → 352.9; 207.0 → 483.4; 201.1 → 453.7 $/MWh). The committed headline (−188.8 MW) cannot be held fence-feasibly at its temperature at all (recirculating fraction 0.73 against 0.5; LCOE 1,584). The design geometry's ignited points gain nothing (the wall violation there is set by geometry, current and temperature, not by the ignition margin). Every held state sits on the falling branch of the ignition curve (below).
4. **Left alone at its swept density, every probed ignited plasma settles far outside the fences.** The stable root at fixed density lies at 20–41 keV with the wall peak at 2.3–5.4× its limit and beta at 1.4–2.6× its limit (probe § Reading (b)); the excess alpha power carrying it there is several times the installed heating. The model has no physics that arrests this. `operating-point-closure` L-002 (a solved-temperature form is the wrong architecture at this model state) stands and is reinforced: its revisit condition — a stable feasible burn window inside the limits — is not met at the swept densities of any probed point.
5. **The whole 13–18 keV window is on the falling branch, driven points included.** `d(p_aux_required)/dT` is negative at every probed point (−5 to −115 MW/keV) and at every density-controlled state (probe § Reading (c)). A driven point there is held by feedback on the heating — its control authority is the auxiliary power itself, which can be turned down to zero. An ignited point has no such authority: no non-negative heating closes its balance, and adding loss is a mechanism the model does not have and no source quantifies. **This is the physics content of the second inequality:** `p_aux_required ≥ 0` is the condition that the installed heating system can hold the point, not a fiat and not a claim that ignition is infeasible. The baseline sits on the falling branch too (−18.5 MW/keV); with its heating fixed at 49.08 MW its other equilibrium is at 21.2 keV with the wall at 2.06× the limit.
6. **The model's fence checks the hold, not the access.** At the paper's own coordinates the fixed-density ramp from cold needs a maximum of 311 MW coupled at 3 keV (113 MW at 12 keV) against 50 installed; lowering the density lowers the ramp maximum (63 MW at 0.3×) but raises the heating needed to hold 14.63 keV (63–78 MW), and no fixed-density path probed reaches point A under 50 MW (probe § Reading (d)). A two-dimensional start-up path, which is what the paper's 50 MW is the minimum of, was not mapped. The access requirement is therefore **not** this goal's to model: it is a named follow-on (§ Limits), and the model text discloses that the fence's installed side is the hold ceiling in the paper's point-B sense.
