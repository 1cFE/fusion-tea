## Independent financial check

The live generic plant places fuel handling in CAS22 once, includes it in the CAS2x subtotal, applies CAS29 contingency to that subtotal, then calculates CAS30 from post-contingency CAS20. Preconstruction enters overnight later. See `models/designs/generic_mfe/mfe_plant.sysml:500`, `:520`, `:537` and `:595`.

Let d be the selected fuel-account change and L its newly identified direct installation; let c be CAS29 contingency, k the construction-time-adjusted indirect factor and cs the separate supplementary contingency. At fixed physical inputs and other accounts, the live equations imply ΔCAS20=(1+c)d and ΔCAS30=k(1+c)d. Excluding `(1+c)L` from freight therefore gives Δfreight-base=(1+c)(d−L). The design's tax term uses ΔCAS20, and insurance uses Δ(CAS20+CAS30). All supplementary terms then receive `(1+cs)`. Consequently its stated ΔCAS50 and Δovernight identities are correct. A comparison between two active new-method cases correctly substitutes ΔL for L.

The current generic installation subtotal excludes fuel processing (`generic_mfe/mfe_plant.sysml:457`). Spares use CAS23–28, not fuel equipment. The proposed freight exclusion removes contingency-loaded installation without changing prior cooling/facility exclusions. Existing `Supplementary Cost` confirms shipping, tax, insurance and internal contingency placement (`models/library/analyses/mfe_account_costs.sysml:599`). The design's requirement for positive-c and nonzero-cs tests is necessary to expose mistakes hidden by current zero defaults.

The existing reported CAS60 is excluded from `total_capital`; the headline DCF and separate 1cfe capital-charge route retain their distinct financing treatments (`generic_mfe/mfe_plant.sysml:602`, `:727`, `:751`). The design preserves those treatments and correctly requires both outputs to respond through their current consumers. It does not add reported IDC a second time to overnight capital. Historical package freight/tax/insurance content remains unknown; the retained generic charges are explicit proxies, not a source-proven decomposition.
