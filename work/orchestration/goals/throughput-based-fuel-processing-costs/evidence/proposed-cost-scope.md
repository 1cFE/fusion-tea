# Proposed costing scenario and account boundary

[AGENT] This is a proposal for source/design review, not an implemented price or a claim that the plant has selected qualified equipment. It uses the conventional process described in the T-002 research report and preserves every existing physical fuel-flow and loss assumption.

## Process basis

Price the represented plasma-exhaust function as conventional palladium-alloy cleanup and impurity treatment followed by cryogenic isotope separation. The running D+T inlet is the WI-069 processor output; it is neither recovered flow nor calendar-average demand. Near-equimolar isotope composition is computed, but source-like impurity burden and cleanup to less than 1 ppm noncondensibles are explicit external applicability conditions. Approximately atmospheric,20K-class separation service is the source reference. The source cascade provides conventional isotope separation; no column or product-service discount is taken for a simpler desired DT product. No spare train or guaranteed recovery is asserted. Existing 99% recovery remains a conditional physical assumption, not a cost-source guarantee.

The new ITER sources support conventional process engineering above the modeled isotope flow. ORNL supplies the historical economic law. Neither evidence stream certifies commercial performance or validates the exponent empirically. The use is a conditional conceptual estimate, with source-price uncertainty separated from physical demand.
