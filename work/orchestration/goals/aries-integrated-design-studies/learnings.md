# Learnings: Conditional integrated ARIES design studies

Accepted learning entries are append-only.

## L-001 — Local exchanger savings are distinct from supplied-fuel effects

- **Evidence:** local-response record @ a396a2e0 and independent local-review.md.
- **Scope:** Tested45k–55k areas under provisional U/price assumptions, fixed physical baseline; no minimum adequate area or hydraulic qualification.
- **Implication:** Couple supplied area choices while reporting unchanged power/fuel and purchased cost separately; retain inadequate controls.
- **Supersedes:** none.
- **Accepted by:** Round1 review,2026-09-22.

## L-002 — Density ordering changes with the fixed supply scenario

- **Evidence:** local-response record @ a396a2e0, paired24maps and independent replay.
- **Scope:** Imposed density-demand scenarios with selected hardware held; confinement/control unsupported.
- **Implication:** Examine a bounded interior interval while holding feed/service fixed; separate the zero-purchase floor from physical performance claims.
- **Supersedes:** none.
- **Accepted by:** Round1 review,2026-09-22.

## L-003 — The interior demand benefit is conditional on fixed breeder feed

- **Evidence:** Coupled-design record @ d976da47 and eight independent native replays.
- **Scope:** The tested 4.875e20 density scenario with 45k/45k areas; imposed demand and supplied feed remain unqualified.
- **Implication:** Compare the baseline and reduced-area demand candidates under identical uncertainty settings and both supply cases; report inventory savings separately from purchase-floor effects.
- **Supersedes:** none.
- **Accepted by:** Round 2 review, 2026-09-22.

## L-004 — Purchased-area savings survive the tested assumptions

- **Authority:** [AGENT] Evidence-derived conclusion.
- **Evidence:** Robustness record @ d57ee5cd, 42 matched area comparisons and evidence/final-review.md.
- **Scope:** Four fixed representatives, 21 OAT settings and two fixed supply scenarios; no joint uncertainty distribution or physical minimum.
- **Implication:** Retain the 0.190957–0.636126 USD2004/MWh area saving as conditional on supplied thermal and cost relationships.
- **Supersedes:** none.
- **Accepted by:** Independent final review, 2026-09-22.

## L-005 — Demand rankings reverse under supplied-fuel and economic assumptions

- **Authority:** [AGENT] Evidence-derived conclusion.
- **Evidence:** Robustness matched comparisons and eight independent native replays in evidence/final-review.md.
- **Scope:** Near-floor feed100 advantage reverses at low neutron multiplication, availability and T price; high-density disadvantage reverses at low availability and T price.
- **Implication:** Report gross demand, fixed feed, purchases and every cost contribution together; these results do not qualify an operating optimum.
- **Supersedes:** none.
- **Accepted by:** Independent final review, 2026-09-22.

## L-006 — The conditional question required study adaptation without model changes

- **Authority:** [AGENT] Evidence-derived conclusion.
- **Evidence:** All three frozen study records and evidence/final-review.md.
- **Scope:** Unchanged integrated executable and semantic fingerprints; thin explicit-map and reporting support.
- **Implication:** Reuse the native package for these bounded comparisons; broader physical claims still require the missing engineering models.
- **Supersedes:** none.
- **Accepted by:** Independent final review, 2026-09-22.
