# Learnings: layout-based facilities

Accepted by the independent non-author reviewer and the Round 1 coordinator coverage check on 2026-09-19. These are AGENT findings, not owner-originated requirements. Evidence: `answer.md`, `evidence/final-review-and-grade.md` and immutable study `20260918-layout-based-facilities@5f97d5f2`.

## L-001 — Separate cost selection from physical and calendar controls

Matched old/new accounting selectors can attribute cost consequences only when the compared geometry, equipment and calendar remain fixed. Both study control pairs preserve 662 physical/calendar/layout channels and all 25 predicate statuses while changing the facility account. Their old-account outputs also reproduce the separately retained entering model. Different plant scenarios remain separate comparisons.

## L-002 — Check dated initial demand and persistent shared resources

Steady throughput and one-batch storage are insufficient for this maintenance layout. Initial receipt deadlines, persistent clean/dirty inventories and shared carrier/service reservations expose failures that average demand misses. Resizing storage can meet a count while leaving outage, initial delivery or neighboring-building conflicts. The retained study cases distinguish those mechanisms.

## L-003 — Supplement equation agreement with behavioral counterexamples

Two implementations can agree while sharing an omitted physical or schedule rule. Counterexamples for equipment envelope classes, late initial delivery, shared-resource demand and zero-event branches exposed missing checks in this work. The final numerical agreement is software evidence; it does not independently qualify assumptions or physical designs.
