## Finding

The admissible Stellaris paper supplies a conceptual maintenance flow and a hall diagram with specific functions. It does not supply a dimensioned plant layout, station processing times, storage residence times, or a facility cost estimate. Registered PROCESS documentation offers differentiated volume-cost coefficients, but its mixed model families and inconsistent unit labels prevent direct adoption. The strongest immediate next steps are to recover the original PROCESS building/cost equations and their rate basis, and obtain a maintenance logistics reference for the facility functions that Stellaris identifies.

No verified evidence here demonstrates whether the historical reactor/maintenance rates include structural shielding, special floor loading, installed services, heavy foundations or facility handling equipment. The separate variable names suggest separable costs, but only original equations and their sources can establish the accounting boundary. This inventory therefore does not recommend numerical adoption of those rates yet.

`knowledge/sources/mit_canes_capital_cost_evaluation_of_advanced_water_cooled/output.md:624-636` supplies a second, independently registered civil-cost method: distinguish safety/non-safety concrete and structural/reinforcing steel; track containment strategy; retain fixed support functions and HVAC/formwork/ductwork effects. It explains why enclosed volume or concrete alone does not predict building cost. Its 2018 cost basis and water-reactor calibration are recorded at `knowledge/SOURCE_INDEX.md:1036-1049`. This supports a commodity/function costing alternative, but no hot-cell transfer or numerical unit-rate validation was completed here.

`knowledge/sources/doe_netl_2002_process_equipment_cost_estimation_final_report/output.md:79-105` distinguishes purchased equipment from field setting and installation bulks. Its chemical-process equipment method is useful to define boundaries, not to price a nuclear hot cell. The installed-helium research note at `knowledge/research/pending/20260918-143536_installed-helium-cooling-cost-methods.md` carries explicit scope/price-year cautions but is historical to the now-advanced cooling work. The current cooling account map and implementation, inventoried separately by the coordinator, control overlap with CAS21; this note does not reinstate an old cooling gap.

## Focused acquisition questions

1. [AGENT] Recover primary PROCESS building-sizing and original/Kovari cost equations, with their cited civil-cost reference. Resolve coefficient units, family/year, included services/shielding/installation, and whether maintenance costs depend on maintenance-building volume, component size or aggregate allowance. If the original reference is blocked, seek an admissible independent nuclear-facility quantity/cost basis instead of treating code defaults as sufficient.
2. [AGENT] Seek maintenance-task and parallel-system methodology from Stellaris reference [343]: Farquhar et al., *Parametric modelling of EU-DEMO remote maintenance strategies and concepts*, Fusion Engineering and Design 145 (2019) 29–32, DOI `10.1016/j.fusengdes.2019.04.097`. First screen it; tokamak module counts and access geometry would remain analogues. Ask for explicit task times, dependencies and resource occupancy, not an availability target alone.
3. [AGENT] Seek credible hot-cell design evidence linking component envelopes to receipt, processing, clean assembly, waste separation and storage capacity, including decay-storage residence and simultaneous campaigns. ITER or EU-DEMO facilities may provide applicable function/clearance examples after screening, but need explicit transfer limits to the much heavier Stellaris module.
4. [AGENT] Seek a source-supported conceptual shielding/service basis tied to radiation and occupancy assumptions, plus installed civil/ventilation/handling costs with price year and exclusions. Stellaris reference [344], Nuclear Ventilation Forum design guidance (2009), is a named lead for containment ventilation; it is not an established hot-cell price or shielding source.
5. [AGENT] Obtain whole-sector bounding dimensions/extraction stroke, handling-route width, and station clearance rationale. Existing component volume and vessel-end openings cannot establish these by themselves. Treat provisional envelopes and processing/storage assumptions as explicit scenario inputs; do not optimize them to recover inherited costs or availability.

This inventory establishes useful source inputs and a concrete acquisition path. It does not yet establish R9.S3, a qualified plant layout, source-verified numerical facility costs or a feasible maintenance calendar.
