# Independent facility-cost source review

2026-09-18. Fresh non-author review of LF-01/LF-04/LF-09 and the brief's admitted sources. No model inspection or final R9.S grade. All recommendations below are [AGENT]. Original report pp138, 140–142 were checked visually after markdown extraction returned empty content; pp136–137 were checked in the PDF text layer.

**Verdict: conditional method available; facility completeness remains unproven.** PROCESS supplies actual historical equations and function-specific rates, supporting a conceptual relative-cost scenario with explicit transfer assumptions. It does not establish procurement accuracy or applicability merely because volume appears in the equation. Prefer the 1990 method for transparent account separation; review the concrete geometry and account map before implementation.

**Units and arithmetic.** The [PROCESS scope statement](../../../../../knowledge/sources/ukaea_process_cost_model_scope_and_historical_references/output.md) §1990 cost model expressly places its algorithms in 1990 dollars. The [variable definitions](../../../../../knowledge/sources/ukaea_process_cost_variable_definitions_and_historical/output.md) identify 1990/2014 selectors. Their reactor/maintenance building labels incorrectly say M$/m³: [acc21](../../../../../knowledge/sources/ukaea_process_original_cost_model_superconducting_tf_coil/output.md), lines 3937–4041, multiplies volume by the coefficient and `1e-6` to produce M$. Thus reactor 400, maintenance 260, active assembly shop 460, tritium 370, electrical 380, administration 180, control 350, general shops 115 and cryogenic 460 are numerically $/m³. At LSA=4, 1,000 m³ reactor building costs 0.4 M$; LSA factors are 0.68/0.84/0.92/1.0. Select explicitly; do not silently claim a safety credit. Use consistent source-volume definitions: the original report distinguishes internal volumes from external costing volumes (p137).

**Installed scope and history.** acc21 explicitly includes equipment, materials and installation labour, excluding engineering/construction management. [ORNL/FEDC-87/7](../../../../../knowledge/sources/etr_iter_systems_code_ornl_fedc_87_7_1988/raw.pdf), pp140–142 (PDF indices149–151), distinguishes delivered/build-to-print equipment and first-of-kind component engineering from indirect plant engineering, procurement and construction management. Its defaults primarily follow September1987 TIBER II; maintenance generally follows 1986 CIT estimates. These are ancestors, not permission to relabel PROCESS's 1990 coefficients as 1987. Detailed scaling is referred to FEDC-M-88-SE-004, not reproduced here. The report expressly limits use to relative comparisons because detailed projects include more equipment and higher costs. Any conversion into the model's monetary year needs a named index; inflation alone does not validate technology transfer.

**Services and shielding.** acc2274 separately charges `1e-6 × 1000 × (volrci + wsvol)^0.8 × fkind` M$. The coefficient's nominal $/m³ label is dimensionally misleading for exponent0.8; preserve the empirical equation and its volume domain. It is nuclear ventilation, not demonstrated coverage of every HVAC service. No inspected building rate proves biological-shield thickness, dose adequacy or a separately identifiable shielding cost. Keep reactor shielding/cooling equipment distinct; assign facility shielding and services explicitly, with unresolved coverage visible.

**Handling and duplication.** Original pp138–140 put overhead cranes in facility costs, outside maintenance equipment. Transfer flasks and development are excluded; inspection systems and hot-cell machine tools are future additions. Retaining a CAS22 allowance does not demonstrate these functions are priced. Retiring CAS21 groups must preserve cranes, site work, land, service buildings and hot-cell functions, without charging them again as new equipment. Retain indirects once; explicitly map CAS10/21/22/29/30/50/71/72 and decommissioning.

**2014 alternative.** [Kovari implementation](../../../../../knowledge/sources/ukaea_process_kovari_2014_cost_algorithm_building_and/output.md), lines1568–1606 and1862–1903, uses $1,283/m³ tokamak-complex and $270/m³ light-building rates. The complex excludes hot cell. Remote handling separately bundles movable equipment and an active maintenance facility with fixed equipment, scaled by armour/first-wall/blanket mass. Its warning against individual-building outputs and bundled maintenance scope make it unsuitable for unsupported room-specific rates. Adding that whole maintenance bundle to a new hot-cell shell and retained CAS22 allowance risks duplication.

**Release condition:** demonstrate functional analogy, volume boundaries, monetary conversion, accounting exclusions and sensitivity to uncertain rates. Missing detailed rate derivation limits confidence; missing handling/shielding/services coverage blocks a claim of complete facility cost, not the conditional comparative method itself.
