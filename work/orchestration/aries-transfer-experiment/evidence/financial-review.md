## Allowed accounting boundary

[AGENT] The unchanged `1cfe-Form LCOE` definition in `models/library/analyses/mfe_account_costs.sysml:931` accepts annual capital, annual O&M/replacement and annual fuel costs over annual delivered electricity. It can receive an explicitly selected annual charge on an identified partial capital account. That result is a partial annual cost contribution per MWh, not total plant LCOE. Converting a lifetime lump sum to an annual stream requires a named calendar-life/annualization assumption. Full-power life converts to calendar life by dividing by availability; applying availability again to that already-normalized life can double-count lost production.

[AGENT] The `1cfe-Form Capital Charge` inputs are specifically overnight capital and separate IDC. A financed source total must not be silently relabeled overnight capital. If its arithmetic is reused, the design must either preserve a justified overnight/IDC decomposition or explicitly identify the semantic accounting adaptation. Existing DCF midpoint IDC must not be applied to the source total that already contains financing. O&M, replacement, fuel and decommissioning need independently identified annual amounts and scopes before inclusion; source percentages alone do not justify fitting missing terms to 77.6.

[AGENT] New source/design should keep selected capital, annualization, lifetime, availability, output power and account scope visible and independent. Initial purchase and later replacements are distinct expenditures; the same replacement stream must not enter both an upfront total and annual replacement costs. WI-087 net shaft output is not a valid net-electric denominator without an additional electrical boundary model. Existing scalar arithmetic is reusable; reference-cost reconciliation and full capital/LCOE prediction remain open.
