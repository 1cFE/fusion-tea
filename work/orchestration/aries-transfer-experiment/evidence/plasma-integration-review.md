## What the inspected source supports

- [INHERITED: Lyon p701 Eq. (3)] The density law uses `r/<a>`; Figure 12 on p702 labels its abscissa `<r/a>`. These labels establish a normalized radial presentation, but the inspected pages do not define a radial volume derivative or a transformation from this coordinate to toroidal flux. They do not establish that `2*rho` is the actual ARIES 3D volume weight.
- [INHERITED: Lyon p701 and p702 Fig. 12] Ion and electron temperatures are assumed equal. The temperature shape was selected to match the pressure profile used in MHD studies. Figure 12 supplies a plotted reference shape, not an exact analytical temperature function. Choosing a convenient temperature polynomial would remain an explicit modeling assumption unless additional source evidence supplies it.
- [INHERITED: Lyon p701] The Sudo comparison uses a line-averaged electron density. It is not interchangeable with a volume mean or Eq. (3)'s amplitude. The existing amplitude-versus-axis ambiguity remains relevant to any proposed normalization.
- [INHERITED: Lyon p702 Section V.D] Charge balance is `n_e(r)=n_DT(r)+2*n_He(r)+sum(Z*n_Z(r))`. Electron density therefore does not alone determine the reacting deuterium/tritium densities. Replacing the reaction product by `n_e^2/4` requires a separately stated pure equal-mixture assumption; it is not the source's impurity-bearing reference plasma.
- [INHERITED: Lyon p703] Helium ash is calculated from reaction and confinement behavior; the reference average ratio `<n_He>/<n_e>` is 3.35%, with `tau_He*/tau_E=6`. An average ratio does not define the local helium profile. The reference iron impurity density is a constant fraction of electron density, but that fraction is selected through the radiated-power target. Treating it as a known independent composition constant would require additional evidence or a disclosed scenario assumption.
