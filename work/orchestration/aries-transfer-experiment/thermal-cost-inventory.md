## Bounded execution options

### Option A: helium relation transfer and boundary falsification

[AGENT] This is the most useful thermal next step. Create one isolated SysML occurrence of the existing primary-loop calculation and run it through generated wrappers and TEAx. Keep the shared library and Stellaris plant unchanged. The deliverable is a table of source boundaries, computed outputs and discrepancies, not a fitted ARIES power balance.

[AGENT] First resolve the heat-boundary mismatch from retained primary evidence. Raffray p734 Table II prints helium inlet/module outlet 386/456 C, 10 MPa, pressure drop 0.30 MPa, helium heat removal 1192 MW, mass flow 3261 kg/s, pumping power 156 MW, and explicitly includes 141 MW friction and 111 MW transferred from PbLi in helium heat removal. The existing loop expects source heat excluding recovered pumping work. Inserting 1192 as that source heat and then adding compression work counts the friction contribution twice. Subtracting 141 changes the relation to the printed temperature/flow pair. This is an auditable boundary question; do not tune cp or compressor efficiency to conceal it.

[AGENT] The numbers above were checked directly against the retained `outputs-page-09.png` image, not only its extraction. Source root: `.project/active/aries-comparison-preparation/post-reveal-results/post-reveal-v1/source-evidence/retained/knowledge/holdout/aries-cs/extracted/20260920-r3/08-FST-Raffray/`. The existing extraction also retains p736 Fig12/13. Read those images and adjacent p737 discussion before selecting the conditional boundary. The paper's engineering case must remain separate from the final systems case.

[AGENT] Minimum work: one short analysis contract with input roles and source snapshot, one isolated SysML occurrence, reuse/carry of the existing typed completion, generated execution, and focused review. Use supplied under/over flow ceilings to exercise the existing capacity margin without changing required flow. Missing compressor/drive efficiency can be an explicitly agent-selected scenario for relation testing, but then pumping power is not a validated ARIES prediction. No cost claim accompanies this limited circuit experiment.

### Option B: published financial convention reconciliation

[AGENT] Isolate the existing annual-cost/energy and capital-charge definitions in a small SysML accounting assembly. The `1cfe-Form LCOE` arithmetic provides a simpler boundary than forcing the source into the headline DCF convention. This tests numerical accounting reuse without depending on geometry or coolant physics.

[AGENT] Minimum work: a source-verified convention table, a small accounting assembly, generated execution under at least the published convention and one explicitly different convention, and a review of double counting. Required sources are already retained Lyon Eq7 and associated parameter/account tables, but this audit did not verify all numerical inputs needed for that mapping. Do not promise 77.6 USD2004/MWh reproduction until the bridge has been checked. This is conditioned accounting, not independent plant prediction.

### Option C: civil takeoff pricing

[AGENT] `Facility Civil Cost` can run unchanged on independently supplied concrete/formwork/rebar quantities and rates. It would demonstrate exact commodity-to-cost reuse. The reviewed ARIES evidence currently provides account totals, not a verified six-quantity civil takeoff; therefore an ARIES-specific execution is source-blocked at this scope. A generic scenario would prove execution, but would add less transfer evidence than Options A or B.
