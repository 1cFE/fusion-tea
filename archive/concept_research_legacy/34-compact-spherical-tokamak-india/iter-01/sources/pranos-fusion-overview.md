## Founders Background
- **Shaurya Kaushal**: PhD in Computational Physics from JNCASR Bengaluru (2017-2024). Prior roles at UK Atomic Energy Authority (UKAEA), JNCASR, and CSIR-CEERI. Research areas: Computational Fluid Dynamics, Econophysics, Lattice Boltzmann Method, Applied Epidemiology, Polymer Rheology, Geophysical Fluid Dynamics, MEMS/NEMS. 11 publications, 149 citations. Note: PhD research was NOT in plasma physics.
- **Roshan George**: Limited public information. Co-founder.
- Both work from a tiny office at JNCASR Bengaluru "where they work with plasma in a glass globe."

## Technology Description
- "Developing modular compact spherical tokamaks for distributed clean energy"
- "Leverages advanced magnetic confinement systems inspired by Tokamaks and Stellarators"
- Uses "high-performance computing, plasma software, and machine learning algorithms to optimize plasma stability and efficiency"
- Fusion Energy Base profile: "pursuing a magnetic confinement fusion concept and is investigating both tokamak and stellarator concepts"
- Target: 2,500 modular fusion reactors, each 50 MW capacity, collectively generating 3,000 GWh daily
- "Technology combines lightweight atoms in controlled reactions that naturally shutdown if disrupted, producing no greenhouse gases or long-lived radioactive waste"

## Indian Fusion Context
- **IPR (Institute for Plasma Research, Gandhinagar)**:
  - Operates ADITYA-U (upgraded limiter→divertor tokamak, operational since 1989)
  - Operates SST-1 (Steady State Superconducting Tokamak, first plasma 2013)
  - Commissioned SS-ST (Small-Scale Spherical Tokamak), first plasma December 11, 2025 — India's first spherical tokamak
- **AIC-IPR Plasmatech Innovation Foundation**: Atal Incubation Centre at IPR, launched 2023, supports fusion-focused startups with mentoring, facilities, technology licensing
- India is a member of the ITER project
