## Pointers, paths and qualifications

- A repository link keeps its words and is followed by the file's path from the repository root as `code.path` (muted, dotted underline), as in the sample's Figure 2 caption. A link that is a sentence on its own ("Generated pipeline.") becomes "Generated pipeline: `path`." A link into a section of an evidence note shows the path and the section name in quotes; the evidence panel beside it holds the content.
- Web links and the main-post link stay links. "section 2.1" and the seven-step list become in-page links. Every heading gets its GitHub id (`21-modeling-a-component-separate-its-equations-from-the-plants-choices`, `272-map-feasibility-and-cost-together`, and so on).
- The scope paragraphs in 2.1 and 2.2 and the "dated model snapshots" paragraph in 2.7 are set as `.note` blocks (labels Scope, Scope, Snapshot). Nothing else is set apart.
- Figures are numbered in page order, the markdown's five and the new ones together; each keeps the markdown's alt text and caption. Each new visual gets a two-sentence reading guide in the body beside it.
- Every code block and evidence panel names its file and the reading it comes from: the 19 September 2026 capture (`graph-evidence.md`) or the 17 September study's frozen package. See source issue 1.
- Data for the interactive figures is embedded in the page as JSON blocks; no fetches. Controls (slider, chips, toggle) are styled in the page's own style block. No change to `write-up.css` is proposed.

## Interactive figures, for approval

Each reads in full with scripts disabled; the script only adds the live part.

- **I-A, 2.1, the field equation live.** Job: show the first equation of the model doing its work as a calculator. Two inputs, plasma radius `R` (10 to 14 m) and coil ampere-turns `I_coil` (12 to 16 MA-turn); `n_coils` 48 and `k_link` 0.7731 shown fixed; readout `B_axis`. Facts: the equation and defaults (Figure 2), the plant values (2.6.2 JSON), and the reference result 9.0 T (the generated function's docstring). It evaluates this one equation only; the readout says so. Without scripts: the reference evaluation as a static line.
- **I-B, 2.3, the graph explored.** Job: make "following the arrows" concrete. Choosing a node (a row of chips; clicking the SVG also works) highlights what it depends on and what depends on it, and a readout lists its exact bindings from the 18-edge list, for example choose Axis field: everything below it lights, including all three checks. Facts: the edges in `graph-evidence.json`, already in the SVG's titles. Without scripts: the SVG and the 18-edge panel.
- **I-C, 2.4.1, the surrogate.** Job: show what the handwritten function does at a design point. A thickness slider (0.50 to 1.10 m) over a chart of the five stored nodes and the piecewise-linear line; readout: interpolated TBR, or "undefined, outside 0.60 to 1.00 m" past the ends. Facts: the five nodes and the interpolation in `blanket_tritium_breeding_impl.py`. Without scripts: the chart and the five-node table.
- **I-E, 2.7.2, the map read point by point.** Job: let the reader ask what each mark is. Hovering or focusing a mark shows its radius, ampere-turns, pass or fail, the checks it failed, its conditional LCOE (withheld for the two invalid power accounts), and its field, divertor and heating margins. A toggle recolors the failing marks by the check they fail: the field limit (126 marks, small radius), the divertor heat-load limit alone (37), divertor plus installed heating (47), burn hold plus field (the two invalid accounts). Facts: `feasibility-cost-map.csv` columns. The page names `sustainment_ok` the installed-heating limit, following the prose and the report's margin units; please correct if wrong. Without scripts: the two panels as in the markdown.

The 2.7.1 comparison stays static; three cards side by side read better than a toggle, and only three of the four change combinations were evaluated.
