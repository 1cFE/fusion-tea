### F-1: Pacific Fusion commercial plant design point (250 MWe) not captured in LCOE model

- **Target:** Section 5 (LCOE-Relevant Parameters table) and Section 2 (Modeling Recommendation)
- **Finding:** Both new sources (ANS News article and GlobeNewswire press release, both dated April 24, 2025) specify Pacific Fusion's commercial power plant target as **250 net MWe with a footprint of ≤25 acres**. The analysis uses the Z-IFE 1000 MWe reference plant throughout the LCOE discussion and parameter table. This matters because the Z-IFE study showed COE scales significantly with plant size (1000 MWe, 0.5 Hz → 7.0 ¢/kWeh; 2000 MWe, 0.5 Hz → 5.7 ¢/kWeh) — a 250 MWe plant would sit in a worse economy-of-scale position than either reference case. The analysis's current framing implicitly treats 1000 MWe as the relevant design point for competitiveness comparisons; that assumption is wrong relative to Pacific Fusion's stated commercial target.
- **Recommendation:** Add a row to the Section 5 parameter table for "Commercial plant net output (Pacific Fusion target)" = 250 MWe with source attribution. Add a note in the Section 2 Modeling Recommendation paragraph clarifying that the 1000 MWe Z-IFE reference substantially understates LCOE risk for a 250 MWe first plant — economies-of-scale favor larger plants, and Pacific Fusion's commercial target is 4× smaller than the Z-IFE baseline used for all current COE estimates.
- **Priority:** important

### F-2: General Atomics partnership scope narrows target fabrication gap uncertainty

- **Target:** Section 3 (Target Fabrication at Scale — TRL 3) and Section 6 (Gap #2)
- **Finding:** Both new sources announce an expanded Pacific Fusion + General Atomics partnership (April 24, 2025) covering **full-scale power plant component design including cryogenics, manufacturing scale, and target fabrication**. General Atomics is also providing computational modeling and DIII-D National Fusion Facility access. Gap #2 in Section 6 is currently framed as "truly-unknown" with no credible path to resolution cited. The GA partnership is directly relevant: GA has extensive ICF target fabrication experience (NIF target vendor) and cryogenic systems expertise. This does not resolve the gap, but it represents a meaningful organizational development that should be noted in the gap's "Source Recommendation" column — it is now more plausible that target fabrication expertise is being acquired rather than having no path.
- **Recommendation:** Add a brief note in Section 3 (Target Fabrication at Scale) noting the April 2025 GA partnership and its stated scope (cryogenics, manufacturing scale, target fabrication). Update Gap #2 in Section 6 to change "Source Recommendation" from "IFE target fabrication literature (GA IFE Workshop)" to also include "Pacific Fusion / General Atomics partnership outputs" and add a parenthetical noting that GA's involvement (announced April 2025) represents an organizational step toward addressing this gap. Do not upgrade the gap criticality — it remains blocking.
- **Priority:** minor
