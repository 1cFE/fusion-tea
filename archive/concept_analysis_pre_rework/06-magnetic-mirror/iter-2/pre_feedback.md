### F-1: eta_th=0.70 standardization conflates DEC efficiency with thermal-cycle efficiency and overrides the framework's own physics carve-out
- **Target:** `model_setup.py` lines 99–106 (eta_th=0.70) and Section 2/5 of `analysis.md` covering the p-B11 thermal vs. direct conversion split.
- **Category:** model
- **Finding:** The model sets `eta_th=0.70` with the comment "standardized from 0.2 per scoring_framework.md (Energy Capture: Direct (charged particle))". This is the wrong application of the canonical table for three compounding reasons:
  1. **The canonical 0.70 in `scoring_framework.md` (line 320) is the DEC efficiency**, not a thermal-cycle efficiency. The framework's own table row for "Direct (charged particle, ICC, alpha collection)" cites "TAE ICC, mirror DEC" as the analogues — these are direct conversion targets, not thermal Rankine/Brayton cycles. In `costingfe`, the parameter that corresponds to DEC efficiency is `eta_de`, which this model *separately* sets to 0.70 (line 113). Setting `eta_th=0.70` therefore double-counts the DEC pathway and incorrectly upgrades the residual thermal/radiation channel.
  2. **`scoring_framework.md` lines 325–331 explicitly carve out 06-magnetic-mirror as a justified deviation case**: "06-magnetic-mirror's η_th=0.20 reflects this" (bremsstrahlung partial wall absorption). The current standardization comment ("standardized from 0.2") is the exact value the framework names as physics-grounded — and the standardization erases it without engaging the carve-out clause.
  3. **The in-file rationale contradicts the value**: the comment block on lines 100–106 still describes 0.20 as the correct physics ("only synchrotron radiation and bremsstrahlung losses are captured thermally", "only ~15-25% of fusion power is expected to appear as thermal"). The narrative and the numeric value disagree by 3.5×.
  This is structurally different from concepts like 11 or 23 where the Hybrid (0.55) canonical correctly captures the blended pathway. For 06, the framework already provides the right tools: `f_dec`, `eta_de`, and `eta_th` are *separate* parameters for a reason, and the canonical-by-category lookup loses that resolution.
- **Recommendation:**
  1. Revert `eta_th` to 0.20 (or the physics value of choice in the 0.15–0.30 band) with an inline comment of the form: `# DEVIATION: scoring_framework.md §"Justified deviations" explicitly names 06-magnetic-mirror as a physics-forced derating case (bremsstrahlung partial wall absorption). Per-category canonical (0.70 Direct CP) is inapplicable because eta_de=0.70 separately captures the DEC channel; eta_th must reflect only the thermal/radiation residual.`
  2. Cross-check that `f_dec`, `eta_de`, and `eta_th` together yield a power balance consistent with the published PRX Energy 2025 physics-limit envelope. If the three together imply Q_eng outside [0.5, 2.0] at p-B11 plausible gains, flag explicitly in Section 5.
  3. Add a 2–3 sentence paragraph to Section 2 (Power Conversion) explaining why the canonical Direct-CP eta_th does not apply: the framework's category lookup conflates "DEC plant efficiency" (eta_de) with "thermal-cycle efficiency on the residual thermal fraction" (eta_th), and for 06 these must be set independently.
  4. Run a small sensitivity sweep in the model: eta_th ∈ {0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30} at fixed eta_de=0.70, f_dec≈0.85. Report LCOE deltas. The expected sensitivity is small (residual thermal channel is ≤15% of energy), but quantifying it converts the carve-out from an assertion into a tested claim.
- **Priority:** important

### F-2: scoring_framework.md category-to-eta_th lookup is the upstream root cause — flag for framework-level fix
- **Target:** `analysis.md` Section 2 or Section 5; secondary impact on the per-concept standardization pattern across 06, 08, 31 (Direct/Hybrid categories).
- **Category:** analysis
- **Finding:** The "Direct (charged particle)" and "Direct (inductive)" rows of the scoring_framework.md canonical table (lines 319–320) assign their efficiency value to `eta_th` rather than to `eta_de` or to a combined `eta_overall`. This is a definitional bug at the framework layer: in `costingfe`, those efficiencies are not thermal-cycle parameters and feed a different leg of the power balance. The standardization comment pattern ("standardized from X per scoring_framework.md") therefore propagates the conflation into any p-B11 / aneutronic / DEC-coupled concept (06, 08, plus the Hybrid blend pattern in 31). For 06 specifically, the carve-out clause exists but is silently ignored by the per-category lookup.
- **Recommendation:** Add a finding to Section 2 (Power Conversion) noting that the eta_th standardization for DEC-dominant concepts is a framework-table artifact, not a concept-specific choice, and that the correct application requires (a) routing the Direct canonical to `eta_de` not `eta_th`, or (b) invoking the explicit "Justified deviations" clause. This is a one-paragraph methodological note, not a request to redesign the framework. The analysis should not assert standardization compliance when the result violates the framework's named carve-out.
- **Priority:** minor
