  INTERPRETATION:
  ISS04 extrapolated to this geometry predicts τ_E ≈ 76 ms at ī=0.35,
  yielding n·τ_E ≈ 2.7e+19 m⁻³·s — ~11× below the Lawson
  ignition threshold. This is Gap #7 quantified: Q=∞ closure is NOT supported
  by ISS04 extrapolation at T=10 keV. The design likely relies on one or more of:
    (a) Higher operating temperature (20–30 keV where <σv>/T² peaks for D-T)
    (b) Confinement improvement beyond ISS04 from high-field QI optimization
    (c) Plasma volume larger than 200 m³ (UNCERTAIN; no published geometry detail)
  The beta limit (5%) and Sudo density limit are NOT binding at the 2 GW design
  density — these constraints leave headroom; the ignition gap is confinement time.
  ISS95 and LHD scalings bracket ISS04; none closes the ignition gap at T=10 keV.
