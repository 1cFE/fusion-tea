Modeling notes:
  - This model represents a physically impossible scenario with today's
    technology: the 'avalanche' gain mechanism (gain >500) has zero
    experimental confirmation and the concept is ~10,000× below breakeven.
    The model estimates the LCOE floor only — if the concept works.
  - The energy conversion method is unresolved: the 2018 patent describes
    direct electrostatic conversion at -1.4 MV (no steam turbine needed).
    The 2025 website says 'conventional steam cycle generator'. This model
    uses the steam cycle assumption. Direct conversion at eta_th=0.70 would
    reduce the required laser power by ~2× and reduce LCOE significantly.
  - p_implosion=5.71 MW assumes the aspirational scenario math closes.
    The patent's stated parameters (30 kJ × gain 500 = 15 MW fusion) would
    give ~5 MWe net — a factor of 190× below the 1 GW company target.
  - Target factory cost ($400M) is a rough estimate. The per-shot dual-
    component consumable challenge (fuel pellet + capacitor-coil assembly
    at 31.5M units/year) has no precedent and no published cost study.
  - Laser system capital cost is among the largest uncertainties; 1 Hz PW
    CPA laser systems have no commercial supply chain. The framework's
    laser driver capital is an IFE default derived from indirect-drive DT
    ICF studies and may not be representative of HB11's USPL architecture.
