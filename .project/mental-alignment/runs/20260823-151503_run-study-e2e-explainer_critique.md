# External critique — run-study e2e explainer skeleton

Reviewer stance: fusion systems-code / TEA background, no investment in the project. Verdicts: FAIR (real weakness), UNFAIR-BUT-EXPECTED (readers will think it; pre-empt), DEFUSED (artifacts already answer it). Ranked most damaging first within each lane.

## Lane A — the substance

**A1. The "independent oracle" is a same-team transcription of the same SysML text — and for the hardest calc it is *the identical function*.** `verify_stellaris.py:1-8` says it mirrors "the calc defs line-for-line"; its `N_INTERVALS` block (line ~15) says "EXACT mirror of the handwritten impl … do not change one copy without the other"; `generated/handwritten/mfe_plasma_scaling/dt_fusion_power_impl.py:19-20` says "the oracle carries the identical function." Worst deviations of 4.0e-16 / 4.27e-16 (both `verification_summary.json`) are the signature of float-identical algorithms, not independent recomputation. The channel map was also calibrated against executed package evidence (`ANNEX.md § Oracle`, the annual_om 158% story). What verification actually licenses — and record § 13 states it correctly — is transcription fidelity of codegen+teax, not model correctness. **Verdict: FAIR against the skeleton's § 1/§ 3 phrase "independent implementation"; DEFUSED in the records.** Page action: rename it ("a hand-written mirror of the model's equations, sharing no code with the generated package"), state in one sentence what it can catch (codegen/execution bugs) and cannot (a wrong equation, since both sides transcribe the same SysML), and keep the § 3 station honest about the two contractually-shared functions.

**A2. Compositionality was asserted, never demonstrated: nothing was swapped.** Both A/Bs are scalar overrides on one sealed package — study 1's arms are a 3-value triple (`20260821…/study.py:34-43`), study 2's a 4-value block (`20260823…/study.py:44-48`); both records § 12: "the arms differ only in three/four input values," one fingerprint, one `predicate_ir`. No part def replaced, no package regenerated, no CAS rollup re-derived over a different tree. Any cost code with a config file does this. The costed-component ladder (skeleton § 2, "swap a component, the rollup still works") is a claim about the model source that no committed run exercises. **Verdict: FAIR.** Page action: drop "compositionality demonstrated"; present the ladder as the *designed* mechanism and the A/Bs as parameter-block swaps on one sealed artifact, with component-level swap named as not yet shown.

**A3. "Six viability constraints" deflates to at most three that can decide anything.** `tbr_ok` compares two held inputs (1.074 vs 1.05, both `design_attribute` in `model_contract.json`) — constant at every point of every study (record 1 § 4 "inert"). `net_positive` can never read `violated`: the CAS10 √p_net crashes the case first (finding `20260823…#1`), i.e. the viability check is unreachable behind the cost chain. Study 1 additionally had `beta_ok`/`peak_field_ok` inert by construction. No confinement closure, no coil-stress/radial-build loop, no availability or financing coupling (findings #1–#4 across both studies). The plant "physics" is fixed-profile 0-D algebra. **Verdict: FAIR; DEFUSED in the records/log, not in the skeleton's § 1-2 framing.** Page action: wherever "6 constraints" appears, say plainly: one is a held-input check, one is unreachable, and the live fences are beta, peak field, recirculation, wall load — all algebraic inequalities, no engineering loops.

**A4. The Nb3Sn headline is a design-point artifact stated as a conductor verdict, resting on a 0.004–0.016 knife-edge in held assumptions.** "Feasible nowhere" holds only at REBCO's geometry and temperature (R 12.7, a 1.3, Point A) — record § 17: "no claim that arm-nb3sn is infeasible at another geometry… the recirculation fence that closes the arm is geometry-dependent." A real Nb3Sn plant is redesigned around its conductor (larger R, lower B — the entire pre-HTS design lineage); the model can't ask that question. The certification addendum shows the closing node sits 0.004–0.016 of rec_frac above threshold with `f_carnot_cryo` and `vol_cold_cryo` held at the edge least favourable to Nb3Sn (empty in all six sourced combinations, but only at grid resolution). And "cheaper at every point" is pointwise LCOE at points the arm's own verdicts reject — record § 6: "the raw ordering is not a result." The skeleton's § 4 headline resurrects exactly the claim the record disclaims. **Verdict: FAIR.** See B2 for the fix.

**A5. Skeleton's verification sentence overstates coverage.** Skeleton line 112: "every channel vs the independent oracle < 1e-9." Actual: 10 channels of 75 outputs, at 12 of 3,792 and 48 of 8,288 sampled cases (`verification_summary.json` `channels_checked`, `sampling`). `p_fus` is not compared; in study 2 `p_cryo` — the one power-balance channel the arms differ through — is compared only indirectly (record 2 § 13 "Not covered" item 1). Five `pb__*` channels aren't even recordable in the store (finding #5); study 2's operand CSV is oracle-side, joined by row order with the arms sorted oppositely — a naive positional join gives 148 wrong rows (addendum, finding #6 sharpened). **Verdict: FAIR against the skeleton; DEFUSED in records.** Page action: "10 channels at stratified sample points, all six verdicts re-derived" — never "every channel"; keep the store-gap in the honesty layer, stated as an evidence-architecture defect, since it is one.

**A6. "173 parameters" counts constants and transcribed defaults as design inputs.** Contract breakdown (computed from `contracts/model_contract.json`): 118 `design_attribute`, 45 `library_default`, 10 `usage_literal`. The 118 include `mev_to_joules`, `E_fus`, and ~30 1costingFE building-block base costs (`bldg_*`, `*_base`, `om_annual_ref`). Across both studies ~12 distinct keys ever moved. **Verdict: UNFAIR-BUT-EXPECTED** — 173 is a true count of bound entry points and sealing them all *is* the point — but a reader will divide 173 by "12 swept, 2 studies." Page action: give the 118/45/10 split and the swept count next to the 173; the honest number is stronger than the big one.

**A7. The engineered windows contain the answer by construction.** Study 2's grid embeds the exact Nb3Sn ceiling node 4.69 T = 13.0/2.7667 and densifies both bands where the pre-run oracle scan had already located the fences (`study.py:59-65`, record § 11). Feasible fractions (24.2 %, 0 %) are window-relative; ANNEX § Validity masks says outright the windows "are not sourced design bounds." **Verdict: DEFUSED** (record § 11 both studies discloses "engineered" and its cost) — but only if the page carries the disclosure at the same altitude as the maps, as skeleton § 4's honesty card intends. Don't let the 24.2 % appear anywhere without its window.

**A8. Verification precision is theater next to input uncertainty.** 4e-16 parity on a model whose `p_pump` is held ~100× low (finding #3), whose cold volume and Carnot fraction are held, and whose LCOE is quoted to 6 significant figures (275.264, 204.104). **Verdict: UNFAIR-BUT-EXPECTED** (the records never claim accuracy). Page action: one sentence — "verification bounds transcription error, not model error; model accuracy is unquantified" — and round LCOE to 3 figures on the page.

## Lane B — the story

**B1 (owner's q1). Yes — § 4 must lead with the capability verdict, not the domain findings.** The page's own problem statement is "the test is running general studies" (§ 1); the reader arrives at § 4 asking "so can you?" Leading with domain findings invites the domain expert to evaluate them *as fusion results*, and per A3/A4 they cannot survive that reading — they are findings about a package with no confinement closure. **Verdict: FAIR.** Restructure § 4: open with what the two runs demonstrated about the capability (three-line intake → sealed execution → verified verdicts → every gap filed, 9/20 min turnaround), then present the two findings explicitly graded as "what this model could and couldn't push back on," in the records' own voice ("a statement about this package, not about stellarators," record 2 § 17).

**B2 (owner's q2). "Cheaper everywhere and feasible nowhere" is a confusing way to state something simpler — and half of it the record disclaims.** "Cheaper everywhere" is the trivial arithmetic of a 7× cheaper conductor price in a model where cost and feasibility never couple; it's an LCOE at non-plants. The real, defensible finding: *the cheap conductor never gets to bid — at the Stellaris design point the model rejects every Nb3Sn plant before price can matter, and what closes the region is the 4.5 K cryo load, not the field ceiling alone* (record 2 § 6). That is a statement about feasibility-before-cost ordering, and it doubles as the model-gap exhibit (no geometry re-optimization, finding #4/#3). **Verdict: FAIR — reframe; keep "cheaper everywhere" only as a subordinate "and note the price ordering is not a result" line.**

**B3. "Production discipline" vs the ledger: two studies, 22 discovery-log rows, ~11 of them the pipeline breaking in new places, 5 addenda correcting the records themselves.** The append-only correction machinery is the process *working* — the administrator's recount caught real errors (31→20, the sampling overreach) — but a hostile reader will count rows. **Verdict: UNFAIR-BUT-EXPECTED.** Page action: present the log as burn-in evidence with the count owned ("two studies, 22 filed findings, zero silent fixes"); avoid the word "production." The skeleton's "deliberately simple model, run with production discipline" should become "…run under a disciplined, auditable process."

**B4. Codegen sentence: the skeleton's proposed phrasing is now verified and should be used exactly.** The wrappers' "GAP: code generator does NOT implement calc logic" banner (e.g. `modules/mfe_plasma_scaling/dt_fusion_power.py:80`) is stale relative to the impls: all but two are `AUTO_IMPLEMENTED = True`; `dt_fusion_power_impl.py` and `levelized_replacement_cost_impl.py` are hand-written normative (`AUTO_IMPLEMENTED = False`), and `IMPLEMENTATION_BACKLOG.md`'s two unchecked rows are stale checkboxes, not missing code. **Verdict: DEFUSED** — but the page must say "all but two calcs auto-implemented from the SysML expressions; two hand-implemented and pinned," and must not screenshot the GAP banner.

## Lane C — the omissions

**C1. "Why not just a Python script?" — the oracle is the counterfactual, sitting in the repo.** `verify_stellaris.py` is 432 lines, computes the whole plant, and would run the same 8,288 points. The page never answers what SysML+codegen+teax buys over it (or over running 1costingFE, the acknowledged source of most cost relations and constraint thresholds — `mfe_viability.sysml` doc comments cite `validation.py` throughout). **Verdict: FAIR omission.** Add a short "why the machinery" panel: typed contracts, constraint identity, sealed fingerprints, cross-concept reuse — and name 1costingFE as the costing basis.

**C2. No positioning against real systems codes.** A PROCESS/SYCOMORE/ARIES reader will note: no self-consistent physics, no optimizer, no engineering loops, one concept, one design point — and the project's stated goal is cross-concept comparison, with zero evidence a second concept passes through codegen. **Verdict: UNFAIR-BUT-EXPECTED.** Pre-empt in a collapsible: what those codes do that this doesn't (yet), what this does that they don't (model-as-source, mechanical derivation, auditable study records), and the roadmap claim kept modest.

**C3. Oracle retirement leaves the verification story dated.** `STUDY_POLICY.md § 10` [OWNER]: the oracle retires from the study contract after Item 6. The page's whole § 3 verification station describes a check future studies won't have. **Verdict: FAIR omission risk; the skeleton's Judgment already flags it — keep it, and say what remains (baseline pin, fingerprints, verdict re-derivation).**

**C4. All three roles are the same vendor's agents.** Executor, critic, and "fresh administrator" are all Claude; the independent read is an LLM recount, not a human audit. It did catch real errors mechanically (recomputation from CSVs), which is the honest defense. **Verdict: UNFAIR-BUT-EXPECTED.** State plainly on the page who/what each role was; never imply human review that didn't happen.

**C5. No uncertainty, and numbers that invite false comparison.** A TEA reader expects ranges or at least a sensitivity table on the held economics; quoting 275.264 $/MWh invites "PROCESS says X" comparisons the model can't win. **Verdict: FAIR.** Never present any LCOE as a plant estimate; keep every number attached to "of this package."
