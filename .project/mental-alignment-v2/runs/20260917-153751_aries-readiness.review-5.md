## Findings

1. The TLDR is four separate assertions, but it does not give the reader the one central distinction that makes the rest intelligible: readiness has three different meanings (model depth, comparison acceptance, and freeze/reproducibility). It calls the rubric a single readiness test and leaves the later distinction to the table at lines 74–79. That makes the summary too thin for a reader who sees nothing else, and helps explain the owner's complaint that it “doesn't really explain anything.” (location: lines 48–54; cites: `design_synthesis.md` — “One mental model,” “# TLDR: the compressed answer for a reader who sees nothing else,” and “Important stuff up front.”)

2. The document follows the owner's coverage list in order: holdout, rubric, evolution, Stellaris replication, feasibility space, verification, then reveal. The sections are individually useful, but this makes the reader reassemble the relationship among them instead of learning it through a chosen teaching sequence. The only cross-cutting structure, the three readiness tests, appears inside the rubric section after the opening. (location: headings and sequence at lines 56–156, especially lines 66–82; cites: shared feedback — “Coverage list used as the outline”: “Build one mental model and weave the required material into that model instead of giving each requested topic its own section.”)

3. Several passages preserve more numerical and verification detail than the explanation needs after its point has landed. The two parallel LCOE conventions, five-decimal control results, 224/226 numeric comparisons, 19/20 verdicts, 97 tests, and 174 rows do not change the reader's conclusion: the package was reproducibly checked, but that cannot settle the scientific discrepancies. These details turn the latter third into a dossier and compete with the requested explanation of what the results mean. (location: lines 103–119 and 131–141; cites: `design_synthesis.md` — “Editorial selection” and “An explainer, not a dossier”; shared feedback — “Detail continues after the point lands.”)

(location: lines 60–62, 72, 101–103, 131–137, and 154; cites: `design_synthesis.md` — “Exact names and definitions,” “Clear on one reading,” and “Definitions before measurements.”)
