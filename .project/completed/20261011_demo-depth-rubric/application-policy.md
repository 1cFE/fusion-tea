# Applying the depth rubric without choosing the design

Effective September 20, 2026. [AGENT, ratified by the owner's enforcement request] This prospective application policy implements [MR-7](../../../modeling_project/REQUIREMENTS.md#mr-7-preserve-design-choices-separate-evaluation-from-design-selection). It leaves frozen `rubric.md`, original target levels, assessments and archived evidence unchanged.

Depth and architectural compliance are separate assessments. A high depth score does not establish that design choices remain available to the intended analysis. Report MR-7 compliance separately as compliant, violated or unverified for the assessed scope. A goal whose outcome includes MR-7 remediation cannot close its technical work solely because the depth target was reached.

The rubric's phrases “sized components”, “coil sizing” and “design-closed” specify represented engineering detail, not permission to bind installed capacity to computed demand. A component evaluated from a supplied design with explicit performance and capacity checks can provide that detail. Any automatic selection policy must satisfy MR-7; the rubric does not authorize one by itself.

Future grading and remediation plans must inspect the relevant variable roles and bindings. Preserve historical grades as historical results under their cited rubric revision; do not retroactively claim they tested MR-7. Where a row appears to require a modeling choice that conflicts with MR-7, surface that conflict rather than silently treating the row as overriding the project requirement.
