## Design decisions

These decisions are agent-originated. TEAx owns numeric publication and evidence schema `v3`; codegen retains its existing supported scalar-field emission. Fusion-tea reuses its column maps through a `required_channels` route argument. An evaluator wrapper validates successful evidence before the runner persists it and leaves execution failures to the runner. No proposal executes twice for validation, and all-failed studies still record their failed cases. Column maps do not affect stored evidence or execution identity; changed exports validate against the existing stored cases. Every exported case remains checked independently.

The three local exporters on `main` are in scope for PR #111. The five later exporters have a tested local integration at `/tmp/fusion-tea-numeric-evidence-demo`, branch `fix/numeric-study-evidence-demo`, commit `c089d8fb`. Its 112 focused tests cover all eight exporters and the exact heating reproduction. It remains local because its base, local `feat/demo-maturation`, contains sixteen unrelated commits not present on the remote branch. Historical CSVs and stores remain evidence of their original runs; missing values cannot be recovered by re-querying. A v3 rerun starts a fresh store and records the TEAx revision without changing the generated package's fingerprint.
