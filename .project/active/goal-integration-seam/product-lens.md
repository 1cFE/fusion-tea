Point (re-derived): Integration is the second hop in the loop with no callable boundary, and Item 3 repairs it at the producers. Audited model work plus the caller's expected lineage go in; exactly one verified, study-ready candidate — package, manifest, pin, semantic and executable fingerprint, verification evidence — comes out, or a named blocker does. Every gate is the producer's own, invoked in the proven order and never reimplemented or relaxed; native artifacts are cited, not restated; nothing half-promoted survives a refusal; a second call on unchanged inputs names the same identity. The goal layer must be able to act on the return: it asks "Is there **one** study-ready candidate?", and its own discipline requires it to tell an operational accident it may retry from a semantic result that changes what work is justified. And a person who did not build it must be able to operate the whole thing from the seam's own documentation.
