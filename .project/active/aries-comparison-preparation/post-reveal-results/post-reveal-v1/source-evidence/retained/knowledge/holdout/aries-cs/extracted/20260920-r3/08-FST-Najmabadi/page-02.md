It should be noted that while Dmin ~plasma-tomidcoil distance! is the relevant parameter for arriving at the coils that generate the desired stellarator configuration, the distance between the plasma and the front of the coils is a more relevant engineering constraint. As such, a coil winding pack with a rectangular cross section ~thinner in the radial direction! would lead to a smaller Dmin for a given plasma to the front of the coil distance.
