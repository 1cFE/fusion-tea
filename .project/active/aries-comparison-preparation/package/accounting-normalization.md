## Finance conventions

[INHERITED: current model] Default financing uses discount/interest i = 0.07 per year, annual escalation g = 0.02, construction T = 8 years, operating horizon N = 30 years and one module. These are scenario conventions; the sources do not establish a consistent nominal/real and common-currency-year basis for all capital. The lifecycle calendar is live, with bundled blanket/divertor replacement at the fluence-derived interval, seven-month planned outage and zero assumed residual unplanned fraction. Availability is computed productive full-power years divided by the calendar horizon. Coil life is a held ten-FPY anchor; omitted coil replacement and whole-plant maintenance are not free engineering solutions.

[INHERITED: `models/library/analyses/mfe_lcoe_dcf.sysml`] Headline LCOE = [overnight × (1+i)^(T/2) × CRF + CAS70 + CAS80] / [8760 × net MW × availability]. CRF = i/[1−(1+i)^(-N)], with CRF(0)=1/N. The midpoint construction multiplier represents the retained even-spend convention. Adding reported CAS60 to that capital base would double apply financing. Headline denominator has no extra module multiplier; this package freezes one module.

[INHERITED: `models/library/analyses/mfe_account_costs.sysml`] The comparison convention computes reported CAS60 = overnight × {[(1+i)^T−1]/(iT)−1}, with its zero-rate limit, and CAS90 = CRF × (overnight+CAS60). Its LCOE = (CAS90+CAS70+CAS80)/(8760 × net MW × module count × availability). The two construction conventions differ and both outputs remain separately labeled.

[INHERITED: same source and `models/library/analyses/mfe_lifecycle.sysml`] CAS71 O&M and CAS80 fuel each use A1 = raw annual cost × (1+g)^T, PV = A1 × [1−((1+g)/(1+i))^N]/(i−g), then CRF × PV. At equal rates the PV limit is A1 × N/(1+i). Payments begin at operating year one. CAS72 instead comes from discounted dated bundled replacement events in the live calendar, not an independent annualized replacement allowance. CAS70 = CAS71+CAS72; total annual cost = CAS70+CAS80. Replacement event cost covers blanket and divertor capital times module count; labor, waste and coil replacements remain excluded. Calendar availability is used consistently by both LCOE outputs.

[AGENT] After owner-triggered reveal, retain immutable source transcription: raw value, printed unit, currency/year, page/table, account boundary, module count, technology, financing and uncertainty. 3. [AGENT] Match physical/account boundaries before conversion. Merge or split only when source-resolved disjoint children demonstrate the mapping. Unknown first-order scope, omitted technology, an unavailable producer or missing reference remains unresolved. Never allocate a reference aggregate using the model's own fractions to fabricate independent component evidence.
4. [AGENT] Apply dimensional unit conversions with explicit factors and source evidence. Convert module basis only for quantities whose scaling and common operating state are demonstrated; do not divide shared buildings or site accounts blindly. Preserve raw values beside converted values. Ratios always use model/reference.
5. [AGENT] For money, establish a dated line-level currency and purchasing-power bridge, identify the chosen index, dates and exchange-rate convention, and retain every factor. If either date or appropriate index is missing, mark normalization unavailable. The frozen raw comparison and its verdict remain unchanged. A post-reveal conversion amendment cannot improve or replace that verdict. Apply a normalization to the formal comparison only when its exact evidence-selection and conversion algorithm was frozen before reveal; otherwise report new evidence-derived normalization solely as a separate diagnostic. This package freezes no monetary year-adjustment algorithm and permits no such formal adjustment. Never automatically normalize mixed-year totals.
6. [AGENT] For finance, retain the published raw LCOE and both frozen model LCOEs. If supported cashflow, rate, timing, lifetime, availability, module and energy conventions allow a common calculation, label it a separate normalized finance diagnostic. Do not overwrite the forward component-cost result or claim normalization repairs missing physical scope.

Report each numerical band, structural evidence, C220107 disclosures, source limitations and unresolved applicability.
