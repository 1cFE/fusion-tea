Current native and current oracle both return −3.637978807091713e−11 A and −5.551115123125783e−16 fractional margin. The strict native conductor predicate is **violated**. Burn-hold is also **violated** in both cases. The complete native response maps are retained in the probe receipt.

Compare historical predicates only where the model/operands remain applicable; preserve recorded historical verdicts separately. No tolerance belongs in the physical conductor predicate, and no all-pass interpretation is justified by the small magnitude. The remaining historical output/predicate assertions must run after the scalar repair; this probe does not certify them.

[INFERRED] Proposed reviewable scalar bound for the two observed area comparisons: `rel_tol=0`, `abs_tol=4*max(ulp(old), ulp(current))`, or 4.336808689942018e−19 m² at these magnitudes. This covers the measured one/two-ULP rearrangement while remaining far below an engineering change.
