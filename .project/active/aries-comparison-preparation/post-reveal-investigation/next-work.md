# Next scientific work after the field audit

[AGENT] Recommendation based on this investigation. This is proposed follow-up work, not an owner-originated requirement or a new authorization to change the model or execute the reference comparison.

## First: represent whether the field calculation applies

The current equations can return finite numbers when their transferred geometry is unqualified. Add a separately reviewed applicability contract for axis and peak field. It must distinguish numerical evaluation, the conditions supported by the magnetic-field model, and physical adequacy of the selected equipment. Propagate unsupported or unknown field applicability to consumers, including plasma and economics. An independently calculated cost subtotal can remain available; a dependent finite number cannot automatically become a scientifically supported prediction.

The contract should use the actual supported coil configuration and scaling assumptions. Do not substitute an arbitrary “only the baseline is allowed” rule or silently alter geometry to enforce similarity. A scalar radius ratio alone does not prove full three-dimensional coil similarity. Supplied design choices must remain unchanged and any evaluation direction must be explicit under MR-7.

## Then: qualify a field calculation for the intended design family

Inventory available coil geometry, current-family definitions and winding-pack dimensions before selecting a method. Determine whether the source evidence supports the axis-linkage factor, peak-field behavior and pack-size dependence for that family. Where the required geometry is available, a finite-pack magnetic calculation or a configuration-specific surrogate can be independently checked. Where geometry or fitted coefficients are absent, record the missing evidence and restrict the claimed capability. Do not invent coil geometry, fitted coefficients or current choices to reproduce the published ARIES field.

Validation must distinguish proportional-current response at fixed geometry, geometrically similar scaling, and changed coil shape/count/current distribution. It must test the effect of an independently supplied winding-pack size. Reproducing a calibration point or translating the same formula into another language does not validate transfer to a new shape. Keep separate test points and explicit supported conditions, and retain all comparison failures as historical evidence.

## Decide the next experiment from the qualified capability

The recommended next development target is a supported field model for our declared coil family. Its success criterion is justified magnetic behavior, not agreement with an ARIES output. An ARIES-design evaluation additionally needs source-compatible coil configuration, conductor technology, geometry and operating choices. Three reference plasma scalars with held equipment remain a conditional transfer experiment and cannot establish reconstruction of the full reference plant.

Broader conductor-performance support comes after identifying the required technology and operating domain. The two numbers exposed here do not define a scientifically justified new interval. Retain published magnetic fields as observations for assessment, never as implicit sizing targets.

## Execution infrastructure

The additive native diagnostic work in this investigation addresses loss of independent arithmetic when a module fails. It is a separate evidence type, tested with synthetic faults. It does not qualify the field approximation or replace the adopted comparison runner. Integration into normal studies and any new runtime/package adoption need explicit identity, consumer and scientific-status checks. The current reference request is not rerun as part of this investigation.
