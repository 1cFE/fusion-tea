Each period contains six independent and six mirrored coils, so each of the six types occurs eight times. PDF pp. 2 and 22; TEXT:185–192 and 1789–1793. The repetition count is arithmetic from the explicitly stated symmetry, not an additional coil dataset.
- **Reference field:** Table 2 calls 9.0 T the axis-averaged magnetic field strength, not a point field at the geometric major radius. It calls 24.9 T the peak conductor field and gives 15.4 MA as the peak single-coil current. PDF p. 3, inspected Table 2 image. This is not a spatial field map or an integration prescription.

Its geometric constraints are scalar minima (coil–plasma distance 1.37 m, coil–coil separation 0.67 m, curvature radius 0.64 m), not coordinates. PDF p. 4, inspected Table 3 image. A circular radius formed by summing radial-build layers does not recover these filaments.

## Pack geometry and field calculation

The paper selects square winding packs, with 20 × 20 mm unit cells, and varies turn count by family. Pack orientation is chosen to place a flat face tangent to the plasma at each point; tape orientation is separately aligned with the magnetic field. These are design descriptions. They do not supply the spatial frame along each coil required to place its square pack. PDF p. 22, inspected full-page image; TEXT around 1715–1763.

The paper explicitly states that peak field depends heavily on pack size. Its reported calculation uses a three-dimensional magnetostatic finite-element model in COMSOL and uniform current density across each pack cross-section. PDF p. 22; TEXT:1806–1818. Screening currents and field quality are outside that short analysis. Fig. 41 describes a half-module simulation including the field generated by Stellaris and shows field magnitude on pack surfaces; the prose gives a maximum inside the pack of 24.59 T. PDF p. 23; TEXT:1987–1995. This report does not equate the Table 2 value (24.9), Table 8 value (24.6), and prose value (24.59), or resolve the paper's unprovided numerical setup. The passages do not explicitly specify whether the COMSOL field calculation includes the finite-beta plasma-current field; this remains an input/convention question.

## Exact missing-data contract and sufficiency judgment

[AGENT] A reproducible calculation needs these inputs or an equivalent complete representation:

1. The six oriented closed centreline curves in physical units, their version/configuration identity, coordinate system, symmetry transforms, coil placements and current direction conventions. A complete 48-coil representation is equivalent.
2. Family ampere-turn normalization and unrounded source currents if precise reproduction is required; independently selected turns and turn currents where discrete windings are modeled. Common rescaling is distinct from changing family ratios.
3. Pack dimensions by family, spatial cross-section frames and twist along every curve, and the represented current distribution. Discrete conductor locations are necessary for a turn-resolved calculation; continuous pack fields require a declared finite-volume model.
4. The axis curve and averaging definition, including vacuum versus finite-beta convention. A finite-beta total-field claim additionally needs the plasma-current/equilibrium contribution and its scaling assumptions.
5. A reference solver result or field map with convergence information and precise peak search convention (surface, interior, all coils, vector norm or conductor-normal component). Off-design qualification needs calculations spanning the independently allowed pack/shape/current variations and separate validation cases.

**Filament Biot–Savart:** current magnitudes and symmetry are partially specified, but executable curves and axis are absent; local data are insufficient. Filaments alone would still not determine finite-pack conductor peak field.

**Finite-pack calculation:** family dimensions and a uniform-current assumption are supplied, but the curves, local pack frames and numerical reference setup are absent; local data are insufficient.

**Calibrated scaling:** published points support a disclosed anchor and physical similarity arguments conditional on a fully fixed normalized configuration and current distribution. They do not establish an error bound, identify both pack coefficients, or validate independently varied pack dimensions, coil shapes, coil counts or family ratios. Aggregate major/minor radii and minimum clearances cannot recover actual nonplanar coil paths. The existing scalar public inputs therefore cannot prove the geometry conditions needed for configuration-specific scaling.
