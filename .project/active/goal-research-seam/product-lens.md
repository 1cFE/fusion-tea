Findings:
- design-F1 [DON'T] The `--holdout-ack` override takes an invariant the owner and `PROTOCOL.md` own and hands it to whoever runs the script, without saying that is what it is doing. The design's own B4 ("script-owned is what binds; prompt text does not") condemns it. — `knowledge/holdout/aries-cs/PROTOCOL.md` §3 (barred principle + exception path), §4 (derived-artifact rule), §6 (log) (**owner**); spec R-D1, R-D4 (**[HARD]**) — disposition: BLOCK (expected: the flag is deleted and a term hit is always `OPERATOR_QUEUE`, with the owner's §6 log as the human path; any surviving override is a protocol change and an owner question, not a design call)
