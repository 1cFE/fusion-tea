# Status Report — Stellarator Demo Maturation and ARIES-CS Readiness

**Date:** 2026-09-07

## Bottom line

The maturation phase is producing real progress, but the project is not yet ready to reveal ARIES-CS. The main blocker is contractual: the owner-ratified reveal-readiness condition required by the maturation concept does not exist. The technical reason to wait is narrower than “the model is immature”: current evidence is split across several pins, study optima still exploit an unbounded and unpriced minor radius, primary-loop pumping remains held despite its 21% LCOE consequence, and the evidence-publication suite currently fails closed on 75 cases.

## Evidence of progress

- The blind and model-facing clean room remain intact. `knowledge/holdout/aries-cs/PROTOCOL.md` is still `sealed`, with no logged violation.
- Anchor-B expectations are already pre-committed: all three structural correspondences; derived-quantity ratios in `[1/3, 3]`; component-cost ratios in `[0.5, 2.0]`; C220107 excluded or footnoted; misses reported as findings.
- The initial rubric plus the three formal re-grades directionally place 11 of 21 applicable cells at their written targets. This is not a current-tip certification because those grades cite different model/package pins.
- Goal `magnet-closure` moved R3.P `1 → 3` and R3.S `2 → 3`, reaching both targets.
- Goal `operating-point-closure` moved R1.P `2 → 3`, reaching target and making confinement/sustainment push back on the operating point.
- Goal `wall-and-heating` moved R4.P `1 → 2` while R4.S remained `2`, reaching both written targets; it also replaced the wall fence with a source-anchored computed-peak check.
- Goals `stored-energy-basis` and `burn-control` corrected the ash-profile basis and made feasibility two-sided. The current package no longer counts a negative auxiliary-power requirement as a feasible operating point.
- Multiple committed, independently administered studies now exercise the study layer. The demo epic's criteria and stage map have not been updated to reflect that evidence.

## Gaps that matter to Anchor B

- **Readiness is undefined.** The maturation concept requires a written, owner-ratified trigger for Item 7. No artifact supplies it.
- **There is no single current grade.** The formal gains are credible, but the project cannot point to one consolidated rubric assessment at the comparison candidate pin.
- **Geometry can game the answer.** Minor radius is unbounded above in the explored window and is not priced through coil geometry. Study optima run to the `a` boundary; the wall-peak calibration is carried away from its anchor without a re-anchoring rule. This directly threatens radial-build, coil-mass, and component-cost comparisons.
- **Primary-loop power is still a held assumption.** The `p_pump` correction moved baseline LCOE by 21% and materially changed feasibility. The evidence supports a broad plant-level range, not a loop calculation. This is the largest unresolved power-flow risk for B-3.
- **Lifecycle behavior is not closed.** Computed first-wall life changes replacement cost but does not determine outage duration or availability. Availability still multiplies every $/MWh as a held value.
- **Important breadth remains shallow.** TBR, divertor behavior, power-cycle efficiency, fuel-cycle inventory/throughput, vacuum pumping, buildings/layout, and estimate uncertainty remain below target. Not all must reach target before reveal, but each needs an explicit “model, bound, or report as not comparable” disposition.
- **Evidence publication is not clean.** `tests/study` currently reports 75 fail-closed failures for uncovered study records. Multi-output package fields can still become silent blank columns, and the manifest does not pin the executor revision. Those defects affect the comparison's power-flow evidence and reproducibility.
- **Tracking is stale.** The demo epic says `On Hold`, Item 10 says `Active`, criterion 5 says studies are not started, and the Run-Study epic says its first consumer is only partly closed. The board cannot currently answer the user's question without reconstructing history.

## Recommended reveal-readiness condition

This is an `[AGENT]` proposal for owner ratification, not a settled criterion.

Reveal when all of the following are true at one candidate commit and package pin:

1. A fresh consolidated grading exists. R1, R2a, R3, and R4 remain at target; R2b/R11, R7, and R8 either meet target or carry owner-accepted bounded limitations; every other below-target row has an explicit comparison disposition.
2. No headline optimum depends on an unbounded, unpriced design variable or on a feasibility predicate already known to be semantically incomplete.
3. The model runs one clean-room, off-design transfer study that changes geometry and technology together, with every Anchor-B derived-quantity and component-cost channel present and independently checked.
4. A sensitivity envelope exists for the few held assumptions that dominate the Anchor-B axes: pumping/primary loop, heating coupling, wall-peak transfer, availability/outage, and estimate uncertainty.
5. The comparison machinery has been dry-run blind against synthetic reference data: B-2 mapping, B-3/B-4 ratios, missing/not-comparable handling, C220107 exclusion, immutable raw observations, and pass/fail report generation.
6. The study/model batteries are green for the comparison record contract; required channels cannot publish blank; model commit, package fingerprints, manifest, and executor revision are pinned.
7. Quarantine integrity is content-free verified, the AACE B-7 cross-check is complete, the model is frozen, and the owner explicitly triggers PROTOCOL §6.

## Recommended sequence before reveal

1. **Repair the measurement surface first.** Fix fail-closed publication coverage, silent blank multi-output channels, and executor-revision capture. Then update epic tracking so the real study evidence counts.
2. **Run WI-044 as the next model goal.** Bound and price minor radius through a radial-build/coil-bore/coil-length relationship and define how the wall-peak calibration transfers or is bounded away from its anchor.
3. **Close the primary-loop and cycle seam.** Model flow, pressure drop, pumping power, coolant outlet temperature, and cycle efficiency far enough that R7/R8 power flows respond to design choices. Treat the sourced 1–6% pumping evidence as a validation range, not the calculation.
4. **Close lifetime into availability.** Connect component life to replacement schedule, outage duration, and availability, then re-run the wall/heating studies. This joins R2b and R11 and removes a held multiplier from every cost result.
5. **Do a breadth disposition pass.** Prioritize minimum credible treatment of TBR/fuel cycle and divertor heat exhaust; explicitly classify the remaining rubric gaps as modeled, bounded, or not comparable before seeing ARIES-CS.
6. **Run the blind comparison rehearsal and consolidated re-grade.** Use a synthetic reference, freeze the candidate pin, produce the owner decision packet, and only then decide whether to reveal.

## Immediate next action

Write and ratify the reveal-readiness condition, then mint the evidence-publication repair and WI-044. Do not start Item 7 until those are complete and the consolidated re-grade exists.
