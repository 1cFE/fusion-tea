# Critique: 18-p-b11-frc

## Defects (must-fix before publication)
- F2.d is hedged: "Mixed but leaning failure pole" — the rubric requires committing to one pole, and the trace's own evidence (no commercial NBI supply chain at fusion scale) supports a clean failure-pole call rather than a "leaning" hedge.
- Dominant leverage at the concept level is a compound assertion ("F2.c at the leverage pole reinforced by the tritium/Li-6/Be carrier being absent") rather than the single stage/factor coordinate the rubric demands; the parenthetical "Among the formal codes the strongest single one is F2.c" reads as a forced single-pick after a dodge.
- Dominant failure mode reasoning slips out of rubric vocabulary: "the underlying mechanism is the foundational physics binary" is framed as something the F-factor codes don't natively express, then mapped onto F2.b — this is an admission that the trace is using F2.b as a proxy for an out-of-vocabulary concern; either argue F2.b cleanly or flag the methodology gap explicitly.
- "Realistic additional capital to actual Stage-2 entry: likely $2–4B more" has no anchor in the dossier/analysis/explorer quotes provided in the trace — appears invented.
- "~50–80× T_i extrapolation" in the dominant-failure rationale is a specific quantitative claim with no citation to the inputs.
- F4.b uses non-rubric phrasing "Mixed-leaning-R&D" instead of committing to one of the rubric's three options (volume / R&D / mixed); the rubric explicitly lists "mixed" as an allowed value, so the "leaning" qualifier is gratuitous.
- F3.a slack/bottleneck tag is asserted as "bottleneck-likely at fleet scale" — the "-likely" qualifier softens what the rubric expects to be a committed tag.

## Soft issues (optional polish)
- F2.d's "slack-for-FOAK-only" / "bottleneck at fleet scale" split is principled (different time horizons), but the rubric's F2.d slack/bottleneck tag is specifically for FOAK; the fleet-scale qualifier belongs under F3.a where it is in fact repeated, creating mild double-counting.
- "Output format implication" recommending a costing extension over SysML is a tooling/scoping recommendation that goes beyond what the dominant-coordinate output is meant to drive — it may belong, but it is not a direct consequence of the (Stage 2 F2.b, Stage 2 F2.c) coordinate.
- E2.a discussion is thorough but the final assessment ("Weak") could be tighter — the DJT/Trump Media aside is editorial.
- The cross-stage carrier section asserts "single concept in the pipeline that pays zero across the sharpest fleet-scale supply constraint" — true-ish but uncited against the full 38-concept set; other aneutronic concepts (HB11, LPPFusion, etc., named elsewhere in the trace) also pay zero.

## Overall verdict
- Not publication-ready as-is: the dominant-leverage coordinate is a compound hedge and the dominant-failure coordinate openly admits it's a vocabulary proxy — fixing those two coordinate commitments is the single biggest blocker.
