# Magnet material comparison study annex

This package evaluates two explicitly supplied winding designs, REBCO tape at 20 K and Nb₃Sn strand cable at 4.5 K, at the same specified magnetic duty in the same envelope, and compares current margin, inventory, fit, refrigeration demand and subsystem cost. Model authority is the reviewed design `work/active/WI-099_magnet-conductor-alternatives/design.md` (with its § 7 clarifications) under the released comparison contract `work/orchestration/goals/magnet-material-comparison/evidence/comparison-contract.md` (r3). The contract's limits (§ 2 stated limits, § 9 effects not evaluated) remain part of that authority.

## Package and execution

The generated package is `exploration/magnet_materials/magnet_materials_tea`, generated by `exploration/magnet_materials/build.py` from `exploration/magnet_materials/input_models/` (the two canonical files `models/library/analyses/magnet_conductor_alternatives.sysml` and `models/designs/magnet_materials/magnet_subsystem.sysml`). Eight handwritten bodies live under `exploration/magnet_materials/bodies/`. Snapshot, census and build hashes are beside the package.

`study_route.py` uses the stock strict `ProvisionalPackageLoader`, `PreparedEvaluator`, `StudyRunner`, `PreparedListStrategy`, `StudyStore` and `StudyQuery`. `case_point` maps a design § 6 case record (`duty`, `economics`, `nb3sn`, `rebco`) to the complete 163-key entry map through the reviewed `interface_data.py`; 27 fixed design values (law domains, 77 K and 300 K stage temperatures, NIST coefficients) come from the interface. It performs no physical calculation. Glue ledger: none.

**Never evaluate the package on its generated defaults.** `eps_intrinsic` is a negative design literal, which the toolchain renders as a constant, so its entry key `magnet_subsystem__subsystem__nb3sn__conductor__eps_intrinsic_in` is left unbound in the design with the neutral default 0.0; every case point supplies the reference −0.003 (implementation notes, deviation 1). Every study goes through `case_point` or the manifest point.

## Baseline pin

The manifest's pinned point is the anchor D (EU DEMO TF) reference case at 10 T on the common-P pairing under the reference rule family: Nb₃Sn 438 strands with a 30 kW cold-stage rating, REBCO 342 tapes with a 30 kW rating, the areas from construction P at that duty, `shield_static` 1,102,000 W (design § 7 A6). Headline `magnet_subsystem__subsystem__pair__cost_difference` = 237,622,172.77682102 USD2021/yr; all ten constraints satisfied. These are independent selections from the declared offer policy, not outputs of a sizing search.

`prepare_interface.py` discovers the entry, channel and constraint catalogs from the generated contracts and writes `interface_data.py` and `manifest.json`. The manifest's oracle block names `exploration.magnet_materials.studies.oracle_entry`, which was not written at build time; a study binds the oracle through its own record-local adapter until that module exists (see Oracle).

## Declared ties

None. Every design § 6 attribute emits exactly one entry key, so each axis group is a plain fan-out list. The 27 fixed design values are not axes. The case generator coordinates several attributes at once (an anchor's seven duty attributes, an offer's nine quantities, a pairing's construction-rule inputs); these are explicit scenario choices recorded in every case, not physical identities between attributes.

## Oracle

`exploration/magnet_materials/oracle.py` is the independent oracle, written from the contract and the design only (T-005 oracle author; notes in `oracle-notes.md`). It differs by method where the implementer had a choice: Brent–Dekker for the Nb₃Sn current-sharing root against bisection, Gauss–Legendre in ln T for the NIST 316 conductivity integral against Simpson. `evaluate_case(case)` takes a § 6-keyed case and returns every § 2 output.

A study record supplies `oracle_entry.py` exposing `evaluate(point)` (entry map in, package channel names out) and `operand_bindings()` (each assert's operand to the part-level alias of the calc output it binds, read from `magnet_subsystem.sysml`). The adapter maps names only. Nine constant formula channels (`eps_min`, `k_a`, `k_d`, `k_g`, `k_i` per material) are fixed design values, not oracle outputs, and are excluded from comparison and listed.

Tolerance is the contract's (§ 8): a channel agrees at relative deviation below 1e−9 or absolute error below 1e−9 per unit. The absolute clause matters for margins at allowance (design D1): reference copper and steel margins lie in [0, 1e−6] mm², where a relative test alone measures floating-point rounding. Verdicts are re-derived from the oracle's operands through the package's predicate IR with exact comparisons.

## Candidate range and validity masks

The candidate set is the declared case list `cases.json` written by `declare_cases.py` from `offer_policy.py` (contract § 5): anchors D and S; fields 8–13 T on both and the REBCO-only extension 14, 16, 18, 20 T on D; pairings common-P, native, common-C; rule families reference, both-temperature, both-fraction; the reference, insufficient (⌊0.9 n⌋) and generous (⌈1.2 n⌉) element offers and the insufficient (next lower listed) refrigerator; 36 one-at-a-time variants as re-evaluated reference offers and as variant offers. Every case is executed and retained whatever its status: unsupported, edge and law-only Nb₃Sn evaluations, fit failures and capacity failures stay in the store with their verdicts. Where a policy offer cannot exist (Nb₃Sn at −0.6 % strain and 20 T) the reference offer is carried and labelled; where the rating list is exhausted the 75 kW rating is offered and fails capacity.

No validity mask is applied. The window is engineered: it is the contract's declared grid of offers, not a sourced operating envelope, and no continuous boundary or optimum is claimed from it. Byte-identical entry maps occur where a variant's value equals the pairing's reference or an economics-only variant leaves the offer unchanged; the lifecycle evaluates each distinct point once and every declared case keeps its row with its aliases named.

## Accounting and claim limits

Each material's subsystem includes its supplied winding (superconductor, copper, steel, solder), the cold-stage and 77 K intercept refrigeration demand and refrigerator capital, annualized with a common capital recovery factor and electricity price. Steel is an allowance, not a stress calculation; quench protection beyond the copper allowance, irradiation, AC losses, joints and leads design, layer grading, field change from winding size and plasma performance are not evaluated (contract § 9). A case passing every evaluated check is a screening pass. Preference is defined only over matched pairs where both offers pass every check; unsupported and failed offers carry no ranking. Anchor D's turn length and cold-load terms are bounded assumptions that scale totals. Material prices are used as the model documents them (2026-era for solder) without conversion (design § 7 A16).
