# Validation Review: Concept 11 — Realta Magnetic Mirror

**Concept**: 11-magnetic-mirror (Realta Fusion CoSMo / Hammir)
**Model under review**: [`exploration/concept_analysis/analyses/11-magnetic-mirror/model_setup.py`](../exploration/concept_analysis/analyses/11-magnetic-mirror/model_setup.py)
**Primary source**: Frank et al., *Confinement performance predictions for a high field axisymmetric tandem mirror*, arXiv:2411.06644 **v2** (Apr 2025)
**Reviewer**: Mallory Snowden (with Claude assistance)
**Review date**: 2026-05-28
**Reported LCOE in `model_output`**: 104 $/MWh at 500 MWe
**Frank-corrected LCOE**: **327 $/MWh** (+214%)

---

## TL;DR

Concept 11's cost model produces a defensible-looking LCOE that is roughly a factor of two too low because the model:

1. **Targets a 500 MWe plant size that Realta has never published.** The 500 MWe number is sourced from a 1983 MARS reactor study, not from Realta. Realta's only stated pilot target is "> 50 MWe" (Hammir, per NASEM criteria).
2. **Mixes parameters from incompatible cases of the same source paper.** The chamber length is justified at one power density; the plasma radius is taken from a different, lower-density operating point; the result is internally inconsistent.
3. **Uses a power-conversion efficiency stack that does not match Realta's published design.** Thermal efficiency, direct energy conversion bonus, and NBI wall-plug efficiency all silently diverge from the values in Frank et al.
4. **Inherits commercial-scale balance-of-plant loads** without rescaling them for a pilot-scale plant, producing a recirculating power fraction (58%) that exceeds the cost framework's own validity threshold (50%).

The cost model's headline LCOE silently extrapolates a pilot-scale device to commercial scale using framework defaults rather than Realta-specific physics, and the corrected number (327 $/MWh) is the framework's own answer when forced to honor Frank et al.'s actual design point.

The agentic synthesis pipeline produced a `model_setup.py` that does not faithfully represent Realta Fusion's published design. The 104 $/MWh headline LCOE is an artifact of three independent failures the pipeline did not catch: source-mixing across operating points within a single reference paper, silent overrides of documented efficiency parameters, and commercial-scale balance-of-plant loads applied to a pilot-scale plant. ## What is being reviewed

Concept 11 is one of 39 fusion concepts that get a deterministic Levelized Cost of Electricity (LCOE) score in this project. For each concept, a file `model_setup.py` declares the engineering parameters of the plant (size, geometry, efficiencies, recirculating power loads). Those parameters are fed to a cost-modeling library (`1costingfe`) that produces an LCOE.

The expectation is that `model_setup.py` faithfully represents what the developer (here, Realta Fusion) has actually published — not a hypothetical extrapolation. When the model and the published design diverge, the LCOE is no longer about the real concept; it is about whatever the model is silently substituting.

This review compares concept 11's `model_setup.py` line-by-line against Realta Fusion's primary published reference: Frank et al. v2 (Apr 2025), specifically Table 2 column "Optimum Consistent T_e" — Realta's own self-consistent pilot design point.

## Glossary (for outside readers)

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **P_fus** | Total fusion power (heat released by D-T fusion reactions) — measured in MW of thermal power. |
| **P_input** | Heating power delivered to the plasma (here, all from Neutral Beam Injection). Measured in MW. |
| **P_net** | Net electric power delivered to the grid after subtracting all on-site power consumption. Measured in MWe. |
| **Q** (scientific) | P_fus / P_input — how much fusion power you get per unit heating power. Realta claims Q = 8.75 at design point. |
| **Recirculating power** | Power generated by the plant that has to be used to keep the plant running (NBI, pumps, cryoplant, etc.) before any goes to the grid. Expressed as a fraction of gross electric output. |
| **eta_th** | Thermal-to-electric conversion efficiency. Typical values 35–50%. |
| **eta_pin** | Wall-plug efficiency of the heating system (electric in → heating power delivered to plasma). |
| **DEC** | Direct Energy Conversion — capturing the kinetic energy of charged particles directly as electricity without going through a thermal cycle. |
| **BOP** | Balance of Plant — all the supporting systems (cooling, tritium plant, magnets, etc.) that consume electric power. |

## Finding 1 — Power output and mirror dimensions sourced from inconsistent references

**What the model claims:** The plant produces 500 MWe net at a chamber 70 m long with a plasma radius of 0.75 m. Fusion power is implicitly ~490 MW (via a "~7 MWt/m" power-density rule applied to a 70 m chamber).

**What Frank et al. v2 actually says** (Table 2 "Optimum Consistent T_e", page 27):

| Parameter | Frank et al. v2 | Concept 11 model | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Central cell length (ℓ_c) | 50 m | 70 m | model larger by +40% |
| Central cell radius (a_c) | 0.86 m | 0.75 m | model **smaller** by −13% |
| Fusion power (P_fus) | 350 MW | ~490 MW (geometric) / 1052 MW (framework back-solve) | model 1.4× to 3.0× larger |
| Net electric (P_net) | ~142 MWe (Frank §3.2 eq. 3.14) or > 50 MWe (NASEM pilot floor) | 500 MWe | model 3.5× to 10× larger |
| Q (scientific) | 8.75 | implicit 26 (at 500 MWe scale-up) | model ~3× more optimistic |

The model justifies its 500 MWe target with a quote attributed to "MARS/MINIMARS found mirror LCOE saturates near ~600 MWe (1983 technology analogue)" — a 1983 reactor study unrelated to Realta. The same model file admits in its own comment: *"UNCERTAIN: Realta has published no commercial plant size target."*

The "7 MWt/m" power density used to justify the 70 m chamber comes from Frank's Table 2 (Optimum Consistent T_e: 350 MW / 50 m = 7 MW/m). The plasma radius cited in the model's comment (0.54–0.78 m range) comes from Frank's Table 3, a *different* operating point with half the power density (Optimum case: 175 MW / 50 m = 3.5 MW/m). The model mixes parameters from these two cases without acknowledging that they describe distinct operating points.

Net effect: the plasma radius shrinks while the chamber lengthens, and the fusion power is claimed to grow ~3× — a geometry that cannot deliver the claimed power output at the cited operating physics.

## Finding 2 — Power conversion efficiency stack does not reconcile with the source

The model declares a hybrid thermal+DEC conversion stack with several values that do not match Frank et al.'s simple Brayton-cycle assumption:

| Parameter | Frank et al. (§3.2) | Concept 11 model | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thermal-to-electric (eta_th) | 0.50 (Brayton, citing Schleicher 2001) | **0.55** in code; **0.36** in docstring | Silent +5 pp override of a documented value. Model docstring still cites 0.36 (MARS Rankine analogue); actual code uses 0.55 per a separate framework standardization. Print statements report the wrong value. |
| Direct energy conversion (eta_de) | not used | 0.54 | Frank routes all fusion power through Brayton. The model adds a DEC channel taken from a 1983 MARS analogue. |
| DEC fraction (f_dec) | not applicable | 0.20 | Same as above — credits 20% of fusion power through a more efficient pathway that the source design does not use. |
| NBI wall-plug (eta_pin vs η_NBI) | η_NBI = 0.60 | eta_pin = 0.50 | Model 10 pp more pessimistic on heating-system efficiency. |
| Neutron multiplier (mn / C_mult) | 1.10 | 1.10 | ✓ Matches |

The first three rows together credit concept 11 with a conversion bonus (the DEC channel) that Frank et al. explicitly does *not* claim. Frank mentions DEC (Moir & Barr 1973) once on page 14 as a possible *future* enhancement; the design point is pure thermal. The fifth row is independent — it makes the model's NBI heating draw more expensive than Frank's accounting.

The docstring–code drift on `eta_th` is the most concerning of these because it makes the model unfaithful to its own documentation: a reader trusting the docstring (0.36) sees a different efficiency than the calculation uses (0.55). Source citations in the comments name MARS 1983; the actual computation uses a framework default that has no connection to MARS.

## Finding 3 — Recirculating power loads are commercial-scale defaults applied to a pilot

The cost framework's recirculating-power calculation sums several plant-aux loads that the user (concept author) supplies as scalar MW values:

| Load | Concept 11 setting | Physical scaling | Appropriate at 350 MW P_fus? |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-wall + blanket cooling (`p_cool`) | 22 MWe | ∝ P_fus (heat removed) | No — about 3× too high. Should be ~7 MWe at 2% of P_fus. |
| Tritium plant (`p_trit`) | 10 MWe | ∝ D-T burn rate, i.e. ∝ P_fus | No — about 2× too high. Should be ~5 MWe. |
| Coolant pumping (`p_pump`) | 1.5 MWe | ∝ thermal power | Approximately OK. |
| Solenoid coils (`p_coils`) | 8 MWe | geometry-driven | Defensible at this scale. |
| Cryoplant (`p_cryo`) | 2 MWe | cold-mass-driven | Defensible. |
| Housekeeping (`p_house`) | 4 MWe | genuinely near-constant | Defensible. |

These values were inherited from the costingFE framework's commercial-scale defaults (`steady_state_mirror.yaml`), which were calibrated against ~1 GWe reactor scales. They were *not* rescaled to the pilot scale (350 MW fusion → ~95 MWe net) that Frank et al.'s design point actually represents.

The result is a recirculating-power fraction of **58.4%** when the model is forced to honor Frank's published P_fus = 350 MW. The cost framework itself raises a warning at this point — *"Recirculating fraction = 0.584 > 0.5 → excessive parasitic power load"* — i.e., the cost calculation has stepped outside its own declared region of validity.

Of the 134 MWe of recirculating power the framework computes:
- 80 MWe is NBI wall-plug draw (P_input / eta_pin = 40 / 0.5)
- 47.5 MWe is the fixed BOP loads above
- 6.9 MWe is the BOP fraction (f_sub × gross)

Roughly 20 of the 47.5 MWe of BOP loads is a pilot-vs-commercial scaling artifact rather than physics — i.e., real power loads that would not exist at this plant scale.

For comparison, Frank et al.'s screening formula in §3.2 accounts for only ~67 MWe of recirc (NBI wall-plug alone), giving net electric ~142 MWe at 350 MW fusion — versus the framework's 95.7 MWe at the same fusion power. The 46 MWe gap is approximately the unrescaled BOP loads plus the eta_pin mismatch.

## Cumulative impact on LCOE

Re-running the cost model with Frank et al. v2 Table 2 values as the binding constraint (P_fus = 350 MW, Q = 8.75, P_input = 40 MW, ℓ_c = 50 m, a_c = 0.86 m), letting the cost framework back-solve P_net under its existing efficiency stack:

| | LCOE | Overnight cost | Capital | P_net |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `model_output` (as-shipped, 500 MWe) | 104 $/MWh | 7,477 $/kW | 3,739 M$ | 500 MWe |
| Frank-corrected (P_fus = 350 MW binding) | **327 $/MWh** | **25,236 $/kW** | 2,415 M$ | 95.7 MWe |
| Change | **+214%** | +237% | −35% | −81% |

Capital actually drops by a third (the plant is smaller), but net electric output drops by a factor of five, so $/MWh rises by a factor of three. The 327 $/MWh figure should itself be treated as an order-of-magnitude estimate, since the framework is operating outside its validity envelope (58% recirc) — the true answer is uncertain by perhaps ±30%, but it is unambiguously several times higher than the 104 $/MWh currently displayed.

The single biggest driver is the 500 MWe → ~95 MWe correction to net electric output. The geometry correction (50 m × 0.86 m vs 70 m × 0.75 m) is approximately neutral on LCOE. The efficiency stack and recirc inflation each contribute on the order of $20–40/MWh.

## Recommended corrective actions

### Per-concept fixes (concept 11 specifically)

1. **Pin to Frank et al. v2 Table 2, "Optimum Consistent T_e".** Remove the 500 MWe MARS extrapolation. The `model_setup.py` should target Frank's actual design point (P_fus = 350 MW, P_net ≈ 95–140 MWe depending on which efficiency stack is used). Any commercial extrapolation should be a separately-labeled `forward()` call with an explicit citation for the size target.

2. **Reconcile the efficiency stack to Frank:**
   - Set `eta_th = 0.50` (Brayton, matching Frank §3.2 — remove the silent 0.36 → 0.55 standardization).
   - Set `eta_de = 0.0` and `f_dec = 0.0` (Frank uses no DEC at design point).
   - Set `eta_pin = 0.60` (matching Frank's η_NBI).
   - Resolve the docstring–code mismatch on `eta_th`: either correct the docstring to match the standardized value or revert the code to the documented MARS analogue and choose one.

3. **Rescale the BOP loads to the actual plant size.** Express the three scale-dependent loads as fractions of P_fus rather than fixed scalars:
   - `p_cool ≈ 0.02 × P_fus` (~7 MWe at 350 MW)
   - `p_trit ≈ 0.015 × P_fus` (~5 MWe)
   - `p_pump ≈ 0.005 × P_fus` (~1.5 MWe)
   Keep `p_coils`, `p_cryo`, `p_house` as scalars (these are genuinely scale-independent). This single change removes ~20 MWe of spurious recirculating power.

4. **Remove the "linear scaling thesis" justification** from the docstring. It is an unvalidated Realta marketing claim, and applying it at the 500 MWe extrapolation implies a scientific Q of ~26, far beyond what Realta has claimed (Q = 8.75). Replace with the simple statement: *"Hammir pilot design point. Commercial extrapolation not modeled."*

### Framework / process fixes (apply repo-wide)

5. **Source-freshness check** before scoring. The concept 11 model was anchored to Frank et al. v1 (Nov 2024). v2 (Apr 2025) was already public when the analysis was synthesized but absent from the research pool. An automated check that latest-arxiv-version-of-pinned-citation matches what is in `knowledge/concept_research/{concept}/iter-NN/sources/` would have caught this. Trigger re-synthesis when a check fails.

6. **Internal consistency checks** at score-publish time, per concept:
   - Q closure: framework's implicit Q (= back-solved P_fus / declared P_input) within ±25% of the cited Q.
   - Geometric closure: chamber volume × cited power density within ±30% of cited P_fus.
   - Recirc validity: cost framework's `rec_frac < 0.5` warning surfaces as a concept-level confidence downgrade.
   Each check is cheap, source-agnostic, and would have caught a different specific failure in concept 11.

7. **Dual-form BOP load API** in `1costingfe`. Allow `p_cool`, `p_trit`, `p_pump` to be specified either as scalar MW or as a fraction of P_fus, with the concept YAML choosing the form appropriate for the architecture. Same architectural pattern that PR #14 used to move `burn_fraction` from global default to per-concept. Backward compatible.

## What this review does not address

- The structural and supply-chain features of concept 11 (modularity, customization, plant complexity, technical feasibility) are scored independently of LCOE and are not under review here.
- The data availability score and gap report for concept 11 are tracked separately and were addressed in PRs #32 and #33.
- Other concepts in the corpus likely have similar failure modes (small pilots inheriting commercial-scale BOP loads) — concept 6 (Pale Blue p-B11 mirror), concept 12 (OpenStar levitated dipole), concept 19 (Zephyr orbital dipole), concept 27 (EMC2 Polywell), concept 35 (Deutelio PoloMac), and concept 38 (SHINE) are plausible candidates for the same audit. A systematic sweep is in scope for follow-up work, not this review.
