Modeling notes:
  - eta_th=0.90 approximates direct EM recovery; the ~5% neutron power
    fraction is also converted at 90% instead of ~40% thermal, introducing
    a small upward bias in net electric. Neutron fraction:
    helion-website-technology.md §Fuel; analysis.md §S5.
  - He-3 fuel cost: $2M/kg (costing_constants.yaml u_he3=2000000).
    This is the optimistic self-production (self-bred He3) cost estimate.
    Market spot: $2,000–15,000 per NTP liter (~$16,000–120,000/g).
    Source: analysis.md §S4 Key Materials (Helium-3 section).
    UNCERTAIN: He3 startup inventory requirement not published.
    If significant external He3 purchase is needed before self-breeding
    is established, startup fuel cost could dominate LCOE.
    Source: analysis.md §S2 Challenge 4; §S6 Gap 3.
  - Rep rate is the dominant LCOE lever. analysis.md §S2 Challenge 2:
    '1 Hz → 2 Hz doubles energy output from identical capital.'
    ARPA-E design: 2 Hz; Polaris target: ~1 Hz (not yet demonstrated publicly).
    Trenta demonstrated only ~0.002 Hz (500-1000x below commercial target).
    Source: analysis.md §S5; docslib-helion-arpa-e-presentation.md §Power.
  - Coil cost ($5M/mod) covers aluminum pulsed EM solenoids. Key uncertainty:
    fatigue life at 40 T field under ~10^9 pulsed loading cycles per module.
    Source: analysis.md §S3 (Compression to Reactor Field, TRL 3).
  - Capacitor bank ($10M/mod) assumes NOAK volume manufacturing ~$0.50/J.
    Current commercial $5/J → Polaris-scale hardware ≈ $250M/mod.
    Helion's in-house manufacturing is the mitigating strategy.
    Source: analysis.md §S4 (High-Voltage Capacitors and IGBT Switches).
  - No independent TEA exists for Helion (analysis.md §S7 Cross-Concept Notes).
    All quantitative values derive from sparse public disclosures.
    Uncertainty bounds here are substantially wider than for tokamak concepts
    that have Araiinejad & Shirvan (2025) or equivalent peer-reviewed TEA.
