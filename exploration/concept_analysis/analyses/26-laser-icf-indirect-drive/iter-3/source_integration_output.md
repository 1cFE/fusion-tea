### F-1: FLiBe inventory data fills a listed blocking gap
- **Target:** Section 4 (FLiBe Molten Salt discussion) and Section 5 (Missing Parameters table row "FLiBe inventory volume and cost")
- **Category:** analysis
- **Finding:** The analysis lists "FLiBe inventory volume and cost" as "truly-unknown / blocking" with the note "No IFE source has this; Moir 1994 is only baseline in 1994$." The OSIRIS/SOMBRERO conceptual design report (osti-servlets-purl-833813) provides: total FLiBe plant inventory = 940,000 kg (~940 tonnes) for a 1000 MWe IFE plant with a Flibe blanket/spray system. It also provides blanket flow rate (4,598 kg/s) and spray flow rate (2,265 kg/s) at Flibe inlet 500°C / outlet 650°C, plus spray manifold pressure 2.1 MPa — directly applicable as a proxy for Xcimer's HYLIFE-derived design. The gap status "no IFE source has this" is now incorrect.
- **Recommendation:** Update the Section 5 Missing Parameters row for "FLiBe inventory volume and cost" from "truly-unknown" to "partially-sourced," add a note that the OSIRIS 1992 study (osti-servlets-purl-833813) provides 940,000 kg total inventory and flow rate data for a 1000 MWe FLiBe IFE plant as the historical proxy. Add a corresponding row to the Available Parameters table (with confidence "low" / 1992 design values, 1992 dollars, inflation adjustment required). Update Section 4's FLiBe paragraph to note OSIRIS as the only available FLiBe inventory baseline alongside Moir HYLIFE-II.
- **Priority:** important

### F-2: Historical system availability 68–69% challenges the 75% placeholder
- **Target:** Section 2 (Dominant LCOE uncertainty 6 — Plant availability) and Section 5 (Missing Parameters row "Capacity factor")
- **Category:** model
- **Finding:** The analysis calls availability "the single largest LCOE driver" (elasticity −0.97), uses 75% as "an unvalidated placeholder," and states "no rep-rate-coupled availability model exists in available sources." The OSIRIS/SOMBRERO report provides a systematic subsystem availability breakdown for two 1000 MWe IFE designs: total availability 0.69 (Osiris, 4.6 Hz indirect drive) and 0.68 (SOMBRERO, 6.7 Hz KrF direct drive), composed of Driver 0.87/0.89, Reactor 0.90/0.89, Target 0.92/0.90, BOP 0.96/0.96. It also provides first-wall lifetimes of 1.8 full-power-years (Osiris FLiBe spray wall) and 5 fpy (SOMBRERO C/C dry wall). These are the only available historical IFE availability estimates — and they run below the 75% placeholder. The claim that "no availability model exists in available sources" is now incorrect.
- **Recommendation:** Add OSIRIS/SOMBRERO availability figures (0.68–0.69 total; subsystem breakdown) to the Section 5 Available Parameters table (confidence "low" / 1992 design study). Update Section 2 uncertainty 6 to cite this historical anchor and note that two complete IFE plant studies modeled 68–69% availability — slightly below the current 75% placeholder. Update the sensitivity sweep in model_setup.py to include 0.68 as a historically-grounded lower-bound scenario alongside the current 75% baseline, given that availability is the dominant LCOE driver.
- **Priority:** blocking

### F-3: SOMBRERO/OSIRIS as IFE historical cost benchmarks
- **Target:** Section 5 (Available Parameters) and Section 7 (Cross-Concept Notes / Nearest neighbors)
- **Category:** analysis
- **Finding:** The analysis has no completed IFE power plant study cost estimate in its parameter table. The OSIRIS/SOMBRERO report provides COE = 5.6 ¢/kWh (Osiris, HIB indirect drive, 1000 MWe) and 6.7 ¢/kWh (SOMBRERO, KrF direct drive, 1000 MWe) in 1992 constant dollars. SOMBRERO uses the same driver technology class as Xcimer (e-beam pumped KrF laser, 7.5% wall-plug efficiency, 3.4 MJ driver, 6.7 Hz rep rate) and the same blanket approach differs — Li₂O granules with dry C/C wall rather than FLiBe. These are the only peer-reviewed bottom-up IFE plant costs in available sources and provide essential historical anchoring for Xcimer's 30× cost reduction claim. Additionally, SOMBRERO's target gain design point of 118 directly explains why G > 100 is the cited IFE viability threshold — this lineage is not documented in the analysis. SOMBRERO should also appear in Section 7 cross-concept notes as the direct technological predecessor to Xcimer.
- **Recommendation:** Add OSIRIS and SOMBRERO COE estimates (5.6 and 6.7 ¢/kWh, 1992 constant dollars) to the Section 5 Available Parameters table with a note that inflation adjustment to 2024$ approximately doubles these figures and that they represent pre-learning-curve FOAK design studies. Add a sentence in Section 7 naming SOMBRERO as the direct technological antecedent to Xcimer's KrF approach (same driver class, same design era gain targets), so the cross-concept note explains why G > 100 is the established threshold — it was the 1992 design-study baseline, not an arbitrary criterion.
- **Priority:** important
