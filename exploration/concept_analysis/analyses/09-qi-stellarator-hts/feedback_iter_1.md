VERDICT: FINDINGS

### F-1: No modeling approach recommendation
- **Target:** Goal 4 — Modeling Approach (no corresponding section in the analysis)
- **Finding:** The analysis never states whether a parameterized cost framework (1costingfe) or free-form modeling is appropriate for this concept, nor why. This is required by Goal 4. The analysis identifies the dominant cost uncertainty (3D HTS coil manufacturing) and notes that no commercial precedent exists, but it doesn't translate this into a modeling strategy recommendation — e.g., whether the coil cost should be parameterized as a multiplier on the tokamak reference, treated as a free parameter with a wide prior, or modeled bottom-up from conductor demand.
- **Recommendation:** Add 1–2 paragraphs — either at the end of Section 2 or as a brief Section 2.5 — stating the recommended modeling approach and rationale. At minimum: (1) identify whether 1costingfe is applicable or whether free-form modeling is needed for this concept and why, and (2) state how the 3D coil cost premium (the dominant unknown) should be represented in the model (e.g., as a multiplicative factor over the wound-coil tokamak cost, bounded by Brown 2018 as a floor and the W7-X/SMC data as the ceiling when available).
- **Priority:** blocking

### F-2: Key hypotheses not stated as testable propositions
- **Target:** Goal 4, Section 2
- **Finding:** Section 2 is well-structured and explicitly ranked by LCOE impact, but it frames the top bets as characterizations of uncertainty ("the uncertainty range on coil CAPEX is wider… than for any other MFE concept") rather than testable propositions. Goal 4 requires hypotheses the cost model should test — specific claims that can be confirmed or falsified. The top three ranked challenges have clear testable forms that are not stated: e.g., whether the 3D coil manufacturing premium stays below a threshold that preserves LCOE competitiveness; whether Stellaris' lower beta and larger implied machine volume cancels the capacity factor benefit from disruption-free operation; whether the 1.07 TBR holds against realistic engineering losses. These are framed as open questions in the text but never crystallized as propositions.
- **Recommendation:** At the end of Section 2, add a short "Key Hypotheses" block (3 bullet points, one per top-ranked challenge) that states each bet as a testable proposition with a specific condition. Example form: "H1: The 3D HTS stellarator coil manufacturing cost premium over a wound tokamak coil of equivalent field strength is less than X%. If false (premium exceeds X%), stellarator CAPEX is uncompetitive regardless of availability benefit." The exact thresholds can be rough — the point is to give the cost model a falsifiable target, not a precise number.
- **Priority:** important

### F-3: CAS-level cost structure differences not mapped
- **Target:** Goal 3, Section 7 (Cross-Concept Notes)
- **Finding:** The Section 7 divergence table identifies structural differences between the QI stellarator and the HTS compact tokamak with qualitative LCOE implications, but it does not map those differences to CAS accounts. It is therefore unclear which CAS categories carry a premium, which carry a discount, and which are cost-neutral relative to the reference concept. For a concept where the dominant cost driver (3D coil geometry) is fundamentally structural, the absence of CAS-level framing leaves a gap in Goal 3 — the analysis characterizes cost implications but doesn't anchor them to the TEA cost decomposition framework.
- **Recommendation:** Add a CAS-level mapping sub-table in Section 7 (or a brief paragraph structured around CAS categories). At minimum, identify the direction and rough magnitude of the delta for the most affected accounts: magnet system (3D coil premium — large positive delta), current drive / heating (ECRH only, no NBI/ICRF, no CS — negative delta), heat transport (WCLL water-cooled vs. FLiBe — lower-temperature, simpler, smaller delta), and power conversion (EUROFER97-constrained steam Rankine — small negative delta from lower cycle efficiency vs. ARC). Neutral accounts (tritium fuel cycle, BoP structure) can be noted as such.
- **Priority:** important
