======================================================================
  Laser system:     KrF excimer ASPEN, 10 MJ/pulse, 0.5 Hz → 5 MW avg optical output
                    Source: [XEC] §Executive Summary; [an] §S5 Table
  iter-4 note:      NRL has moved primary gas-laser IFE program to ArF (193 nm).
                    KrF heritage stands as rep-rate/efficiency milestone but
                    Xcimer has no active government co-development at KrF scale.
                    Source: Optica OPN Jun 2023 §Glass vs. gas
  iter-5 note:      Xcimer Phoenix (1–2 kJ, Q2 2026) first private-sector
                    electron-beam excimer laser. Roadmap milestone confirmed;
                    does not change model parameters. Source: [i5] §Section 3
  iter-6 note:      Betti 2024 [B24]: KrF ~3 THz bandwidth is partial answer
                    to implosion symmetry gap; ArF (~10 THz) is the leading
                    government path. No model parameter change.

  Laser capital:    NOAK $70.0/J × 10 MJ = $700M  (range: $600M – $800M)
                    FOAK $110.0/J × 10 MJ = $1100M
  UNCERTAIN:        Self-reported; no independent verification.
                    Depends on capacitor cost <$0.40/J (current market ~$10/J).
                    Source: [XEC] §Xcimer Laser Cost and Schedule; [an] §S5 Table

  Thermal eff:      45% He Brayton (base) vs 33% Steam Rankine (H-3 alt)
  UNCERTAIN:        Blocking gap; HYLIFE heritage = Brayton; [sci] implies steam.
                    Source: [an] §S2 Challenge 5; §S5 Missing Parameters gap #2

  Laser wall-plug:  7% (NOAK KrF demonstrated at Electra 750 J)
  UNCERTAIN:        Xcimer aspirational target 10%; not demonstrated at MJ scale.
                    H-2 also tests 5% lower bound (→ Qsci_min = 200 for viability).
                    Source: [XEC] §Challenge 3; [an] §S5 Table; [an] §S2 H-2

  Availability:     85% upper scenario
  UNCERTAIN:        No maintenance model published. FLiBe liquid wall eliminates
                    first-wall replacement outages (advantage), but pump/nozzle
                    maintenance interval unknown.
  iter-6 (F-1):     HYLIFE-II [HY2] reports 75% CF (conservative) / 85%
                    (optimistic) for non-driver plant systems — IFE heritage
                    anchor for availability. Driver O&M component still unknown.
                    Source: [i6si] §F-1; [an] §S5 gap #3 (→ not-yet-sourced)

  FLiBe pumping:    15 MW (estimated; UNCERTAIN)
                    Framework default (1 MW) calibrated for thin-coolant IFE.
                    Thick-liquid-wall jet pump: no published estimate.
                    Source: [an] §S2 Challenge 4; [XEC] §Xcimer's Chamber Design

  Qsci (implied):   124 (from inverse power balance at 400 MWe)
                    XEC target ~250. At Qsci ~124 and η_wpe=7%:
                    Qwp = 124 × 7% = 8.7 (XEC claims ~17.5 at Qsci=250, η_wpe=7%). [XEC] §Challenge 3
  iter-6 (F-2):     Betti [B24] — gains of ~100× are 'unclear at the moment';

                    bound. Independent risk characterization, not model change.
