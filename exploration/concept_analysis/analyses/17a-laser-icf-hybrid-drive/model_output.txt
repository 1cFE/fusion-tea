                    Source: [XEC] §Executive Summary; [an] §S5 Table

                    FOAK $110.0/J × 10 MJ = $1100M
  UNCERTAIN:        Self-reported; no independent verification.
                    Depends on capacitor cost <$0.40/J (current market ~$10/J).
                    Source: [XEC] §Xcimer Laser Cost and Schedule; [an] §S5 Table

  Thermal eff:      45% He Brayton (base) vs 33% Steam Rankine (H-3 alt)
  UNCERTAIN:        Blocking gap; HYLIFE heritage = Brayton; [sci] implies steam.
                    Source: [an] §S2 Challenge 5; §S5 Missing Parameters gap #2

  Laser wall-plug:  7% (NOAK KrF demonstrated at Electra 750 J)
  UNCERTAIN:        Xcimer aspirational target 10%; not demonstrated at MJ scale.
                    Source: [XEC] §Challenge 3; [an] §S5 Table

  Availability:     85% upper scenario
  UNCERTAIN:        No maintenance model published. FLiBe liquid wall eliminates
                    first-wall replacement outages (advantage), but pump/nozzle
                    maintenance interval unknown. Source: [an] §S5 gap #3

  FLiBe pumping:    15 MW (estimated; UNCERTAIN)
                    Framework default (1 MW) calibrated for thin-coolant IFE.
                    Thick-liquid-wall jet pump: no published estimate.
                    Source: [an] §S2 Challenge 4; [XEC] §Xcimer's Chamber Design

  Qsci (implied):   211 (from inverse power balance at 400 MWe)
                    XEC target ~250. At Qsci ~211 and η_wpe=7%:
                    Qwp = 211 × 7% = 14.7 (XEC claims ~17.5 at Qsci=250, η_wpe=7%). [XEC] §Challenge 3

  Target factory:   Framework default (target_factory_base IFE scaling)
  CAS27 / FLiBe:    Framework default (FLiBe inventory at Xcimer scale unknown)
                    Default PbLi-calibrated; likely understates FLiBe/Be cost.
                    Source: [an] §S6 gap #7
  Magnets C220103:  0.0 M$ override — no superconducting coils in IFE
                    Source: [app]; [an] §S7 cross-concept table
