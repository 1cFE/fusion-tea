VERDICT: FINDINGS

### F-1: No modeling approach recommendation (1costingfe vs. free-form)
- **Target:** Section 2 and Section 7 (Modeling Approach — Goal 4)
- **Finding:** The analysis never states whether 1costingfe or free-form modeling is appropriate for the levitated dipole. This is a complete absence, not a framing gap. Given that absolute capital cost figures are deliberately withheld and no independent TEA exists, the modeling approach decision is non-obvious and load-bearing: the analyst must either map to a reference concept cost structure (1costingfe) or build free-form from mass and material analogues. The analysis assembles all the ingredients (mass breakdown by component, tape quantity, material unit costs) but never synthesizes them into a modeling recommendation. A cost modeler reading this analysis cannot determine where to start.
- **Recommendation:** Add a paragraph — either at the end of Section 2 or as a brief dedicated subsection — that explicitly states whether 1costingfe or free-form modeling is recommended, which reference concept would serve as the base if 1costingfe, and what the primary analogue estimation path is for the three major cost components (core magnet, blanket/shield, BOP). If free-form is recommended because absolute cost figures are unavailable, state that explicitly and identify the specific OpenStar paper quantities that anchor each estimate.
- **Priority:** blocking

### F-2: LCOE-sensitivity parameters not named; hypotheses not framed as testable propositions
- **Target:** Section 2 (Goal 4)
- **Finding:** Section 2 ranks challenges by LCOE impact (Critical/High/Moderate), but this is a ranking of *challenges* — it does not convert to named sensitivity parameters or testable propositions. "Confinement scaling extrapolation" is a challenge; the corresponding sensitivity parameter is τ_e or Q_sci with a range. "Sacrificial coil replacement" is a challenge; the testable hypothesis is "If annual tape replacement cost exceeds $X/MWe-yr, the concept is uncompetitive on LCOE with [reference concept]." Neither the 2-3 parameters with highest LCOE leverage nor any testable conditional propositions appear in the analysis. The analysis framing leaves a cost modeler without guidance on what to vary in sensitivity runs or how to interpret results.
- **Recommendation:** Add a closing paragraph to Section 2 (or a "Key Hypotheses" subsection) that: (a) explicitly names the 2-3 parameters with highest continuous LCOE leverage for this concept, with approximate ranges (candidates: Q_sci tied to confinement scaling, annual sacrificial coil replacement cost as % of capex, thermal efficiency given unspecified BOP cycle); and (b) states 2-3 hypotheses the cost model should test, each as a conditional ("If [parameter condition], then [LCOE implication]"), not as open questions.
- **Priority:** important

### F-3: Nearest-neighbor positioning limited to one concept; no consolidated differentiator list vs. conventional tokamak
- **Target:** Section 7 and concept framing (Goals 1-2)
- **Finding:** Section 7 provides a detailed comparison with the spherical tokamak HTS — useful and specific. However, only one nearest-neighbor concept is named. The levitated dipole's conceptual neighborhood includes at minimum one additional comparator: the field-reversed configuration (FRC), which shares the compact high-beta MFE approach, absence of linking field lines to a wall, and similar challenges with plasma physics uncertainty as the dominant cost lever. Naming only one comparator leaves the analysis underspecified on Goal 1. Additionally, differentiators from a *conventional tokamak* — the standard baseline for all non-mainstream concepts — are distributed across the narrative but never consolidated. Key differences (no plasma current, no central solenoid, no disruptions, turbulent pinch vs. neoclassical confinement, levitated internal coil vs. external TF/PF coil set) are present in the text but not surfaced as a compact list that a reader unfamiliar with the concept could use.
- **Recommendation:** Add a brief second comparison in Section 7 (3-5 sentences) identifying one additional nearest neighbor — FRC is the natural choice — noting what the levitated dipole shares vs. diverges on in TEA terms. Add a compact bullet list (4-6 items) of explicit differentiators from a conventional tokamak at the top of Section 7 or in a positioning paragraph in Section 1, covering at minimum: confinement mechanism, coil topology, disruption behavior, and operating mode basis.
- **Priority:** important
