======================================================================
PHYSICS VIABILITY ANALYSIS
======================================================================

The p-B11 fusion reaction requires ion temperatures ~150 keV —
~150× above the C-2W/Norman record of ~1 keV (recent campaigns, OSTI 2441289).
Q_plasma > 1 has never been demonstrated for p-B11 in ANY confinement device.

NBI efficiency correction (OSTI 2441289, Nuclear Fusion 2024):
  η_NBI is a two-step chain, not a single wall-plug number:
    η_NBI_source  ≈ 0.55–0.65  (wall-plug → beam, NBI source efficiency)
    η_NBI_couple  ≈ 0.35–0.50  (beam → plasma: duct losses <50% + 15% shine-through)
    η_NBI_total   ≈ 0.20–0.32  (product; 0.45 upper bound, optimistic)
  Prior analyses using η_NBI~0.55 were citing the source-only step;
  plasma-coupled efficiency is ~2× lower per C-2W experimental data.

Mathematical Q_plasma breakeven (rated power, ignoring auxiliaries):
  Q_break = 1 / [(1 − f_rad) × η_conv × η_NBI]

  Branch B (steam, f_rad=0.15, η_th=0.32, η_NBI=0.26):  Q_break ≈ 14.1
  Branch B (steam, f_rad=0.15, η_th=0.32, η_NBI=0.20):  Q_break ≈ 18.4  (conservative)
  Branch B (steam, f_rad=0.15, η_th=0.32, η_NBI=0.40):  Q_break ≈  9.2  (optimistic)
  Branch C (ICC,   f_rad=0.15, η_icc=0.90, η_NBI=0.26): Q_break ≈  5.0

Commercial minimum (with 50 MWe margin and auxiliaries):
  Branch B, η_NBI=0.26:  Q_plasma ≥ 25–35  (roughly 2× prior estimate of 10–15)
  Branch B, η_NBI=0.20:  Q_plasma ≥ 35–50
  Branch B, η_NBI=0.40:  Q_plasma ≥ 15–20

COMPOUNDING PENALTY (Mulder et al. arXiv:2103.12451):
  At sub-unity capacity factor, NBI must remain at ~full power for FRC
  stability. Effective recirculating fraction rises above rated-power
  calculation. Capacity factor and Q_plasma interact multiplicatively.
  Early Da Vinci (undemonstrated CW FRC) may operate at CF 60–75%,
  raising the effective Q_plasma requirement above the values above.

Three model branches:
  A — Physics fails (Q_plasma < threshold): no LCOE output.
  B — Da Vinci steam baseline (Q threshold met, ~32% η).
  C — ICC upgrade (Q threshold met, ~90% η): long-term economic vision.

======================================================================
KEY BINDING CONSTRAINTS (top 3 LCOE drivers)
======================================================================

  1. Q_PLASMA — The fundamental physics unknown (binding constraint)
     Q_plasma > 1 has never been demonstrated for p-B11 in any device.
     C-2W/Norman achieved ~1 keV total temperature (OSTI 2441289); Da Vinci
     requires ~150 keV ion temperature — a ~150× extrapolation with no validated
     scaling law. Q_plasma is not just the top sensitivity parameter: it determines
     whether the concept produces ANY net electricity. Below Q ≈ 14.1 (steam,
     corrected η_NBI=0.26), there is no LCOE — Branch A has no meaningful cost
     model output. The prior breakeven of Q≈6.7 assumed η_NBI=0.55 (source only).
     Status: Copernicus (unbuilt) is the intended intermediate-scale validation.
     Resolution timeline: unknown; no published Copernicus parameters.

  2. NBI CAPITAL COST & RECIRCULATING POWER FRACTION (jointly binding)
     NBI is the dominant capital account (C220104 ≈ $260M at $10M/MW × 26 MW).
     It is also the dominant recirculating load (~100 MW wall-plug at baseline,
     from 26 MW plasma / η_NBI=0.26 — more than doubling the prior ~49 MW estimate
     that used the uncorrected η_NBI=0.55). These two effects reinforce: high NBI
     cost → high capital LCOE component; high NBI recirculating load → low net
     output → high LCOE denominator. At $10M/MW_NBI, NBI accounts for >50% of
     CAS22. No commercial supply chain exists for fusion-scale high-energy NBI.
     Compounding: at CF < 85%, NBI stays at full power but plant output drops,
     raising the effective recirc fraction [arxiv-2103-12451.md].

  3. ENERGY CONVERSION ARCHITECTURE — ICC vs. Steam (structural LCOE gap)
     Da Vinci's steam baseline (~32%) vs. ICC target (~90%) represents a ~3×
     difference in gross electricity from identical fusion power. At the corrected
     Q=30 baseline, Branch B LCOE ≈ $119/MWh vs. Branch C ≈ $44/MWh — ICC
     delivers roughly 2.7× lower LCOE. ICC is patent-stage only — zero
     experimental demonstration at MeV-range fusion product energies.
     The near-term Da Vinci plant cannot realize the core economic advantage
     of the aneutronic fuel choice (direct conversion) — it pays the capital
     cost of an aneutronic system but achieves only steam cycle efficiency.
     Insight: p-B11 FRC LCOE is only competitive if BOTH the physics (Q_plasma
     threshold) AND the ICC technology succeed. Steam-only p-B11 FRC is
     economically inferior to any D-T concept with equivalent Q_plasma.
