══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
KEY BINDING CONSTRAINTS — top 3 LCOE drivers
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. FUSION GAIN Q: THE SINGLE BLOCKING CONSTRAINT
     Q is the product of an undemonstrated physics mechanism — there is no
     validated path from acoustic cavitation (~16,000 K) to D-D fusion
     (~10^8 K). The demonstrated temperature gap is ~4 orders of magnitude.
     Until Q > 3 is demonstrated in a credible replicated experiment,
     all downstream LCOE calculations are purely hypothetical.
     LCOE leverage: Q=5 → ~30-40 ¢/kWh; Q=10 → ~10-15 ¢/kWh;
     Q=25 → ~3-5 ¢/kWh. Net-positive operation requires Q ≥ ~3.
     STATUS: Not demonstrated. Taleyarkhan (2002) claims discredited.

  2. CAPITAL COST — D₂O VESSEL DOMINATES
     At baseline (3m radius vessel, $700K/m³ D₂O), the D₂O fill alone
     costs ~$79M per module ($317M for 4 modules).
     This is unavoidable for the concept: the D₂O IS the fusion medium.
     Higher Q → more power per vessel → lower $/kWe → lower LCOE.
     The D₂O cost is a fixed capital item, so plant availability and
     capacity factor strongly leverage the amortization.
     Potential mitigation: use deuterated acetone (cheaper per kg?) but
     no industrial-scale supply chain exists; radioactivity concerns apply.

  3. RECIRCULATING POWER FRACTION (DRIVER EFFICIENCY)
     The acoustic driver draws full electrical power continuously.
     At Q=5: driver recirculates ~20-35% of gross electric.
     At Q=10: driver recirculates ~11% of gross electric (baseline).
     PZT transducer efficiency (85-95%) is already high and not a
     major lever — the binding factor is Q, not transducer efficiency.
     Transducer capital cost ($500/kW → $42.5M/module) is secondary to
     D₂O vessel cost and shield cost in the capital breakdown.

  NOTE: This model makes dozens of speculative assumptions about a concept
  that has not demonstrated fusion. All scenario LCOEs should be treated as
  existence proofs ('could be economic IF physics works') rather than
  predictions. The analysis.md §Section 2 identifies 5 blocking uncertainties.
