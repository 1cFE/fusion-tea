KEY BINDING CONSTRAINTS — top 3 LCOE drivers
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. FUSION GAIN Q: THE SINGLE BLOCKING SCIENTIFIC CONSTRAINT
     Q is the product of an undemonstrated physics mechanism.
     Demonstrated sonoluminescence: ~16,000 K (Flannigan & Suslick 2010).
     Required for D-D thermonuclear fusion: ~10⁸ K.
     Temperature gap: ~4 orders of magnitude — no published mechanism bridges it.
     Net-positive operation (baseline params) requires Q ≥ ~3.5.
     LCOE leverage: Q=5 → ~35–50 ¢/kWh; Q=10 → ~12–15 ¢/kWh;
     Q=25 → ~3–5 ¢/kWh (all conditional on physics being achievable).
     STATUS: Not demonstrated. Taleyarkhan (2002) claims discredited (2008).

  2. D₂O VESSEL CAPITAL COST — $56M fill + ~$15M structure per module
     2023 UN Comtrade: D₂O global export prices $300–$475/kg (volume-weighted
     ~$450/kg); India+Canada supply ~80% of global exports by value.
     A 3m-radius vessel holds ~113 m³ of D₂O at 1,105 kg/m³.
     D₂O fill alone: ~$56M per module (4 modules = ~$225M).
     This is unavoidable — D₂O IS the fusion medium.
     Higher Q → more fusion power per vessel → lower $/kWe → lower LCOE.
     Potential mitigation: deuterated acetone (cheaper per kg) but no
     industrial supply and radiation compatibility unknown.

  3. RECIRCULATING POWER / Q THRESHOLD — SENSITIVE TO η_DRIVER
     The acoustic driver draws full electrical power continuously.
     Breakeven requires: Q_sci × η_driver × η_thermal > 1 + aux_fraction.
     At η_driver=0.85, η_th=0.35: Q_sci × 0.298 > 1 → Q_sci > 3.36 (baseline).
     At η_driver=0.65, η_th=0.35: Q_sci × 0.228 > 1 → Q_sci > 4.39.
     At η_driver=0.55, η_th=0.35: Q_sci × 0.193 > 1 → Q_sci > 5.19.
     The η_driver=0.85 assumption (unsupported — only Kp≥0.55 measured) raises
     the Q breakeven threshold by ~50% if the true efficiency is closer to 0.55.
     LCOE elasticity: |ε(η_driver)| ≈ 0.52 — nearly equal to |ε(Q)| ≈ 0.53.
     Both Q and η_driver are speculative; see driver efficiency sweep above.
     Ref: APC International 90-4040 (Kp≥0.55); analysis.md §Section 3.

  NOTE: This model makes dozens of speculative assumptions about a concept
  that has not demonstrated fusion. All LCOE values should be treated as
  'LCOE IF physics works at stated Q' — existence proofs, not forecasts.
  See analysis.md §Section 2 for 5 blocking uncertainties and §Section 6
  for the full data gap inventory (15 items, 9 blocking).
