### F-1: γ sensitivity sweep conflates two distinct physical scenarios
- **Target:** model_setup.py — sensitivity sweep and conservative scenario
- **Category:** model
- **Finding:** The analysis narrative (Section 2) explicitly states that γ=0.2 requires either (a) 156 MW beam for the same 980 MW fusion power, or (b) 490 MW fusion at 78 MW beam — two distinct operational branches with different LCOE implications. The sensitivity sweep implements only branch (b): it varies fusion power while holding e-beam at 78 MW. This underestimates the γ=0.2 penalty for a plant designed at 980 MW fusion capacity, because a plant configured for 301 MWe that now only produces 89 MWe suffers a severe underutilization penalty (the capital cost is fixed). The conservative scenario (γ=0.2, FOAK) shows p_net=75 MWe with 490 MW fusion — also branch (b). The correct γ=0.2 scenario should simultaneously set p_fus=490 MW AND p_beam=156 MW (or equivalently p_fus=980 MW, p_beam=156 MW), using the Park 2025 scaling relation that ties beam power and fusion power to γ. Mixing the two branches in different parts of the model means no scenario correctly represents the true physics of a γ deviation.
- **Recommendation:** Implement a parameterized γ variable in model_setup.py that computes both p_fus and p_beam from γ using the Park 2025 relation (p_beam ∝ γ, p_fus ∝ 1 for fixed beam or p_fus ∝ 1/γ for fixed confinement state). Add a dedicated γ sweep where both quantities update together. The conservative scenario should use the physically correct coupled values; the current proxy (p_fus only) may be retained as a separate labeled sensitivity but should not be labeled as the γ sweep.
- **Priority:** blocking

### F-2: Blanket cost (C220101) not flagged as HIGH UNCERT despite TRL 1 and geometry-specific challenge
- **Target:** model_setup.py — CAS22 account C220101
- **Category:** model
- **Finding:** The model correctly flags the SC coil system ($150M) and e-beam injection system ($100M) as HIGH UNCERT concept-specific overrides. However, C220101 Blanket/First Wall is assigned $7.3M via a generic analogue formula with no HIGH UNCERT flag. The analysis devotes substantial attention to the blanket geometry challenge (Section 3: TRL 1; Section 6, gap #5: blocking) — the polyhedral coil geometry creates a neutron-shadowing problem with no proposed engineering solution. The analogue formula that produces $7.3M is derived from conventional toroidal MFE geometry and is not applicable to a 6-faced polyhedral cusp. For reference, ARIES-class D-T reactor studies estimate blanket costs at $50–150M for comparable neutron power levels. The current $7.3M placeholder understates a known concept-specific cost uncertainty by potentially an order of magnitude, making the blanket the only major cost driver identified in the analysis that is not reflected in the model's uncertainty flags.
- **Recommendation:** Convert C220101 to a concept-specific override with a wider uncertainty range (e.g., $50–200M, with $75M as baseline analogue), flag it as HIGH UNCERT, and add a blanket cost sensitivity sweep. Include a note in the model output that the analogue formula is inapplicable given the polyhedral geometry and that the actual cost depends on an unperformed neutronics study.
- **Priority:** important

### F-3: Key risks described as narratives rather than testable propositions
- **Target:** Section 2
- **Category:** analysis
- **Finding:** Goal 4 requires that key hypotheses be stated as testable propositions with pass/fail criteria, not as open uncertainties. Section 2 correctly identifies γ, virtual cathode formation, and bremsstrahlung balance as the three critical physics bets, but frames them as narrative uncertainty statements ("the entire design hinges on γ=0.1," "this theoretical tension is not resolved"). The analysis agent and future readers cannot determine from the current framing what experimental result would cause the TEA scenarios to update, at what threshold, or within what timeline. As written, the risk section is a narrative risk inventory, not a decision-gate structure.
- **Recommendation:** Add three explicit testable propositions to Section 2: H1: "Wiffle-Ball confinement achieves γ ≤ 0.1 at commercial plasma density n~10²¹ m⁻³; test: FPNS program; if false (γ > 0.1), net output collapses to near-breakeven and the moderate scenario is invalidated." H2: "Virtual cathode formation is achievable at commercial densities without the ~200,000 A electron supply rate calculated by University of Sydney (2019); test: independent experiment or EMC2 counter-publication; if false, the core confinement mechanism fails regardless of γ." H3: "Bremsstrahlung radiation does not eliminate net energy gain for a thermalized 20 keV D-T plasma at reactor density; test: first-principles calculation using Park 2025's thermalized plasma assumption; if false, gross thermal output is reduced by ~5–10%." These turn the narrative into conditional branches that can be tracked as FPNS data emerges.
- **Priority:** important
