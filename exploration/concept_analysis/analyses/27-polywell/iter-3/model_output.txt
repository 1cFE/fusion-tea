KEY BINDING CONSTRAINTS (in order of LCOE leverage)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. LOSS REDUCTION FACTOR γ (proxy: p_fus_MW sweep)
     The entire cost model rests on the unvalidated assumption γ=0.1.
     γ controls the electron loss rate from the Wiffleball cusp.
     At γ=0.2, p_fus halves to ~490 MW (or beam power doubles to 156 MW)
     and LCOE roughly doubles. No experiment has validated γ=0.1 at
     any plasma density above WB-X's sub-microsecond pulses.
     University of Sydney (2019) found 'little or no trace of virtual
     electrode formation' at higher densities — contested by EMC2 but
     unrefuted in peer-reviewed literature.
     STATUS: Blocking. FPNS program is the next validation milestone.

  2. THERMAL EFFICIENCY / ENERGY CONVERSION ARCHITECTURE
     No thermal cycle, coolant, or BOP has been specified by EMC2.
     The sweep shows ±5 ¢/kWh variation between 33% and 50% efficiency.
     80% of fusion energy (14.1 MeV neutrons) flows into a blanket with
     unspecified cooling. Without a defined thermal circuit, net electric
     output is unknown within ±50%. The 40% Rankine analogue is a
     placeholder, not an engineering assessment.
     STATUS: Blocking for serious LCOE estimation.

  3. CAPITAL COST UNCERTAINTY (coil + e-beam overrides dominate CAS22)
     The two concept-specific CAS22 overrides (C220103=$150M coils,
     C220104=$100M e-beam) together represent ~$250M of the per-module
     CAS22. These are analogues with ×2–3 uncertainty. The sweep shows
     coil cost is the larger lever (no existing SC Polywell design).
     Unlike MFE tokamaks, no engineering cost study or supplier quote
     exists for 6-sided polyhedral SC coils at 4.5 T.
     STATUS: High uncertainty, reducible with an engineering design study.

  4. BLANKET/FIRST WALL COST (C220101) — POLYHEDRAL GEOMETRY CHALLENGE
     The standard toroidal MFE analogue ($7.3M) is inapplicable to the
     6-faced polyhedral cusp. Coils on each face create neutron-shadowing
     with no proposed engineering solution (TRL 1, analysis.md §Section 3
     and §Section 6 gap #5: blocking). ARIES-class D-T studies estimate
     $50–150M for comparable neutron power levels; the model override
     baseline of $75M may understate by 2× if novel breeding geometry is
     required. The blanket cost sensitivity sweep quantifies this range.
     STATUS: High uncertainty; blocking for blanket design feasibility.
