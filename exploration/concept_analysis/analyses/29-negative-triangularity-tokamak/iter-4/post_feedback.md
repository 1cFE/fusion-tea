### F-1: H_NA sweep captures only heating-capital channel, understating ohmic-only benefit
- **Target:** Model — H_NA sensitivity sweep (Carried-Forward F-1 table)
- **Category:** model
- **Finding:** The H_NA sweep parameterizes only C220104 (ICRF capital cost) linearly from $150M → $0 as H_NA goes 1.0 → 2.0, producing a narrow 87.4–91.6 $/MWh range at 1 GWe (~4.6% improvement). But the two-scenario comparison table directly above it shows Q_eng = 7.71 (ICRF) vs. 16.04 (ohmic-only) — a factor-of-two difference in electrical efficiency. That Q_eng shift, driven by eliminated recirculating power from the 40 MW ICRF system, is the economically significant channel; the $150M capital saving is secondary. The sweep table presents ohmic-only as a minor improvement when the physics benefit — roughly doubling Q_eng — would, if wired to net electric output, produce a substantially larger LCOE reduction. The model note acknowledges "P_fus/Q_p not wired" but the effect is to make the most important NT scenario branch appear inconsequential in the parametric table.
- **Recommendation:** Extend the H_NA sweep to also scale net electric output proportionally with Q_eng at each H_NA step (using the Q_eng values from the binary scenario table as endpoints), so the full LCOE range reflects both the capital-cost and recirculating-power channels. Add a note in the sweep header distinguishing the capital-only channel from the physics channel and quantifying the contribution of each to the total LCOE delta between the binary scenarios.
- **Priority:** important

### F-2: Section 2 conflates concept viability risks with LCOE sensitivity parameters
- **Target:** Section 2 (challenge ranking)
- **Category:** analysis
- **Finding:** Section 2 ranks seven challenges by "impact on LCOE uncertainty" but uses concept viability framing (physics risks that could make the concept not work at all) rather than LCOE sensitivity magnitude (parameters that move LCOE most per unit change). The model sensitivity table shows the top engineering LCOE levers are availability (elasticity −0.96) and construction time (+0.40), with TF coil cost next (±50% → 78.8–104.4 $/MWh range at 1 GWe, a ±14% LCOE swing). None of these appear in Section 2's "Critical" tier — which instead holds NT confinement scaling and commercial scaling path (both existential physics risks, not sensitivity parameters). Section 2 never explicitly names availability, construction time, or TF coil cost as the top 2–3 LCOE sensitivity levers for this concept. A reader following the analysis to build a sensitivity sweep would prioritize the wrong parameters.
- **Recommendation:** Add a short paragraph at the end of Section 2 — or restructure the challenge ranking — that explicitly separates (a) concept viability risks (NT confinement scaling, ohmic-only hypothesis) from (b) LCOE sensitivity parameters (availability, TF coil cost, construction time). Name the top 2–3 LCOE elasticity drivers for this concept and note that availability and TF coil cost dominate regardless of NT-specific physics outcomes. This does not diminish the physics risk discussion; it adds the sensitivity-specific framing required to orient the cost model.
- **Priority:** important

### F-3: Q_eng calibration gap vs. MANTA reference is unresolved and propagates into 1 GWe scaling
- **Target:** Model — key assumptions block and 1 GWe scaled result
- **Category:** model
- **Finding:** The model reports Q_eng = 1.74 vs. MANTA's published Q_e = 2.4 — a 28% discrepancy — with only the note "gap reflects differing recirculating-power accounting conventions." This gap is not reconciled, and it propagates into the 1 GWe scaling: the scaled result ($91.6/MWh, Q_eng = 7.71) derives from a reference point that overestimates recirculating power fraction relative to MANTA. If the model's recirculating power load is overstated, then the 1 GWe LCOE is pessimistic, but by an unquantified amount. For an analysis where the commercial-scale LCOE ($91.6/MWh vs. ARIES-ACT $64/MWh) is a central comparison, a ~28% error in Q_eng at the reference point is large enough to matter to the conclusions.
- **Recommendation:** Add a reconciliation note in the model's assumptions block identifying which recirculating power loads are included in the model's Q_eng accounting but not in MANTA's Q_e = 2.4 (e.g., cryogenic loads, housekeeping power, tritium processing). Quantify whether correcting the Q_eng to match MANTA would change the 1 GWe LCOE by more or less than 5 $/MWh, and flag this as a model calibration uncertainty in the key assumptions.
- **Priority:** important
