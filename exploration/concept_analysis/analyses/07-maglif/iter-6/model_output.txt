KEY ASSUMPTIONS
=================================================================
  Scenario A / B net electric:  1000 / 250 MWe
  Availability:                 85%   (UNCERTAIN: thick-LW success scenario)
  Lifetime:                     30 yr
  Thermal efficiency:           42%   (Brayton-Rankine, steel chamber)
  Driver wall-plug efficiency:  60%   (LTD; IMG claims 90%, unverified)
  Driver capital:               $372M (LTD Z-IFE median; IMG may be 5-10× lower)
  Rep rate (implied):           ~0.5 Hz (Z-IFE optimized frozen-FLiBe RTL case)
  No superconducting magnets    (HTS/Nb3Sn fully eliminated; $5M Cu coils only)
  Thick liquid FLiBe wall       (scheduled FW replacement eliminated IF demonstrated)
  Frozen-FLiBe RTL              (eliminates 170 MWe steel RTL remanufacturing load)

CRITICAL UNCERTAINTIES NOT CAPTURED IN THIS ESTIMATE:
  1. Rep rate achievement: COE scales ~10:1 with rep rate
     (0.1 Hz → ~20 c/kWh; 1.0 Hz → ~5 c/kWh at 1 GWe per Z-IFE)
  2. Per-shot consumable O&M: target cost must reach <$2/shot
     ($0.70/shot historical steel RTL; cryo ice-layer targets orders-of-magnitude higher)
  3. Gain scaling unvalidated: χ ≈ 0.1 on Z; GJ-class yields undemonstrated
     (simulation-anchored at 50-60 MA per arXiv:2504.10680; not experimentally tested)
  4. IMG driver capital: may be 2-5× lower than $372M LTD reference
     (PMF §3.2.4: must decrease 5-10× from $5/J current commercial pricing)
  5. Chamber lifetime under GJ-scale repetitive shots: untested environment
  6. ITER-6 new: 250 MWe commercial target (Scenario B) faces economy-of-scale
     penalty vs. 1000 MWe Z-IFE reference — all published COE figures are lower
     bounds for Pacific Fusion's actual commercial design point.
     Source: ans-news-2025-04-24-article-6980-pacific-fusion-fusing.md

  See analysis.md §Section 2 and §Section 6 for full gap inventory.
