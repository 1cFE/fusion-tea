VERDICT: FINDINGS

### F-1: Free-form model recommendation not specified to the level needed for implementation
- **Target:** Section 2 (Modeling Recommendation) and model output consistency check
- **Finding:** The analysis correctly identifies that 1costingfe/ARIES/PROCESS are inapplicable to MagLIF (Goal 4) and calls for a free-form parametric model treating rep rate, yield/shot, driver $/J, and target $/shot as the four primary free variables. However, neither the algebraic COE formula nor the parametric sweep structure is specified. The consequence is visible in the model output: the current model is implemented in 1costingfe with overrides, producing a sensitivity table where `availability` dominates at elasticity −0.90 while the three stated dominant LCOE levers (rep rate, target $/shot, driver $/J) all show elasticity ~0 because they are overridden constants. A reader consuming only the sensitivity table would conclude that availability is the key parameter to optimize — the opposite of the analysis's central claim that rep rate is the 10:1 LCOE multiplier. The model output's own footnote acknowledges this discrepancy, but the fix requires the analysis to specify the model structure, not just acknowledge the limitation.
- **Recommendation:** Add a "Free-form model specification" paragraph to the Section 2 modeling recommendation. It should include: (1) the algebraic COE expression — COE = (FCR × C_capital + C_om_fixed + C_om_variable) / (rep_rate × yield_per_shot × eta_th × availability × 8760 hr/yr), with C_capital decomposed into driver ($/J × E_driver_J), chamber, BOP, and RTL/target factory terms; (2) three explicit parametric sweep tables: LCOE vs. rep rate at 0.1/0.25/0.5/1.0/1.5 Hz (holding yield and capital constant), LCOE vs. target cost at $0.10/$1/$10/$100/shot (at 0.5 Hz reference), and LCOE vs. driver capital at $0.10/$0.50/$1.0/$5.0/J (at 0.5 Hz, $1/shot target). These sweep tables directly test the three hypotheses already stated in Section 2.
- **Priority:** important
