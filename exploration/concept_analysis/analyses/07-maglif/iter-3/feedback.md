VERDICT: FINDINGS

### F-1: Missing cost disposition labels on three key differentiators
- **Target:** Key Differentiators section (before Section 1)
- **Finding:** Five of the eight key differentiators have explicit "Cost disposition:" labels, but three do not: *per-shot consumables*, *novel driver capital cost category*, and *thick liquid wall*. The cost implications of these three appear in Section 2 prose but are absent at the summary level. This is consequential because the novel driver capital and thick liquid wall differentiators are among MagLIF's most distinctive cost features — a reader scanning the differentiator list will see that driver capital is "96% of plant capital" but won't see a stated disposition (dominant novel account; no intrinsic advantage vs. MFE, must be reduced to reach commercial economics). The thick liquid wall is described as potentially eliminating scheduled maintenance but no disposition (conditional cost advantage — neutral if undemonstrated) appears at the bullet level.
- **Recommendation:** Add "Cost disposition:" lines to the three unlabeled bullets. Suggested framings: (1) novel driver capital — "novel dominant cost account, not an advantage vs. MFE; competitive economics require 5–10× cost reduction from current ~$5/J commercial baseline"; (2) per-shot consumables — "cost penalty vs. MFE; creates a variable O&M floor with no analogue; magnitude unknown at commercial scale but potentially binding"; (3) thick liquid wall — "conditional cost advantage — eliminates capacity-factor-limiting maintenance if demonstrated; cost neutral if thick-liquid-wall design fails to validate."
- **Priority:** important

### F-2: Driver wall-plug efficiency absent from Section 5 parameter table
- **Target:** Section 5 (LCOE-Relevant Parameters)
- **Finding:** Driver wall-plug efficiency (~90% for IMG, vs. ~5–15% for laser IFE drivers) is identified as a key differentiator in Section 7 and implied in Section 3, but has no row in the Section 5 parameter table. For a pulsed concept where net plant output = gross fusion power × (1 − recirculating fraction), and recirculating fraction is directly set by driver efficiency, this is a first-order modeling parameter. The free-form parametric model recommended in Section 2 will need an explicit driver efficiency handle to compute net output under different architecture assumptions (LTD at 60% vs. IMG at 90%). The ~90% claim for IMG also carries medium confidence and has not been validated at plant scale — this uncertainty should appear in the table like other low-confidence parameters.
- **Recommendation:** Add a parameter row for "Driver wall-plug efficiency (IMG, claimed)" = ~90% with low-to-medium confidence, and a corresponding derived row for "Recirculating power fraction at reference yield/shot" (computed as driver_energy_in / gross_electric_out). State the LCOE consequence: at 90% efficiency the recirculating fraction is ~10–15%; at laser-class 15% efficiency it would be ~50–85%, roughly tripling the required gross output for the same net delivery. This translation is what makes the efficiency advantage TEA-relevant rather than just a physics claim.
- **Priority:** important
