## How the oracle is built (mental model)

- **The plant oracle cannot run a material instance.** `verify_stellaris.compute()` evaluates the plant REBCO law on every call, and that law refuses any supply temperature other than 20 K and any field outside 20–32 T (`exploration/stellarator_e2e/verify_stellaris.py:902-909`). So every Nb₃Sn instance, and every REBCO instance below 20 T, raises there. Design § 6.3 gives the plant oracle "every channel whose producer is an unchanged plant calc" in material instances; that ownership can only be realized by composing the plant oracle's function-level pieces (surfaced as G2).
- **So the chain is recomposed.** `_plant_chain(p, legs)` calls every function-level piece of the plant oracle family unchanged (list below) and re-derives the inline equations of `compute()`, each against its SysML definition. With `legs=None` it is the reference chain; with material legs it applies the rebound legs of design §§ 2.1–2.9.
- **Two proofs that the recomposition is the plant oracle.** (a) With reference legs, all 1,362 outputs equal `verify_stellaris.compute()` bit for bit (`test_reference_chain_is_bit_identical`). (b) At the REBCO bridge (20 K, 24.9 T, where the plant oracle can run the same plant inputs), all 1,288 plant-owned material channels equal the plant oracle bit for bit (`test_composed_material_equals_plant_oracle_where_the_plant_oracle_can_run`).
- **The rebound legs.** The arm-modified `B_peak` feeds stress, strain, `peak_field_ok`, both Round 1 laws, the area steel rule, `pack_field` and `shape_branch` (D3, D13). The plant REBCO law is gated to zeros. The rollup reads `winding_sum.cost`. `pb.p_cryo` reads the staged refrigeration electricity. `tf_power` reads the staged drive. The cryo package price is the Green capital. The cold and intercept screens read the staged demands. The dormant plant chains (tape metres, 0.20-Carnot cold chain, thermal inventory) still run and are reported (K23).

## Equations as implemented

Units: A, T, K, m, kg, W unless named; Round 1 areas mm²; Round 1 money and the Green capital USD2021 inside the mixed-year plant (K14).

- **Gated plant REBCO law (§ 2.1, B1).** `enabled` ∉ {0, 1} (including NaN and Booleans) raises. At 0, all 11 outputs and `evaluation_defined` are 0.0 before any domain check. At 1, `verify_stellaris._conductor_current` unchanged plus `evaluation_defined = 1.0`. Material instances always run at 0.
- **Arm-modified peak field (§ 2.2, B2).** Guards in order: `R − a_coil > 0`, `R_ref − a_coil_ref > 0`, finite arm inputs, `wp_side > 0`. Then `bore_norm = [R/(R − a_coil)]/[R_ref/(R_ref − a_coil_ref)]`, `x = R/wp_side`, `ratio_eff = peak_ratio + arm_slope·(x − arm_x_ref)`, refuse `ratio_eff ≤ 0`, `B_peak = (B_axis·ratio_eff)·bore_norm`. At slope 0 this is bit-identical to `verify_stellaris.py:1077`.
- **`'Material Winding Adapter'`.** `turn_length = f_set·c_coil`; `pack_area_per_turn = wp_side·wp_side·1e6/reference_turns`.
- **`'Material Winding Cost'`.** `cost = sc_cost + materials_cost + cost_helium + winding_fabrication_cost` (Round 1 inventory, then the plant's helium and winding operations, N3, N4).
- **`'Pack Field Checks'`.** `R_over_sqrt_A_wp = R/wp_side`; `ampere_floor = μ0·I_coil/(4·wp_side)`; `ampere_floor_margin = B_peak − ampere_floor`. `I_coil = reference_turns × turn_current`.
- **`'REBCO Shape Branch'`.** `shape_mode = 1.0 if B_peak > B_knot_max else 0.0` (NaN gives 0.0).
- **`'Staged Static Loads'`.** `area_cold = n_coils·c_coil·4·(wp_side + 2 t_case)`; `q_radiation = area_cold·eps_eff·σ·(T_shield⁴ − T_cold⁴)`; `conduction_ref = n_coils·g_per_coil·k_c·(T_shield − T_conduction_ref)`; `shield_static = shield_area_ratio·area_cold·q_MLI − q_radiation + n_coils·g_per_coil·k_s·(T_amb − T_shield) − conduction_ref`; `p_joint_ref = p_fixed_MW·1e6`. At 20 K: 266.68 W, 590.28 W, 7,561.69 W, as the design states.
- **`'Staged Drive Power'`.** `p_drive = (q_leads + (q_shield − shield_static) + joint_drive_fraction·q_joints)·1e-6` [MW]. At 20 K and 50 kA it equals the pin's 0.050267327222555655 MW.
- **Round 1 pieces**, bound as design §§ 2.5–2.7 bind them: `nb3sn_cable_critical_surface` / `rebco_cable_critical_surface` (`T_supply = T_cold_cryo`, `B_peak` from the glue leg, `shape_mode` from the branch), `winding_turn_area_screen` (`available_area = pack_area_per_turn`), `winding_inventory_and_cost` (plant densities and prices, `manufacturing_per_m = 0`), `magnet_cold_stage_load` (`nuclear_density = q_nuc_cryo`, `cold_volume = vol_cold_total`, radiation, conduction and joints from the static loads), `staged_refrigeration_screen` (`rating_cold = rated_cold_W`, `f_carnot_shield` the plant's). At 20 K the staged `q_cold` and `q_shield` equal the pin's demands to the last digit (21,933.902368719853 W, 41,599.953939961626 W).
- **Seam insertions.** `pb.p_cryo = refrigeration.p_in_total_MW`; `tf_power = p_tf + staged_drive.p_drive`; `cryo_cost = refrigeration.refrigerator_capital`, refused if negative or nonfinite (the 'Supplied Auxiliary Cooling Cost' domain); `magnet_capital_rollup = winding_sum.cost + structure + insulation stock`; the cold screen demand is `cold_stage.q_cold` with the plant's `demand_available_in` flag; the intercept screen demand is `cold_stage.q_shield` with availability `true` (or a supplied Boolean `cryoplant__intercept_demand_available`). Both screens use `oracle_capability.screen` with the cryogenic-state applicability and support the capability oracle computed.
- **Verdicts (73).** The 67 plant predicates are evaluated from the reference package's own predicate IR (`exploration/stellarator_e2e/pkg/stellarator_tea/contracts/model_contract.json`) with `oracle_entry.OPERAND_BINDINGS` re-prefixed, Kleene three-valued (the catalog's `evaluation_semantics`). E6 re-points `reference_conductor_current_ok` to `magnet__conductor__acceptance_margin`. The six new checks use Round 1's constraint defs (`models/library/analyses/magnet_conductor_alternatives.sysml:266-295`) and design § 2.4's 'Ampere Floor': `magnet__acceptance_ok` (`supported ≥ 1 and acceptance_margin ≥ 0`), `magnet__copper_ok`, `magnet__steel_ok`, `magnet__pack_area_ok` (`area.fit_margin ≥ 0`), `magnet__ampere_floor_ok`, `cryoplant__capacity_ok`. Verdicts are keyed by suffix-stripped local identity, since the material constraint-id hashes are the build's.
- **Structure-mass rule (recorded-design check).** `m_rule = 11,615,600 kg × (W_mag/111 GJ) × variant`; the check reports the relative residual of the supplied `m_support` and a verdict at a stated tolerance (default 1e-9).
- **LCOE breakdown (D2).** `lcoe_breakdown` sums the § 5.2 capital groups and multiplies each by `idc·crf/E`, with `E = 8760·p_net·availability`, `idc = (1+d)^(Yc/2)`, `crf` from `oracle_finance`; CAS71, CAS72 total and CAS80 are divided by `E`. `closure_residuals` recomputes magnet capital, aux cooling, powercore, BOP, CAS22, CAS2x, CAS20, overnight, total, the CAS70 annual total and LCOE from the accounts through the plant's identities.
- **Labels (study-side, for cross-check only).** `free_capacity = [cryoplant__rated_intercept_W]` (D17), `envelope_flag`, `green_extrapolated`, `arm_extrapolated` (`R/√A_wp` outside 25–40), the two beta verdicts (0.05, 0.04), and for REBCO `extrapolated` (20 < B ≤ 24), `beyond_law_extents` (24 < B ≤ 25), `above_stellaris_envelope` (B > 24.9).

## Ambiguities in the design or contract (flagged; resolution stated)

- **G1. NIST `k_c` collides with the plant's `k_c`.** Design § 2.7 declares the NIST fit as 'Staged Cryoplant' attributes `k_a … k_i`. 'Cryoplant' already owns `k_c`, the support conductance integral (`models/library/structure/mfe_plant_systems.sysml:554`, bound 5.39362701769 at `stellarator_plant.sysml:1387`), and `static_loads` binds that `k_c` while `cold_stage` would bind NIST `k_c` (0.2543). My first run showed the consequence: with one key for both, conduction dropped from 590.28 W to 27.83 W. Resolution here: `cryoplant__k_c` is the plant's key; the NIST fit is Round 1's constant; the other eight NIST names are accepted only at Round 1's values. The implementer needs a distinct name for NIST `k_c`.
- **G2. The plant oracle cannot own material-instance channels as § 6.3 states.** Its `compute()` refuses every 4.5 K point and every REBCO point below 20 T (evidenced by `test_plant_oracle_refuses_material_points_the_composition_evaluates`). Resolution: plant-owned material channels are produced by the plant oracle's imported pieces plus re-derived inline equations, and are proven equal to the plant oracle wherever it can run.
- **G3. The basis-bridge identity holds only on the plant's own basis.** The design's bridge (4 mm tape, n = 1.5·n_ref = 169.06, construction C, 80 $/m) cannot reproduce plant magnet capital. Its conductor and material metres are f_set/f_wp_vol = 0.991 of the plant's (D12, confirmed to 1e−12), and it prices 4 mm tape. Resolution: the "exactly" test uses the composition-implied count on the set basis (6 mm tape, n = `parallel_tapes_set`, 20 $/m, per-turn areas = plant fractions × the volume basis). The D12 gap is tested separately.
- **G4. The structure-mass anchor is rounded.** Contract § 5 prints 11,615.6 t. The pin's supplied mass is 11,615.604482575215 t, so the reference design itself fails the rule check at 1e−9 (residual 3.86e−7) and passes at 1e−6. The contract states no check tolerance and no mass rounding. Resolution: the rule uses the printed constant and the check reports the residual at a caller-stated tolerance.
- **G5. Three verdict owners are unnamed.** § 6.3 and D3 do not assign `magnet__acceptance_ok`, `magnet__copper_ok`, `magnet__steel_ok`. Resolution: round1, since their operands are Round 1 outputs and their predicates are Round 1 constraint defs.
- **G6. The staged cold load omits the plant's structure-nuclear term.** The plant's cold load adds `q_nuc_structure·m_support/ρ` (`mfe_cryo_inventory.sysml:62`); design § 2.7 binds only `q_nuc_cryo·vol_cold_total`. It is 0 at the pin, but `cryoplant__q_nuc_structure` is a live entry key, and a case that varies it would move only the dormant plant chain, not the staged cold load. Resolution: implemented as the design states.
- **G7. The K8 fallbacks change dormant channels.** If probe P1 fails and R1/R2's fallbacks are used, `power_supplies.p_tf` becomes calculated and the staged electricity runs through `p_cryo` with `f_uplift_cryo = 0`. Then `cryo_elec.p_elec` and the `direct_electric_capability` demand carry the staged value. The oracle implements the primary seams. A fallback build needs these channels re-owned.
- **G8. B2's new guard is not quite neutral.** `ratio_eff ≤ 0` also refuses a non-positive supplied `peak_ratio` at slope 0, which the unmodified body accepts. Every evaluable reference has a positive ratio, so the regression cannot see it. Resolution: implemented as B2 states.
- **G9. Entry-key names are partly unspecified.** The design names families, not every emitted key. The schema above (`magnet__<Round 1 name>`, `cryoplant__<name>`, `magnet__coil__arm_*`, `magnet__pack_field__mu0_in`, `magnet__conductor__eps_intrinsic_in`, optional `cryoplant__intercept_demand_available`) must be mapped by the package's `oracle_entry.py` if codegen emits other names. Missing plant keys default to the pin, which equals the material design file's defaults only for the REBCO bridge. The Nb₃Sn default design lives in `reference_designs.json`, which I did not read, so callers must pass complete recorded inputs.
- **G10. Label bands.** REBCO `extrapolated` and `beyond_law_extents` take 20 < B ≤ 24 and 24 < B ≤ 25. The Nb₃Sn "edge 13 T" flag is not computed: contract § 4 prints 12/13/14/14.5 T while Round 1's bounds are 12.2/13.5/14.5 T.
- **G11. Two § 5.2 items are not channels.** The decomposition's "supplied `cas28_capital`" is an input, so it is read from inputs. Test 4(a)'s pipeline-wiring checks are outside any oracle's reach.
- **G12. Coil 0's pack.** The 13.44 T floor at coil 0 reproduces with the Stellaris pack side 0.36 m (A = 0.1296 m²). Table 8's rounded 0.130 m² gives 13.42 T.
