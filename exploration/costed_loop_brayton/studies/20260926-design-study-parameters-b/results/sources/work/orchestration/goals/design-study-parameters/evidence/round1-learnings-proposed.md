# Round 1 learnings, proposed (accepted delta goes to `learnings.md` at the round review)

- L-001 [AGENT] On the costed C-1 assembly the passing region of the flow × ratio grid is bounded below by heat removal at the exchanger (the recuperated heater inlet against the 773 K source through 50 MW/K) and above by the compressor rating, then by nonpositive net; the ridge of net lies one grid step on the failing side where the boundary lies inside the grid (2,250–3,000 kg/s), so the best passing point (2,250 / 1.5183 or 2,750 / 1.375, about 595 MW) is 22 MW below the electricity-only best (2,500 / 1.425, 7.8 MW unmet). Evidence: `…/20260926-design-study-parameters/record.md` § 6, `results/readout.md` § 2–3, findings #1.
- L-002 [AGENT] Within one priced inventory the LCOE ranking of operating points is the net-electricity ranking; the tritium purchase carries most of the paired delta (−408 of −446 USD/MWh at the best point) and the plant side the rest (−38). Between inventories only the booked purchases differ; the ARIES-selected inventory prices the same points 8–11 USD/MWh lower and supports none of them (helium-duty package 1,500 MW against a constant 3,301 MW duty). Findings #5; contract § 6 confirmed on the 200 grid points (the sensitivities S4–S6 vary numerator terms by design).
- L-003 [AGENT] The exchanger area is the priced hardware lever: 75,000 m² (+29 MUSD) moves the boundary at 2,500 kg/s from 1.45 to 1.40 and buys +74 MW of passing net for +0.7 USD/MWh at unchanged net. Finding #2; a modeling item would be needed to make the area a chosen design variable with its own screen.
- L-004 [AGENT] The fixed-efficiency treatment's first-order effect is one grid step of boundary (compressor 0.85 or turbine 0.90 fails 2,250 / 1.5183; 2,500 / 1.45 passes under every level), and the loop pump law's ×2 fails every boundary anchor through the friction heat it delivers while leaving the slack-rich starting point passing. Findings #3, #4; the operating claim is scoped near the design flow.
- L-005 [AGENT] The verifier's relative rule cannot pass a near-zero difference channel (a 0.363 K hot-bound margin at 1.5e-9 relative, 5e-10 K absolute); the manifest transcription from the contract carried neither the precedent package's `unmet` classes nor any class for the margins. Whether such channels get declared absolute classes before execution is the owner's ruling under G-001; if ruled so, the rule is that a manifest carries every class of the precedent package on the same bodies plus one for every near-zero difference channel. Finding #6.
- L-006 [AGENT] A sensitivity anchor rule of the form "best point plus its grid neighbours" can collapse onto the starting point on a refined grid; declaring an extra anchor before execution and retaining the first preparation keeps the record honest. Finding #7.
- L-007 [AGENT] The starting configuration's auxiliary register sums to 65 MW electric (40 + 10 + 5 + 5 + 5), not the 60 MW written in `evidence/starting-configuration.md` § 4; the stored `auxiliary_electric` 240.281 = 175.281 + 65.0 confirms it. Recorded in the T-005 return; the evidence file is left as written.
