## Q4 Fixed-efficiency treatment

Read: § 8(a); the compressor and expander bodies (efficiency is a scalar input, outlet temperature and work follow from it, no map); screen § 5. The heater inlet depends on both efficiencies (k through the turbine's, the cold temperature through compressor 3's), so S1 does move the heat-removal boundary and the ridge. The best passing point, 2,500 kg/s / 1.45, sits at the design flow and 4.5 % below the design ratio, where a fixed efficiency is conventional. Finding (`note`): honest as a declared model assumption; no narrowing needed before execution. Two wording fixes: the flow window is −20 % / +60 % about 2,500 (2,000–4,000), not ±60 %; and S1 is a uniform level offset, so it cannot test a penalty that grows with distance from the design point. The answer should state that rankings between flows far from 2,500 kg/s (4,000 / 1.20 at 476 MW against 2,000 / 1.70 at 442 MW) are model exploration at fixed efficiency, not an operating claim.
