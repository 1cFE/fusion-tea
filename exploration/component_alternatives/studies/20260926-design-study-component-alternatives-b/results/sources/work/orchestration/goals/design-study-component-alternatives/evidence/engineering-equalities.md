# What the model must balance

This is a plain-language reading of the independently reviewed fourth design, `work/active/WI-096_matched-conversion-subsystems/design.md` §§2–6. It introduces no new model decision.

## Why these variable roles are appropriate

Source heat and compressor ratios remain designer choices. Purchased equipment remains a designer choice. None is silently changed to make a case pass.

The primary bypass fraction is an operating control action on purchased hardware. Water flow is the operating flow needed to achieve a specified gas outlet with purchased cooler UA and pumps. Recuperator effectiveness follows the chosen UA and actual gas flow. These calculated quantities have explicit engineering reasons and were covered by the fourth-submission independent review.

The additions include physical cooler behavior, pump accounting and a recuperator relationship, as well as closure and accounting. The reviewed scope counts five substantive new or modified bodies. One is a newly written iterative cooler calculation, used for three coolers; the existing network and bypass algorithms are reused. This is the bounded scope that passed review. Another physical solve or a major new model would require the mandated stop.
