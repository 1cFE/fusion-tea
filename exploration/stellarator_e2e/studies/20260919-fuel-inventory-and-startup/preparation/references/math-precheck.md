- **Streams and stock:** `F=B/f`, `U=F−B`, `L=(1−r)U` imply `F−rU=B+L`. The proposed serial stocks `Fτfeed`, `Fτplasma`, `Uτexhaust`, `rUτprocess`, and `Jτbreeder` match the stated loss boundaries. Processing receives recovered exhaust; breeder extraction acts only on gross bred supply. Reserve and usable buffer are separate. Permanent loss is not retained inventory. A different physical loss location would require different stage stocks.
- **Startup:** The stated cumulative demand is continuous and piecewise linear. Its maximum on the declared horizon is attained at an endpoint or return-time breakpoint. Evaluating `0, τR, τB, H` is sufficient when `H≥max(τR,τB)`. Add feed prefill, usable buffer and reserve once; do not add operating stage inventory again. Gross blanket fill is internally produced. Prefilled feed supports immediate injection while storage replenishes it; adding feed residence to the return delay would duplicate its startup treatment.
- **Hand check:** In arbitrary consistent mass/time units, take `B=1`, `f=0.5`, `r=1`, `η=1`, `J=1`, `τR=2`, `τB=3`, `H=4`. Demand at the four candidate times is `0,4,5,5`; startup draw is 5, and eventual burn/recycle/breeding balance is zero. Setting `J=0` instead gives endpoint demand 6 and continuing shortage 1. Zero delays give zero startup draw when usable breeding covers burn plus loss.
- **Decay allowance:** Let total held mass be `M(t)`. With purchased initial supply `M0+A`, gross production bounded by `JH`, and all other physical terms removing mass, `M(t)≤M0+A+JH`. Thus cumulative decay is at most `λH(M0+A+JH)=A` for the proposed `A=k(M0+JH)/(1−k)`. This includes allowance self-decay. It is conservative and dimensionally correct; reject `k≥1` and nonfinite results. Replacement access within the declared abstract boundary is necessary. This is not a transport-constrained startup guarantee.
- **Operating balance:** Preserve `(B+L+λI+G)/(ηB)` (`mfe_fuel_cycle.sysml:88`). The proposed continuing-shortage formula assumes `G=0`; enforce that scope or include `G`. Nominal flow-times-residence stocks with separate decay replacement are coherent short-residence conceptual sizing. They are not exact decaying plug-flow stocks. Calendar decay applies throughout maintained storage; productive availability multiplies operating rates only.
