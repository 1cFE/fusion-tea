## Findings and conditions

- **Source transfer is honest.** Original tables support delivery 20/30 minutes, combined cleanup/separation 1.3/4/5 hours, breeding-zone 0.1–1 day, separate extraction near-online or 1–5 days, and reserve 24 hours at disrupted fraction 25%. These are source scenarios, not demonstrated helium/PbLi plant properties. Eq. 3.1 is a compartment ODE; deterministic delay remains an explicitly different approximation. The source reserve uses its own injection rate including fueling efficiency. Using this model's `F=B/f` is an adaptation of the interruption policy, not adoption of that efficiency. The design and research preserve that distinction.
- **Changed topology conserves the stated streams.** Combined processor stock is `Uτ_process`, with `rU` returned and `(1−r)U` removed at the outlet. Both breeder stocks use gross `J`, with extraction efficiency applied once after extraction. This avoids unsupported residence splitting and duplicate recovery penalties.
- **Plasma prefill is derived correctly.** Substituting `u=1−ρ²` gives `∫n_T dV=n_T0 V/(1+α_n)` for `α_n>−1`. Feed and plasma are prefilled once. Immediate steady plasma exhaust is coherent with constant particle stock and `F−B−U=0`; no additional plasma startup lag is needed in this approximation.
- **Startup and decay remain sound.** The four-event deficit maximum, separate prefill/reserve, and gross-breeding decay bound from the preliminary review still apply with `τR=τ_process`, `τB=τ_blanket+τ_extract`. Active rejection of nonzero growth resolves the prior condition. Ongoing deficit and calendar decay remain separate outputs.
- **Interface and verification plan are sufficient for implementation.** Preserve dormant legacy inventory, gate production-dependent interpretation on breeding applicability, and verify independently recomputed burn equals the existing fuel account. Finite inputs/intermediates, rate/fraction domains, nonnegative times/stocks, `k<1`, exact activation/applicability flags, and unsupported producer states need executable checks. The proposed independent oracle, event trajectories, profile integration, availability invariance and legacy regression cases address the material risks.

Unsupported retention, bypass and permeation remain unresolved omissions; the model may report conditional represented inventory without presenting it as qualified total plant inventory. No further owner decision is required for this bounded implementation. No full suites were run.
