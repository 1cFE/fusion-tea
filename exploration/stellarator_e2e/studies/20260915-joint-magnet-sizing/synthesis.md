# Executor reading: joint magnet sizing

**Author:** implementation/report agent for the study executor. **Date:** 2026-09-15 local. **Status:** executor interpretation prepared from completed results for the final record freeze; not an independent administrator review. Evidence is confined to this study directory. The final snapshot and its digest are maintained by the coordinator in [record.md](record.md).

The study asks whether one conductor inventory can carry the required current, fit an explicit casing allocation and pass the existing plant limits. It holds 50 kA turn current, 6 mm×56 µm tape at 20 K, operating allowance 0.8 and selected field ceiling 24.9 T. Default current sizing buys 1% extra physical inventory. [Protocol](protocol.md).

**No sampled default design meets all twenty predicates.** All 324 default cases meet current capacity, 154 fit, and none satisfies the other eighteen together. The full 347-case cohort contains one historical 30 T/orientation3 pass, which is outside the default comparison. No default cheapest-feasible choice exists. The default feasible fraction 0% is outside the 5–95% diagnostic band: H1 did not establish a bounded feasible region. Endpoint checks from a rejected anchor do not establish its boundary. [Analysis](results/analysis.json), [edge checks](results/edge-scan.json).

The reference needs 239.984 installed reference-effective tapes versus 112.709 entering, and 0.275949 m² pack area versus 0.129600 m². At the held reference field it requires 0.585309 m radial exterior and 0.548441 m transverse cavity. Enlarging allocation to 0.60/0.60 m passes fit, but the resulting field 25.2973 T exceeds the 24.9 T ceiling; divertor load also remains excessive. Current sizing repairs the inventory mismatch and reveals residual constraints. It does not rescue the reference. [Report](report.md).

Two failures show the conflict clearly. The R12.7/a1.35/16.2 MA-turn anchor passes nineteen predicates but reaches 26.8255 T. The R13.1/a1.45/15.4 MA-turn anchor passes field/current/fit but exceeds divertor load by 1.15687 MW/m² and loop flow by 20.1952 kg/s. Raising allowed limits is not a disposition supported by this study. Material 1.10/1.35, hypothetical orientation 2 and reduced-retention sensitivities change inventory and cost, but none produces an all-twenty pass at its four anchors. [Per-point evidence](results/analysis.json).

**Framing:** major/minor radius, ampere-turns and radial/transverse allocation remain bounded search choices, with no feasible-region or optimum claim. Mode/reserve and performance are sensitivities. Historical envelopes remain controls. Shape variations are local fit diagnostics, excluded from economic comparison. Radial allocation changes field, inventory and costs; transverse allocation changes local fit without full casing consequences, revealing a limitation rather than free space.

**H2 and H3:** native sizing now carries current demand through physical inventory into geometry, procurement and downstream consequences. Allocation precedes field and sizing, so this declared problem is acyclic. All 75,646 mapped scalar and 6,940 predicate comparisons pass; maximum count/area/operating-fraction closure residuals are below 1.4e−15. This supports internal consistency. The separate multiplier 1 exact-boundary sign disagreement remains explicit. [Verification](results/oracle-all-points.json), [residuals](results/sizing-residuals.json), [boundary diagnostic](results/exact-boundary-diagnostic.json).

**Findings carried forward:** retain the native current-sizing dependency; carry the bounded default failure and quantified field/divertor/flow deficits; preserve missing geometry/angle/construction and manufacturing evidence as limits. Required capability thresholds are requirements, not achieved gains. The original cavity would require 4.79079 times default capability at held field. Actual enlargement must reevaluate field. ## What the record does not support

There is no global infeasibility proof, continuous feasible boundary, global economic optimum, qualified tape/cable product, three-dimensional fit certification, detailed casing/support design or complete manufactured-magnet quotation. Performance scenarios omit premiums, while the length-based winding proxy does not price increased cross-section effort. Mixed price bases and unpriced manufacturing prevent interpreting the priced subtotal as a complete factory quote. Entering attribution uses the frozen older oracle, not an older-native rerun. The final independent assessment and record freeze are coordinator responsibilities; this executor reading does not certify either.
