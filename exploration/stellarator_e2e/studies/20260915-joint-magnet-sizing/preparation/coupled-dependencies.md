# Coupled magnet dependency assessment

No production model, study, source registry or quarantine state was changed. The quarantine protocol was read; barred source content was not opened. ## Finding and minimum formulation

[AGENT] The current package can represent the inventory required by a chosen current-loading scenario through its density input, but it cannot yet derive that density from tape performance. A small native optional sizing calculation can close this dependency without an iterative solve. Keep radial allocation and transverse cavity independent proposal inputs. Radial allocation determines coil-center position and actual peak field before current sizing. Current sizing then determines the pack and its fit. It must not enlarge its own allocation until it passes.

[AGENT DERIVATION] Define full tape area `A_t = width*thickness*1e6` in mm², tape fraction `f_t = 1-f_Cu-f_solder-f_steel-f_He`, available per-tape critical current `Ic_available = I_ref*(width/0.004)*(B_peak/20)^(-0.6)*material*orientation*cabling*degradation*sharing`, and declared target operating fraction `u_target` with `0 < u_target <= u_allow`. The required effective density is `j_eff_target = u_target*f_t*Ic_available/A_t` in A/mm². It corresponds to `N_ref_required = 50000/(u_target*Ic_available)` continuous parallel tapes at fixed 50 kA turn current. Keeping the existing grade stage requires `j_reference_derived = q*j_eff_target`, where `q=(B_max/B_grade_ref)^field_exponent`, so its division by q returns the target effective density. Applying grade enlargement a second time to an already field-sized density purchases additional inventory and no longer means minimum current-required sizing.

[AGENT] Put this dependency inside the native model rather than deriving the density per proposal in a study script. An explicit mode can select inherited declared density or derived reference density; the inherited mode preserves the adverse nominal case. The new mode should expose the target, required count and derived densities. The downstream current calculation must still reconstruct capacity from actual procurement inventory. Share the same declared performance law and domain between sizing and checking, while retaining an independently written oracle. Do not feed the downstream inventory-dependent current calculation back into sizing; extract or duplicate only its tape-performance kernel with consistency checks.

[AGENT] Exact current-boundary sizing makes the current predicate an algebraic consequence of the sizing assumption, not independent evidence that the magnet is qualified. Floating-point reconstruction can put an exact target on either side of zero. Use an explicitly declared target below the allowance for production studies if reserve is intended; choose and disclose its value rather than hiding an epsilon or clipping a negative margin. Preserve exact-boundary tests separately. The selected-field predicate remains separate and continues to require `B_peak <= B_max`; the cancellation of q in current-sized mode means B_max becomes a declared envelope check rather than an additional inventory lever in that mode. If extra inventory for a higher selected envelope is desired, that is a different, explicitly named sizing rule.

## Inventory identity and dependency map

[INHERITED: models/library/analyses/mfe_winding_pack_cost.sysml; work/orchestration/goals/absolute-conductor-current-margin/evidence/interface-assessment.md] Coil current is ampere-turn demand, not conductor operating current. With coil count n, reference ampere-turns I, distribution factors f_set and f_vol, circumference c and nominal section area A_wp, conductor length is `L_c=n*I*f_set*c/I_turn`; tape length is `L_t=n*A_wp*f_vol*c*f_t/(width*thickness)`. Thus `N_set=L_t/L_c` and `N_ref=N_set*f_set/f_vol=I_turn*f_t/(j_eff*A_t)`. Turns add series length, not parallel capacity. Fixed 50 kA gives reference turns `I/50000`; the model represents continuous turns/tape count, not a manufacture-ready integer stack. f_set and f_vol differ and must not be silently equated.

## Defensible domains and useful analytical bounds

[INHERITED: models/library/analyses/mfe_conductor_current.sysml] Retain exact 20 K, full composite thickness 56 µm, width 4–6 mm, finite positive parameters, retention and allowable fractions in (0,1], and peak field 20–32 T. Above 24 T requires the explicit extrapolation switch. The 32 T cutoff is an engineering guard, not measurement authority. The narrower 20–30 T interval used previously is suitable for continuity of exploratory evidence. An evaluated 30–32 T case must remain visibly extrapolative. Require live and reference `R-r > 0` and positive cavity dimensions.

[AGENT DERIVATION] Geometry permits an exact feasibility bound without inventing a physical aspect-ratio range. Let `s=sqrt(I/(1e6*j_eff))`, `g=2*(ground+assembly)`, `X=coil_t-2*wall-g`, `Y=interior_y-g`, with internal build fractions fx and fy. Positive X and Y are necessary. For aspect ratio a defined by `nominal_x=s*sqrt(a)` and `nominal_y=s/sqrt(a)`, local fit requires `[s*(1+fy)/Y]^2 <= a <= [X/(s*(1+fx))]^2`. The interval exists iff `s²*(1+fx)*(1+fy) <= X*Y`. This is a local rectangular screening bound, not a source-backed manufacturing aspect-ratio bound. Use it to explain failed cases; report any explored aspect-ratio/cavity limits as study assumptions, because admitted evidence supplies no qualified numerical design window.

[AGENT] Square sections are the source anchor. A defensible first comparison holds aspect ratio at one and varies independently declared radial/transverse allocations. An aspect-ratio sensitivity can be separate and conditional, because the preceding missing couplings become more significant as section shape departs from the anchor. Do not call a numerical search upper bound a physical engineering limit. Useful radial bounds also follow from the modeled peak-field cutoff and `coil_t < 2*(R-vessel_outer)`, but other subsystem predicates can bind earlier.

[AGENT DERIVATION, fixed-field illustration only] At 24.9 T with default performance, 6 mm width, 56 µm thickness, 9% tape and target 0.8, available tape current is approximately 263.04 A; required reference tape count is 237.61; effective density is 56.37 A/mm². At I=15.4 MA this implies square-equivalent side 0.52270 m and approximately 2.108 times the entering pack volume at fixed circumference. With inherited wall 25 mm, ground 3 mm/face, assembly 2 mm/face, fx=0 and fy=0.025, square-fit demand is radial allocation at least 0.58270 m and transverse cavity at least 0.54577 m. These are explanatory fixed-field lower-demand calculations, not a coupled feasible design: raising allocation raises peak field, circumference and inventory again. Numbers were evaluated directly with awk from the displayed equations; no package or study was executed.

## Default versus enhanced performance

[INHERITED: work/orchestration/goals/absolute-conductor-current-margin/evidence/performance-research.md] Default 200 A per 4 mm at 20 T is a rounded manufacturing inference, transferred to the selected construction; it is not a measured product guarantee. Default material/orientation/retention factors are one. The source production band of 700–1000 A/mm² at 20 T corresponds to 235.2–336 A for the 6 mm × 56 µm scenario; the reported full 500–1400 A/mm² range corresponds to 168–470.4 A. Those are manufacturing observations, not qualified minima or confidence bounds. The 330–405 A transferred representative-sample range has unresolved construction identity.

[AGENT] Keep default performance as its own result. Stronger material or assumed alignment can reduce required inventory linearly and reduce side as the square root of that ratio, but price, actual construction and qualification do not automatically follow. In particular, the prior orientation-factor-three passes are an assumed enhanced case; a factor of three is not an admitted worst-location guarantee. Combining manufacturing scatter and material multipliers as independent benefits can count the same improvement twice. The original Stellaris alignment design and this uniform perpendicular-peak scalar scenario have different premises; a scalar failure does not refute the original design.

## Remaining questions and evidence limits

[AGENT] The design must choose whether current-sized mode means exact current-required inventory at actual peak field or deliberately larger inventory sized to the selected envelope. It must also state any operating reserve below the allowable fraction. These are modeling choices, not unresolved source facts that justify silently fitting a normalization. Exact 50 kA cable construction, integer turns/stacks, local weakest-coil loading, angular/strain performance, allowed cavity/section envelope, casing stiffness, thermal geometry and manufacturing cost remain unresolved engineering evidence.

[INHERITED: work/orchestration/goals/magnet-manufacturing-cost-completeness/account-ledger.md] Priced quantities remain an incomplete manufacturing account: tape and constituent procurement, conditional sheet stock, length-based winding operations and all-in energy-derived supports. Larger section has no calibrated winding-effort multiplier; ground stock, impregnation, joints, handling, qualification, waste and yield remain unresolved. A priced subtotal from joint sizing is not a manufacturing-complete quotation, and a conjunction of current/fit/existing predicates remains conditional model feasibility.
