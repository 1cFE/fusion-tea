The 360 gram-moles/day convention describes molecular isotope gas. With the source's approximate 5 g/mol DT, it gives about 1.8 kg/day and agrees with the rounded ORNL mass reference. It is not 360 moles of tritium atoms/day, and F is not total carrier/impurity mass. Preserve ORNL's own mass reference when reproducing that correlation.

## Findings that block the proposed transfer

1. **The actual capacity lies beyond the original local cost guidance.** [AGENT] Current exhaust demand, 12.9117940450 kg D+T/day, is 7.184714457 times the ORNL reference. The 0.3 law would multiply either process-row cost by 1.806853136 before price conversion. These are arithmetic checks, not an applicable cost result. ORNL's deliberate systems-code extrapolation is relevant engineering evidence, but a transfer rationale must explain why its process and scale assumptions remain appropriate here. Bartlit's limited factor statements cannot supply that rationale alone. Do not invent a train count or treat those statements as certified equipment maximums.

2. **The source feed boundary differs from the available processor interface.** [AGENT] The verified producer represents plasma exhaust isotope load before recovery. It establishes neither fueler reject flow nor carrier/impurity removal. ORNL sums additional feeds. Blanket T production is 0.488226529 kg/day, but that is not extraction-system gas capacity. Adding it mechanically would give 13.400020574 kg/day and a 7.456386092 reference ratio while changing isotope composition. It would not establish that PbLi extraction products can enter cleanup or distillation directly. Keep extraction, conditioning and recovered-isotope routing explicit; do not assign an invented equimolar D companion to blanket T.

3. **The process premise is not yet established.** [AGENT] The plant currently has functional stocks, not a selected cleanup technology or cryogenic separation train. The source supplies a plausible candidate, including product-purity and conditioning duties, but the current plant has no corresponding feed specification, pressure, purity or redundancy requirement. Equimolar exhaust isotope arithmetic is compatible with the primary ISS isotope ratio; it does not prove compatibility with the complete source process. The four columns form one interlinked separation process, not four independent redundant trains. No full-train redundancy is established by the inspected sources.

4. **The cost rows are not a complete installed plant.** [AGENT] Bartlit Table II excludes cleanup glovebox and guaranteed purification, and excludes guaranteed isotope separation and freely supplied LN2 storage dewars. Section III separates process design/staff work and installation design/inspection from subsystem totals, and notes omitted intersystem tubing/control work. The cleanup and distiller rows cannot replace the full processing-plus-containment allowance while claiming its other functions remain priced. Source-installed amounts also need reconciliation with downstream project charges. Source operating and replacement costs are not established by these capital rows.

5. **Price conversion remains an implementation prerequisite.** [AGENT] Preserve each raw expenditure year and distinguish it from the routine's 1986 output basis. A declared, sourced conversion to the model's price year is required. Neither a generic 1988 label nor one arbitrary multiplier applied to the mixed-year table reproduces the original algorithm.
