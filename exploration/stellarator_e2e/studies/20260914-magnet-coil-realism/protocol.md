## What changed in the model, and what this study is not

WI-058 (`work/active/WI-058_coil-winding-length-from-bore/`, audit PASS at `a6286a55`): `c_coil = c_coil_ref × (a_coil / a_coil_ref)` on the coil-centre bore (`rb.r_coil_centre = a + 1.85 m`), `c_coil_ref = 25.0` m at `a_coil_ref` 3.15 m, `k_coil` retired; the major radius no longer enters the winding length. Consumers of the length: the winding-pack cold volume, the ampere-metres, the winding procurement (tape, winding fabrication, material inventory) and through the cold volume the cryoplant load. Not touched: the peak field, stress, strain, stored energy, casing mass, tape price, reference density, selected envelope, coil count. At the entering pin the grounding probe read the winding procurement and the cryoplant load bit-constant in `a` on the design column and exactly proportional to `R`.

This is a sensitivity study of an engineered window. It claims no boundary, no optimum, no qualified design range. The committed 3,751-point plant-closure window is not re-run. **The design column (R 12.7, 15.4 MA, default density) is expected to carry no 18-feasible point:** `divertor_heat_ok` is violated at `a` 1.3 and the entering-pin probe reads the conductor ceiling exceeded from `a` 1.4 (25.16 T against 24.9). Its `a`-minimum of LCOE is therefore reported as a price reading over points that are not 18-feasible, with the verdict set at that point, never as a "cheapest machine". Whether the `c0113` / `c0130` coordinates are 18-feasible at the current pin is a run result; if a column's points are infeasible the 18-feasible subset of that transect and its minimum are reported, labelled, rather than a "moved" machine.

## Expected responses (predeclared; the run decides)

From the WI-058 off-design predictions (`work/active/WI-058_coil-winding-length-from-bore/prototype/proto_results.json`, executed and matched by the oracle within 5.5e-16) and the entering-pin probe:

- Along `a` at fixed `R`, `I_coil`, `B_max`: `c_coil`, the winding-pack volume, tape, winding fabrication, material inventory and winding procurement scale by `(a + 1.85) / 3.15`; the cryoplant electrical rises with the cold volume; casing structure follows the stored energy as before (WI-044); peak field, stress, strain, stored energy, casing mass and `p_th` are unmoved by the length. At `a` 2.2 on the design column: ratio 1.2857, procurement about $2,019.05M, magnet capital about $2,099.6M, LCOE 129.35 against the entering pin's 122.51.
- Along `R` at fixed `a`: the winding chain is invariant to the double; every other channel moves as before.
- Verdict flips relative to the entering-pin references can come only through channels the length reaches: `recirc_ok` (through the cryoplant electrical) and `net_positive` (same path). **The goal's (b) expectation that the conductor ceiling, stress or strain may catch flips is narrowed for round 1:** `peak_field_ok`, `wp_stress_ok` and `cond_strain_ok` do not read the length (WI-058 design D3), so under this increment only `recirc_ok` and `net_positive` can move. The run reports every flip by case id with the operand that moved.
- The price minimum along `a` on the design column sat at `a` 2.0 (119.75, oracle-side, over points that are not 18-feasible) at the entering pin; the added magnet and cryoplant cost is monotone increasing in `a` on a column, so the argmin can only move to a smaller `a` or stay — the run decides, on each column, with the verdict set at the minimum stated.
