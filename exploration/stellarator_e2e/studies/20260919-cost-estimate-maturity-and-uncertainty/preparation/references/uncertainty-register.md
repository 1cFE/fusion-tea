# Uncertainty register

[AGENT] Author register, 2026-09-19. This records the evidence and proposed numerical treatment for the integrated fourteen-circuit reference identified in [account review](evidence/account-review.md). Exact amounts, design inputs, monetary bases, account producers and the four existing failed engineering predicates remain there and in its [functional account map](evidence/accounts/functional-accounts.csv). This register does not substitute historical eighteen-circuit or r2 design totals.

The [method proposal](evidence/method-research.md) defines a finite conditional source-interpretation/model-analogy envelope. The [independent source/interface review](evidence/method-review.md) approves the shared stainless-price cases with stated limits; overall method review and final R12.S grading remain separate. No probability distribution, likelihood, confidence level or complete plant accuracy interval is established. “Unbounded” below means the inspected evidence supplies no defensible finite range for that uncertainty, not that the physical quantity can grow without limit. “Unpriced” means a supported amount is absent; it is not a zero-cost assumption.

Roles in the final column identify the engineering discipline needed for the next evidence. They do not assign work to a named person or authorize scope or financial-policy changes.

## Use and review boundary

[AGENT] U01–U04 quantify only selected evidence-conditioned variations. U05–U15 and U26 contain significant unbounded prices, scaling, quantities and operating expenses; U16–U20 identify scenario choices and model/financial limitations; U21–U25 preserve missing or uncertain coverage. Contingency does not convert the latter rows into priced scope. A finite reported envelope must name its included IDs and show the major exclusions alongside it.

[AGENT] Verify source endpoint reproduction, unit/year conversion exactly once, unchanged310 baseline, common-driver account response, replacement expense, disjoint sums and unchanged electricity for the fixed-design ensemble. Report the evaluated population, every execution error and every violated engineering predicate. Assigning a maturity class or passing software checks cannot establish a feasible plant or a plant-wide confidence interval. Final R12.S review must assess functional detail, maturity and this limited uncertainty treatment together.
