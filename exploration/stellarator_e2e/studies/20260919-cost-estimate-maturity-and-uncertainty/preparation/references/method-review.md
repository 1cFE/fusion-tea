## Source and interface verdict: PASS

The proposed 240/310/360 USD2017/kg cases are defensible conditional source-family scenarios. ANL printed26 states a rounded 120,000 USD2017/ton carbon-steel forging basis; printed27 reports a stainless/carbon relative factor of two to three and recommends 310,000 USD/ton delivered stainless components after historical checks. I inspected both original page images. Multiplying the fixed carbon basis by two and three produces 240,000 and 360,000 USD/ton; the 310,000 recommendation lies within that interval. The source uses 626 MT for the carbon vessel head and approximately 74.7 million USD2017, consistent with the adopted metric-ton interpretation and roughly 120 USD/kg. Its 307,072 and 313,065 USD/ton historical checks are corroborating point estimates, not bounds.

The source supports a relative judgment about finished nuclear stainless construction, conditional on its carbon reference. It does not quantify uncertainty in the reference itself, modern target fabrication, high-pressure helium or salt equipment, geometry, advanced alloys, or escalation. These remain unbounded by the cases. Calling the result a conditional source-interpretation/model-analogy envelope is appropriate only while those exclusions remain prominent. No distribution, likelihood, confidence interval or full-plant accuracy claim follows.

One shared raw fabricated-stainless input owned by heat transport is the appropriate interface. The inspected implementation contains exactly four relevant hardcoded310 bills: exchanger supply, primary pipes, secondary pipes and future tube-bundle replacement. Existing percentage installation/removal terms must move with their current bases. Existing general costscale also multiplies machine and inventory costs, so it cannot isolate this source driver. The proposed distinct calc formal preserves the common source dependence without changing quantities or adding equipment producers. Keep existing delivered-cost exclusions tied to the changed initial fabrication bills; future replacement does not enter the initial delivered exclusion.

The upstream WI-067 design explicitly transfers ANL finished-component fabrication to piping and transfers NETL field installation separately. Its final scoped cooling review retains pressure qualification, pipe routing, tubesheet geometry, service life and missing auxiliaries as limitations. A source-rate range cannot remove those qualifications.
