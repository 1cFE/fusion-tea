# T-001 geometry reconciliation

[AGENT research and recommendations; no owner decision implied.] The inherited 300 mm quantity is a generic full radial-build coil layer. It is neither a published Stellaris cavity nor a published Stellaris pack width. The current local fit failure is arithmetically correct for the declared scenario, but transferring that failure to published Stellaris is unsupported. The paper provides a 250 mm average magnet-boundary-to-center span and 300–360 mm nominal winding-pack sections. It does not provide the minimum local casing cavity, exterior wall profile, ground insulation and assembly tolerances needed to certify fit. No numerical cavity replacement is justified by this evidence.

## What the raw fit failure says

[AGENT arithmetic attribution] With nominal side 0.360 m and aspect ratio 1, x internal addition zero, ground 0.003 m/face and assembly 0.002 m/face, required x is 0.370 m. The 0.300 m exterior allocation minus 0.050 m of walls gives 0.250 m cavity, hence margin −0.120 m. In y, 2.5% internal addition gives 0.369 m pack, 0.379 m required width and +0.021 m margin against 0.400 m cavity. This screen fails its own declared x geometry even before any material-current expansion. The bare nominal pack is already 60 mm larger than the generic exterior allocation.

[AGENT inference, conditional illustration only] Doubling Figure 34's 0.25 m magnet half-span gives 0.50 m only if the casing is locally symmetric about the center and the averaged span represents the station being tested. Keeping the existing assumed 25 mm walls would then give 0.45 m cavity and +0.08 m x margin at the nominal pack. That arithmetic does not prove those assumptions, a minimum local cavity, manufacturing fit or global clearance. It must not be installed as a recovered published-reference pass. Increasing the existing `coil_t` would additionally move the centerline proxy and change peak field, winding procurement, stored energy, support mass and shield geometry. Those effects are separate from obtaining a larger cavity.

## Source integration and bounded correction recommendation

[AGENT conclusion] The paper goes beyond disconnected subsystem sketches: p.18 says the 3D neutronics geometry separately includes casing and pack; p.24 relates local casing walls to the support model; p.27 models a coupled pack/support assembly. But it does not publish the dimensioned local cavity/tolerance contract needed by this model's fit predicate. Thus “the source has only subsystem geometry” is too strong. “The published information does not determine the required minimum cavity and manufactured envelope” is supported.

[AGENT recommendation] Correct reference attribution and applicability before changing numerical geometry. Preserve the original 300 mm/25 mm wall/3 mm ground/2 mm clearance scenario and raw −120 mm result. Classify published-reference local fit as unresolved rather than failed or passed. Independently expose local exterior/cavity geometry if future source-conditioned reconstruction is implemented; sharing a variable with a circular global build requires an explicit verified correspondence. Do not change a dimension just to pass the screen.

[AGENT recommendation] A source-average radial-build scenario can legitimately use Figure 34's individual averages with the endpoint convention made explicit. It would be a separate approximate source reconstruction, not a local manufacturing check. Reanchor normalized field/energy/length laws consistently at that geometry if the scientific purpose is source reconstruction, and preserve old-anchor controls to attribute numerical changes. Supplied source center/field/length values earn no independent prediction credit. The detailed design and review must decide whether this bounded additional scenario is necessary; this research alone does not authorize a numerical production correction.

[AGENT unresolved requirements] Local geometry needs a station-indexed, dimensioned cavity and exterior, pack orientation and offset, corner/fillet limits, nominal versus maximum manufactured envelope, sheet inclusion/count convention, ground insulation, assembly/tolerance stack and temperature/load state. A global fit claim additionally needs coil-to-coil clearance and insertion/assembly geometry. These quantities require source CAD/drawings or author/manufacturer clarification; the published figure cannot supply them by inversion. No production model, oracle, generated package or frozen comparison artifact was changed.
