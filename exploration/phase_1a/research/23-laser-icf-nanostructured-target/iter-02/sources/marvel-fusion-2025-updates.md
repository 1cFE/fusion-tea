## EU EIC Accelerator Project: CFE-NANO

- **Full name**: Commercial Fusion Energy with Short-Pulse High-Intensity Lasers and Nanostructured Fuel Targets
- **Project ID**: 101189082
- **Duration**: October 1, 2024 – March 31, 2027
- **EU contribution**: €2,489,221.49
- **Key detail**: "Over 2,000 experimental validations across three years"
- **Pilot powerplant target**: 100 MW capacity by 2033
- **Partners**: Siemens Energy (pilot powerplant), CSU, Stanford University, LMU Munich, Thales (industrialization)
- **Colorado demonstration facility**: Completion by 2027 with proprietary laser systems
- **100% climate action alignment**

## LION 2 Experimental Chamber (July 2025)

- Inaugurated July 17, 2025 at CALA (Centre for Advanced Laser Applications), LMU Garching
- Enables "previously unfeasible experiments on laser-matter interaction and high-field ion acceleration"
- Allows Marvel Fusion to "validate critical parameters of its laser-based fusion technology"
- Bavaria's "Master Plan for Nuclear Fusion": ~€100M for fusion research ecosystem
- Bavaria funded €2.5M for CALA laser center expansion
- Research partnership between LMU and Marvel Fusion since 2022

## EIC STEP Program (Nov 2025)

- Marvel Fusion advanced in EIC STEP program, potential €30M investment
- FusioTile laser optics project also announced

## Updated Timeline

- LION 2 at CALA: Operational (July 2025)
- ATLAS Facility at CSU: Opening mid-2026, experiments early 2027
- Pilot powerplant: 100 MW by 2033 (updated from earlier ~2032 prototype)
- Commercial fusion power plant: ~2036
