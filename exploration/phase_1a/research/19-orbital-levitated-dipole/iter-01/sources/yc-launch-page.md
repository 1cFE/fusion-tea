## Key Technical Details

- **Confinement**: Dipole magnetic confinement — "inside-out" configuration using a single coil generating magnetized plasma extending into external vacuum
- **Magnet Technology**: HTS magnets, ~10x more field per kg improvement in last decade
- **Coil size**: Meter-scale dipole coil, fits within SpaceX Falcon 9 launch constraints
- **Magnetized volume**: Would "exceed that of ITER" (6-meter plasma radius terrestrial reactor)
- **Confinement scaling**: τₑ ~ R² (confinement time proportional to squared device radius)
- **Power target**: Megawatt-class power generation in orbit
- **Cost comparison**: ISS solar arrays ~$1B/MW installed; ITER ~$650M/MW
- **Physics basis**: LDX Nature Physics 2010 experimental results
- **Fuel type**: Not specified on this page
- **Heating**: Not specified on this page
- **Energy capture**: Not specified on this page — "power delivery via beaming partners" mentioned in concept description elsewhere
