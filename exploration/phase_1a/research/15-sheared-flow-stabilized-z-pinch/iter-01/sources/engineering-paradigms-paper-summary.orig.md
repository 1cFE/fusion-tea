# Engineering Paradigms for SFS Z-Pinch Fusion Energy (2023)

Source: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/15361055.2023.2209131
Authors: Zap Energy team (Fusion Science and Technology journal)

## Key Technical Details

- **Reactor thermal power**: 190 MWt per reactor
- **Repetition rate**: 10 Hz (pulsed)
- **Blanket**: Liquid LiPb eutectic (17% lithium, 83% lead)
  - Serves as: outer electrode, primary heat transfer medium, biological shield, tritium breeding medium
  - TBR ~1.1 with 3-m blanket
- **First wall**: LiPb cascades over a weir wall under gravity to form the first wall and plasma cavity
- **No external magnets**: SFS Z-pinch requires neither externally driven compression nor magnetic coils
- **MHD advantage**: Absence of external magnetic fields eliminates MHD forces on liquid metal coolant — major simplification
- **Energy conversion**: LiPb to steam cycle (thermal)
- **Drive efficiency**: ~70% wall-plug to plasma cathode efficiency
- **Neutron management**: Monte Carlo simulations show confined neutron irradiation zone near pinch region; LiPb blanket provides shielding
- **Power flexibility**: Repetitively pulsed design allows variable output by adjusting duty cycle and coolant flow rate
- **Vertical orientation**: Z-pinch oriented vertically in reactor concept
- **Reactor size**: ~3 meters tall
