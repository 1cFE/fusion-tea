# Zap Energy — How It Works (Website)

Source: https://www.zapenergy.com/how-it-works

## Key Technical Details

- **Confinement**: Sheared-flow stabilization confines plasma using the magnetic field generated by a powerful electrical current in the plasma instead of using external magnets
- **Mechanism**: Plasma organized into layers flowing at different speeds; faster outer layers smooth down instabilities, keeping slower inside layers steady
- **Heating**: The higher the current sent through the plasma, the tighter it will be constricted by magnetic fields and the hotter and denser it will become
- **Fuel**: Deuterium and tritium isotopes; generates high-energy helium and neutrons
- **Operation**: Pulsed mode — designed to produce ten plasma pulses per second (10 Hz)
- **Driver**: Advanced capacitor banks deliver electrical pulses; FuZE-Q designed to operate up to one megaamp of current
- **Energy conversion**: Liquid metal walls capture fusion energy
- **Tritium breeding**: Mix of lead and lithium in the walls produces tritium, which feeds back into the plant as fuel
- **No external magnets**: Self-generated magnetic field from plasma current
