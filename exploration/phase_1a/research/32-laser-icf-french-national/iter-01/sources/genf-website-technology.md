# GenF Systems - Technology Page
**Source**: https://genf-systems.com/technology/
**Accessed**: 2026-03-07

## Key Technical Details

- GenF uses Inertial Confinement Fusion (ICF) technology
- High-energy lasers described as "the most powerful lasers in the world, capable of delivering large amount of energy to a small target of only a few millimeters"
- Cryogenic deuterium-tritium targets "produced" and "injected 10 times per second into the fusion chamber"
- Neutrons from fusion reactions "converted into thermal and then electrical energy"
- Same neutrons "convert lithium into tritium to fuel the target production"
- Commercial reactor "designed to deliver 1GW of power" with service date "in the 2050's"
- Partners: Thales, CEA, CNRS LULI, CELIA
