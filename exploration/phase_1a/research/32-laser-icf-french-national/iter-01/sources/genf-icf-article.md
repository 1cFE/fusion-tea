# GenF - Inertial Confinement Fusion Article
**Source**: https://genf-systems.com/inertial-confinement-fusion/
**Accessed**: 2026-03-07

## Key Technical Details

- GenF selected **direct drive** as their approach ("more mature and efficient scheme for fusion")
- Target: spherical capsule ~2mm diameter, ~1mg DT fuel
- Ignition conditions: ~100 million K
- Energy capture: lithium-based compound blanket absorbs 14.3 MeV neutrons, converts to heat, then electricity "via traditional power plant methods"
- Tritium breeding: lithium blanket regenerates tritium
- Target energy release: up to 100+ MJ per implosion
- Fuel burn fraction: up to 30% of DT fuel
- Direct drive expected gain: 100 or more (vs indirect drive ~15)
- Repetition: "multiple times per second" for GW average power
- Controls hydrodynamic and laser-plasma instabilities as key challenge
