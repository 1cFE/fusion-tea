# Avalanche Energy — 300 kV Milestone Press Release
**Source**: https://www.avalanchefusion.com/news-release/avalanche-energy-completes-final-series-a-voltage-milestone-300-000-volts-in-compact-high-efficiency-prototype-fusion-machine
**Fetched**: 2026-03-08

## Key Technical Details

- **Voltage**: 300,000 volts sustained in steady-state
- **Gap distance**: 2.5 inches
- **Electric field gradient**: Over 4.7 MV/m (later PR says 5,000 kV/m = 5 MV/m)
- **Power consumption**: ~3 watts to maintain the voltage
- **Operation mode**: Steady-state — "significantly more challenging than pulsed high voltage, which only needs to hold for microseconds or milliseconds"
- **Modular design**: Capable of being stacked for scalability
- **Description**: "Magneto-electrostatic fusion architecture"
