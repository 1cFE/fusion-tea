# Talk-Polywell Forum — Orbitron Paper Discussion
**Source**: https://talk-polywell.org/bb/viewtopic.php?t=6587
**Fetched**: 2026-03-08

## Key Technical Analysis

- Forum user Giorgio describes the Orbitron as "an ion trap using crossed electric and magnetic fields"
- Key innovation: high-voltage feedthrough design enabling 300 kV (prior state of art: 30 kV continuous / 50 kV intermittent)
- **Brillouin limit**: Giorgio identifies overcoming this as "make or break" — they've simulated an "area of stability" for density exceeding conventional Brillouin constraints
- **Operation mode speculation**: Giorgio indicates it may be "a pulsed 5kWe reactor" with potential "50/60 Hz repetition rate" — but this is forum speculation, not from the company
- The company's own press releases consistently describe steady-state voltage operation
