# Avalanche Energy — FusionWERX $10M Grant
**Source**: https://www.avalanchefusion.com/news-release/avalanche-energy-awarded-10-million-grant-from-washington-state-to-develop-fusionwerx-neutron-factory
**Fetched**: 2026-03-08

## Key Technical Details

- **Facility**: FusionWERX, Richland, Washington (former Kurion facility)
- **Tritium capability**: Advanced tritium management capabilities, broad scope radioactive materials license
- **Fuel**: D-T for continuous high-flux neutron production
- **Device voltage**: 300 kV at 6 MV/m
- **Neutron target**: "continuous high-flux fusion neutrons at a rate and cost-per-neutron orders of magnitude better than previously achievable"
- **Applications**: Medical/power radioisotopes, radiation-tolerant alloys testing
- **Near-term product**: micro Fusion Prototypical Neutron Source (uFPNS)
- **Long-term**: Q>1 D-T fusion energy testing program
- **Workforce**: ~12 skilled positions initially
