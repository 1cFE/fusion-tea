# IAEA FUSE Portal — Pranos Profile

**Source**: https://nucleus.iaea.org/sites/connect/FUSEpublic/SitePages/PRANOS.aspx
**Accessed**: 2026-03-06

## Key Technical Details

- **Confinement type**: Compact spherical tokamak architectures
- **Fuel**: Deuterium-Tritium (D-T) — explicitly stated
- **Staged experimental program**: Three tokamak configurations named Ragya, Pragya, and PraniQ
- **Digital twin platform**: "Jenga" — integrates high-fidelity physics modules including:
  - Electromagnetics / MHD equilibrium and stability
  - Transport surrogates
  - Neutronics
  - Thermal-structural analysis
  - Plasma-material interaction (PMI)
  - Plant-level systems engineering
- **Engineering progress**: Completed engineering designs for TF (toroidal field) coils and structural support, including stress analysis and CAD modeling
- **Key innovation**: Co-optimization of plasma scenarios with plant systems using computational modeling to "shorten the iteration cycles between simulation, build, and test"

## What is NOT specified

- Magnet type (HTS, LTS, or resistive) for TF coils
- Heating method (NBI, RF, etc.)
- Energy conversion approach
- Plasma parameters (temperature, density, confinement time)
- Machine dimensions or aspect ratio
- Blanket / tritium breeding approach
