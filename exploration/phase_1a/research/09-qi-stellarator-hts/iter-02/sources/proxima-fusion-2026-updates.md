# Proxima Fusion 2026 Updates

## RWE / Bavaria / MPG-IPP MoU (February 26, 2026)

Proxima Fusion signed an agreement with RWE, the Free State of Bavaria, and Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics to build the world's first commercial fusion power plant in Europe.

### Key Details:
- **Alpha demo**: Near IPP in Garching, €2 billion cost, operational in 2030s
  - First stellarator to demonstrate net energy gain (Q>1)
  - Tests and validates key fusion technologies under real-world conditions
- **Stellaris commercial plant**: Gundremmingen, Bavaria (former RWE nuclear fission plant site)
  - Planned to follow Alpha in later 2030s
- **Funding**: ~20% private investment from Proxima, ~20% potential state contribution from Bavaria
  - Bavaria's High-Tech Agenda: up to €400 million for magnetic fusion research
  - RWE willing to participate financially
- **Jobs**: Thousands expected; magnet factory with up to 1,000 positions
