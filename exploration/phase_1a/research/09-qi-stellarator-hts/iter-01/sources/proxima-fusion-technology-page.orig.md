**Source**: https://www.proximafusion.com/technology

## Key Technical Details

- **Confinement**: Quasi-isodynamic (QI) stellarator
- **Magnets**: High Temperature Superconductors (HTS) enable stronger magnetic fields and significant size reduction vs. previous stellarator concepts
- **Plasma currents**: Toroidal currents cancel out to zero — eliminates current-driven instabilities and disruptions
- **Heat exhaust**: Island divertor, demonstrated on W7-AS and W7-X at Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics

## Stellaris Design
- First QI stellarator-based power plant design that simultaneously meets all major physics and engineering constraints
- Demonstrated through electromagnetic, structural, thermal, and neutronic simulations
- Designed to produce more power per unit volume than any stellarator power plant designed before

## Timeline
