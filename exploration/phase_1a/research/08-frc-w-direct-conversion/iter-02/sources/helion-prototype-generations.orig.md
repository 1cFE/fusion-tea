# Helion Energy Prototype Generations

**Sources**: Wikipedia (Helion Energy), web search results
**Fetched**: 2026-03-07

## Prototype Timeline

| Gen | Name | Year | Key Achievements |
|-----|------|------|-----------------|
| 1-3 | (early prototypes) | 2013-2014 | Initial FRC experiments |
| 4 | Grande | 2014 | 4 T magnetic compression, cm-scale FRCs, 5 keV plasma temp |
| 5 | Venti | 2018 | 7 T magnetic fields, 2 keV ion temperature at high density |
| 6 | Trenta | 2021 | 100M°C (8 keV ions, >1 keV electrons), >8 T compression, >10,000 pulses over 16 months |
| 7 | Polaris | 2024-present | 150M°C (13 keV), D-T fusion demonstrated (Jan 2026), largest FRCs to date, 15 T+ target |
| 8 | Orion | Under construction | 50 MWe commercial plant, Malaga WA, target 2028 |

## Lab Experiment Heritage
- FRX-L at LANL (1999-2012): High-density FRC research, plasma injector for MTF
- FRCHX at LANL/AFRL: Solid liner compression of FRC plasmas using Shiva Star
- Helion's IPA (Inductive Plasmoid Accelerator) experiments at MSNW LLC (2005-2012) — precursor to Helion
- Founded 2013 by David Kirtley, John Slough, Chris Pihl, George Votroubek (all from University of Washington / MSNW LLC)
