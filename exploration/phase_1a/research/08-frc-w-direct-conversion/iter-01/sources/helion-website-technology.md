## Key Technical Details

### Confinement
- Field Reversed Configuration (FRC)
- Magneto-inertial fusion: two FRCs accelerated to >300 km/s from opposite ends, collide and merge
- FRC is high-beta (ratio of plasma pressure to magnetic pressure close to 1)

### Fuel
- D-He3 target commercial fuel cycle
- D-He3 requires ~200 million degrees C
- D-He3 reaction yields 18.3 MeV (3.6 MeV alpha + 14.7 MeV proton)
- DD side reactions produce He3 directly (50%) and tritium (50%) which decays to He3 (t½ = 12.3 yr)
- D-He3 releases "only 5% of its energy in the form of fast neutrons" (from Wikipedia search snippet)
- Polaris will run on D-D, D-T, and D-He3 (progression)

### Energy Capture
- Direct inductive energy recovery via Faraday's law
- Expanding plasma pushes back on magnetic field, inducing current in coils
- "Like regenerative braking in an electric vehicle"
- No steam cycle required
- 95% of input energy after each pulse must be recovered for net electricity
- Magnets run at >90% energy efficiency

### Magnets / Coils
- Pulsed electromagnets (NOT superconducting)
- "Regular aluminum magnets" (Contrary Research, quoting CEO Kirtley)
- Cylindrical skeleton of electromagnetic coils
- Energy carried through coaxial cables with copper, aluminum, and custom-metal alloys (~720 miles total)
- Compression fields >10 T demonstrated (Trenta)
- 15 T+ target (Polaris)
- 20 T ARPA-E experiment, 40 T reactor target (DocsLib presentation)

### Capacitor Bank
- Thousands of high-voltage pulsed capacitors
- >50 MJ total energy storage
- Charged to tens of thousands of volts
- Some built in-house, some purchased externally

### Plasma Parameters
- 100 million °C (9 keV) achieved on Trenta
- 150 million °C achieved on Polaris (Feb 2026)
- Plasma lifetimes >1 ms
- 8 keV ion temperature, 1 keV electron temperature (Trenta)

### Repetition Rate
- Trenta: ~1 pulse per 10 minutes
- Polaris target: 1 Hz (one pulse per second)
- Commercial target: higher — possibly 2 Hz (DocsLib) to 10 Hz or even 60 Hz (long-term)

### Power Output
- Polaris: demonstrate net electricity (proof of concept)
- First commercial plant (Orion): 50 MWe+ (Microsoft PPA, target 2028)
- Future: 500 MWe (Nucor partnership)
- Modular design philosophy ("shipping container sized")

### Neutron Management
- D-He3 produces fewer neutrons than D-T
- 2.45 MeV neutrons from DD side reactions (vs 14.1 MeV from D-T)
- Shielding: borated polyethylene and borated concrete shield vault
- Similar to hospital particle beam shielding
- "One meter" solid barrier around reactor
