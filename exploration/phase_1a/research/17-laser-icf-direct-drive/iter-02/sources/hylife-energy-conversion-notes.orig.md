# HYLIFE Energy Conversion - Research Notes
**Date**: 2026-03-07

## Ambiguity in Xcimer's Energy Conversion

Two potentially conflicting descriptions:

1. **Xcimer Science page** (xcimer.energy/science/): "circulating coolant absorbs and carries away the heat to generate steam, which in turn drives turbines to produce electricity"

2. **HYLIFE heritage / IFE literature**: "exchange heat with helium to drive a gas turbine that extracts 45% of the fusion energy as electricity" — this is the helium Brayton cycle described in HYLIFE-type concepts

The Science page language ("generate steam") could be:
- Simplified marketing language (using "steam" generically)
- An actual design choice change from the HYLIFE heritage He Brayton
- Describing a combined/bottoming cycle

## HYLIFE-II Reference Design
- Heavy-ion driver (5 MJ), 350 MJ fusion yield, 6 Hz → 940 MWe
- FLiBe (Li₂BeF₄) molten salt jets
- 30-year first wall lifetime
- Published 1994, Fusion Technology journal

## HYLIFE-III Updates (Xcimer)
- Much larger fusion yield due to enhanced driver energy (10+ MJ excimer laser)
- Sub-Hz repetition rate (compensated by higher yield per shot)
- FLiBe confirmed as blanket/shield material
- 2024 paper: "Preliminary nuclear analysis of HYLIFE-III" in Fusion Engineering and Design
- TBR analyzed for various FLiBe thicknesses

## Resolution
For the combined row, `Thermal (unspecified)` remains the safest classification. Xcimer's specific cycle (He Brayton vs steam Rankine) has contradictory signals. Focused Energy explicitly states steam cycle.
