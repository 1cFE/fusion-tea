# Neo Fusion / Fusion Energy Technology Co., Ltd — Company Profile

## Identity

- **English name**: Neo Fusion (per FusionXInvest) / NeoFusion
- **Chinese name**: 聚变新能(安徽)有限公司 — Fusion Energy Technology Co., Ltd
- **Website**: http://jbxnah.com
- **Founded**: 2023
- **Location**: Hefei, Anhui, China (adjacent to CRAFT campus at ASIPP)

## Ownership

- Majority state-owned:
  - **China National Petroleum Corporation (CNPC)** — 20% stake
  - **Hefei Science Island** (controlled by Chinese Academy of Sciences) — significant stake
- Other investors: NIO Capital (reduced exposure Dec 2024)
- Registered capital increased from 5 billion to 14.5 billion yuan (~$2B USD)

## Funding

- $214 million secured (May 2023 round)
- CNPC and CAS significantly expanded capital

## Mission

- Design and development of BEST tokamak fusion device
- Plans to commercialize fusion technology within 20 years
- Part of China's national fusion strategy

## Relationship to ASIPP

- Neo Fusion / Fusion Energy Technology Co., Ltd is the corporate vehicle for BEST construction
- ASIPP (Institute of Plasma Physics, CAS) provides scientific leadership and expertise
- BEST is built next to ASIPP's CRAFT (Comprehensive Research Facility for Fusion Technology) campus
- Construction started June 2023
