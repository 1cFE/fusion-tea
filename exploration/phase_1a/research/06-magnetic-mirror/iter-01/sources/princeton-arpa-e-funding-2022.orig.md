# Princeton: Fisch Receives Funding for 'Unlikely but Fantastic' Clean Energy Technology

**Source**: https://www.princeton.edu/news/2022/03/10/fisch-receives-funding-unlikely-fantastic-clean-energy-technology
**Date**: March 10, 2022

## Key Facts
- **Funding**: $1.5 million from ARPA-E OPEN 2021 program
- **PI**: Nat Fisch, professor of astrophysical sciences, Princeton University
- **Status**: Purely theoretical research — no experiments yet at time of article

## Technical Approach
- Exploit the pB11 fusion reaction (proton + boron-11 → 3 helium-4)
- Key challenge: at required temperatures (~150-300 keV), electrons produce x-rays (bremsstrahlung) that carry away more energy than fusion produces
- Innovation: hold protons and boron at **different temperatures** and **mostly in different locations**
- Method: "whirling the plasma vigorously" so lighter protons reside in a hotter region, heavier boron ions in a colder region (centrifugal separation)
- Mass disparity between protons (1 amu) and boron-11 (11 amu) enables this separation

## Quotes
- Fisch calls pB11 "the holy grail of really clean, really abundant fusion energy"
- "a real long shot" requiring fundamental research
- Aims to solve "the important problem of recirculating efficiently the energy lost as x-rays"
