## Key Technical Details (from full text fetch)

### Device: FF-2B
- Megampere dense plasma focus
- Anode radius: 2.8 cm
- Maximum current: 2.7 MA
- Construction cost: <$1 million for large megampere devices

### Fuel Preparation
- Isotopically pure decaborane (B¹⁰H₁₄), 0.07% B-10 content
- B/H ratio: 0.71 (adjustable by adding/removing H₂)
- Expected fill pressure: 10 torr
- Heating temperature: 90°C

### Plasma Conditions
- Ion energy record: >200 keV (mean), ten-shot mean 125 keV
- Ion density: up to 10²¹/cm³ (DPF devices generally)
- Expected max density with decaborane: 7.3 × 10¹⁸/cm³
- Peak Alfvén velocities: ~6.6 cm/μs
- nτ target: >2 × 10¹³ s/cm³ (current best: 2.4 × 10¹² s/cm³)

### Diagnostics
- Secondary neutron and gamma-ray reactions (primary pB11 produces no neutrons)
- Time-of-flight PMT detectors at 11.5 m and 17.5 m from device axis

### Energy Conversion
- Capacitor-to-x-ray conversion efficiency: >10%
- Quantum magnetic field effect reduces bremsstrahlung
- Simulations: fusion power can exceed bremsstrahlung by factor ~2
- Target: 80% burn-up of fuel in plasmoid
