# NearStar Fusion — Technology Overview

**Sources**: nearstarfusion.com, nearstarfusion.com/learn-more, climateinsider.com, fox40.com (press releases), nsf.gov (SBIR award #2304408)
**Retrieved**: 2026-03-07

## Company Overview

NearStar Fusion is developing Magnetized Target Impact Fusion (MTIF), a hybrid approach combining magnetic confinement and inertial confinement. Based in Virginia, collaborating with University of Alabama in Huntsville and Texas A&M.

## Technology Approach: MTIF

### Classification Note
NearStar describes MTIF as combining MCF and ICF into a hybrid approach. This is closer to MIF (Magneto-Inertial Fusion) than pure IFE/Projectile ICF. The concept "compresses, heats and magnetizes fusion fuel simultaneously" using the railgun driver.

### Core Driver: Plasma Armature Railgun
- Launches 50-gram fuel capsules
- Velocity: 10 km/s (22,369 mph, ~30x speed of sound)
- Kinetic energy: over 1 MJ per shot
- Repetition rate: 1 Hz (once per second)
- Uses COTS (Commercial-Off-The-Shelf) technologies

### Fuel
- Primary: Deuterium-only (D-D) — explicitly avoids tritium
- Backup: D-T mixture mentioned as secondary option
- Deuterium described as "inexpensive and universally available in our oceans"
- Company emphasizes avoiding "significant technical complexities and cost associated with the use of tritium fuel"

### Target Design
- Solid pusher impacting cavity prefilled with deuterium (or D-T)
- Based on Sandia Z-Machine method of imploding metallic fuel target liners
- Conducting experiments at Texas A&M Hypervelocity Impact Laboratory (HVIL)
- Computer modeling with University of Alabama in Huntsville

### Neutron Management
- Molten lead system for first wall
- Minimizes neutron embrittlement damage
- Enables use of conventional materials (tungsten alloys)
- Described as addressing "the First Wall Problem"

### Energy Conversion
- Can "retrofit the heat source in traditional hydrocarbon power plants"
- Leverages existing turbines and infrastructure
- Implies thermal conversion but specific cycle not stated

### Power Plant Design
- Modular architecture
- Mass-produced components, rapid on-site assembly
- Scalable: 50 MW to 1 GW+ capacity
- Each machine: 50–100 MW baseload electricity
- Integration with existing power grids or microgrids

### Timeline
- Break-even experiments: ~5 years from now
- Prototype power plant: ~10 years
- Very early stage — NSF SBIR Phase I funding ($275K, 2023)

## Key Distinctions from Pure Projectile ICF
1. **Magnetized target** — fuel is magnetized, not just inertially compressed
2. **D-D fuel preference** — avoids tritium entirely
3. **Railgun driver** — plasma armature railgun, not electromagnetic gun
4. **Hybrid MIF concept** — explicitly described as combining MCF + ICF
