# Acceleron Fusion ARPA-E Presentation (July 2025)

**Source**: https://arpa-e.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/Day2_09_Newburg.pdf
**Author**: Seth Newburg, Ph.D., Co-founder and President, Acceleron Fusion and NK Labs
**Event**: ARPA-E Fusion Programs Annual Meeting, July 8-9, 2025

## Key Technical Details

### Muon Source Innovation
- **Active-target muon source**: Accelerator fires ion beam into target; muons collected by fields inside the target
- **GEANT4 simulation**: Currently shows 3.0 GeV energy per negative pion and muon exiting the target
- **ML optimization**: Bayesian optimization of geometric parameters in GEANT4 simulations to improve muon source design
- Design can likely be improved with further engineering

### Energy Balance (from slide 5 diagram)
- **Energy cost per muon**: 3 GeV/muon
- **Fusions per muon**: 300 (sticking-imposed limit)
- **Each fusion**: ~25 MeV (17.6 MeV + breeding energy)
- **Total fusion energy per muon**: 300 × 25 MeV = 7.5 GeV
- **Heat recycling**: 2.5 GeV recovered
- **Net available**: 6.4 GeV from fusion + recycling
- **Accelerator cost**: 3 GeV → after 64% efficiency = need ~4.7 GeV input
- **Recirculating power**: 47%
- **Balance of plant**: Brayton cycle mentioned in cost slide

### Fusion Experiments
- **Location**: Paul Scherrer Institute (PSI), Switzerland, using πE1.2 beam area
- **Equipment**: Diamond anvil cell for DT compression, neutron detectors, tritium glovebox
- **Fuel states tested**: Liquid hydrogen, liquid deuterium, liquid DT (60/40 mix)
- **Achievement**: Compression of liquid DT to solid, then heating under pressure to high-density fluid
- **Oct 2024 run**: 28 hours continuous fusion with 60/40 DT, after 100+ hours testing with deuterium
- **Pressure/temperature**: Reached conditions beyond previously measured range

### Cost Target (slide 21)
- **Target LCOE**: $0.025/kWh (labeled "Fusion (?)")
- **Assumptions**: Superconducting accelerator, Brayton cycle balance of plant, revenue from heat sales
- **Compared to**: Coal $0.089, Biomass $0.077, Nuclear fission $0.071, Gas $0.043

### Roadmap
- **Energy breakeven test**: At Brookhaven National Laboratory, target within five years (~2030)
- **Plant target**: 100 MW (from company website)
