# Tokamak Energy Heating Systems

## ST40 Current Systems
- **NBI**: Existing neutral beam injection system
- **ECRH/Gyrotron**: 1 MW gyrotron from Kyoto Fusioneering, delivered January 2025
  - Frequencies: 104 GHz or 137 GHz (tuned to electron cyclotron resonance in ST40)
  - Purpose: plasma heating, current drive, and plasma control
  - Key advantage: can be positioned away from device (unlike NBI which must be close)
  - Plans to use both NBI + ECRH simultaneously on ST40

## Pilot Plant Heating Strategy
- ECRH/gyrotron technology described as "crucial for future power plants"
- Solves key spherical tokamak challenge: limited space for central solenoid
- Gyrotron-driven current drive reduces dependence on central solenoid for plasma current
- Combined NBI + ECRH approach being tested on ST40 to inform pilot plant design
- Specific MW-level heating power for ST-E1 NOT disclosed
