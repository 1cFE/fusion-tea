## Neutral Beam Injection (NBI)
- ST40 has NBI heating installed (approximately 2 MW based on paper title)
- Used for initial 100M°C milestone

## Electron Cyclotron Resonance Heating (ECRH)
- 1 MW gyrotron built by Kyoto Fusioneering, delivered Dec 2024
- Frequencies: tunable 104 GHz or 137 GHz (matching electron cyclotron resonance in ST40)
- Originally, a 2 MW ECRH system was under development (2 × 1 MW gyrotrons at 105/140 GHz)
- ECRH solves key ST challenge: limited space for central solenoid → ECRH provides non-inductive current drive

## Combined Heating Strategy
- Plans to use NBI + ECRH simultaneously on ST40
- Building understanding of optimal balance between heating methods
- Both methods expected for future machines (ST80-HTS, ST-E1)

## No ICRH
- No mention of ICRH (Ion Cyclotron Resonance Heating) in any Tokamak Energy literature
