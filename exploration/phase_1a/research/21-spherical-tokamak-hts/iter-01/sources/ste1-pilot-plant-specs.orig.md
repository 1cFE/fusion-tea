## Design Evolution

### Earlier specs (2023 announcement)
- Aspect ratio: 2.0
- Major radius: 4.25 m
- Toroidal field: 4.25 T
- Fusion power: 800 MW
- Net electrical output: 85 MW
- Blanket: liquid lithium tritium breeding blanket

### Revision D (DPP 2025, pre-conceptual design)
- Major radius: 5.0 m
- Aspect ratio: 2.3
- On-axis toroidal field: 5.25 T
- Net electrical output: 450-750 MW (depending on technology and physics assumptions)
- Blanket: outboard-only liquid lithium breeder blanket
- Tritium breeding ratio: 1.2
- Maintenance scheme integrated early in design process

## Notes
- The revision D specs represent a significant evolution from the 2023 announcement
- Machine grew from 4.25m to 5.0m major radius
- Field increased from 4.25T to 5.25T
- Net power target increased dramatically (85 MW → 450-750 MW)
- Note: earlier 200 MWe figure from ANS article may have been an intermediate design point
- Part of US DOE Milestone-Based Fusion Development Program
- Target: grid-connected electricity in the early 2030s (now more likely mid-2030s)

## Power Conversion
- Not explicitly specified by Tokamak Energy
- STEP programme research (related UK spherical tokamak) evaluates steam Rankine, hybrid steam-ORC, and sCO2 cycles
- Pulsed operation creates need for thermal energy storage (e.g., molten salt) to buffer power cycle
