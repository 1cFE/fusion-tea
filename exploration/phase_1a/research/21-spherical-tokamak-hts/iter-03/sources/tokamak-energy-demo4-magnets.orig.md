# Tokamak Energy: Demo4 HTS Magnet Breakthrough (Nov 2025)

**Source**: Tokamak Energy press release, November 19, 2025
**URL**: https://tokamakenergy.com/2025/11/19/tokamak-energy-announces-fusion-power-plant-magnet-technology-breakthrough/
**Additional coverage**: https://www.neimagazine.com/news/tokamak-energy-announces-magnet-breakthrough/

## Key Technical Details

- **System**: Demo4 — complete set of HTS magnets in tokamak configuration
- **Field strength**: 11.8 Tesla achieved at -243°C (30 K)
- **Coil configuration**: 14 toroidal field magnets + 2 poloidal field magnets
- **Current**: 7 million ampere turns through centre column
- **Material**: REBCO (rare earth barium copper oxide) HTS tape
- **Current density**: HTS delivers ~200x the current density of copper

## Significance

- World-first high field platform to generate and study fusion-relevant forces across a complete system coil set
- While single HTS magnets demonstrated before (CFS 20 T in 2021), Demo4 validates a complete magnet system
- Further testing continuing, next results expected early 2026

## Relation to ST-E1

- Demo4 validates the HTS magnet approach for ST-E1
- The 11.8 T at the coil is consistent with the 5.25 T on-axis field target for ST-E1 (field at coil is always higher than on-axis due to 1/R dependence)
- Confirms capability for the planned magnet system
