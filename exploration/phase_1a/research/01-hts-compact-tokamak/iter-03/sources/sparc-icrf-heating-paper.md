# SPARC ICRF Heating - Journal of Plasma Physics (2020)

**Source**: Lin, Wright et al., "Physics basis for the ICRF system of the SPARC tokamak," Journal of Plasma Physics, vol. 86, 865860506, 2020.
**URL**: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-plasma-physics/article/physics-basis-for-the-icrf-system-of-the-sparc-tokamak/22016DD64F3C5CAD47563A1E4AE59934

## Key Technical Details

- ICRF heating is the **sole auxiliary heating method** on SPARC
- Delivers up to **25 MW** of coupled power at **120 MHz**
- Two 4-strap toroidally aligned antennas in each of 7 ports
- Operates for both full-field (Bt0 ~ 12 T) D-T and reduced field (Bt0 ~ 8 T) D-D operation
- Uses fast wave at ~120 MHz
- Good wave penetration and strong single-pass absorption expected for D-T(3He), D(3He), D(H) and 4He(H) heating scenarios
- ICRF chosen because it is the only method that can penetrate SPARC plasmas, heat fusion ions, and can be built cost-effectively

## Classification Note

While ICRF is often associated with `RF (ICRH)` in the schema, the 120 MHz frequency is technically in the ion cyclotron range of frequencies. This is standard ICRH.
