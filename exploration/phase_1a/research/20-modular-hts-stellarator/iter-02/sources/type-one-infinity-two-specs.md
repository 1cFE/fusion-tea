# Type One Energy — Infinity Two Technical Specifications

**Source**: Journal of Plasma Physics (2025), Vol. 91; company press releases; APS DPP 2025
**Extracted**: 2026-03-06 (iter-02)

## Key Parameters

- **Fusion power**: 800 MW (upgradeable to 3000 MW)
- **Net electric power**: 350 MWe
- **Fusion gain**: Q > 40, with access to ignition
- **Magnetic field**: 9 T (on-axis)
- **Major radius**: 12.5 m
- **Minor radius**: 1.25 m
- **Aspect ratio**: 10
- **Number of field periods**: 4
- **Configuration**: Quasi-isodynamic, maximum-J optimized
- **Bootstrap current**: ~2 kA (very low)
- **Volume-averaged electron density**: ~2×10²⁰ m⁻³

## Heating

- **Primary**: ECRH (Electron Cyclotron Resonance Heating)
- **Fueling**: Pellet injection (also supports edge density gradient to reduce ITG turbulence)
- "The only envisioned external sources required for Infinity Two operation are pellet injection and ECRH"

## Blanket and Tritium

- **Type**: HCPB (Helium-Cooled Pebble Bed) — gas-cooled solid breeder
- **TBR**: 1.30 (confirmed by OpenMC neutronics with 300M particles)
- **Selection rationale**: "Neutronics performance at applicable blanket build depths, and the relatively mature technology basis"
- FLiBe considered where "shielding efficiency is a primary consideration"

## Thermal Cycle

- Rankine cycle with reheat
- Thermal efficiency >30%

## Operations

- 2-year operating cycle
- 30-day planned maintenance outages
- Steady-state, disruption-free operation

## Magnet Technology

- Modular HTS REBCO coils
- Modular superconducting magnets following W7-X heritage
- CFS partnership for magnet development

## Design Review

- Formal Initial Design Review completed May 27, 2025
- TVA partnership for pilot plant site (mid-2030s target)
- Infinity One test platform: construction 2026, operation 2029
