# Deuterium-Tritium Levitated Dipole Fusion Power Plants

**Source**: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.20564v1
**Authors**: T. Simpson, R.A. Badcock, T. Berry, C.S. Chisholm, P.J. Fimognari, P. Fisher, D.T. Garnier, K. Lenagh-Glue, B. Leuw, R. Mataira, L. Meadows, T. McIntosh, J. Poata, K. Richardson, B. Smith, A. Simpson, J.D. Tyler, T. Wordsworth (OpenStar Technologies Limited, Wellington, New Zealand)
**Type**: Preprint (arXiv)

## Key Technical Details

### Fuel
- Deuterium-Tritium (DT) fuel cycle
- DT required for rapid deployment due to lower plasma triple product requirements

### Magnet
- REBCO HTS tape in cable-in-conduit conductor (CICC)
- Peak field: 23 T at core magnet
- Operating current density: 0-300 A/mm²
- Two-section coil: sacrificial section (~20% of coil, ~1-year neutron damage lifetime) + semi-permanent section (decade-scale)
- On-board superconducting transformer-rectifier ("flux pump") power supply
- Cooled by neon slush (melting point 24.6 K)

### Heating
- Ion-cyclotron resonance heating (ICRH) as baseline
- Also evaluated ECRH and NBI

### Tritium Breeding
- Li₂O ceramic blanket
- Target TBR: 1.1
- 10B(n,α)³H reaction in B₄C shield also produces tritium

### Neutron Management
- Layered tungsten and boron carbide (B₄C) composite shield
- Only ~25% of fusion neutrons pass through core magnet region due to geometry
- 92% of heat deposited in neutron shield radiated to first wall
- Two-temperature shield: hot shield (>2000 K) and warm shield (~600°C)
- 1 MW-year/m² neutron fluence threshold for tungsten replacement

### Operation Mode
- Pulsed operation with periodic maintenance docking
- >95% duty cycle achievable
- Operates until cryogenic reservoir melts, then magnet docked for coolant replacement

### Energy Conversion
- Thermal power plant (steam cycle implied)
- Neutron shield radiant heat → first wall → tritium breeding blanket → thermal conversion

### Reactor Performance
- Reactor A (conservative Bohm scaling): ~667 MW fusion, ~208 MW net electric
- Core magnet volume ~2x smaller than equivalent tokamak

### Vacuum Vessel
- Two-layer: outer reinforced concrete dome + inner thin Inconel 718 with tungsten coating
- Large bottom opening for core magnet removal
- ~2 m maintenance cavity between blanket and outer wall

### Plasma Physics
- Marginal interchange-mode MHD stability
- Local β₀ ~ 3 optimal
- Bohm-like confinement scaling (conservative baseline)
- Peaked pressure profile: p₀ ∝ (Rlcfs/R₀)^(20/3)
