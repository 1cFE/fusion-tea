# Design and Initial Results from the "Junior" Levitated Dipole Experiment

**Source**: https://arxiv.org/html/2508.17691v1
**Authors**: OpenStar Technologies team (same group)
**Type**: Preprint (arXiv) / also ScienceDirect

## Junior Core Magnet Specs
- 2nd-gen HTS (REBCO tape)
- 14 non-insulated (NI) solder impregnated HTS coils in series
- Design field: 5.63 T max at windings; achieved 2.35 T (at 42% of design current)
- Design current: 1.44 kA; achieved 600 A
- Operating temperature: ~25 K (forced helium cooling)
- Floating mass: 550 kg
- Joint resistance: 8.6 ± 0.5 μΩ
- Stored energy achieved: 0.095 MJ (record for flux pump delivery)

## Vacuum Chamber (Marsden)
- Inner radius: 2.6 m (5.2 m wide)
- Max height: 2.54 m
- Material: 304L stainless steel, 16.5 mm wall thickness

## Heating
- ECRH: Two magnetron heads at 2.45 GHz, 15 kW each
- Cavity heating (small first-pass absorption, multiple reflections)
- Planned additions: 33 kW at 6.4 GHz klystron, 10 kW at 10.5 GHz

## First Plasma (Late 2024)
- Fuel: Helium-4 (not hydrogen isotopes yet)
- 17 discharges over two days
- Magnet mechanically supported (not yet levitated)
- Flat line-integrated density profiles (radially localized plasma source)
- End-point losses dominated transport (supported config)

## Key Notes
- Built in under 2 years for less than $10M USD
- "Marsden" = the vacuum chamber name
- "Junior" = the core magnet name
