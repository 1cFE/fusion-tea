# OpenStar 2026 Funding and Tahi Timeline

**Sources**: Multiple news outlets (Feb-Mar 2026)
**Extracted**: 2026-03-07 (iter-02)

## February 2026 Milestone

OpenStar became the first commercial company to create and confine plasma using a levitated dipole reactor. Junior prototype achieved plasma at ~300,000°C lasting 20 seconds in a 5.2m vacuum chamber.

## NZ Government Funding

NZD 35 million (~USD 21 million) from Regional Infrastructure Fund for Tahi development.

## Prototype Roadmap

| Prototype | Target | Key Goal |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Junior | Operational (2024-present) | First plasma, levitated confinement demo |
| Tahi | ~2028 (2 years from Feb 2026) | 20 T magnetic field (4x Junior), Lawson criterion demonstration |
| Maui | ~2031 (5 years from Feb 2026) | Neutron production, revenue-generating |
| Tama Nui | TBD | 50-200 MW electricity, commercial power |

## Tahi Specifications

- Magnetic field: up to 20 T (4x stronger than Junior's ~5.6 T)
- Goal: Place dipole on Lawson criterion curve (nTτ)
- Demonstrate decoupled energy and particle transport
