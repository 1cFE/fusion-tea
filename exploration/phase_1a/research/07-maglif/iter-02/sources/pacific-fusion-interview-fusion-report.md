## Key Technical Details

### Demonstration System Architecture
- **Facility dimensions**: 73 m × 80 m (slightly larger than a soccer field)
- **Number of pulser modules**: 156 sets arranged spherically around the target area
- **Module diameter**: ~1.9 m each
- **Bricks per module**: 320 organized into 32 rings/stages of 10 bricks each
- **Brick voltage**: ±100 kV per brick
- **Capacitance per brick**: 160 nF
- **Energy per capacitor**: 800 joules
- **Total stored energy**: ~80 MJ across all 156 modules
- **Energy delivered to target**: ~10% of stored energy (~8 MJ)
- **Current**: 60+ MA in ~100 ns

### Target Chamber
- **Water tank** filled with de-ionized water
- Serves as dielectric medium AND for neutron/X-ray absorption
- **Insulator stack cylinder diameter**: 6 meters

### Performance Goals
- Facility-level energy gain 100× higher than NIF
- 1,000× price-performance improvement vs NIF by 2030
- One-tenth the cost of NIF
- Energy coupling efficiency potentially 50× better than laser ICF

### Not Disclosed
- Repetition rate
- Yield per shot (specific number beyond ">100 MJ")
- Blanket type for power plant
- Thermal cycle
- Tritium breeding approach
- Commercial plant architecture (distinct from demo)
