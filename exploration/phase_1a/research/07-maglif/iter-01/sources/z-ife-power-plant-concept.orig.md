## Z-IFE Power Plant Design

### Recyclable Transmission Line (RTL)
- Uses recyclable material for magnetically-insulated transmission line connecting pulsed power accelerator to z-pinch target
- Intense on-site manufacturing system rebuilds transmission lines, wire arrays and hohlraums

### Repetition Rate
- 0.1 Hz per power unit (one shot every ~10 seconds)
- A 0.5 MA cavity fired in repetitive mode at one shot every 10.25 seconds (~0.1 Hz)
- Z-pinch driven ICF can operate at high yields (1 to 30 GJ) with low pulse rate (0.01-0.1 Hz)

### Blanket Design
- Lithium-bearing coolant blanket provides:
  1. Absorption of fusion neutron energy (primary function)
  2. Breeding of tritium (fuel)
  3. Protection of first wall from neutron damage
- FLiBe (lithium fluoride-beryllium fluoride) identified as candidate material
- May use FLiBe in solid state as portion of RTL

### Energy Conversion
- Thermal conversion: neutron energy captured in blanket → heat → electricity
- Specific cycle type (steam Rankine vs sCO2 Brayton) not specified in available summaries

### Yield
- High-yield paradigm: 500 MJ to several GJ per shot
- Low repetition rate compensated by high yield per pulse
