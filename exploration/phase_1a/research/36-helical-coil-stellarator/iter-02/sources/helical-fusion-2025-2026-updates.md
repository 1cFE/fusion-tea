# Helical Fusion 2025-2026 Updates

## October 2025: HTS Coil Milestone

- Successfully demonstrated HTS coil at 40 kA under 7 T external magnetic field at 15 K (-258°C)
- HTS cable: 30 layers of REBCO, conductor cross-section ~3 cm, length >4 m
- World's first successful demonstration of uninsulated large-scale HTS conductor-based coil
- Coil designed for commercial fusion plant scale use
- Sources: ANS Nuclear Newswire 2025-10-29, BusinessWire 2025-10-26

## 2026: Coil Manufacturing Machine

- Completed new coil manufacturing machine in collaboration with Sugino Machine
- Designed to wind HTS cable into spiral-shaped coils for Helix HARUKA
- Machine to be transported to demonstration site; on-site assembly scheduled to begin in 2026
- Source: global.helicalfusion.com

## December 2025: Series A Extension

- Raised additional $5.5M (Series A extension)
- Total funding: ¥5.2 billion ($35.3M)
- Source: BusinessWire 2025-12-04

## Roadmap

- **Helix HARUKA**: Integrated Demonstration Device — assembly 2026, performance tests by end of 2020s
  - Two pillars: HTS magnets + integrated blanket/divertor system
- **Helix KANATA**: Fusion pilot plant — steady-state power generation in 2030s
  - 50 MWe-class (HESTIA design basis)
- **First commercial plant**: 100 MWe-class

## GALOP Blanket Testing System

- GAs-driven Liquid metal OPeration
- Installed at NIFS research facility, Toki City, Gifu Prefecture
- Developed with Sukegawa Electric Co., Ltd.
- Validates novel liquid metal pump mechanism (gas-driven, no rotating components)
- Specific liquid metal composition not publicly disclosed

## Tohoku University Materials Collaboration (2024)

- High-manganese alumina-formed austenitic steel with silicon addition
- Non-magnetic, low-activation, corrosion-resistant structural material for blanket
- Published in Nuclear Materials and Energy, March 2024

## Energy Capture — sCO2 Evidence

- Helical Fusion lists "power generation systems (CO2 gas turbines)" among 14 collaborative research areas
- NIFS Oroshhi-2 platform has sCO2 gas turbine demonstration plan (Ishiyama & Tanaka, 2019)
- 20 kW(e) demonstration at 20% efficiency was determined feasible
- Commercial target: >50% efficiency at 800-1200 K working temperature
- Not yet definitively confirmed as HESTIA baseline, but strong directional evidence
