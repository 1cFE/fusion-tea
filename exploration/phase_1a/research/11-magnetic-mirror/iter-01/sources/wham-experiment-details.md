# WHAM - Wisconsin HTS Axisymmetric Mirror

Source: https://wham.physics.wisc.edu/
Additional: https://wippl.wisc.edu/wisconsin-hts-axisymmetric-mirror/

## Key Technical Details

### Magnet System
- REBCO HTS magnets from Commonwealth Fusion Systems
- 17 Tesla in bore, >20 T on the magnets themselves
- World record magnetic field strength for magnetically confined plasmas

### Heating Methods
- ECH: 110 GHz gyrotron microwave beam (used for first plasma, ionizes in 4 T region)
- NBI: Neutral beam injection to fuel ions
- HHFW: High Harmonic Fast Wave RF heating to accelerate NBI-injected ions in situ

### Target Plasma Parameters
- Electron temperature: 1 keV
- Average ion energy: 20 keV
- Quasi-stationary plasmas (duration >> ion slowing down and confinement times)
- Density approaching plasma pressure limit

### Funding & Partners
- $10M ARPA-E grant (awarded 2020)
- UW-Madison, MIT, Commonwealth Fusion Systems partnership
- First plasma: July 15, 2024

### End Product Goal
- Realistic conceptual design of a low-cost ($100M) Break Even Axisymmetric Tandem (BEAT)
