# Realta Fusion - Fusion Hub Startup Spotlight

Source: https://fusionhub.substack.com/p/fusion-startup-spotlight-realta-fusion

## Key Technical Details

### Fuel & Reaction
- Deuterium-Tritium (DT) fuel cycle
- 80% of output energy in neutrons → heat via collisions in reactor blanket

### Heating Methods
- Neutral Beam Injection (NBI): increases density in end plugs
- Electron Cyclotron Heating (ECH): heats electrons and increases end plug potentials

### Magnet Specifications
- High Temperature Superconducting (HTS) REBCO magnets
- Mirror ratio of 10 or higher (vs historical max of ~2)
- "$50 million in REBCO tape alone for WHAM++"

### Energy Conversion
- Direct Energy Conversion: "axisymmetric ferromagnetic venetian blinds" to convert lost ion beams into electrical current
- Thermal blanket for neutron energy capture
- Industrial heat as primary near-term application

### Plasma Configuration
- Tandem mirror design with central cell and end plugs
- Loss cone and ambipolar hole in velocity space

### Stabilization
- Kinetic stability: "Sloshing ions" to stabilize drift cyclotron loss cone (DCLC) and Alfvén ion cyclotron instabilities
- MHD stability: Vortex stabilization via sheared azimuthal flows; expander regions with good curvature

### Device Timeline
- WHAM: Simple mirror validation device ($10M ARPA-E)
- WHAM++: Scientific breakeven demonstration
- Anvil: Commercial-scale high-field simple mirror (end-plug physics demonstrator)
- HAMMIR: Tandem mirror pilot plant for industrial heat and electricity
