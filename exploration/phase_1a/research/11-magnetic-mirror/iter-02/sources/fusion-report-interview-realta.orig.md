# The Fusion Report - Interview with Realta Fusion (Aug 2025)

**Source**: https://thefusionreport.substack.com/p/interview-with-realta-fusion
**Retrieved**: 2026-03-07

## Key Technical Details

### Fuel
- D-T fuel for first-generation systems

### Energy Conversion
- Neutron energy captured through **thermal blankets** (which also produce tritium from lithium)
- Charged helium "ash" captured via **direct energy conversion** as it exits the fusion chamber
- Dual approach "lowers the Q required to reach net-electric while still using DT fuel"
- Blankets produce tritium from lithium (confirms tritium breeding is planned, lithium-based)

### Performance Scaling
- 50-meter center cell: Q > 5
- Longer center cells: Q > 10 possible
- ~7 MW per meter as center cell lengthens
- Input power remains constant despite increased output
- Theoretical: 500 MW from Q=20 system

### Magnets
- HTS magnets, 17 T (WHAM uses CFS-built magnets)

### Instabilities
- DCLC instability addressed in recent papers
