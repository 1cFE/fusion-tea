# MANTA: A Negative-Triangularity NASEM-Compliant Fusion Pilot Plant

**Source**: Rutherford et al. (2024), Plasma Physics and Controlled Fusion, 66(10), 105006
**DOI**: 10.1088/1361-6587/ad6708
**ArXiv**: 2405.20243
**Retrieved**: 2026-03-06

## Overview

MANTA (Modular Adjustable Negative Triangularity ARC-class) is a design study for a compact, negative-triangularity fusion pilot plant (FPP). It is the closest published reference design to what Firefly Fusion is developing, though MANTA is a community academic study, not a Firefly design.

- **Configuration**: Pulsed, radiative, ELM-free tokamak with negative triangularity
- **Fusion power**: 450 MW
- **Plasma gain Q**: 11.5
- **Net electricity**: 90 MW (electricity gain factor ~2.4)
- **Overnight cost**: $3.4 billion (meets NASEM < $5B requirement)

## Key Technical Parameters

### Blanket Design
- **Type**: Liquid immersion FLiBe tank (2LiF-BeF₂ molten salt)
- **Configuration**: Toroidally continuous tank; FLiBe flows down and around the vacuum vessel
- **Dual purpose**: Tritium breeding AND neutron shielding
- **TBR**: 1.15 (tritium self-sufficient)
- **Blanket power multiplication factor**: 1.11
- **Modularity**: FLiBe tank and vacuum vessel function as a single replaceable assembly

### Magnets
- **Type**: REBCO HTS (all magnets)
- **TF field**: 11 T on-axis
- **TF coils**: Demountable, for access to FLiBe tank and VV
- **TF coil lifetime**: 3100 ± 400 MW·yr
- **PF coil lifetime**: ≥ 890 ± 40 MW·yr

### Heating System
- **Primary**: ICRF (Ion Cyclotron Range of Frequencies) minority heating
- **Power**: 40 MW auxiliary
- **Frequency**: 110 MHz (³He minority species at 2.5% fraction)
- **Note**: On-axis damping achievable with existing high-power tetrodes
- **No ECRH, NBI, or LH systems in the design**

### Operation Mode
- **Pulsed** with ~15 minute inductive pulses
- **Inter-pulse length**: 2 minutes
- **Current drive**: Inductive via large central solenoid (high aspect ratio provides CS space)
- **Power to SOL**: Only 23.5 MW (very low — enabled by NT L-mode edge)

### Neutron Shielding
- Integrated with FLiBe blanket (blanket serves as both breeder and shield)
- Iterative optimization of shielding and breeding performed together

## Relevance to Firefly Fusion

MANTA is the closest published NT tokamak reference design to Firefly's concept:
- Both target compact, high-field NT tokamaks
- Both use REBCO HTS magnets
- MANTA's ARC-class heritage aligns with Firefly's approach
- Key difference: MANTA is pulsed; Firefly's baseline CSV says "Continuous"
- Ball et al. (co-founder's group) analyzed ohmic-only versions of MANTA showing Q≈500 at ~1 GW fusion power

MANTA is NOT a Firefly design — it's a community reference study led by Rutherford et al. at MIT/Princeton. But it represents the state-of-the-art for NT reactor engineering.
