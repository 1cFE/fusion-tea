# Firefly Fusion Interview (GreyB / Scouted)

**Source**: https://greyb.com/blog/firefly-fusion-scouted-interview
**Accessed**: 2026-03-06

## Key Technical Specifications (from CEO Rustem Ospanov)

- **Major radius**: ~2 to 2.5 meters
- **Magnetic field**: 10-12 T (HTS superconducting magnets — commercial target)
- **Fusion gain**: Q > 5
- **Fusion power**: 50-100 MW
- **Heating input**: 20-30 MW
- **Plasma shape**: Negative triangularity (inverted "D" shape)

## Key Claims
- Compact design with negative triangularity
- Experiments in US (DIII-D) and Switzerland (TCV) validate the approach
- NT configuration stabilizes plasma edge and improves heat exhaust
- Can produce 50-100 MW fusion power with 5x less heating power than conventional approaches
- HTS superconducting magnets enable compact, high-field design

## Notes
- Interview discusses HTS magnets (10-12 T) as the target technology
- This is for the commercial concept; LUCIOLE prototype uses copper magnets
- Specific fuel isotope (D-T) not explicitly stated in this interview, but "superheated hydrogen plasma" mentioned
- No details on tritium breeding, energy capture, or blanket design
