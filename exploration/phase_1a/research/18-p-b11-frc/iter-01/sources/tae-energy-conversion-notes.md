# TAE Technologies Energy Conversion - Conflicting Sources
**Retrieved**: 2026-03-07

## Source 1: TAE FAQ (tae.com/faq-fusion/)
Describes THERMAL conversion:
"Hydrogen and boron atoms will fuse inside a stable, superhot plasma to produce energetic light. When that light heats the walls of the plasma confinement vessel, a network of pipes will transport that heat via working fluid to a steam generator. The steam spins a turbine which drives an electric generator, similar to conventional power plants."

## Source 2: Patent US7459654 (Tri Alpha Energy / UC / UF)
Describes Inverse Cyclotron Converter (ICC):
- Hollow cylinder electrodes decelerate alpha particles
- Magnetic cusp converts linear to circular motion
- Energy collected as ions spiral past quadrupole electrodes
- Maximum projected efficiency: 90%

## Source 3: Grokipedia / Technical Literature
ICC details:
- Tapering magnetic field decelerates alphas
- Segmented electrodes, ~5 MHz under 0.6 T fields
- >90% conversion efficiency target for 3 MeV alphas

## Source 4: New Atlas Interview
Mentions TWO approaches:
1. Steam turbine (for Da Vinci prototype initially)
2. "Solid-state energy capture" under early development — "like solar cells on steroids" converting soft X-rays directly to DC

## Assessment
TAE has patented direct energy conversion (ICC) and discusses it in technical contexts. Their public FAQ describes thermal/steam conversion. The New Atlas interview suggests Da Vinci may start with steam turbines, with direct conversion as a future upgrade. The "X-ray to DC" concept appears to be a third, even more speculative approach.

For the differentiation table, the long-term vision is clearly Direct (charged particle) via ICC, but near-term may use thermal conversion as a bridge.
