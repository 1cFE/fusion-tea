# Gauss Fusion CDR Expert Panel Review (January 2026)

**Source**: Startbase news + Gauss Fusion press releases
**URL**: https://www.startbase.com/news/expertengremium-bestaetigt-designkonzept-fuer-fusionskraftwerk-von-gauss-fusion/

## Panel Details
- **Chair**: Prof. Dr. Hartmut Zohm, Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics (IPP)
- **Size**: 13 European fusion and industry experts
- **Timing**: Review completed early 2026 (CDR submitted October 2025)

## Key Findings
- Panel praised Gauss Fusion's "holistic, systems engineering approach"
- Noted comprehensive plant perspective (not isolated technologies)
- CDR confirmed as credible basis for proceeding to engineering phase

## CDR Scope
- 1,000+ pages of technical detail
- Developed over 3 years with European industrial partners
- Covers: architecture, design basis, safety framework, qualification strategy,
  system engineering, lifecycle operations, radioactive waste

## Post-Review Status
- Gauss Fusion entered engineering/technical design phase
- Site mapping study completed (150 industrial clusters, 900 sites across Europe)
- EUR 15-18 billion estimated for first-of-a-kind plant by mid-2040s
