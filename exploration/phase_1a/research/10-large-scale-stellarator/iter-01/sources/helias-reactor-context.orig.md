# HELIAS Reactor Studies — Context for Gauss Fusion GIGA

## HSR4/18 (HELIAS Reactor, 4 field periods, R=18m)
- 40 non-planar superconducting modular coils (10 per field period)
- NbTi superconducting cables in original study
- Max magnetic field: ~10 T (original); GIGA upgrades to 12-13 T coil field
- Major radius: 18 m
- Fusion power: 3000 MW (thermal)
- Configuration: quasi-isodynamic (QI)
- Effective helical ripple < 1% across plasma cross-section

## Blanket Studies for HELIAS
- DCLL (Dual Coolant Lithium Lead) blanket studied by CIEMAT for HELIAS
- LiPb (lead-lithium eutectic) breeder: 84.3% Pb, 15.7% Li
- SiC inserts for MHD pressure drop reduction
- HELIAS-5B: helium-cooled pebble bed (HCPB) blanket also studied
- Breeding blanket sectors fit between encircling coils, sector-based maintenance

## W7-X → HELIAS → GIGA Lineage
- W7-X (Wendelstein 7-X) = first HELIAS-line stellarator
- W7-X: 5 field periods, R=5.5m, QI optimized
- HELIAS reactor studies: HSR5/22 (5 periods, R=22m) and HSR4/18 (4 periods, R=18m)
- GIGA matches HSR4/18 geometry with modern magnet technology upgrades
