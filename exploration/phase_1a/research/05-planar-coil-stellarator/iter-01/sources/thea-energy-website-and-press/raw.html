Fusion energy made faster and simpler

We’re reinventing the world’s energy supply with clean and abundant fusion power.

A practical approach to commercializing fusion

Thea Energy is creating a limitless source of zero emission energy for a sustainable future. We’re building the most economical and scalable fusion energy system utilizing arrays of mass manufacturable magnets and dynamic software controls.

Our approach eliminates the prohibitively complex 3D magnet coils required in prior generations of stellarator architectures. These modular coils were extremely difficult and expensive to manufacture, assemble, and maintain.

By transferring the complexity of 3D magnet coils to the software stack, we leverage the significant developments in computing and control systems. The result is a radically simplifying (and cost-reducing) new paradigm, with hardware systems that can be built in simpler, less expensive ways, while solving long-standing barriers to fusion energy.

Our first system is more than a science project. Eos is a neutron source stellarator that will achieve world leading fusion performance, near-term commercial operation, and de-risk Thea Energy’s first fusion power plant, Helios.

Why Fusion

Bedrock scientific basis

The stellarator is a well-studied magnetic confinement fusion technology and the only system that creates all required magnetic fields using highly efficient, superconducting coils.

Thea Energy’s breakthroughs radically transformed the stellarator, tailoring systems to be simpler and more economical than previously thought possible, while leveraging this strong scientific foundation.
