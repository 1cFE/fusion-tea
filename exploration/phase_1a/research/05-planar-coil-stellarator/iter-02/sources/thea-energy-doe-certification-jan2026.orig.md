# DOE Certification of Helios Preconceptual Design

**Source**: Thea Energy press release
**URL**: https://thea.energy/press-release/u-s-department-of-energy-certifies-thea-energys-fusion-pilot-plant-preconceptual-design/
**Date**: January 13, 2026
**Relevance**: DOE milestone certification, timeline updates

## Key Facts

- Thea Energy is the **first awardee company** to receive DOE certification of its pilot plant design under the Milestone-Based Fusion Development Program
- Certification followed review by independent fusion experts
- 200-page detailed report submitted to DOE

## Three Technical Innovations Highlighted

1. Software-controlled magnet coils adaptable to real-world conditions
2. Stellarator "divertor" exhaust system capable of fusion power operations (world's first)
3. Stellarator sector-based maintenance scheme enabling long-term plant viability

## Eos Updates

- 50% fewer coils than originally proposed (optimization breakthrough)
- Now requires only one size shaping coil
- Site selection expected 2026 (5 states in consideration)
- Online by 2030
- Purpose: power-plant-relevant, steady-state fusion

## Helios Timeline

- Operational in the 2030s (following Eos)
