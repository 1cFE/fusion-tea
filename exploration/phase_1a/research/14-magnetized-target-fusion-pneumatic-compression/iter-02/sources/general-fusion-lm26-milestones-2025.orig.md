# General Fusion LM26 Milestones (2025)

## Timeline

- **Aug 2023**: LM26 announced
- **Late 2024**: LM26 assembly complete
- **March 2025**: First plasma achieved — forming plasmas daily
- **April 29, 2025**: First plasma compression with lithium liner achieved
  - Ion temperature and density increases observed
  - Lithium liner effectively trapped plasma's magnetic field
- **May 2025**: Metal Tech News coverage of lithium compression results

## Technical Details

- PI3 plasma injector: energy confinement time >10 ms (confirmed by peer review)
- LM26 compression uses electromagnetic theta-pinch of solid lithium liner (surrogate for commercial pneumatic pistons)
- Pioneered method for creating solid lithium rings for compression experiments
- LM26 is 50% scale of commercial plasma

## Commercial Targets

- **Power output**: 300 MWe (~150,000 Canadian homes)
- **Repetition rate**: ~1 Hz (once per second)
- **Compression**: Pneumatic pistons + liquid metal (not electromagnetic as in LM26)
