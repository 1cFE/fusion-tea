# Comparison of Fuel Cycles for Lead-Lithium and Pure Lithium Liquid Metal Walls in a Magnetized Target Fusion Power Plant

**Source**: Fusion Science and Technology, 2025
**DOI**: 10.1080/15361055.2025.2526266
**URL**: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/15361055.2025.2526266

## Key Findings (from search result abstracts)

- General Fusion is developing a commercial fusion power plant based on MTF
- Commercial design: spherical torus of D-T plasma in a large (~4 m diameter) cavity formed in liquid metal
- Cavity collapsed by an array of pneumatic piston drivers
- **Two candidate liquid metal compositions being evaluated**: lead-lithium eutectic (LLE) and pure lithium (Li)
- Paper assesses impact on tritium fuel cycle for each option

### Tritium Inventory Differences
- LLE design: >80% of in-process tritium inventory in isotope separation system
- Li design: >60% of in-process tritium inventory in blanket material itself
- Different extraction technologies required for each

## Significance for Dossier

Confirms that the liquid metal composition for the commercial plant has NOT been finalized — both Li and PbLi remain under consideration as of 2025. This matches the existing dossier note about composition uncertainty.

Also confirms:
- "Pneumatic piston drivers" terminology for the commercial design
- ~4 m diameter cavity (commercial scale)
- D-T fuel cycle
- Liquid metal serves as both compression medium and tritium breeder
