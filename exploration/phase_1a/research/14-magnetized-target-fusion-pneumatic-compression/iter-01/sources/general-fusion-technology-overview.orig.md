# General Fusion Technology Overview

Source: https://generalfusion.com/fusion-technology/ (fetched 2026-03-07)

## Key Technical Details

- Uses Magnetized Target Fusion (MTF) — combines magnetic confinement and inertial compression
- Plasma: Compact toroid (spherical tokamak configuration) injected into compression chamber
- First compact toroid plasma injector at power plant scale built in 2010
- 24 prototypes and over 200,000 plasma experiments
- Liquid metal: Liquid lithium (commercial design uses liquid metal wall)
- Liquid lithium brought together with plasma in 2019
- Wall designed to: absorb neutrons, protect machine, breed fuel, provide heat transfer
- 34 peer-reviewed publications, 210 patents issued/pending

## LM26 (Lawson Machine 26)
- Launched 2023, designed/built/assembled in under 2 years
- Located in Richmond, British Columbia
- Targets: 1 keV → 10 keV → 100% Lawson criterion
- Energy confinement time exceeding 10 milliseconds demonstrated
- Designed to achieve 10 keV (100M °C) by 2025
- Progress toward scientific breakeven equivalent by 2026
