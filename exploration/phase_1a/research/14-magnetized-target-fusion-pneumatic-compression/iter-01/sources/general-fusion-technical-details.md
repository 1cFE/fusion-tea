# General Fusion Technical Details (Compiled)

Sources: Multiple (see citations below), fetched 2026-03-07

## Compression System
- Commercial design: Steam-driven pistons compress liquid metal liner
- LM26 demo: Electromagnetic coils compress solid lithium liner (theta-pinch)
- LM26 coils: 2 meters diameter, 11,000 kg, 18 MJ of electrical current
- Prototype Zero (P0): 1:4 scale of LM26, 48 coil turns, up to 1 MJ energy
- P0 compression: 0.7-1 ms at radial velocities >300 m/s
- Target compression ratio needed: 12 (achieved ~8 in water tests with <10% perturbation)
Source: COMSOL story, Fusion Conclusion blog, Hackaday

## Plasma Type
- Shifted from Field-Reversed Configuration to spherical tokamak target
- Marshall gun (coaxial plasma gun) forms the compact toroid
- Self-stable compact toroid configuration
- Best pre-compression results: ~400 eV, density 6×10¹⁹/m³, confinement ~10-12 ms
Source: Fusion Conclusion blog, Hackaday

## Compression Results (Peer-reviewed, Nuclear Fusion journal)
- First compression of spherical tokamak plasma with collapsing metal liner
- Neutron yield: >600 million neutrons/second in one shot
- Ion temperature: ~0.63 keV during compression
- Plasma density: ~190× increase during compression
- Magnetic field: >13× increase during compression
Source: General Fusion press release (2025)

## Liquid Metal
- Original design: Lead-lithium (PbLi)
- Current/demo design: Pure lithium (lower plasma radiation losses)
- Commercial plant may return to lead-lithium for materials compatibility
- Tritium breeding ratio target: ~1.5
Source: Fusion Conclusion blog, Wikipedia/search results

## Repetition Rate
- Commercial target: ~1 Hz (once per second)
- Current LM26: approximately one cycle per day
Source: COMSOL story, Hackaday

## Energy Conversion
- Liquid metal captures heat → heat exchanger → steam → turbine → electricity
- Thermal (steam) cycle
Source: Interesting Engineering, search results

## Tritium Breeding
- Liquid lithium breeds tritium via neutron capture
- Liquid metal wall serves dual purpose: breeding + shielding
Source: General Fusion website, search results

## Lab Experiments (MTF field, not just General Fusion)
- LANL: FRX-L (FRC-Liner) experiment — theta pinch FRC compressed by metal liner Z-pinch
- LANL + AFRL: Metal liner compression of FRC plasmas
- NRL: Linus experiment (1970s-80s) — rotating liquid metal liner compression
- HyperJet Fusion: Plasma Jet Driven MIF (PJMIF) — 36-gun demo on PLX at LANL
Source: LANL MTF page, ARPA-E, search results

## Timeline
- Commercial deployment target: early to mid-2030s
- Single MTF plant target: power for ~150,000 homes
