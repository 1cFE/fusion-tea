ABOUT US

The Team and Technology for an Energy Abundant World

HB11 Energy is developing fusion as a safe, green baseload power source while commercialising core fusion technologies. The laser and laser targets businesses, through scale, allow for faster improvement in fusion technology cost and performance.

Not dependent on intermittent renewables or dangerous & difficult radioactive fuels, Laser Boron Fusion is the long-term solution for climate and ever increasing demand for power. The viable alternative to conventional uranium nuclear power.

As rapidly advancing laser technology accelerates the timeline for commercial fusion energy, our global team of fusion experts, engineers and award-winning physicists are making it a reality.

Executive Team

Dr. Warren McKenzie

Managing Director

Managing Director of HB11 Energy, Warren McKenzie, PhD (Materials Science / Nanotechnology) is a science entrepreneur, materials scientist and Fellow of the Royal Society of New South Wales. He has held two post-doctorate research positions, at Trinity College in Dublin and at the University of NSW.

BIO

Greg Ainsworth

Chief Operating Officer

A former media executive, policy advisor, and technology investor, Greg’s expertise is in building organisations as they grow from $0 to $100 million. As Senior VP at AMC Networks in New York, Greg launched TV service, AMC+ (amcplus.com) alongside leading a team managing 35 ad-supported TV streaming apps. Earlier in his career he ran a health services business in New York, and for 2.5 years worked as a policy advisor in the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet in Australia.

BIO

Dr. Sergey Pikuz

Chief Technology Officer

Dr. Pikuz leads the technology team at HB11 Energy. Dr. Pikuz’s research background is in experimental high energy density physics (HEDP) with high-power optical lasers, X-ray free electron lasers and X-ray plasma diagnostics. Having led international contributions to major laser user facilities, and with a long career of “suitcase science”, Dr. Pikuz maintains an exceptional global network of laser engineers, and physicists.

Technology Leadership

Prof. Tom Mehlhorn

Lead Theoretician

Prof. Mehlhorn leads HB11 Energy’s theoretical physics effort, developing our understanding of fusion ignition and burn. Prof. Mehlhorn is an Adjunct Professor at the Nuclear Engineering & Radiological Sciences Department, University of Michigan

BIO

Dr. Max Tabak

Consultant Scientist

Dr. Tabak is a key contributor in HB11 Energy’s fuel physics program. He is  broadly experienced and a highly esteemed expert in inertial fusion physics. He is a  winner of the Edward Teller Award in 2005, and joint winner of the John Dawson Award in 2006. Max’s manuscript, Ignition and High Gain with Ultrapowerful Lasers, is the #1 most cited in the history of laser fusion.

Board of Directors

Dr. Warren McKenzie

Managing Director

Co-founder and Managing Director of HB11 Energy, Warren McKenzie, PhD (Materials Science / Nanotechnology) is a science entrepreneur, materials scientist and Fellow of the Royal Society of New South Wales. BIO

Prof. Heinrich Hora

Non-Executive Director

Co-founder of HB11 and globally renowned physicist, Hora is Professor Emeritus in Theoretical Physics of the University of New South Wales (UNSW Sydney), Hora has made contributions to solid state physics, optical properties of plasma with relativistic and quantum effects and nonlinear dynamics with applications of lasers for producing nuclear fusion energy. It was Heinrich who in the 1970s predicted that the HB11 approach would become viable when laser technology evolved to its current state.

As Prof. Hora moves into retirement, he is represented on the board by his daughter, Dorle Minikin.
