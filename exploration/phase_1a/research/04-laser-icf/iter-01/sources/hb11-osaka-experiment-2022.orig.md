# HB11 Energy - Osaka University LFEX Experiment (2022)
**Paper**: "In-Target Proton-Boron Nuclear Fusion Using a PW-Class Laser"
**Published**: Applied Sciences, vol. 12(3), 1444 (2022)
**DOI**: https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/12/3/1444
**Lead scientists**: Dimitri Batani (HB11 Lead Scientist), Daniele Margarone
**Facility**: LFEX petawatt laser, Institute of Laser Engineering, Osaka University, Japan
**Funding**: Czech Republic Ministry of Education + EU EUROfusion consortium

## Key Results
- Nuclear reactions between protons and boron-11 nuclei initiated in laser-generated plasma
- Laser: PW-class, relativistic intensities (~3 × 10^19 W/cm²)
- Target: 0.2 mm thick boron nitride (BN)
- Measured alpha particle flux: ~10^10/sr
- 10× more fusion reactions than previous results at same facility using "pitcher-catcher" geometry
- HB11 claims "world-first 'material' number of fusion reactions by a private company"

## Significance
- Demonstrated p-B11 fusion reactions driven by petawatt laser
- Still ~4 orders of magnitude away from net energy gain
- "In-target" geometry (direct irradiation) proved more efficient than "pitcher-catcher" (separate proton acceleration)

## Context
- This was NOT a demonstration of the full HB11 reactor concept (no kilotesla magnetic field, no direct conversion)
- It demonstrated the fundamental p-B11 reaction driven by ultrashort laser pulses
