# HB11 Energy - Technology Page
**Source**: https://hb11.energy/our-technology/
**Fetched**: 2026-03-07

## Key Technical Details

- **Approach**: Inertial Confinement Fusion (ICF) with Fast Ignition
- **Laser system**: "Thousands of commercial lasers" rather than single massive laser; two-pulse system using nanosecond and picosecond pulses
- **Fuel**: Hydrogen-Boron (p-11B); reaction creates three helium ions (alpha particles) releasing 8.7 MeV
- **Pellet injection rate**: ~1 per second
- **Energy density**: "A million times denser than oil"
- **Method**: "Focused energy accelerates protons and initiates fusion within a compressed fuel pellet"
- **Power generation**: "Conventional steam cycle generator"
- **Energy recycling**: "A portion of the energy produced is recycled into the laser system"

## Notable
- The technology page (as of 2025) explicitly states "conventional steam cycle generator" for power generation
- This contrasts with earlier patent (US10410752B2) and papers describing direct electrostatic conversion
- The page calls their approach "Proton Fast Ignition" — reducing temperature and energy requirements
