# HB11 Energy - Patent US10410752B2 / US20170125129A1
**Source**: https://patents.google.com/patent/US20170125129A1/en
**Title**: "Method for Generating Electrical Energy by Laser-Based Nuclear Fusion and Laser Reactor"
**Granted**: 2018
**Fetched**: 2026-03-07

## Reactor Geometry
- **Outer vessel**: Stainless steel spherical wall, 10 mm thick, diameter >= 1 m
- **Fuel**: Solid-state cylindrical HB11 body, 1 cm length x 0.2 mm diameter
- **Fuel positioning**: Held along magnetic field axis via quartz fibers
- **Cover layer**: Material with atomic weight >100 (e.g., silver), thickness ~5 microns

## Laser Specifications

### Fusion Laser (Picosecond)
- Duration: <5 ps (picoseconds)
- Power: >1 petawatt
- Intensity: >=10^17 W/cm²
- Contrast ratio: >=10^6
- Energy per pulse example: 30 kJ (= 30 PW for 1 ps)

### Magnetic Field Laser (Nanosecond)
- Duration: <20 ns
- Energy: >100 J

## Magnetic Field Generation
- **Method**: Laser-driven capacitor-coil target (two parallel nickel plates, 2 mm thick, 3 cm wide, connected by two coil windings)
- **Field strength**: >=1 kT (kilotesla); examples cite 4.5 kT and 10 kT
- **Purpose**: Radial trapping of the cylindrical reaction to prevent losses

## Energy Conversion — Direct Electrostatic
- **Method**: Direct electrostatic conversion of alpha particles
- **Bias voltage**: Reaction chamber at -1.4 MV relative to outer spherical wall
- **Faraday cage**: Between reaction chamber and outer wall to shield static field
- **Alpha capture**: Double-charged alpha particles pass through Faraday cage mesh, release kinetic energy on spherical energy conversion device
- **Output**: 714 Amperes discharge current via HVDC transmission

## Performance Targets
- Energy gain: >500 (enhanced: >1000) per laser energy expended
- Energy per reaction: ~1 GJ (~280 kWh)
- Repetition rate: 1 reaction per second
- Sequential loading of magnetic field devices for continuous/quasi-continuous generation
