# HB11 Energy - New Atlas Article (2020)

**Source**: https://newatlas.com/energy/hb11-hydrogen-boron-fusion-clean-energy/
**Fetched**: 2026-03-07

## Key Technical Details

### Reactor Design
- "A largely empty metal sphere, where a modestly sized HB11 fuel pellet is held in the center, with apertures on different sides for the two lasers."
- One laser establishes magnetic containment field
- Second laser triggers 'avalanche' fusion chain reaction

### Energy Conversion — DIRECT (contradicts current website)
- "The alpha particles generated by the reaction would create an electrical flow that can be channeled almost directly into an existing power grid **with no need for a heat exchanger or steam turbine generator**."
- "The reaction produces naked helium atoms, they don't have electrons, so they have a positive charge. We just have to collect that charge."
- Charged particles collected on a metal plate to generate electrical current directly

### Laser
- Petawatt-class lasers required

### Performance Claims (early)
- "Reaction rates a billion times higher than predicted" (early simulation results)

## Notes
- This 2020 article describes **direct conversion** consistent with the 2018 patent, explicitly stating NO steam turbine needed.
- The current (2025) HB11 website says "conventional steam cycle generator" — significant design evolution.
- The New Atlas article may reflect older messaging from HB11 when the direct conversion concept was the baseline.
