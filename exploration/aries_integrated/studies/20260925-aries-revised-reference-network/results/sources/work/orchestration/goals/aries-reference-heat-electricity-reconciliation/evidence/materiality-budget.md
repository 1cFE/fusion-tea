# Materiality budget — declared before refinement studies

[AGENT] Declared 2026-09-25 under T-003, before any refinement or attribution study runs. Revised the same day after the fresh source-check review r1; corrections are marked `[r1]`. It is derived from the printed precision of the source and the purpose of the assessment (explain a ≈ 204 MW net difference by subsystem). It is not to be relaxed afterwards to accept an observed residual; a residual larger than the budget stays material and must be attributed, bounded or reported unresolved.

| Quantity | Budget | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Net electric power | ±15 MW | Purpose-driven attribution resolution, not source precision `[r1]`: Table IV pins 1000 and 1253 to the MW and 0.43 × 2916 reproduces 1253 within 1 MW, so the source itself supports ≈ ±2 MW. The ±15 MW line is the size below which a subsystem cause is not worth separating in this assessment; it equals ±0.5 percentage point of the two-figure 43% constant on 2916 MW (±14.6 MW). |
| Gross electric power | ±15 MW | Same basis; purpose-driven above the ≈ ±2 MW the source supports `[r1]`. |
| Total thermal power to the cycle | ±10 MW | Table IV prints 2916 MW to the MW; the model's 2917.8 MW is inside this. The composition ambiguity (which auxiliary power the source returns as heat, up to ≈ 60 MW) is not absorbed here: it is carried as an explicit bounded ledger item with its own effect on gross (≈ 0.43 × 60 ≈ 26 MW). |
| Heat delivered per circuit (He, PbLi, divertor) | ±10 MW each | Derived `[r1]`. Scaling rule for Lyon-case comparison values: deposition, conducted exchange and nuclear shares scale linearly with fusion power (× 2436/2365 = 1.0300); friction terms (141, 24 MW) are held at printed values because primary flows are held. Source ambiguity under that rule: printed rounding ±1 MW; the 1 MW blanket residual; whether the 111 MW exchange scales (3.3 MW on He and PbLi); whether friction scales (4.2 MW He, 0.7 MW divertor); the unlabelled ≈ 67 MW divertor term's scaling (2 MW). The helium items sum to ≈ 9.5 MW and the PbLi items to ≈ 5.3 MW `[r2]`; the ±10 MW line holds with little margin on helium and is stated as a purpose-driven resolution at that level. The scaling rule itself is a comparison convention, stated, not a source fact. |
| Unremoved (unmet) heat | 0 MW above the ledger tolerance (2.24e-6 MW) | The reference assumes all thermal power is converted; any positive unmet heat is a material discrepancy that must be corrected, explained or bounded, never tolerated. |
| Turbine inlet temperature | ±5 K about 708 °C | Anchored to printed values `[r1]`: PbLi outlet 738 °C (Table II) minus the 30 °C hot/cold-leg difference (Table III) = 708 °C; Fig. 12's 707 °C is the same value drawn as typical. ±5 K covers the printed rounding of both terms. |

Source ambiguities are bounded items, not tolerance, and are listed explicitly in the contract and the ledger: L-thermal (which auxiliary power the systems paper returns as heat, up to ≈ 60 MW thermal); L-divertor (the ≈ 67 MW unlabelled share of Raffray Table V's 186 MW, presumed neutron heating `[r1]`); Fig. 12 typical temperatures; the missing cycle flow; the 253-versus-252 MW itemisation of recirculating power `[r1]`.
