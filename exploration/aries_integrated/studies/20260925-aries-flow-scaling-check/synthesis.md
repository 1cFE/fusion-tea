# Executor synthesis — 20260925-aries-flow-scaling-check

[AGENT] Executor reading by the round-3 coordinator.

## What the study set out to do

Quantify the cross-paper mapping artefact the Q1 thermal-cycle review found (duties scaled to 2436 MW, primary flows held at Raffray's 2365 MW values) and narrow the cycle-flow threshold for complete heat removal with the network.

## What it found

- The artefact is real and ≈ 34 MW on the PbLi stage, but most of it moves rather than disappears: with all three flows scaled the 1600 kg/s network case's unremoved heat falls by only 14.195 MW (to 95.681) because the helium stage, bounded by its 456 °C hot inlet, takes 20.091 MW more of the shortfall as the heater inlet rises with the turbine inlet. Net improves by 12.757 MW to 892.449; the case remains not steady.
- The helium flow itself is irrelevant to the helium-stage limit (−0.3 MW); the hot-inlet bound is what binds, as the Q1 review said.
- Complete removal at 0.95 recuperation needs more than 1650 kg/s and at most 1700 kg/s with the network, for either mapping; the 1700 kg/s steady case is unchanged in plant outputs by the flow scaling.

## Findings carried forward

#1 (artefact size and migration), #2 (threshold bracket 1650–1700). #3 is a declared seam; #4 a process note.

## What the record does not support

Any reproduction of the reference; a finer threshold; any hydraulic or cost claim.
