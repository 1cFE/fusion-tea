## Checks

1. Mechanism, by reading (not executing). Package: `exploration/aries_integrated/native_completions/lifecycle/lifecycle_cashflow_accounts_impl.py:37-38,47` sums burn + loss + decay with Python `sum` in float64, order (burn + loss) + decay, then `external_shortfall = max(makeup − annual_feed, 0.)` and `curtailed_feed = max(annual_feed − makeup, 0.)`; `exploration/aries_integrated/native_completions/equipment/annual_selected_fuel_impl.py:13` forms the same float sum. Checker: `exploration/aries_integrated/studies/lifecycle_oracle.py` computes `gross_feed = d(burn) + d(loss) + d(decay)` in Decimal, the same left-to-right order, takes the same two max(·, 0) differences and converts to float on return; `exploration/aries_integrated/studies/lifecycle_bindings.py:21-23,44` binds them to the four channels and feeds `new_feed_kg` from the stored `annual_recovery_kg`. When the stored feed equals the package's float sum exactly, the package side is exactly 0.0 and the Decimal side exposes the float sum's rounding error. Two roundings bound that error at about one ulp of 139 kg/year, 2.8e-14 kg/year; the observed 6.8e-15 lies inside. Confirmed numerical, not a modelling difference.

The package itself already reconciles this quantity with an absolute floor of 1e-12 (`lifecycle_cashflow_accounts_impl.py:39`), consistent with the class.

3. Completeness. The lifecycle comparison catalog (`lifecycle_bindings.py:68-72`) is FIELDS + FLAGS for `lifecycle` and `source_lifecycle`, plus price `lcoe`, `crf`, `levelized`, `annual_energy` and boundary `years`. The only max(·, 0) differences of makeup and feed in FIELDS are `external_shortfall` and `curtailed_feed`; two owners give the four channels. `gross_makeup` carries the same rounding at relative 5e-17 and passes. FLAGS are constants on both sides (`lifecycle_bindings.py:25`; `lifecycle_cashflow_accounts_impl.py:47`), so verdict parity holds by construction. Both rounding directions are covered: feed above the exact sum shows on `curtailed_feed` (the observed case), feed below it on `external_shortfall`.

## Findings

"Sum burn + loss + decay in different orders" names the wrong cause. Replace with: "the package sums in float64 and the checker in Decimal, so the package's float sum carries a rounding error of at most about one ulp (≈ 3e-14 kg/year at 139 kg/year), which the checker exposes at exact feed equality". Same class, same bound, correct cause. Amend the declaration and all four manifest entries.
