## Assessment and dispositions

1. [AGENT] **Sensitivity framing passes.** Both declared groups are valid, include their single authoritative causal key and have no external ties. Both reach computed net power; neither reaches the held efficiency-times-gain heuristic. `no_constraint_response` is false for both. Runbook step 4's explicit special owner-ruling trigger therefore does not apply. Reachability establishes no response sign, monotonicity or boundary. The protocol preserves those distinctions.

2. [AGENT] **The planned response window is appropriately bounded.** The oracle's power identity factors net generation into beam energy times repetition rate times a coefficient determined by held gain, efficiencies and blanket multiplication. Positive beam/rate changes need not change its sign. The candidate scan and engineered provenance are appropriate for sensitivity, with no optimization or engineering-bound claim. Disposition: execute and retain the proposed scan, then record the selected window and observed edges before running the response proposals, as already planned. Scan disagreement with the premise requires the protocol's stated revision and re-critique.

3. [AGENT] **Held inputs and fixed diagnostics are coherent.** Use the complete manifest baseline for ordinary cases, overriding only the named beam or rate. The oracle's `counterexample` overrides efficiency to 0.1, gain to 100, blanket multiplication to 0.6 and thermal efficiency to 0.3. Its `zero` case overrides efficiency to 0.1, gain to 100, blanket multiplication to 1, rate to 5 Hz and thermal efficiency to 0.2. In particular, the zero case's rate differs from the ordinary 4.6 Hz anchor. Disposition: preserve the protocol's case roles and record the complete effective inputs; these coordinated fixed diagnostics do not enlarge the declared sensitivity axes or justify a response claim about efficiency or gain.

4. [AGENT] **The direct API route fits.** Five unique one-factor cases plus two coordinated diagnostics form a prepared list rather than one Cartesian product. `StudyRunner` with `PreparedListStrategy` is expressly supported by the current runbook, steps 8–9. The older policy § 6 grid-only list does not require a new route decision: the reviewed plan follows the current native route contract. Disposition: establish actual loadability through baseline execution and preflight, then record the exercised route rationale. No sweep arithmetic or solve belongs in study glue.

5. [AGENT] **Price and verdict semantics pass.** Hawker mixed-basis $/MWh and Meier 1988 cents/kWh remain separate. The named net predicate, generating flag and finite positive price determine price eligibility. Both named verdicts determine reported whole-model feasibility. A satisfied heuristic cannot rescue a violated net gate, and a non-generating zero sentinel cannot become a price observation. Disposition: keep both verdicts and both generating flags in all case evidence; exclude diagnostics from the ordinary response summary as planned.

6. [AGENT] **Verification and review plans pass.** Verifying all seven cases covers every observed verdict combination without sampling omissions. Thirty channels, independently bound predicate operands and annual cash flows provide a meaningful check. The integer-year oracle scope and retained handwritten final quotient are disclosed. The oracle shares selected inputs and source assumptions with the audited model; agreement does not independently validate those assumptions or engineering capacity. Disposition: retain both oracle files and numerical verification evidence, record applicable 1costingFE comparison coverage or its applicability limit under policy § 10, and complete the planned correctness, honesty and readability outcomes before freezing the record.
