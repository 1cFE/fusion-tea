# WI-098 whole-plant conversion design

Submission r2 resolves review F1–F3 through explicit overhead accounts, one source-input migration and separate D/Li6 purchase equations. No implementation is authorized by this document alone. Decisions below are [AGENT] design choices unless explicitly marked inherited. Requirements are in [spec.md](spec.md); quantities, ownership and proposed binding names are in [configuration.md](configuration.md).

## 1. Result and premise

Build an additive native plant assembly that keeps the repaired steam and Brayton conversion models and adds a shared supplied reactor, primary equipment, fuel system, station power and lifecycle accounts. Both branches consume the same source and fixed reactor inventory. The model calculates both complete plant LCOEs and net exports; study code selects and reports supported rows.

The result is conditional economics for a supplied thermal source. It is not a validated plasma operating point. A new 48 kA magnet scenario changes axis field to 8.64 T and peak field to 23.904 T. The source is independently supplied at 2500 or 2800 MW before recovery of primary circulation work. No confinement or sustainment claim follows from this pairing. `source_qualified=0` must survive every report, together with the assumed source and blanket transport authority.

This distinction cannot waive component failures. The original magnet has inadequate fit and current capacity. The exact 50 kA larger-pack probe repaired those two checks but remained outside the conductor field domain and failed cryoplant capacities. That offer stays failed in [evidence/magnet-probe/report.md](evidence/magnet-probe/report.md). The present design selects a different, explicit in-domain 48 kA offer and a larger priced cryoplant. Independent review precedes its native evaluation. If its component checks fail, it cannot rank. The inherited 3000 MW primary pressure and divertor failures likewise remain exclusions.

The wider casing still lacks a global manufactured-fit/structural qualification. A named common source installation allowance prices that unresolved scope; it does not change a physical failure into a pass. Reports must distinguish conditional source authority, supported component calculations and unqualified global reactor construction. A conclusion may compare conversion choices under these assumptions; it may not label the complete reactor empirically feasible.

## 4. Fuel and complete electrical boundary

Reuse native fuel flow and full startup inventory calculations with the parameters in configuration.md. For fusion reaction rate `F=P_fusion*1e6/(17.58*MeV_J)`, tritium consumption is `F*m_T`, circulating injection is `F/burn_fraction`, and unrecovered exhaust follows the inherited recycle calculation. Selected stock is 5 kg; assert it exceeds full calculated startup need. Processing capacity is independently selected at 0.00015 kg D+T/s. Stock purchase uses selected 5 kg, not calculated minimum.

At availability `A`, annual gross tritium need is `A*seconds_year*(burn_mass+loss_mass)+selected_stock*lambda*seconds_year`. Annual usable breeding is `A*seconds_year*TBR*F*m_T*extraction_efficiency`. External supply is `max(gross-breeding,0)`; surplus receives no revenue. The lower TBR remains a sensitivity and a separate self-sufficiency failure where applicable. External supply is an explicit assumed purchased feed, not demonstrated breeding.

Define productive annual reactions `R=A*seconds_year*F`, burn fraction `b`, and common exhaust recovery `rho`. One D atom burns per fusion reaction; assume unburnt D shares the T exhaust recovery. Purchased D mass is `M_D=R*m_D*(1+(1-b)/b*(1-rho))`. There is no D decay. For breeder feed, select a conservative proxy: every gross bred T atom consumes one purchased Li6 atom, including the small Li7 contribution represented in total TBR. `M_Li6=R*TBR*m_Li6`, with `m_Li6=6.015122795*1.66053906892e-27 kg`, inherited from `1costingfe/src/costingfe/layers/physics.py:15–20`. Assume no additional loss of untransmuted breeder lithium; this is a zero-loss assumption, not a qualified recovery plant. Li6 purchase is pure-isotope-equivalent replenishment at the declared unit price; no extra enrichment multiplier is applied. Lower extraction increases purchased T but does not reduce Li6 consumption, since lithium was consumed before T extraction. Lower exhaust recovery increases purchased D/T but does not increase neutron-driven breeding.

Annual Li6 feed replaces consumed atoms and keeps the selected breeder composition/inventory constant between maintenance events. Initial PbLi and each full event refill purchase the full selected alloy inventory; event removal discards the maintained remaining inventory, with no salvage credit. Those stocks do not provide a credit against continuous transmutation makeup, so the same consumed atom is never bought in both streams. The proxy's extra Li7-as-Li6 charge is conservative and exposed. Do not add the legacy aggregate DT fuel cost or its blended burn/recovery multiplier. Native checks expose `D_purchased_atoms-D_burned_atoms-D_lost_atoms=0`, `T_external+T_usable-T_need-T_surplus=0`, and `Li6_makeup_atoms-T_gross_bred_atoms=0`; sweeps of TBR, extraction and recovery must preserve them.

For branch `b`, `P_net_b=P_conversion_net_b-P_primary_electric-P_heating_wall-P_coil_drive-P_refrigeration-P_TF_cooling-P_fuel_vacuum-P_house-P_reactor_controls-P_aux_rejection-P_source_allowance`. Existing conversion net already subtracts local pumps, cooling water, controller and shaft import. Gas compressor work is already inside its shaft balance. No term is subtracted twice.

Auxiliary heat rejection is a separately selected common package. Its load is the heating wall-plug loss, refrigerator/TF/fuel/house/control/source-allowance electricity, cold/intercept extracted heat, primary motor losses, and its own electric draw. Coil-drive electricity remains subtracted once from exported power, but its lead/joint heat is already included in cold/intercept extracted heat and receives no second thermal addition. Refrigerator heat to the sink is `refrigeration_MW+cold_MW+intercept_W*1e-6`, initially2.6064868550919478MW. These loads go to that sink; they are not silently added to the conversion condenser or source heat. Its 200 MW thermal rating and 2 MW electric draw at a conditional 25°C site water supply are independent offers, priced at USD2025 50 million including connection and installation. An inherited auxiliary cooling residual is replaced by this offer. Assert duty <= rating and offered conditions match. Quote validity and water availability remain assumptions, with explicit price/duty sensitivities.

During outages, use calculated scenario refrigeration, house load and 2 MW auxiliary-rejection electricity as the selected standby demand; set other equipment off as an operating assumption. Annual export is `8760*A*P_net`; annual imported energy is `8760*(1-A)*P_standby`; also expose their difference. Value imports at USD2025 50/MWh, with 0/50/150 scenarios. LCOE uses discounted exported energy and includes imports as an expense. Nonpositive operating net or nonpositive annual net grid delivery cannot rank. Report both energy quantities so this convention cannot hide imports.

## 5. Disjoint costs and finance

All amounts are USD2025. configuration.md lists every account and its authority. Legacy amounts without a defensible price year become explicitly assumed new USD2025 quotes equal to those numbers; they are not presented as inflation-corrected evidence. Primary equipment and ARIES conversion factors retain their documented CPI treatment. Magnet rates are explicitly requoted and its detailed capture records the change. Price stress ranges are assumptions, not statistical confidence limits.

The native assembly contains readable `Costed Component` leaves for each major reactor scope, each branch conversion purchase group, selected fuel stocks, extra source installation and auxiliary rejection. `source_accounts` sums those leaves, exposes category subtotals and asserts reconciliation. Branch ledgers reference their exclusive conversion leaves. Each branch also owns calculated costed leaves for contingency, indirect engineering/construction services, freight, general spares, tax, construction insurance and nonfuel commissioning. Their equations and exact membership sets are in configuration.md. Existing core installation purchases direct assembly labor; CAS30 purchases engineering, project management and construction support. They are distinct from construction finance. No old whole-plant aggregate is copied.

The overhead convention follows the inherited generic-MFE ordering: contingency on pre-contingency direct costs, indirect services on post-contingency direct costs with construction-time scaling. Freight exclusions retain delivered transport and field-labor distinctions. Supplementary subaccounts are rebuilt individually. Initial T stock replaces the old startup-fuel allowance; a new nonfuel commissioning allowance covers test labor and nonfuel consumables. The dated terminal event replaces the old initial decommissioning provision. Explicit primary and salt spares are excluded from general spares. Overhead rates and membership are new declared estimate assumptions where quote scopes are uncertain. They never change selected equipment quantities.

One part owns finance: `finance.rate=0.05`, `finance.years=30`, `finance.availability=0.80`, `finance.construction_years=8`. Require `years` to be a positive integer; fractional operational horizons fail preparation and a native domain predicate. Construction years and event times may be real. Bind both `steam_ledger.{rate,years,availability}` and `gas_ledger.{rate,years,availability}` to those attributes. Retire the six old input keys from the new generated public inputs. The migration maps old complete points only when their paired rate/years/availability agree; disagreement fails ingestion. Conversion-control replay supplies common availability 0.85; main whole-plant cases use 0.80. All conversion replacement PV therefore uses the same rate/horizon as upstream events.

Let `C0_b` be all initial plant purchases, initial stocks, owner and installation scopes plus rebuilt overhead accounts for branch b. Construction finance is `C0_b*(1+r)^(construction_years/2)` as an explicit midpoint assumption. Future replacement PV is added once and never construction-financed. Initial project overheads do not repeat on replacement events: those event quotes are explicitly assumed inclusive of their own delivery, tax and execution overhead, with removal/labor amounts charged as stated. Annual expense PV is `sum_{y=1..N} annual_expense/(1+r)^y`; energy PV is `sum_{y=1..N} annual_export/(1+r)^y`. Terminal dismantling less salvage is discounted at N. `LCOE_b=PV_cost_b/PV_export_b` in USD/MWh. Use the currency-neutral DCF helper only if its construction argument is zero after initial finance has been applied; otherwise implement these finite sums directly.

Retain the existing conversion service/makeup/replacement streams once, extracting their components rather than adding their full LCOE or accounted PV. Existing 20% year-15 generic conversion replacement excludes explicitly replaced machines/bundles and stocks as before. Explicit upstream replacements are blanket/divertor at irradiation life, magnets at 10 full-power years, primary active machines every 10 calendar years, and a 5% year-20 overhaul of the remaining eligible reactor equipment. The latter excludes buildings/land/stocks, magnet, blanket/divertor, primary machines and all conversion equipment. Event scopes and removal/refill allowances are in configuration.md.

For component full-power life L, event times are `t_k=k*L/A`, retaining only `0<t_k<N`. Calendar replacements similarly exclude an event exactly at N. Blanket life is `18/q_wall_peak` full-power years. Sum the selected blanket and magnet outages plus a selected 0.5-year lifetime allowance for other nonoverlapping outages. Assert the total is <= `N*(1-A)`. This necessary outage budget does not claim a detailed maintenance schedule; it must not silently adjust A. Replacement dates and discounted contributions are exposed for verification.

The old USD50.617 million/year whole-plant O&M estimate is not added wholesale to conversion 2% service. The design adopts 80% of that estimate as a new assumed common reactor staffing/routine-service allocation, with 50/80/100% sensitivity; conversion service is separately retained. This allocation is a new assumption, not a sourced disjoint decomposition. Fuel, electricity imports and explicit replacements are excluded from the assumed routine-service allocation. No fixed decommissioning reserve is added to the terminal event.

Heating-scenario propagation and independent integration remain required before ranking. Any new material failure must be reported before changing the offer.
